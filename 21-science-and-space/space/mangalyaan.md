# Mangalyaan (Mars Orbiter Mission)

## Why It Matters

Mangalyaan, officially known as the Mars Orbiter Mission (MOM), was India's first interplanetary mission and one of ISRO's most significant achievements.

It demonstrated India's capability to reach Mars using cost-effective indigenous technology and established India as a major spacefaring nation.

## What is Mangalyaan?

Mangalyaan was India's first mission to Mars designed to study the Martian atmosphere, surface features, and mineral composition while demonstrating interplanetary mission capability.
