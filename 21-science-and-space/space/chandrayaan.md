# Chandrayaan Programme

## Why It Matters

The Chandrayaan Programme is India's flagship lunar exploration programme developed by ISRO.

It has demonstrated India's capabilities in deep-space exploration, lunar science, spacecraft engineering, and planetary research.

## What is the Chandrayaan Programme?

The Chandrayaan Programme consists of a series of lunar missions launched by India to study the Moon's surface, composition, atmosphere, and resources.

It is one of India's most successful scientific programmes.

---

## Major Missions

| Mission | Year | Achievement |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Chandrayaan-1 | 2008 | Confirmed Presence of Water Molecules |
| Chandrayaan-2 | 2019 | Orbiter Continues Scientific Observations |
| Chandrayaan-3 | 2023 | Successful Lunar Soft Landing |
