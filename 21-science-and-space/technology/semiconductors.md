# Semiconductors

## Why It Matters

Semiconductors are the foundation of modern electronics and digital technology.

They power computers, smartphones, communication systems, automobiles, defence equipment, artificial intelligence systems, and industrial automation, making them critical for economic growth and national security.

## What are Semiconductors?

Semiconductors are materials whose electrical conductivity lies between conductors and insulators.

They are used to manufacture integrated circuits (ICs), microprocessors, memory chips, and other electronic components.
