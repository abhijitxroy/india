# Atomic Energy Programme

## Why It Matters

India's Atomic Energy Programme aims to develop nuclear technology for peaceful purposes including electricity generation, scientific research, healthcare, agriculture, and strategic capabilities.

It plays an important role in energy security, technological advancement, and long-term national development.

## What is the Atomic Energy Programme?

The Atomic Energy Programme is India's long-term strategy for developing and utilizing nuclear technology for civilian and strategic purposes.

It supports electricity generation, scientific research, medicine, industry, and agriculture.

---

## Three-Stage Nuclear Programme

```text
Stage 1
Pressurised Heavy Water Reactors
        ↓
Stage 2
Fast Breeder Reactors
        ↓
Stage 3
Thorium-Based Reactors
```
