# Rajya Sabha

## Overview

The Rajya Sabha is the Upper House of the Parliament of India.

It is also known as the Council of States because it represents the states and Union Territories in the Union legislative process.

Rajya Sabha plays an important role in law-making, policy discussion, and parliamentary oversight.

---

## Why Study Rajya Sabha?

Studying Rajya Sabha helps understand:

- Federalism
- Parliamentary democracy
- Representation of states
- Legislative review
- Constitutional governance
- National policy discussions

It serves as an important balancing institution within Parliament.

---

## Constitutional Position

```text
States & Union Territories
              ↓
Elected Representatives
              ↓
Rajya Sabha Members
              ↓
Rajya Sabha
```

Rajya Sabha represents the interests of states at the national level.

---

## Permanent House

Unlike Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha cannot be dissolved.

```text
Rajya Sabha
      ↓
Permanent Institution
      ↓
Continuity in Governance
```

A portion of members retire periodically and new members are elected.

---

## Major Functions

### Law-Making

- Debates Bills
- Reviews legislation
- Participates in constitutional amendments

### Federal Representation

- Represents states
- Provides state perspectives on national issues

### Policy Discussion

- Debates important national matters
- Reviews government policies

### Parliamentary Oversight

- Questions ministers
- Discusses administration and governance

---

## Rajya Sabha vs Lok Sabha

| Rajya Sabha | Lok Sabha |
|----------|----------|
| Upper House | Lower House |
| Represents states | Represents people |
| Indirectly elected | Directly elected |
| Permanent House | Can be dissolved |
| Limited role in Money Bills | Greater role in Money Bills |

---

## Chairman of Rajya Sabha

The Vice President of India serves as the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

Responsibilities include:

- Presiding over proceedings
- Maintaining order
- Conducting debates
- Ensuring procedural compliance

---

## Importance in Democracy

Rajya Sabha helps ensure that legislative decisions benefit from wider discussion and representation.

It strengthens both democracy and federalism.

---

## Quick Revision

- Rajya Sabha is the Upper House of Parliament.
- It is called the Council of States.
- Members are elected indirectly.
- Rajya Sabha is a permanent House.
- The Vice President serves as its Chairman.
- It represents states and participates in law-making.
