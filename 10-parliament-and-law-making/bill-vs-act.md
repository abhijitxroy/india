

# Bill vs Act in India

## Overview

A Bill and an Act are closely related but not the same.

Every Act begins as a Bill, but not every Bill becomes an Act.

Understanding the difference is essential for understanding how laws are made in India.

---

## Simple Explanation

```text
Bill
  ↓
Discussion
  ↓
Parliament Approval
  ↓
President's Assent
  ↓
Act
```

A Bill is a proposed law.

An Act is an approved law that is legally enforceable.

---

## What is a Bill?

A Bill is a proposal for a new law or a proposal to amend an existing law.

Bills are introduced in Parliament before becoming law.

### Purpose

- Create a new law
- Modify an existing law
- Repeal an existing law

---

## What is an Act?

An Act is a law that has completed the legislative process.

After approval by Parliament and assent by the President, a Bill becomes an Act.

Acts are enforceable throughout the jurisdiction for which they are enacted.

---

## Bill vs Act

| Bill | Act |
|----------|----------|
| Proposed law | Enacted law |
| Under consideration | Legally enforceable |
| Debated in Parliament | Already approved |
| May or may not pass | Has completed the law-making process |

---

## Law-Making Process

```text
Draft Proposal
       ↓
Bill Introduced
       ↓
Parliament Debate
       ↓
Voting
       ↓
Passed by Both Houses
       ↓
President's Assent
       ↓
Act
```

---

## Example

```text
Bill Stage
      ↓
Right to Information Bill
      ↓
Parliament Approval
      ↓
President's Assent
      ↓
Right to Information Act
```

This illustrates how a proposal becomes a law.

---

## Why the Difference Matters

Understanding the difference helps citizens:

- Follow proposed laws
- Understand parliamentary debates
- Track legislative changes
- Understand when a law becomes enforceable

---

## Quick Revision

- A Bill is a proposed law.
- An Act is an approved law.
- Every Act starts as a Bill.
- Not every Bill becomes an Act.
- A Bill becomes an Act after parliamentary approval and presidential assent.
