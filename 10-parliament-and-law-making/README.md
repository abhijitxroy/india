

# Parliament and Law Making in India

## Overview

Parliament is the highest legislative institution of the Union Government.

It is responsible for making laws, approving government expenditure, debating national issues, and ensuring executive accountability.

This section explains how Parliament functions, how laws are made, and how legislative processes shape governance in India.

---

## Why This Section Matters

Studying Parliament helps understand:

- Law-making
- Democratic governance
- Executive accountability
- Budget approval
- Constitutional amendments
- Parliamentary procedures

Parliament is one of the most important pillars of Indian democracy.

## Lok Sabha vs Rajya Sabha

| Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|----------|----------|
| Lower House | Upper House |
| Represents people | Represents states |
| Directly elected | Indirectly elected |
| Greater role in Money Bills | Limited role in Money Bills |
| Can determine government majority | Cannot determine government majority |

---

## Quick Revision

- Parliament is India's highest legislative institution.
- Parliament consists of the President, Lok Sabha, and Rajya Sabha.
- Bills become Acts after completing the legislative process.
- Parliament controls finances and reviews government actions.
- Parliament is central to democratic governance.
