

# Parliament of India

## Overview

The Parliament of India is the highest legislative institution of the Union Government.

It is responsible for making laws, discussing national issues, approving government expenditure, and ensuring accountability of the executive.

Parliament is one of the most important pillars of Indian democracy.

---

## Why Study Parliament?

Studying Parliament helps understand:

- Law-making
- Democratic governance
- Government accountability
- National policy debates
- Budget approval
- Constitutional amendments

Parliament is the primary institution through which citizens are represented at the national level.

---

## Structure of Parliament

```text
President
      ↓
Parliament
      ↓
├── Lok Sabha
└── Rajya Sabha
```

The Parliament of India consists of:

- President
- Lok Sabha (House of the People)
- Rajya Sabha (Council of States)

---

## How Parliament Functions

```text
Citizens
      ↓
Election
      ↓
Members of Parliament
      ↓
Debate & Discussion
      ↓
Law-Making
      ↓
Governance
```

---

## Major Functions

### Law-Making

- Debates Bills
- Passes laws
- Amends laws

### Financial Control

- Approves Budget
- Authorizes expenditure
- Reviews government finances

### Executive Accountability

- Questions ministers
- Discusses policies
- Reviews government performance

### Constitutional Functions

- Constitutional amendments
- National importance debates
- Institutional oversight

---

## Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha

| Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|----------|----------|
| Lower House | Upper House |
| Directly elected | Indirectly elected |
| Represents people | Represents states |
| Greater role in Money Bills | Reviews legislation |

---

## Parliament and Democracy

Parliament provides a platform where elected representatives discuss issues affecting the country.

It helps ensure democratic decision-making and public accountability.
