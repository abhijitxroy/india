# Political Terminology

## Overview

Political terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions commonly used in politics, governance, public administration, elections, law-making, and public policy.

Understanding these terms helps readers better understand political discussions, government functioning, elections, institutions, and public affairs.

---

## Why Study Political Terminology?

Studying political terminology helps:

- Understand political discussions
- Follow elections and governance issues
- Read policy and government documents
- Improve political awareness
- Build foundational knowledge of Indian politics

---

## Essential Political Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Democracy | Government elected by the people |
| Constitution | Supreme law of the country |
| Parliament | National law-making institution |
| Legislature | Institution that makes laws |
| Executive | Institution that implements laws |
| Judiciary | Institution that interprets laws |
| Election | Process of choosing representatives |
| Voting | Casting a vote for a candidate or party |
| Constituency | Area represented by an elected representative |
| Manifesto | Promises and policies announced by a political party |
| Coalition | Multiple parties forming a government together |
| Opposition | Parties not forming the government |
| Cabinet | Senior ministers assisting the Prime Minister |
| Bill | Proposed law |
| Act | Law approved by Parliament |
| Ordinance | Temporary law issued under constitutional provisions |
| Federalism | Sharing of powers between Centre and States |
| Governance | Process of governing and administration |
| Public Policy | Government action to solve public issues |
| Accountability | Responsibility for actions and decisions |
| Transparency | Openness in government functioning |

---

## Election Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Candidate | Person contesting an election |
| Voter | Citizen eligible to vote |
| Polling Booth | Place where voting takes place |
| Electoral Roll | Official voter list |
| Majority | More than half of total votes or seats |
| Hung Assembly | No party gets a majority |
| By-Election | Election held for a vacant seat |
| Election Commission | Constitutional body conducting elections |

---

## Government Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Prime Minister | Head of Government |
| President | Constitutional Head of State |
| Governor | Constitutional head of a State |
| Chief Minister | Head of State Government |
| Ministry | Government department led by a minister |
| Bureaucracy | Administrative machinery of government |
| Civil Services | Permanent administrative services |

---

## Public Administration Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Policy Implementation | Executing government decisions |
| Service Delivery | Providing public services to citizens |
| Decentralization | Transfer of powers to lower levels |
| Local Governance | Governance at local levels |
| Administrative Reform | Improving government functioning |

---

## Quick Revision

- Democracy = Rule by elected representatives.
- Constitution = Supreme law.
- Parliament = National legislature.
- Election = Selection of representatives.
- Manifesto = Party promises.
- Bill = Proposed law.
- Act = Approved law.
- Federalism = Centre-State power sharing.
- Governance = Running government and administration.
- Public Policy = Government solution to public problems.
