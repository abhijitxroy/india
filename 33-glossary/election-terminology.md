# Election Terminology

## Overview

Election terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in elections, voting, political campaigns, representation, and democratic processes.

Understanding these terms helps explain how elections are conducted and how representatives are chosen in a democratic system.

---

## Why Study Election Terminology?

Studying election terminology helps:

- Understand election processes
- Follow political developments
- Learn democratic procedures
- Understand representation and voting systems
- Build knowledge of electoral institutions

---

## Core Election Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Election | Process of choosing representatives |
| Voter | Citizen eligible to vote |
| Candidate | Person contesting an election |
| Constituency | Area represented by an elected representative |
| Voting | Casting a vote in an election |
| Ballot | Method used to record votes |
| Polling Booth | Place where voting takes place |
| Electoral Roll | Official list of voters |
| Election Campaign | Activities to seek voter support |
| Manifesto | Policies and promises announced by a party |

---

## Political Competition Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Political Party | Organized group seeking political power |
| Coalition | Multiple parties forming a government together |
| Opposition | Parties not forming the government |
| Alliance | Cooperation between political parties |
| Majority | More than half of votes or seats |
| Minority | Less than half of votes or seats |
| Hung Assembly | No party obtains a majority |
| Independent Candidate | Candidate not affiliated with a party |

---

## Election Management Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Election Commission | Constitutional body conducting elections |
| Model Code of Conduct | Guidelines during elections |
| Returning Officer | Official responsible for conducting elections |
| Polling Officer | Official managing polling activities |
| Counting | Process of tallying votes |
| Result Declaration | Official announcement of election results |
| By-Election | Election for a vacant seat |

---

## Representation Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Member of Parliament (MP) | Elected representative in Parliament |
| Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA) | Elected representative in a State Assembly |
| Representation | Acting on behalf of citizens |
| Electoral Mandate | Public approval received through elections |
| Constituency Service | Addressing local public concerns |

---

## Quick Revision

- Election = Choosing representatives.
- Voter = Eligible citizen.
- Candidate = Person contesting elections.
- Constituency = Represented area.
- Manifesto = Party promises.
- Majority = More than half.
- Coalition = Multiple parties governing together.
- Election Commission = Conducts elections.
- By-Election = Election for a vacant seat.
- Electoral Mandate = Public support through voting.
