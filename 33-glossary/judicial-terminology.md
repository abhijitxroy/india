
# Judicial Terminology

## Overview

Judicial terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in courts, law, constitutional interpretation, dispute resolution, justice administration, and the judicial system.

Understanding these terms helps explain how courts function and how justice is administered in India.

---

## Why Study Judicial Terminology?

Studying judicial terminology helps:

- Understand court proceedings
- Learn legal and constitutional concepts
- Follow important judgments
- Understand judicial institutions
- Build knowledge of the justice system

---

## Core Judicial Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Judiciary | Institution that interprets and applies laws |
| Court | Institution that resolves disputes and delivers justice |
| Judge | Official who decides legal cases |
| Judgment | Final decision of a court |
| Order | Direction issued by a court |
| Case | Legal dispute brought before a court |
| Litigation | Process of taking legal action |
| Appeal | Request to review a lower court decision |

---

## Constitutional Judicial Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Judicial Review | Power to examine laws and government actions |
| Judicial Activism | Active role of courts in public matters |
| Constitutional Bench | Bench dealing with constitutional issues |
| Writ | Court order protecting legal or constitutional rights |
| Habeas Corpus | Writ against unlawful detention |
| Mandamus | Order directing a public authority to perform its duty |
| Certiorari | Order to review or quash a lower authority decision |
| Quo Warranto | Challenge to a person's authority to hold office |

---

## Court Structure Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Supreme Court | Highest court of India |
| High Court | Highest court in a State or group of States |
| District Court | Principal court at district level |
| Subordinate Court | Court below district level |
| Bench | Group of judges hearing a case |
| Chief Justice | Head of a court |

---

## Legal Process Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Petition | Formal request submitted to a court |
| Plaintiff | Party bringing a civil case |
| Defendant | Party against whom a case is filed |
| Evidence | Information used to prove facts |
| Hearing | Court session to examine a matter |
| Verdict | Decision in a case |
| Bail | Temporary release from custody under conditions |

---

## Quick Revision

- Judiciary = Interprets laws.
- Court = Delivers justice.
- Judge = Decides cases.
- Judgment = Court decision.
- Appeal = Review of a decision.
- Judicial Review = Court examination of laws.
- Writ = Court order protecting rights.
- Supreme Court = Highest court.
- High Court = Highest State-level court.
- Evidence = Information used to prove facts.
