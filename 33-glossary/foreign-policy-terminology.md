# Foreign Policy Terminology

## Overview

Foreign policy terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in diplomacy, international relations, national security, defence, strategic affairs, and global governance.

Understanding these terms helps explain how countries interact, cooperate, compete, and protect their national interests in the international system.

---

## Why Study Foreign Policy Terminology?

Studying foreign policy terminology helps:

- Understand international relations
- Follow diplomatic developments
- Learn foreign policy concepts
- Understand national security discussions
- Build knowledge of global affairs

---

## Core Foreign Policy Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Foreign Policy | A country's approach to international relations |
| Diplomacy | Managing relations between countries |
| National Interest | Goals and priorities of a country |
| Sovereignty | Independent authority of a nation |
| Strategic Autonomy | Independent decision-making in foreign affairs |
| Bilateral Relations | Relations between two countries |
| Multilateral Relations | Relations involving multiple countries |
| International Relations | Study and practice of interactions among countries |

---

## Diplomacy Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Ambassador | Official representative of a country |
| Embassy | Official diplomatic office in another country |
| Consulate | Office providing services to citizens abroad |
| Treaty | Formal agreement between countries |
| Agreement | Understanding reached between countries |
| Negotiation | Discussion to reach a common understanding |
| Summit | Meeting of national leaders |
| Dialogue | Formal discussions between parties |

---

## Security & Defence Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| National Security | Protection of national interests and sovereignty |
| Defence Policy | Strategy for protecting the country |
| Deterrence | Preventing threats through strength and capability |
| Nuclear Doctrine | Policy guiding nuclear strategy |
| Strategic Partnership | Long-term cooperation between countries |
| Counter-Terrorism | Measures against terrorism |
| Border Security | Protection of national borders |

## Quick Revision

- Foreign Policy = International strategy of a country.
- Diplomacy = Managing international relations.
- National Interest = Country's priorities.
- Strategic Autonomy = Independent foreign policy decisions.
- Bilateral Relations = Relations between two countries.
- Multilateral Relations = Relations among multiple countries.
- Deterrence = Preventing threats through capability.
- UN = Global cooperation organization.
- BRICS = Emerging economies grouping.
- G20 = Major economies forum.
