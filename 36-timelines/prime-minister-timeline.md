
# Prime Minister Timeline

## Overview

This timeline explains the succession of Prime Ministers of India from Independence to the present day.

The objective is to help readers understand how political leadership, governance priorities, economic reforms, foreign policy decisions, and national development evolved over time.

---

## Historical Significance

Understanding the Prime Minister timeline helps explain:

- Evolution of Indian democracy
- Major governance changes
- Economic reform periods
- Political transitions
- National development priorities

---

## Prime Minister Timeline

| Period | Prime Minister | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1947-1964 | Jawaharlal Nehru | First Prime Minister, nation-building and institution creation |
| 1964-1966 | Lal Bahadur Shastri | Leadership during a critical transition period |
| 1966-1977 | Indira Gandhi | Major political and economic decisions |
| 1977-1979 | Morarji Desai | First non-Congress Prime Minister |
| 1979-1980 | Charan Singh | Short-term coalition leadership |
| 1980-1984 | Indira Gandhi | Return to office |
| 1984-1989 | Rajiv Gandhi | Technology and modernization initiatives |
| 1989-1990 | V. P. Singh | Coalition politics expansion |
| 1990-1991 | Chandra Shekhar | Transitional government |
| 1991-1996 | P. V. Narasimha Rao | Economic liberalization era |
| 1996 | Atal Bihari Vajpayee | First term |
| 1996-1997 | H. D. Deve Gowda | Coalition government period |
| 1997-1998 | I. K. Gujral | Coalition governance continuation |
| 1998-2004 | Atal Bihari Vajpayee | Coalition stability and infrastructure focus |
| 2004-2014 | Manmohan Singh | Economic growth and policy reforms |
| 2014-Present | Narendra Modi | Governance, digital transformation, and infrastructure expansion |

## Major Turning Points

| Period | Importance |
|----------|----------|
| Nehru Era | Institution building and democratic foundations |
| Indira Gandhi Era | Centralization and major political developments |
| 1977 Transition | First change of ruling party at Centre |
| 1991 Reforms | Economic transformation |
| Coalition Era | Multi-party governance |
| Post-2014 | Majority government era returns |
