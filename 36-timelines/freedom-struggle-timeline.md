
# Freedom Struggle Timeline

## Overview

This timeline explains the major events, movements, leaders, and milestones of India's freedom struggle against British rule.

The objective is to help readers understand how the Indian independence movement evolved and ultimately led to the creation of an independent democratic nation.

---

## Why It Matters

Understanding the freedom struggle helps explain:

- How India gained independence
- Evolution of nationalist movements
- Sacrifices made by freedom fighters
- Development of democratic ideals
- Origins of modern India

---

## Freedom Struggle Timeline

| Year | Event | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1757 | Battle of Plassey | Beginning of British political dominance |
| 1857 | Revolt of 1857 | First large-scale resistance against British rule |
| 1885 | Indian National Congress Founded | Organized political movement begins |
| 1905 | Partition of Bengal | Rise of mass nationalist opposition |
| 1906 | Muslim League Founded | Important political development |
| 1915 | Gandhi Returns to India | New phase of mass mobilization |
| 1919 | Jallianwala Bagh Massacre | Major turning point in nationalist sentiment |
| 1920 | Non-Cooperation Movement | Mass participation in freedom struggle |
| 1930 | Dandi March | Symbolic challenge to colonial rule |
| 1930-34 | Civil Disobedience Movement | Nationwide resistance campaign |
| 1942 | Quit India Movement | Demand for immediate independence |
| 1947 | Independence | End of British rule |
