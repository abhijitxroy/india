# District Administration

## Importance

District administration is the primary link between citizens and the government. Most public services, law and order functions, development programmes, revenue administration, and disaster management activities are coordinated at the district level.

## District Administration Structure

### District Magistrate / District Collector

- Head of district administration
- Coordinates government departments
- Maintains law and order
- Supervises revenue administration
- Leads disaster management activities

### Superintendent of Police

- Head of district police
- Responsible for public safety
- Crime prevention and investigation
- Law enforcement operations

### Subdivision Administration

- Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)
- Administrative coordination below district level
- Revenue and executive functions

### Block Administration

- Block Development Officer (BDO)
- Rural development implementation
- Coordination with Panchayati Raj institutions
