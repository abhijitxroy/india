

# Urban Local Bodies

## Overview

Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) are institutions of local self-government responsible for administration and development in urban areas.

They provide civic services, urban infrastructure, local planning, and public welfare.

Urban Local Bodies were strengthened through the 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.

## Importance

Urban Local Bodies help:

- Strengthen local democracy
- Improve civic service delivery
- Support urban development
- Promote citizen participation
- Enhance local accountability
- Address city and town-level challenges

## Types of Urban Local Bodies

India has three major categories of Urban Local Bodies.

```text
Urban Local Bodies
│
├── Municipal Corporation
├── Municipality
└── Nagar Panchayat
```

## Comparison of Urban Local Bodies

| Type | Area Covered | Political Head | Typical Size | Primary Focus |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Municipal Corporation | Large cities | Mayor | Large population | Major urban governance |
| Municipality | Medium towns | Chairperson | Medium population | Town administration |
| Nagar Panchayat | Transitional urban areas | Chairperson | Smaller population | Rural-to-urban transition |

## Common Functions

### Civic Services

- Water supply
- Sanitation
- Waste management
- Street lighting
- Public health services

### Infrastructure Development

- Roads
- Drainage systems
- Public facilities
- Urban transport support

### Urban Planning

- Building approvals
- Land-use regulation
- Development planning

### Welfare Activities

- Community development
- Social welfare programmes
- Environmental initiatives

## Constitutional Framework

The 74th Constitutional Amendment:

- Provided constitutional status to Urban Local Bodies
- Strengthened local democracy
- Introduced regular elections
- Encouraged decentralisation of governance
- Improved citizen participation

## Elections

Representatives of Urban Local Bodies are elected through municipal elections conducted by State Election Commissions.

Urban areas are divided into wards, and each ward elects councillors.

## Revenue Sources

Urban Local Bodies generate revenue through:

- Property taxes
- User charges
- Fees and licences
- State government grants
- Central government grants

## Challenges

Common challenges include:

- Rapid urbanisation
- Infrastructure pressure
- Financial constraints
- Environmental concerns
- Traffic congestion
- Population growth
