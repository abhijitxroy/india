# Panchayati Raj

## Importance

Panchayati Raj is the system of rural local self-government in India. It enables democratic participation at the grassroots level and allows local communities to take part in planning and development activities.

## Constitutional Foundation

The Panchayati Raj system received constitutional status through the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.

Key objectives include:

- Decentralization of power
- Local self-governance
- People's participation
- Rural development
- Democratic accountability

## Three-Tier Structure

```text
District Level
└── Zila Parishad

Block Level
└── Panchayat Samiti

Village Level
└── Gram Panchayat
```

## Gram Sabha

The Gram Sabha consists of all eligible voters within a village.

It serves as the foundation of rural democracy and helps ensure accountability of local government institutions.

## Major Functions

- Local development planning
- Implementation of welfare programmes
- Rural infrastructure development
- Public service delivery
- Community participation
- Monitoring local administration

## Benefits

- Brings governance closer to citizens
- Encourages local participation
- Improves accountability
- Supports rural development
- Strengthens democracy at the grassroots level
