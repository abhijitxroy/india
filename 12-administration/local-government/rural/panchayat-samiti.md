# Panchayat Samiti

## Overview

Panchayat Samiti is the intermediate tier of the Panchayati Raj system.

It operates at the Block or Taluk level and acts as a link between Gram Panchayats and the Zila Parishad.

The institution coordinates development activities across multiple villages within a block.

## Importance

Panchayat Samiti plays a key role in:

- Rural development planning
- Coordination among Gram Panchayats
- Implementation of government schemes
- Infrastructure development
- Agriculture and irrigation support
- Monitoring local administration

## Position in the Panchayati Raj Structure

```text
Zila Parishad
      ↓
Panchayat Samiti
      ↓
Gram Panchayat
```

## Composition

A Panchayat Samiti generally consists of:

- Elected representatives from the block
- Members of the Legislative Assembly from the area
- Members of Parliament representing the constituency
- Representatives from Gram Panchayats
- Government officials in advisory roles

## Major Functions

### Development Planning

- Prepare block-level development plans
- Coordinate projects across villages
- Allocate resources to local priorities

### Agriculture and Rural Economy

- Support agricultural programmes
- Promote irrigation projects
- Assist rural industries and self-employment initiatives

### Infrastructure Development

- Rural roads
- Drinking water projects
- Public facilities
- Community assets

### Welfare Programmes

- Poverty alleviation schemes
- Social welfare programmes
- Education and health initiatives

## Relationship with Gram Panchayats

Panchayat Samiti supervises and coordinates the work of Gram Panchayats within the block.

It helps resolve inter-village issues and ensures effective implementation of development programmes.

## Relationship with Zila Parishad

Panchayat Samiti functions under the broader district-level planning framework provided by the Zila Parishad.

Development priorities and resources are coordinated between the two levels.
