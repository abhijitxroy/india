# Zila Parishad

## Importance

Zila Parishad is the highest tier of the Panchayati Raj system at the district level. It coordinates development activities across the district and connects rural local governance with district administration.

## Position in Panchayati Raj

```text
Zila Parishad
      ↑
Panchayat Samiti
      ↑
Gram Panchayat
```

## Composition

A Zila Parishad generally consists of:

- Elected representatives from district constituencies
- Chairpersons of Panchayat Samitis
- Members of Parliament and State Legislatures from the district (as per state laws)
- Other nominated members where applicable

## Major Responsibilities

- District-level development planning
- Coordination of Panchayat Samitis
- Monitoring rural development programmes
- Infrastructure development
- Education and healthcare support
- Agricultural and rural welfare initiatives

## Relationship with District Administration

The Zila Parishad works alongside district administration to implement development programmes and improve public service delivery in rural areas.

## Key Facts

- Highest rural local government body at district level
- Part of the three-tier Panchayati Raj system
- Focuses on district-wide rural development
- Coordinates activities across blocks and villages
