
# Indian Police Service (IPS)

## Overview

The Indian Police Service (IPS) is one of the All India Services responsible for policing, public safety, law and order, internal security, and crime prevention.

IPS officers serve at both the Union and State Government levels and play a crucial role in maintaining peace, enforcing laws, and protecting citizens.

The service is a key pillar of India's internal security and governance framework.

---

## Why Study IPS?

Studying the IPS helps understand:

- Law and order administration
- Internal security
- Crime prevention
- Police leadership
- Public safety
- Governance and administration

The IPS is one of the most visible public services in India.

## Major Functions

### Law and Order

- Maintain public order
- Prevent disturbances
- Support peaceful governance

### Crime Prevention

- Investigate crimes
- Strengthen policing systems
- Improve public safety measures

### Internal Security

- Support national security efforts
- Counter security threats
- Coordinate security operations

### Public Protection

- Protect citizens
- Support emergency response
- Assist during disasters and crises
