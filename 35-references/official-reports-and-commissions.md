
# Official Reports & Commissions

## Overview

This document lists important commissions, committees, expert bodies, and official reports that have significantly influenced governance, public administration, constitutional development, economic reforms, social justice, and public policy in India.

---

## Why Use This Resource

Official reports and commissions often:

- Identify governance challenges
- Recommend reforms
- Influence legislation
- Shape public policy
- Guide institutional development

Understanding them helps citizens see how major policy decisions evolve.

---

## Major Commissions & Reports

### Constituent Assembly Committees

Contributed to the drafting of the Constitution.

Key Contributions:

- Constitutional design
- Institutional structure
- Rights framework
- Federal governance model

---

### States Reorganisation Commission (1953)

Recommended reorganization of states.

Significance:

- Linguistic state formation
- Federal restructuring
- Administrative efficiency

---

### Sarkaria Commission (1983)

Examined Centre-State relations.

Focus Areas:

- Federalism
- Administrative coordination
- Constitutional balance

---

### Mandal Commission (1979)

Studied social and educational backwardness.

Impact:

- Reservation policy debates
- Social justice reforms
- OBC policy framework

---

### Punchhi Commission (2007)

Reviewed Centre-State relations.

Key Themes:

- Cooperative federalism
- Governance reforms
- Institutional coordination

---

### Finance Commission Reports

Periodic constitutional commissions.

Responsibilities:

- Tax devolution
- Fiscal federalism
- Resource allocation

---

### Law Commission Reports

Recommendations on legal and judicial reforms.

Areas:

- Legal modernization
- Judicial efficiency
- Legislative reforms
