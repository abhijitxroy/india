
# Judiciary Resources

## Overview

This document lists important judicial resources that help citizens understand India's court system, constitutional interpretation, rule of law, judicial processes, and landmark judgments.

---

## Why Use This Resource

Understanding judicial resources helps citizens learn:

- How courts function
- How rights are protected
- How disputes are resolved
- How constitutional interpretation evolves
- How judicial accountability supports democracy

---

## Primary Judicial Resources

### Supreme Court of India

Highest constitutional court in India.

Key Areas:

- Constitutional interpretation
- Fundamental Rights protection
- Judicial review
- Appeals from lower courts
- Public Interest Litigation (PIL)

---

### High Courts

Highest courts within states and union territories.

Responsibilities:

- Constitutional matters
- Civil cases
- Criminal cases
- Writ jurisdiction

---

### District Judiciary

Foundation of the judicial system.

Handles:

- Civil disputes
- Criminal cases
- Local legal matters

---

### e-Courts System

Digital platform supporting judicial services.

Useful For:

- Case status tracking
- Cause lists
- Court orders
- Digital access to judicial information

---

## Important Judicial Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| Judicial Review | Court review of laws and executive actions |
| Rule of Law | Everyone is subject to the law |
| PIL | Public Interest Litigation |
| Writ Jurisdiction | Constitutional remedies |
| Due Process | Fair legal procedures |
| Appeal | Review by a higher court |
| Precedent | Earlier judgments guiding future cases |
| Basic Structure Doctrine | Constitutional protection principle |
