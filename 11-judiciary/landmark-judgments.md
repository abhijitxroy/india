

# Landmark Judgments of India

## Overview

Landmark judgments are important court decisions that significantly influenced constitutional interpretation, governance, rights, public policy, and the development of Indian democracy.

These judgments help explain how the Constitution has evolved through judicial interpretation.

---

## Why Study Landmark Judgments?

Studying landmark judgments helps understand:

- Constitutional evolution
- Fundamental rights
- Judicial Review
- Separation of powers
- Federalism
- Democratic governance

Many constitutional principles used today emerged through judicial decisions.

---

## Importance of Judicial Decisions

```text
Constitution
      ↓

```

Judgments often clarify how constitutional provisions should be applied.

---

## Major Landmark Judgments

### Kesavananda Bharati Case (1973)

Significance:

- Introduced the Basic Structure Doctrine
- Limited Parliament's amendment powers
- Protected core constitutional principles

---

### Maneka Gandhi Case (1978)

Significance:

- Expanded interpretation of personal liberty
- Strengthened Fundamental Rights protections
- Influenced due process jurisprudence

---

### S.R. Bommai Case (1994)

Significance:

- Strengthened federalism
- Clarified use of President's Rule
- Protected democratic governance in states

---

### Indra Sawhney Case (1992)

Significance:

- Addressed reservation policies
- Clarified affirmative action principles
- Influenced public employment policies

---

### Vishaka Case (1997)

Significance:

- Established workplace protection guidelines
- Advanced gender justice principles
- Influenced later legislation
