

# Judiciary of India

## Overview

The Judiciary is one of the three pillars of the Indian constitutional system.

It is responsible for interpreting laws, protecting rights, resolving disputes, and ensuring that all institutions operate within constitutional limits.

This section explains the structure of the judiciary, the role of courts, protection of Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, and landmark judicial developments.

---

## Why This Section Matters

Studying the judiciary helps understand:

- Rule of Law
- Constitutional Supremacy
- Fundamental Rights Protection
- Judicial Review
- Checks and Balances
- Democratic Governance

The judiciary acts as the guardian of the Constitution.
