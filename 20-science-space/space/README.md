# Space Programme

## Why It Matters

India's space programme is one of the most successful and cost-effective space programmes in the world.

It supports communication, navigation, weather forecasting, disaster management, scientific research, national security, and space exploration.

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) leads India's civilian space activities.
