# Gaganyaan Mission

## Why It Matters

Gaganyaan is India's first human spaceflight programme developed by ISRO.

It aims to demonstrate India's capability to send astronauts into space, safely operate crewed missions, and return them to Earth, marking a major milestone in India's space journey.

## What is Gaganyaan?

Gaganyaan is India's first crewed space mission programme designed to carry Indian astronauts into low Earth orbit and safely return them to Earth.

The mission represents a major step in advanced space technology and national scientific capability.
