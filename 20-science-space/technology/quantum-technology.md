# Quantum Technology

## Why It Matters

Quantum Technology uses the principles of quantum mechanics to develop advanced computing, communication, sensing, and security systems.

It is expected to transform fields such as cybersecurity, scientific research, defence, healthcare, finance, and artificial intelligence.

## What is Quantum Technology?

Quantum Technology applies quantum mechanical phenomena such as superposition, entanglement, and quantum tunnelling to perform tasks that are difficult or impossible for classical systems.
