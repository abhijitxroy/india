# Services Sector

## Economic Importance

The services sector is the largest contributor to India's GDP and plays a major role in employment, innovation, exports, and economic growth.

It includes industries such as information technology, banking, telecommunications, healthcare, education, tourism, and professional services.

## What is the Services Sector?

The services sector consists of economic activities that provide intangible services rather than physical goods.

It supports businesses, consumers, and government operations across the economy.
