# Economic Basics

## Why It Matters

Economic indicators help measure the health, growth, stability, and performance of an economy.

Understanding these concepts is essential before learning banking, taxation, trade, public finance, or economic policy.

### GDP

Measures the total value of goods and services produced.

### Inflation

Measures the rise in prices over time.

### Fiscal Deficit

Difference between government expenditure and revenue.

### Union Budget

Annual financial statement of the Government of India.
