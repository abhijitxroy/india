# Fiscal Deficit

## Economic Importance

Fiscal deficit measures the gap between a government's total expenditure and its total revenue excluding borrowings.

It is an important indicator of the financial health of the government and helps assess borrowing requirements, public finances, and fiscal discipline.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Measures | Government Borrowing Requirement |
| Calculated As | Expenditure Minus Revenue |
| Importance | Fiscal Health Indicator |
| Used By | Government, RBI, Investors |

---

## What is Fiscal Deficit?

Fiscal deficit occurs when government expenditure exceeds government revenue (excluding borrowings) during a financial year.

The gap is usually financed through borrowing.

---

## Fiscal Deficit Formula

```text
Fiscal Deficit
=
Total Expenditure
−
Total Revenue Receipts
```

---

## Sources of Government Revenue

| Source | Example |
|----------|----------|
| Tax Revenue | Income Tax, GST |
| Non-Tax Revenue | Dividends, Fees |
| Capital Receipts | Asset Sales |

---

## Why Fiscal Deficit Occurs

- Higher Government Spending
- Lower Tax Collection
- Economic Slowdown
- Welfare Programmes
- Infrastructure Investment

---

## Impact of Fiscal Deficit

```text
Higher Spending
       ↓
Borrowing
       ↓
Public Debt
       ↓
Economic Impact
```
