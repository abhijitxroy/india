# Inflation

## Economic Importance

Inflation refers to the sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services over time.

It affects purchasing power, savings, investments, business decisions, interest rates, and overall economic stability.

## What is Inflation?

Inflation occurs when the prices of goods and services increase over time, reducing the purchasing power of money.

A moderate level of inflation is considered normal in a growing economy.

---

## Inflation Formula

```text
Inflation Rate
=
(Current Price Index − Previous Price Index)
÷ Previous Price Index × 100
```

---

## Types of Inflation

| Type | Description |
|----------|----------|
| Demand-Pull Inflation | Demand Exceeds Supply |
| Cost-Push Inflation | Production Costs Increase |
| Core Inflation | Excludes Food and Fuel |
| Headline Inflation | Includes All Components |

---

## Causes of Inflation

- Increased Consumer Demand
- Rising Input Costs
- Supply Chain Disruptions
- Higher Energy Prices
- Currency Depreciation

---

## Impact of Inflation

```text
Higher Prices
       ↓
Lower Purchasing Power
       ↓
Consumer Impact
       ↓
Economic Adjustment
```

---

## Advantages and Risks

| Advantages | Risks |
|----------|----------|
| Encourages Spending | Reduced Purchasing Power |
| Supports Growth | Higher Cost of Living |
| Promotes Investment | Savings Erosion |
| Signals Demand | Economic Instability |

---

## How RBI Controls Inflation

- Repo Rate Changes
- Monetary Policy Measures
- Liquidity Management
- Open Market Operations
