
# Infrastructure

## Why It Matters

Infrastructure forms the foundation of economic growth by enabling transportation, logistics, communication, energy supply, and industrial development.

Strong infrastructure improves productivity, attracts investment, creates jobs, and supports long-term national development.

| Energy | Power Generation and Distribution |

### Transport

Roads, railways, ports, and airports.

### Energy

### Logistics

Supply chains and freight movement.

### Digital Infrastructure

Telecom networks, internet connectivity, and digital services.

### Urban Development

Smart cities, housing, and public infrastructure.
