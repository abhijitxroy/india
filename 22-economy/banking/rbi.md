# Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

## Economic Importance

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is India's central bank and the most important institution responsible for monetary policy, banking regulation, currency management, and financial stability.

It plays a critical role in controlling inflation, managing liquidity, regulating banks, and supporting economic growth.

## What is RBI?

The RBI is the central banking institution of India.

It regulates the banking system, issues currency, manages foreign exchange reserves, and formulates monetary policy.

---

## Major Functions

| Function | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| Monetary Policy | Control Inflation and Liquidity |
| Currency Issuance | Issue and Manage Currency |
| Banking Regulation | Supervise Banks |
| Government Banker | Manage Government Accounts |
| Forex Management | Manage Foreign Exchange Reserves |
| Financial Stability | Maintain Stability of Financial System |

## Monetary Policy Role

The RBI uses policy tools such as:

- Repo Rate
- Reverse Repo Rate
- Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR)
- Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)
- Open Market Operations (OMO)

---
