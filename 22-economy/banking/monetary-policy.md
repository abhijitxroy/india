# Monetary Policy

## Why It Matters

Monetary policy refers to the actions taken by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to manage money supply, interest rates, inflation, and liquidity in the economy.

It is one of the most important tools for maintaining price stability and supporting economic growth.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Authority | Reserve Bank of India |
| Main Objective | Price Stability and Growth |
| Key Tool | Repo Rate |
| Policy Body | Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) |

---

## What is Monetary Policy?

Monetary policy is the process through which the RBI regulates the availability and cost of money in the economy.

It helps control inflation, support economic activity, and maintain financial stability.

---

## Objectives

- Control Inflation
- Maintain Price Stability
- Support Economic Growth
- Ensure Financial Stability
- Manage Liquidity
- Promote Credit Flow

---

## Major Policy Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| Repo Rate | Borrowing Rate for Banks |
| Reverse Repo Rate | Deposit Rate for Banks |
| CRR | Cash Reserve Requirement |
| SLR | Statutory Liquidity Requirement |
| Open Market Operations | Liquidity Management |

---

## Policy Transmission

```text
RBI Decision
      ↓
Interest Rates
      ↓
Bank Lending
      ↓
Consumption and Investment
      ↓
Economic Activity
```

---

## Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)

The MPC is responsible for setting India's policy interest rates.

It consists of six members and meets regularly to review inflation and economic conditions.

---

## Expansionary vs Contractionary Policy

| Type | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| Expansionary | Increase Growth and Liquidity |
| Contractionary | Reduce Inflation |
