
# NITI Aayog

## Overview

NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India) is a policy think tank established to support long-term development planning, policy innovation, cooperative federalism, and economic transformation in India.

It replaced the Planning Commission and introduced a new approach to policy formulation and development strategy.

NITI Aayog is one of the most important institutional transformations in modern India's governance and economic framework.

---

## Why Study NITI Aayog?

Studying NITI Aayog helps understand:

- Economic planning
- Public policy
- Governance reforms
- Cooperative federalism
- Development strategy
- Institutional transformation

It plays an important role in shaping India's long-term development vision.
