
# White Revolution

## Overview

The White Revolution transformed India's dairy sector and helped make the country one of the world's largest milk producers.

Through improvements in dairy production, cooperative systems, rural participation, and supply chains, the movement significantly increased milk availability and improved rural livelihoods.

It is one of the most successful agricultural and economic transformations in modern India.

---

## Why Study the White Revolution?

Studying the White Revolution helps understand:

- Rural development
- Dairy economy
- Cooperative movements
- Agricultural transformation
- Food security
- Economic development

It demonstrates how organized rural participation can drive large-scale economic change.

---

## Historical Background

```text
Low Milk Production
         ↓
Need for Dairy Development
         ↓
Cooperative Movement
         ↓
White Revolution
```

The movement emerged from efforts to improve dairy production and strengthen rural incomes.

## Main Objectives

The White Revolution aimed to:

- Increase milk production
- Improve rural livelihoods
- Strengthen dairy cooperatives
- Expand dairy infrastructure
- Improve nutrition and food availability

## Green Revolution vs White Revolution

| Green Revolution | White Revolution |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Focused on food grain production | Focused on milk production |
| Agricultural productivity growth | Dairy sector growth |
| Food security emphasis | Dairy and nutrition emphasis |
| Crop-based transformation | Livestock-based transformation |
