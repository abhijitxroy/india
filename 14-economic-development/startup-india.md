
# Startup India

## Overview

Startup India is a major initiative aimed at promoting entrepreneurship, innovation, job creation, and the growth of startup ecosystems across India.

The initiative seeks to support new businesses, encourage innovation-driven economic growth, and strengthen India's position as a global startup destination.

It is one of the most important entrepreneurship-focused economic transformations in modern India.

---

## Why Study Startup India?

Studying Startup India helps understand:

- Entrepreneurship
- Innovation ecosystems
- Economic development
- Job creation
- Technology-driven growth
- Startup culture in India

The initiative has played an important role in expanding India's innovation economy.
