# Digital India

## Overview

Digital India is a major national initiative aimed at transforming India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy.

The initiative focuses on expanding digital infrastructure, improving access to government services, promoting digital literacy, and accelerating the adoption of technology across sectors.

It is one of the most important digital transformation programs in modern India.

---

## Why Study Digital India?

Studying Digital India helps understand:

- Digital governance
- Technology-led development
- Public service delivery
- Digital infrastructure
- Economic modernization
- Digital inclusion

The initiative has significantly influenced governance, business, and citizen services.

---
