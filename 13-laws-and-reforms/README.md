# Major Laws and Reforms in India

## Overview

This section covers the most important laws, constitutional changes, governance reforms, economic reforms, and social justice reforms that significantly changed India's political, administrative, economic, and social landscape.

The focus is on understanding:

- Why the reform happened
- What problem it tried to solve
- What changed after implementation
- Long-term impact on India
- Political and governance significance

---

## Why This Section Matters

Major laws and reforms explain how India evolved from:

```text
Constitution
      ↓
Governance
      ↓
Reforms
      ↓
Institutional Change
      ↓
Modern India
```

Understanding reforms helps connect history, politics, governance, economy, and public policy.
