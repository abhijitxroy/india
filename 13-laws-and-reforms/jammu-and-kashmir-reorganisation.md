
# Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019

## Overview

The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 introduced major administrative and constitutional changes in the governance structure of Jammu and Kashmir.

The Act reorganized the former State of Jammu and Kashmir and established a new administrative framework.

It is one of the most significant governance reforms in contemporary India.

---

## Why Study the Reorganisation Act?

Studying the Act helps understand:

- Constitutional reforms
- Federalism in India
- Administrative restructuring
- Centre–State relations
- Governance reforms
- Contemporary political developments

It is closely linked with the constitutional changes of 2019.
