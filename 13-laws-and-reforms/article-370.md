# Article 370

## Overview

Article 370 was a constitutional provision that granted special status to the former State of Jammu and Kashmir.

It defined the constitutional relationship between Jammu and Kashmir and the Union of India and provided the state with certain autonomous powers.

In August 2019, major constitutional changes altered this arrangement.

---

## Why Study Article 370?

Studying Article 370 helps understand:

- Constitutional federalism
- Special constitutional provisions
- India–Jammu and Kashmir relations
- Constitutional amendments and reforms
- Centre–State relations
- Contemporary Indian politics

It is one of the most debated constitutional provisions in modern India.

## 2019 Constitutional Changes

In August 2019:

- Article 370 provisions were substantially changed.
- Jammu and Kashmir was reorganized.
- New administrative arrangements were introduced.
- The constitutional relationship with the Union changed significantly.

---

## Arguments in Public Debate

### Supporters of the Change

Common arguments included:

- Greater integration with the Union
- Uniform application of laws
- Administrative simplification
- Development and governance benefits

### Critics of the Change

Common arguments included:

- Federalism concerns
- Constitutional process questions
- Regional representation concerns
- Autonomy-related issues
