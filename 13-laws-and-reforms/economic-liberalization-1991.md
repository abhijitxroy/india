
# Economic Liberalization (1991)

## Overview

Economic Liberalization refers to the major economic reforms introduced in India in 1991 to address economic challenges and accelerate growth.

The reforms reduced government controls in several sectors, encouraged private enterprise, increased integration with the global economy, and transformed India's economic model.

Economic Liberalization is widely regarded as one of the most significant policy reforms in independent India.

---

## Why Study Economic Liberalization?

Studying Economic Liberalization helps understand:

- Economic reforms
- Public policy
- Development strategy
- Globalization
- Market-oriented reforms
- Modern Indian economy

The reforms fundamentally changed India's economic trajectory.
