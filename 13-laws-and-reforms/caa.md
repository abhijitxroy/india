# Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA)

## Overview

The Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA), 2019 amended India's citizenship law by introducing specific provisions related to citizenship eligibility for certain groups of migrants from neighboring countries.

The Act became one of the most widely discussed and debated laws in contemporary India.

---

## Why Study CAA?

Studying the CAA helps understand:

- Citizenship laws
- Constitutional debates
- Migration issues
- Humanitarian considerations
- Public policy reforms
- Contemporary Indian politics

It is an important topic in modern governance and legal discussions.

## Main Objective

The Act sought to modify citizenship eligibility rules for specific categories of migrants who entered India from certain neighboring countries.

Supporters and critics have interpreted the objectives and implications differently.
