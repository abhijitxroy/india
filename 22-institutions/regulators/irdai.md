# Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)

## Why It Matters

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) is the statutory regulator responsible for regulating and developing the insurance sector in India.

It protects policyholders, promotes fair practices, ensures financial stability of insurers, and supports the growth of the insurance industry.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India |
| Established | 1999 |
| Type | Statutory Regulator |
| Legal Basis | IRDA Act, 1999 |
| Sector | Insurance |

---

## What is IRDAI?

IRDAI is India's insurance regulator responsible for supervising insurance companies, protecting policyholders, and ensuring orderly growth of the insurance sector.
