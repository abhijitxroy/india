# Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Why It Matters

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is the statutory regulator of India's securities and capital markets.

It protects investors, regulates market participants, ensures fair trading practices, and promotes the development of efficient and transparent financial markets.

## What is SEBI?

SEBI is India's capital market regulator responsible for overseeing stock exchanges, listed companies, mutual funds, brokers, and other market intermediaries.

It works to protect investor interests and maintain confidence in financial markets.
