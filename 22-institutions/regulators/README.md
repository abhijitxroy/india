# Regulators

## Why It Matters

Regulators supervise and regulate important sectors of the economy to ensure stability, competition, transparency, consumer protection, and fair market practices.

They create rules, monitor compliance, and help maintain confidence in financial markets, telecommunications, insurance, and other critical sectors.
