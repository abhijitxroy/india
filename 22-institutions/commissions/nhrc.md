# National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)

## Why It Matters

The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) is India's premier human rights institution responsible for protecting and promoting human rights.

It investigates violations, reviews safeguards, recommends reforms, and helps strengthen the protection of fundamental human rights.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Name | National Human Rights Commission |

---

## What is NHRC?

NHRC is an independent statutory body established to safeguard human rights and address complaints related to human rights violations.

It acts as a watchdog institution for the protection of dignity, liberty, equality, and justice.

---

## Major Functions

| Function | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| Complaint Investigation | Examine Human Rights Violations |
| Safeguard Review | Review Existing Protections |

| Research | Study Human Rights Issues |

## Key Responsibilities

- Investigate Complaints
- Review Constitutional Safeguards
- Recommend Corrective Measures
- Promote Human Rights Awareness
- Support Research and Education
- Monitor Human Rights Conditions

---

## Importance of NHRC

- Protects Individual Rights
- Strengthens Rule of Law
- Supports Accountability
- Promotes Human Dignity
- Encourages Good Governance
- Improves Public Awareness

---

## Limitations

- Recommendatory Powers Only
- Dependence on Government Action
- Large Complaint Volume
- Resource Constraints
