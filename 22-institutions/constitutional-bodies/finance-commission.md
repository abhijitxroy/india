# Finance Commission

## Why It Matters

The Finance Commission is a constitutional body responsible for recommending the distribution of financial resources between the Union Government and State Governments.

It plays a vital role in strengthening fiscal federalism, ensuring balanced development, and promoting financial stability across India.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Constitutional Basis | Article 280 |
| Type | Constitutional Body |
| Appointed By | President of India |
| Main Role | Distribution of Tax Revenue |
| Frequency | Every Five Years |

---

## What is the Finance Commission?

The Finance Commission is constituted by the President of India to recommend how taxes collected by the Union Government should be shared with states.

It also recommends grants-in-aid and measures to improve the financial position of states.

---

## Major Functions

| Function | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| Tax Devolution | Share Central Taxes with States |
| Grants-in-Aid | Financial Support to States |
| Fiscal Recommendations | Improve Public Finances |
| Federal Balance | Strengthen Centre-State Relations |

---

## Finance Commission Process

```text
President Constitutes Commission
            ↓
Data Collection and Consultation
            ↓
Assessment of State Finances
            ↓
Recommendations
            ↓
Implementation by Government
```
