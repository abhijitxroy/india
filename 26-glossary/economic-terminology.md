
# Economic Terminology

## Overview

Economic terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in economics, public finance, banking, taxation, development, trade, business, and government policy.

Understanding these terms helps explain how economies function, how governments manage resources, and how economic policies affect citizens.

---

## Why Study Economic Terminology?

Studying economic terminology helps:

- Understand economic discussions
- Follow government policies and budgets
- Learn financial and business concepts
- Understand economic reforms
- Build a strong foundation in economics

---

## Core Economic Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Economy | System of production, distribution, and consumption |
| GDP | Total value of goods and services produced in a country |
| GNP | Total value produced by a country's citizens |
| Growth Rate | Increase in economic output over time |
| Development | Improvement in living standards and well-being |
| Productivity | Output produced per unit of input |
| Per Capita Income | Average income per person |
| Inflation | General increase in prices |
| Deflation | General decrease in prices |
| Recession | Significant slowdown in economic activity |

---

## Government Finance Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Budget | Government income and expenditure plan |
| Revenue | Income received by government |
| Expenditure | Money spent by government |
| Fiscal Deficit | Excess of expenditure over revenue |
| Public Debt | Money borrowed by government |
| Tax | Mandatory payment to government |
| Direct Tax | Tax paid directly by individuals or entities |
| Indirect Tax | Tax collected through goods and services |
| Subsidy | Financial support provided by government |

---

## Banking & Financial Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| RBI | Reserve Bank of India |
| Interest Rate | Cost of borrowing money |
| Loan | Borrowed money to be repaid later |
| Deposit | Money kept in a financial institution |
| Credit | Ability to borrow money |
| Liquidity | Ease of converting assets into cash |
| NPA | Loan where repayment is overdue |
| Financial Inclusion | Access to financial services for all |

---

## Trade & Business Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Export | Sale of goods and services abroad |
| Import | Purchase of goods and services from abroad |
| Trade Deficit | Imports exceed exports |
| Investment | Allocation of resources for future returns |
| FDI | Foreign Direct Investment |
| Startup | Newly established business venture |
| Entrepreneurship | Creating and managing businesses |
| Market | Place where buyers and sellers interact |

---

## Development Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Poverty | Lack of basic economic resources |
| Unemployment | People willing to work but unable to find jobs |
| Human Development | Improvement in health, education, and income |
| Infrastructure | Basic facilities supporting economic activity |
| Inclusive Growth | Growth benefiting all sections of society |

---

## Quick Revision

- GDP = Total economic output.
- Inflation = Rise in prices.
- Budget = Government financial plan.
- Fiscal Deficit = Spending exceeds income.
- Tax = Government revenue source.
- RBI = Central bank of India.
- Export = Selling abroad.
- Import = Buying from abroad.
- FDI = Foreign investment in businesses.
- Inclusive Growth = Growth benefiting everyone.
