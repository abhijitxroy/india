# Administrative Terminology

## Overview

Administrative terminology consists of important words and concepts used in public administration, governance, bureaucracy, policy implementation, service delivery, and government management.

Understanding these terms helps explain how government institutions function and how public services are delivered to citizens.

---

## Why Study Administrative Terminology?

Studying administrative terminology helps:

- Understand government functioning
- Learn public administration concepts
- Follow governance reforms
- Understand policy implementation
- Improve knowledge of administrative systems

---

## Core Administrative Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Administration | Managing and implementing government work |
| Governance | Process of governing and decision-making |
| Bureaucracy | Administrative machinery of government |
| Civil Services | Permanent government administrative services |
| Public Administration | Management of public institutions and policies |
| Policy | Government plan or course of action |
| Implementation | Executing a policy or decision |
| Regulation | Rule issued by an authority |
| Compliance | Following rules and regulations |
| Accountability | Responsibility for actions and decisions |
| Transparency | Openness in functioning and decision-making |
| Efficiency | Achieving results with minimum resources |
| Effectiveness | Achieving intended objectives |

---

## Governance Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| E-Governance | Use of technology in governance |
| Digital Governance | Digital delivery of public services |
| Citizen-Centric Governance | Governance focused on citizens' needs |
| Good Governance | Effective, accountable, and transparent governance |
| Administrative Reform | Improvement of government systems |
| Service Delivery | Providing services to citizens |
| Grievance Redressal | Resolving public complaints |

---

## Administrative Structure Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Ministry | Government department headed by a minister |
| Department | Administrative unit within government |
| Secretary | Senior administrative official |
| District Administration | Governance at district level |
| Local Government | Governance at local level |
| Decentralization | Transfer of powers to lower levels |
| Delegation | Assigning authority to another official |

---

## Public Service Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Welfare Scheme | Government program for public welfare |
| Beneficiary | Person receiving government benefits |
| Subsidy | Financial support provided by government |
| Public Service | Service provided by government |
| Monitoring | Tracking progress of programs |
| Evaluation | Assessing outcomes and performance |

## Quick Revision

- Administration = Managing government work.
- Governance = Running government and institutions.
- Bureaucracy = Administrative machinery.
- Civil Services = Permanent government administration.
- Accountability = Responsibility for actions.
- Transparency = Openness in governance.
- E-Governance = Technology-based governance.
- Decentralization = Power sharing with lower levels.
- Service Delivery = Providing public services.
- Administrative Reform = Improving governance systems.
