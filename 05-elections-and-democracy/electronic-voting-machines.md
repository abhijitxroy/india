

# Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs)

## Overview

Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) are devices used to record votes electronically during elections in India.

They were introduced to make elections:

- Faster
- More efficient
- More accurate
- Easier to administer

India is one of the largest users of electronic voting systems in the world.

---

## Why Were EVMs Introduced?

Before EVMs, elections used paper ballots.

Challenges included:

- Invalid votes
- Slow counting
- Large printing requirements
- Logistical difficulties
- Delayed results

EVMs were introduced to improve the election process.

---

## Basic Structure of an EVM

An EVM consists of two main units:

### Ballot Unit (BU)

Used by voters to cast their votes.

It contains:

- Candidate names
- Party symbols
- Voting buttons

---

### Control Unit (CU)

Operated by election officials.

It:

- Stores votes
- Controls voting operations
- Produces vote totals during counting

---

## How Voting Works

```text
Voter Verification
        ↓
Polling Officer Approval
        ↓
Ballot Activated
        ↓
Voter Presses Button
        ↓
Vote Recorded
```

Each voter can cast only one vote.

---

## VVPAT System

VVPAT stands for:

**Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail**

It was introduced to improve transparency.

Process:

```text
Vote Cast
     ↓
Paper Slip Generated
     ↓
Voter Verification
     ↓
Slip Stored Securely
```

The voter can briefly see the printed slip before it is stored securely.

---

## Advantages of EVMs

### Faster Counting

Results can be compiled much more quickly than paper ballots.

---

### Reduced Invalid Votes

Electronic recording reduces errors associated with paper ballots.

---

### Lower Paper Usage

Reduces the need for large-scale ballot printing.

---

### Administrative Efficiency

Simplifies election logistics in a country as large as India.

---

### Faster Result Declaration

Speeds up the overall election process.

---

## Election Commission and EVMs

The Election Commission of India oversees:

- Deployment
- Testing
- Security procedures
- Storage
- Counting processes

EVMs are used according to established election procedures.

---

## Security Measures

Multiple safeguards are used during elections.

Examples include:

- Administrative controls
- Candidate oversight mechanisms
- Secure storage procedures
- Sealing processes
- VVPAT verification procedures

Election procedures include several layers of monitoring and verification.

---

## EVMs and Democracy

```text
Voter
   ↓
EVM
   ↓
Vote Recording
   ↓
Counting
   ↓
Election Result
```

EVMs are a tool used within the broader electoral system.

The credibility of elections depends on institutions, procedures, transparency, and public trust.

---

## Common Misconceptions

### EVMs Are Only One Part of the Election Process

The election process also includes voter registration, polling, monitoring, counting, and legal oversight.

### VVPAT Provides Additional Verification

VVPAT was introduced to improve transparency and voter confidence.

### Technology Alone Does Not Guarantee Fair Elections

Strong institutions and procedures remain important.

## Quick Revision

- Consists of Ballot Unit and Control Unit.
- Used to record votes electronically.

- Enables faster counting and result declaration.
- Used under Election Commission supervision.
