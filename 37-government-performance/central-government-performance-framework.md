
# Central Government Performance Framework

## Overview

This framework explains how citizens can evaluate the performance of the Union Government using measurable indicators, policy outcomes, institutional effectiveness, and public service delivery.

The objective is not to support or oppose any political party.

The objective is to understand how central governments can be assessed using evidence, data, and governance outcomes.

---

## Governance Importance

The Central Government influences:

- National economic policy
- Defence and national security
- Foreign policy
- Infrastructure development
- National welfare programs
- Financial management
- Regulatory institutions

Citizens should understand how these areas can be evaluated.

## Common Mistakes

Avoid evaluating governments only through:

- Political speeches
- Social media narratives
- Individual events
- Short-term headlines

Instead, focus on long-term measurable outcomes.
