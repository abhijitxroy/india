
# Manifesto vs Performance Framework

## Overview

This framework explains how citizens can compare election promises with actual government performance using measurable outcomes, implementation records, budget allocations, and policy results.

The objective is not to judge any political party.

The objective is to help citizens evaluate whether commitments were translated into outcomes.

---

## Governance Importance

Election manifestos influence voting decisions.

Citizens should understand:

- What was promised
- What was implemented
- What was funded
- What was completed
- What outcomes were achieved

---

## Evaluation Framework

```text
Manifesto Promise
        ↓
Policy Announcement
        ↓
Budget Allocation
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Outcomes
        ↓
Citizen Assessment
```

---

## Key Evaluation Areas

| Area | Evaluation Focus |
|----------|----------|
| Economy | Growth, jobs, investment |
| Education | Access, quality, learning outcomes |
| Healthcare | Access, affordability, outcomes |
| Infrastructure | Completion and usage |
| Social Welfare | Coverage and effectiveness |
| Governance | Service delivery and accountability |

---

## Questions Citizens Should Ask

### Was the Promise Clearly Defined?

Examples:

- Specific target
- Defined timeline
- Measurable outcome

---

### Was Funding Provided?

Indicators:

- Budget allocation
- Program funding
- Long-term financial support

---

### Was Implementation Successful?

Indicators:

- Project completion
- Policy execution
- Service delivery
- Administrative effectiveness

---

### Were Outcomes Achieved?

Indicators:

- Citizen benefits
- Economic impact
- Social impact
- Long-term sustainability

---

## Evidence Sources

Citizens should rely on:

- Budget documents
- Economic Survey
- Government reports
- Parliamentary records
- Audit reports
- Official statistics
- Independent research

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid evaluating performance using only:

- Campaign speeches
- Social media content
- Political advertisements
- Individual success stories

Instead compare promises against measurable outcomes.
