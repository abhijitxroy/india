# Indian Civilization Timeline

## Overview

This timeline provides a high-level view of the major civilizations, kingdoms, empires, and historical periods that shaped Bharat (India) from prehistoric settlements to the beginning of modern India.

## Detailed Timeline

| Period | Timeline | Highlights |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Prehistoric India | Before 3300 BCE | Early human settlements, stone tools, farming communities |
| Mehrgarh | c. 7000 BCE | One of the earliest known farming settlements in South Asia |
| Indus Valley Civilization | 3300 BCE - 1300 BCE | Planned cities, trade, drainage systems, Harappa and Mohenjo-daro |
| Vedic Period | 1500 BCE - 600 BCE | Vedas composed, growth of agriculture and kingdoms |
| Mahajanapadas | 600 BCE - 322 BCE | Sixteen major states emerged across northern India |
| Maurya Empire | 322 BCE - 185 BCE | Chandragupta Maurya, Ashoka, first large Indian empire |
| Post-Maurya Era | 185 BCE - 320 CE | Shungas, Satavahanas, Kushans and regional powers |
| Gupta Empire | 320 CE - 550 CE | Often called the Golden Age of Ancient India |
| Regional Kingdoms | 550 CE - 1200 CE | Cholas, Cheras, Pandyas, Palas, Rashtrakutas and others |
| Delhi Sultanate | 1206 CE - 1526 CE | Muslim rule established over large parts of India |
| Mughal Empire | 1526 CE - 1857 CE | Babur, Akbar, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb and successors |
| European Arrival | From 1498 CE | Portuguese, Dutch, French and British traders arrived |
| British Expansion | 1757 CE - 1947 CE | East India Company and later British Raj |
| Independence | 1947 CE | End of British rule and Partition |
| Republic of India | 1950 CE | Constitution came into effect on 26 January 1950 |

## Major Turning Points

### Indus Valley Civilization

- One of the world's earliest urban civilizations
- Advanced city planning and trade networks

### Maurya Empire

- First major political unification of large parts of India
- Ashoka spread Buddhism across Asia

### Gupta Empire

- Significant achievements in science, mathematics, literature and art

### Delhi Sultanate

- Introduced new administrative systems and cultural influences

### Mughal Empire

- Major impact on architecture, administration and culture

### British Rule

- Unified administration across most of India
- Railways, modern bureaucracy and legal systems introduced
- Economic exploitation and colonial control expanded

### Independence and Republic

- India gained independence in 1947
- Constitution adopted in 1950
- World's largest democracy established

## Why This Timeline Matters

Understanding this timeline helps explain:

- How Bharat evolved over thousands of years
- Why different cultures and traditions exist across India
- How political systems changed over time
- How modern India emerged from ancient civilizations and historical transformations
