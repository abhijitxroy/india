

# Harappan Civilization

## What Was the Harappan Civilization?

The Harappan Civilization was one of the world's earliest urban civilizations.

It flourished in the northwestern parts of the Indian subcontinent between approximately 2600 BCE and 1900 BCE.

The civilization is named after Harappa, one of its first major archaeological sites discovered in present-day Pakistan.

The Harappan Civilization is generally considered the mature phase of the broader Indus Valley Civilization.

## Where Was It Located?

Harappan sites have been discovered across:

- Present-day Pakistan
- Gujarat
- Rajasthan
- Haryana
- Punjab
- Jammu region

The civilization spread over a very large area, making it one of the largest civilizations of the ancient world.

---

## Major Cities

### Harappa

- Located in present-day Pakistan.
- One of the first discovered Harappan sites.

### Mohenjo-daro

- Famous for advanced urban planning.
- Home to the Great Bath.

### Dholavira

- Located in present-day Gujarat.
- Known for water management systems.

### Lothal

- Important trading center.
- Known for its dockyard.

### Kalibangan

- Located in Rajasthan.
- Evidence of agricultural activity found.

---

## Key Features

### Planned Cities

Harappan cities were carefully planned.

Features included:

- Grid-pattern streets
- Standardized bricks
- Residential and public areas
- Organized city layouts

### Drainage System

The civilization is famous for its advanced drainage system.

Most houses were connected to covered drains, which was rare in the ancient world.

### Water Management

- Wells were common.
- Reservoirs were built in some cities.
- Efficient water storage systems existed.

### Standardization

The Harappans used:

- Standard weights
- Standard measures
- Standard brick sizes

This suggests organized administration and trade.

---

## Economy

The economy was based on:

- Agriculture
- Animal husbandry
- Craft production
- Trade

Major crops included:

- Wheat
- Barley
- Pulses

Trade occurred both within the civilization and with regions such as Mesopotamia.

---

## Society

Archaeological evidence suggests:

- Urban settlements
- Skilled artisans
- Merchants and traders
- Farmers and laborers

However, much about their social structure remains uncertain.

---

## Writing System

The Harappans developed a script that has not yet been fully deciphered.

Because the script remains undeciphered, many aspects of their government, religion, and daily life are still being studied.

---

## Religion

Archaeologists have found:

- Seals with animal symbols
- Figurines
- Ritual structures

However, the exact nature of Harappan religious beliefs is not fully known.

---

## Why Did It Decline?

Historians believe several factors may have contributed:

- Climate change
- River course changes
- Environmental challenges
- Declining trade networks

No single explanation has been universally accepted.

---

## Significance

The Harappan Civilization:

- Was one of the world's earliest urban civilizations.
- Demonstrated advanced city planning.
- Developed sophisticated drainage systems.
- Built extensive trade networks.
- Influenced later cultural developments in the Indian subcontinent.

## Quick Revision

- Part of the broader Indus Valley Civilization.
- Major cities: Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Dholavira, Lothal.
- Known for urban planning and drainage systems.
- Used standardized weights and measures.
- Engaged in long-distance trade.
- Script remains undeciphered.
- Declined around 1900 BCE onwards.
