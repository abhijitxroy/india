

# India Timelines

## Overview

This section provides chronological timelines covering the major events, institutions, leaders, laws, reforms, and political developments that shaped India.

The objective is to help readers understand how modern India evolved from ancient civilization to a constitutional democratic republic.

---

## Why It Matters

Timelines help connect events across different topics and answer:

- How India evolved over time
- How democracy developed
- How institutions emerged
- How laws and reforms shaped society
- How political leadership changed across generations
