

# Civilization Timeline

## Overview

This timeline provides a high-level view of the evolution of civilization in the Indian subcontinent from prehistoric settlements to modern India.

The objective is to help readers understand the long-term journey that shaped India's society, culture, institutions, governance systems, and national identity.

---

## Why It Matters

Understanding civilization helps explain:

- How India evolved over thousands of years
- Origins of Indian society and culture
- Development of political systems
- Evolution of trade, religion, and knowledge
- Foundations of modern India

---

## Civilization Timeline

| Period | Approximate Timeline | Major Developments |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Prehistoric India | Before 3300 BCE | Early human settlements, hunting and gathering societies |
| Indus Valley Civilization | 3300-1300 BCE | Urban planning, trade networks, organized settlements |
| Vedic Period | 1500-600 BCE | Vedic traditions, kingdoms, social and political evolution |
| Mahajanapada Era | 600-300 BCE | Rise of large states and republics |
| Mauryan Empire | 322-185 BCE | First major political unification of large parts of India |
| Gupta Empire | 320-550 CE | Scientific, cultural, and intellectual growth |
| Early Medieval India | 550-1200 CE | Regional kingdoms and cultural development |
| Delhi Sultanate | 1206-1526 | Expansion of new political and administrative systems |
| Mughal Empire | 1526-1857 | Imperial governance, architecture, administration |
| Colonial India | 1757-1947 | British rule, economic and political transformation |
| Independent India | 1947-Present | Democratic republic and modern nation-building |

|----------|----------|

| Gupta Period | Cultural and scientific achievements |
