# Post-1974 Nuclear Sanctions

India's 1974 nuclear test significantly changed its relationship with the international nuclear technology and trade system.

## Background

India conducted its first nuclear test at Pokhran on **18 May 1974**.

The test led to international concerns about the spread of nuclear technology and contributed to the creation of the **Nuclear Suppliers Group (NSG)** in 1975.
