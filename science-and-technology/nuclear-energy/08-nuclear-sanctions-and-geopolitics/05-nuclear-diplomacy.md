# Nuclear Diplomacy

Nuclear diplomacy is the use of international relations, agreements, institutions, and strategic partnerships to manage nuclear cooperation, technology access, non-proliferation, and energy security.

## India's Nuclear Diplomacy

India's nuclear diplomacy has evolved through several phases.
