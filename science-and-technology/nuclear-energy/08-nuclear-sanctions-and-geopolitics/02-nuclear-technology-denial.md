# Nuclear Technology Denial

Nuclear technology denial refers to the restrictions India faced on access to nuclear materials, equipment, technology, and international cooperation, particularly after the 1974 Pokhran nuclear test.

## Background

India's 1974 nuclear test triggered international concerns about nuclear proliferation.

This contributed to the establishment of the **Nuclear Suppliers Group (NSG)** in 1975 and the strengthening of nuclear export controls.
