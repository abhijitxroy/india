# Nuclear Suppliers Group

The **Nuclear Suppliers Group (NSG)** is an international group of nuclear-supplier countries that coordinates controls on nuclear exports to reduce the risk of nuclear proliferation.

## Origin

The NSG was created in **1975**, following India's 1974 Pokhran nuclear test.
