# India-US Civil Nuclear Agreement

The India-US Civil Nuclear Agreement was a major turning point in India's relationship with the international nuclear system.

## Background

India had faced nuclear technology and fuel restrictions for decades following the 1974 Pokhran nuclear test.

The United States and India began negotiations to establish a framework for civilian nuclear cooperation.
