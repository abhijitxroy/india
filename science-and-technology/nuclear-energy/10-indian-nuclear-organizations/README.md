# Indian Nuclear Organizations

India's nuclear programme is supported by a network of government bodies, research institutions, public-sector enterprises, fuel-cycle organizations, and regulatory institutions.

## Topics

- Department of Atomic Energy (DAE)
- Atomic Energy Commission (AEC)
- Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC)
- Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL)
- Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Limited (BHAVINI)
- Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR)
- Other nuclear institutions
