# Atomic Energy Commission (AEC)

The **Atomic Energy Commission (AEC)** is the apex policy and administrative body responsible for guiding India's atomic-energy programme.

## Establishment

The AEC was established in **1948**.

It was created during the early development of India's national atomic-energy programme and was closely associated with the leadership of **Homi J. Bhabha**.

## Role

The AEC provides high-level direction for India's atomic-energy programme.

Its broad responsibilities include:

- Atomic-energy policy
- Nuclear research and development
- Nuclear power development
- Nuclear technology
- Strategic direction of the atomic-energy programme
