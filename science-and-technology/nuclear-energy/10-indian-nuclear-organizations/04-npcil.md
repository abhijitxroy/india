# Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL)

The **Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL)** is a central public-sector enterprise under the Department of Atomic Energy responsible for designing, constructing, commissioning, operating, and maintaining nuclear power plants in India.

## Establishment

NPCIL was incorporated in **1987**.

It was established to consolidate the commercial generation of electricity from nuclear power plants under a dedicated organization.
