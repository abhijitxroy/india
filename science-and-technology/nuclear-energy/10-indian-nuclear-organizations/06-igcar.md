# Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR)

The **Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR)** is a major research and development centre under the Department of Atomic Energy, focused primarily on fast-reactor technology and associated nuclear science and engineering.

## Establishment

IGCAR was established in **1971** at Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu.

It was created to develop advanced nuclear technologies, particularly fast-breeder reactor technology.
