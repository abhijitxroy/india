# Department of Atomic Energy (DAE)

The **Department of Atomic Energy (DAE)** is the central government department responsible for India's atomic-energy programme.

## Establishment

The DAE was established in **1954** under the direct charge of the Prime Minister.

It provides the institutional framework for India's nuclear-energy and atomic-science activities.

## Major Responsibilities

DAE's broad responsibilities include:

- Nuclear power development
- Nuclear research
- Nuclear fuel-cycle development
- Nuclear technology
- Radioisotope production
- Radiation applications
- Nuclear science
- Atomic-energy policy implementation
