# Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC)

The **Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC)** is India's premier multidisciplinary nuclear research and development centre under the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE).

## Origin

BARC traces its origin to the **Atomic Energy Establishment, Trombay (AEET)**, established in 1954 under the leadership of Homi J. Bhabha.

In **1967**, AEET was renamed BARC in memory of Bhabha.
