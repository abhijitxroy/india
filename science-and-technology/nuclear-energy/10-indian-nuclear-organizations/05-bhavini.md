# Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Limited (BHAVINI)

**Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Limited (BHAVINI)** is a public-sector enterprise under the Department of Atomic Energy responsible for India's fast-breeder reactor programme.

## Establishment

BHAVINI was incorporated in **2003** to focus on the construction and operation of fast-breeder reactors.
