# Other Indian Nuclear Institutions

India's nuclear programme is supported by a wider ecosystem of research institutions, regulatory bodies, public-sector enterprises, and specialized organizations.

## Major Institutions

| Organization | Primary Role |
|---|---|
| **AERB** | Nuclear and radiation safety regulation |
| **UCIL** | Uranium mining and production |
| **NFC** | Nuclear fuel fabrication and related manufacturing |
| **HWPM** | Heavy-water production |
| **BARC** | Nuclear research and technology development |
| **IGCAR** | Fast-reactor research and development |
| **NPCIL** | Nuclear power generation |
| **BHAVINI** | Fast-breeder reactor programme |

## Atomic Energy Regulatory Board

The **Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB)** is responsible for regulatory oversight of nuclear and radiation-related activities within its mandate.
