# Plutonium Management in India

Plutonium management covers the controlled handling, storage, processing, accounting, and utilization of plutonium generated within India's nuclear fuel cycle.

## Source

Plutonium is produced during the irradiation of uranium fuel in nuclear reactors, particularly PHWRs.
