# Nuclear Reprocessing in India

Nuclear reprocessing is the process of treating spent nuclear fuel to recover usable nuclear materials and separate them from highly radioactive fission products.

## Role in India

Reprocessing is an important part of India's **closed nuclear fuel-cycle strategy**.

```text
Spent Fuel
    ↓
Cooling / Storage
    ↓
Reprocessing
    ↓
Recovered Materials
    ↓
Future Fuel
