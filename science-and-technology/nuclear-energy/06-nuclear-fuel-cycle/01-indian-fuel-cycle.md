# Indian Nuclear Fuel Cycle

India follows a long-term strategy based on developing capabilities across the nuclear fuel cycle, from uranium mining to fuel fabrication, reactor operation, reprocessing, and reuse of nuclear materials.

## Basic Flow

```text
Uranium Resources
      ↓
Mining
      ↓
Processing
      ↓
Fuel Fabrication
      ↓
Reactor
      ↓
Spent Fuel
      ↓
Reprocessing
      ↓
Recovered Materials
      ↓
New Fuel
