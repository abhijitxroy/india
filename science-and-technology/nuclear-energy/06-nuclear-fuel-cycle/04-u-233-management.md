# U-233 Management in India

Uranium-233 (U-233) is a fissile material produced from Thorium-232 and is central to India's long-term thorium fuel-cycle strategy.

## Production

U-233 is produced when Thorium-232 absorbs neutrons and undergoes radioactive decay.

```text
Thorium-232
      ↓
Neutron Capture
      ↓
Thorium-233
      ↓
Protactinium-233
      ↓
Uranium-233
