# Closed Fuel Cycle

India's nuclear programme follows a long-term **closed fuel-cycle strategy** in which selected materials from spent nuclear fuel are recovered and reused.

## Basic Concept

```text
Nuclear Fuel
     ↓
Reactor
     ↓
Spent Fuel
     ↓
Reprocessing
     ↓
Recovered Materials
     ↓
New Fuel
     ↓
Reactor
