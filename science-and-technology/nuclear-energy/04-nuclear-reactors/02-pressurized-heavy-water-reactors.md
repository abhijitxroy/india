# Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors in India

Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWRs) form the backbone of India's indigenous thermal-reactor programme.

## Basic Principle

PHWRs use:

- Natural uranium fuel
- Heavy water as moderator
- Heavy water as coolant
- Pressurized primary coolant system
