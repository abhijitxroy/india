# Kalpakkam Nuclear Complex

Kalpakkam, in Tamil Nadu, is one of India's most important nuclear research and fast-reactor development centers.

## Location

Kalpakkam is located on the Tamil Nadu coast, south of Chennai.

It hosts major facilities associated with India's fast-reactor programme and nuclear research.

## Major Institutions

Important organizations and facilities at Kalpakkam include:

- Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR)
- Madras Atomic Power Station (MAPS)
- Fast Breeder Test Reactor (FBTR)
- Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR)
