# Fast Reactor Fuel Cycle

The fast-reactor fuel cycle is an important part of India's second-stage nuclear programme. It connects plutonium recovered from Stage 1 with fast breeder reactors and the eventual thorium-based Stage 3.

## Basic Flow

```text
PHWR Spent Fuel
      ↓
Reprocessing
      ↓
Plutonium
      ↓
Fast Reactor Fuel
      ↓
Fast Breeder Reactor
      ↓
Spent Fuel
      ↓
Reprocessing
      ↓
Recovered Fissile Materials
