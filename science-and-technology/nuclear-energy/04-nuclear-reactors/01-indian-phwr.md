# Indian PHWR

The **Pressurized Heavy Water Reactor (PHWR)** is the main reactor technology developed and deployed in India's first-stage nuclear power programme.

## Role in India

Indian PHWRs use natural uranium fuel and heavy water as moderator and coolant.
