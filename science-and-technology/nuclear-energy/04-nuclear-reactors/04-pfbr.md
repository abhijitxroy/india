# Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR)

The Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR) is India's flagship fast-breeder reactor project and an important part of the country's second-stage nuclear programme.

## Basic Concept

The PFBR is a **sodium-cooled fast reactor** designed to generate electricity while demonstrating fast-breeder technology.

```text
Plutonium-Based Fuel
        ↓
Fast Neutron Reactor
        ↓
Heat
        ↓
Sodium Coolant
        ↓
Steam System
        ↓
Turbine
        ↓
Electricity
