# Origins of the Indian Nuclear Programme

India's nuclear programme began soon after independence and was designed as a long-term national programme for scientific development, energy security, and strategic autonomy.

## Early Foundations

India established the foundations of its nuclear programme in the 1940s.

Key milestones:

- **1945** — Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR) established under Homi J. Bhabha.
- **1948** — Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) established.
- **1954** — Department of Atomic Energy (DAE) established.
- **1956** — India's first research reactor, APSARA, achieved criticality at Trombay.

## Homi J. Bhabha

Homi J. Bhabha was the principal architect of India's early nuclear programme.

His approach emphasized:

- Building indigenous scientific capability
- Developing nuclear research infrastructure
- Training Indian scientists and engineers
- Establishing the complete nuclear fuel cycle
- Using nuclear technology for national development

## Atomic Energy Commission

The Atomic Energy Commission was established in 1948 to guide India's atomic-energy programme.
