# Trombay

Trombay, in Mumbai, became one of the principal centers of India's nuclear research and technology programme.

## Atomic Energy Establishment, Trombay

The **Atomic Energy Establishment, Trombay (AEET)** was established in 1954 under the leadership of Homi J. Bhabha.

Its purpose was to consolidate India's nuclear research and development activities in one major scientific complex.
