# Homi J. Bhabha

Homi Jehangir Bhabha was an Indian physicist and the principal architect of India's early atomic-energy programme.

## Early Scientific Career

Bhabha was born in Bombay in 1909 and trained as a physicist. His early research focused on theoretical physics and cosmic rays.

He worked in Europe before returning to India, where he became a major figure in establishing India's modern scientific institutions.

## Tata Institute of Fundamental Research

In 1945, Bhabha founded the **Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR)** in Bombay with support from the Tata family.

TIFR became an important center for:

- Fundamental physics
- Mathematics
- Cosmic-ray research
- Training scientists
- Nuclear science
