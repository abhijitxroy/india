# India's Three-Stage Nuclear Power Programme

India's three-stage nuclear power programme was designed to make long-term use of India's nuclear resources, particularly its limited uranium resources and large thorium resources.

The programme was developed under the leadership of Homi J. Bhabha.

      ↓
