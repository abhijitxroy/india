# Three-Stage Nuclear Programme

India's three-stage nuclear power programme is a long-term strategy designed to progressively use India's uranium and thorium resources for nuclear energy.

## Stages

```text
Stage 1
PHWRs + Natural Uranium
        ↓
Plutonium

Stage 2
Fast Breeder Reactors
        ↓
Fissile Material + U-233

Stage 3
Thorium + U-233
        ↓
Long-Term Nuclear Energy
