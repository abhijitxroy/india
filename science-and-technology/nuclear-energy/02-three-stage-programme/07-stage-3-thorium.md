# Stage 3 — Thorium

Stage 3 of India's three-stage nuclear power programme focuses on the long-term utilization of India's substantial thorium resources.

## Objective

The objective is to use thorium as a major domestic nuclear resource by converting fertile Thorium-232 into fissile Uranium-233.

Thorium-232
     ↓
