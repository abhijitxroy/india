# Stage 1 — Plutonium Production

Plutonium production is an important link between Stage 1 and Stage 2 of India's three-stage nuclear power programme.

## How Plutonium Is Produced

Stage 1 PHWRs use natural uranium fuel. During reactor operation, some Uranium-238 absorbs neutrons and eventually becomes Plutonium-239.

Uranium-238
     ↓
Neutron Capture
     ↓

     ↓
