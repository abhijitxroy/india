# Stage 1 — Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors

Stage 1 of India's three-stage nuclear power programme is based on **Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWRs)** using natural uranium as fuel.

## Objective

The primary objectives of Stage 1 are:

- Generate nuclear electricity
- Develop indigenous PHWR capability
- Produce plutonium in spent fuel
- Establish the foundation for Stage 2
