# Stage 2 — Thorium Blanket

Thorium can be introduced into India's Stage 2 fast-reactor programme to produce **Uranium-233**, a fissile material that is important for the long-term transition toward Stage 3.

## Basic Concept

Thorium-232 is fertile rather than fissile. It can absorb a neutron and, through radioactive decay, eventually become Uranium-233.
