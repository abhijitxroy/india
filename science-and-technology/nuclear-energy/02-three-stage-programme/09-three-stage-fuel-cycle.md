# Three-Stage Fuel Cycle

India's three-stage nuclear programme is designed as an interconnected fuel cycle rather than three completely independent reactor programmes.

## Overall Flow

```text
Stage 1
PHWRs
  ↓
Spent Fuel
  ↓
Reprocessing
  ↓
Plutonium
  ↓
Stage 2
Fast Breeder Reactors
  ↓
Additional Fissile Material
  ↓
U-233 Production
  ↓
Stage 3
Thorium-Based Systems
