# Stage 3 — Uranium-233

Uranium-233 (U-233) is the key fissile material associated with the third stage of India's three-stage nuclear power programme.

## Why U-233 Matters

Thorium-232 is fertile rather than fissile. It must be converted into U-233 before it can serve as a major nuclear fuel.
