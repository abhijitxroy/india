# Stage 2 — Fast Breeder Reactors

Stage 2 of India's three-stage nuclear power programme focuses on **Fast Breeder Reactors (FBRs)**.

Its purpose is to use plutonium recovered from Stage 1 while generating additional fissile material that can support the long-term nuclear fuel cycle.
