# Indian Nuclear Energy Policy

India's nuclear energy policy aims to use nuclear power as part of a diversified, secure, and low-carbon national energy system while maintaining indigenous technological capability.

## Policy Objectives

Major objectives include:

- Energy security
- Reliable electricity generation
- Low-carbon power generation
- Indigenous technology development
- Efficient use of nuclear resources
- Long-term thorium utilization
- Nuclear safety and security

## Three-Stage Foundation

India's long-term nuclear policy is closely connected to the three-stage programme.
