# Nuclear Energy and Energy Security

Nuclear energy can contribute to India's long-term energy security by providing reliable electricity, diversifying the energy mix, and reducing dependence on imported fossil fuels.

## Why Energy Security Matters

India's energy demand is expected to remain high as the economy, population, industry, transport, and urbanization continue to grow.

A resilient energy system requires:

- Reliable electricity
- Diversified energy sources
- Secure fuel supplies
- Domestic energy capabilities
- Resilient infrastructure
