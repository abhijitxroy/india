# Nuclear Power Targets in India

India's nuclear power expansion is intended to increase the contribution of nuclear energy to the country's electricity system and support long-term energy security and decarbonization.

## Current Direction

India has been pursuing a significant expansion of nuclear generating capacity.

The expansion is based primarily on:

- Indigenous PHWRs
- Fast breeder reactors
- Future advanced reactor technologies
- International civil nuclear cooperation
