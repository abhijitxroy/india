# Pokhran 1974

India conducted its first nuclear explosive test at the Pokhran Test Range in Rajasthan on **18 May 1974**.

The test was officially described by India as a **peaceful nuclear explosion** and was code-named **Smiling Buddha**.

## Background

India's nuclear programme had originally emphasized peaceful applications of atomic energy, including:

- Nuclear power
- Scientific research
- Nuclear medicine
- Agriculture
- Industrial applications

The 1974 test demonstrated that India had developed significant indigenous nuclear capabilities.
