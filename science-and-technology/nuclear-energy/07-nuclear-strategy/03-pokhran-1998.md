# Pokhran 1998

India conducted a series of nuclear tests at the Pokhran Test Range in Rajasthan in **May 1998**.

The tests marked a major turning point in India's declared nuclear-weapons policy and strategic posture.

## The Tests

India conducted five tests on **11 and 13 May 1998**.

The operation was code-named **Shakti**.
