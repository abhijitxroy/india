# India's Nuclear Doctrine

India's nuclear doctrine provides the broad strategic framework governing the role of nuclear weapons in national security.

## Core Principles

India's publicly articulated nuclear doctrine is centered on:

- Credible Minimum Deterrence
- No First Use
- Massive retaliation
- Civilian political control
- Survivable nuclear forces

## Credible Minimum Deterrence

India does not publicly define its nuclear arsenal around numerical parity with another country.

Instead, the objective is to maintain a capability sufficient to deter nuclear attack.
