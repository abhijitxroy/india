# Rare Earths and Monazite

Monazite is an important source of both **thorium** and **rare-earth elements (REEs)**. This makes it relevant to India's nuclear-resource strategy as well as its critical-minerals ecosystem.

## Rare-Earth Elements

Rare-earth elements are a group of 17 elements:

- 15 lanthanides
- Scandium
- Yttrium

They are used in technologies such as:

- Permanent magnets
- Electronics
- Electric motors
- Renewable-energy equipment
- Defense systems
- Catalysts
- Optical technologies

## Monazite Composition

Monazite commonly contains rare-earth elements along with thorium and smaller amounts of uranium.
