# Thorium Resources in India

Thorium is an important strategic nuclear resource for India because the country has significant thorium-bearing mineral resources, particularly in coastal placer deposits.

## Why Thorium Matters

Thorium-232 is fertile rather than fissile. It can be converted into Uranium-233, which can be used as nuclear fuel.
