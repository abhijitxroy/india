# Nuclear Resource Distribution in India

India's nuclear-resource strategy is strongly influenced by the geographic distribution of uranium and thorium-bearing minerals.

## Uranium

Uranium deposits occur in several parts of India.

Important regions include:

- Jharkhand
- Andhra Pradesh
- Telangana
- Rajasthan
- Meghalaya

The **Jaduguda region of Jharkhand** has historically been an important uranium-producing area.

## Thorium

India's thorium resources are primarily associated with monazite-bearing coastal heavy-mineral sands.

Important regions include:

- Kerala
- Tamil Nadu
- Andhra Pradesh
- Odisha
