# Uranium Resources in India

Uranium is the primary naturally occurring fuel resource used in India's existing nuclear power programme.

## Role in India's Nuclear Programme

Natural uranium provides the starting fuel for India's first-stage **Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWRs)**.
