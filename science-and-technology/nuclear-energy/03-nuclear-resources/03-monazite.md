# Monazite

Monazite is a phosphate mineral that is an important source of **rare-earth elements** and contains significant amounts of **thorium**.

## Composition

Monazite commonly contains:

- Rare-earth elements
- Thorium
- Uranium

Its exact composition varies depending on the deposit.

Monazite
├── Rare-Earth Elements
├── Thorium
