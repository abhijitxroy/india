# U-233 Programme

Uranium-233 (U-233) is the key fissile material connecting India's thorium resources with its long-term third-stage nuclear-energy programme.

## Why U-233?

Thorium-232 is fertile, not fissile. It must be converted into U-233 before it can be used as a major nuclear fuel.
