# Indian Thorium Strategy

Thorium is a central component of India's long-term nuclear-energy strategy because India possesses substantial thorium-bearing resources but comparatively limited uranium resources.

## Why Thorium?

Thorium-232 is **fertile**, not fissile. It must be converted into Uranium-233 before it can serve as a major nuclear fuel.
