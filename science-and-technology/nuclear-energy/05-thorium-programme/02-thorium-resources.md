# Thorium Resources in India

India has significant thorium-bearing resources, mainly associated with monazite found in coastal heavy-mineral sands.

## Major Distribution

Important thorium-bearing regions include parts of:

- Kerala
- Tamil Nadu
- Andhra Pradesh
- Odisha
