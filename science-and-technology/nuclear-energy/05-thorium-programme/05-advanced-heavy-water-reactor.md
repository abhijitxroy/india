# Advanced Heavy Water Reactor

The **Advanced Heavy Water Reactor (AHWR)** is an Indian reactor concept developed to demonstrate technologies relevant to long-term thorium utilization.

## Purpose

The AHWR was designed as a technology platform for India's third-stage nuclear programme.

Its objectives include:

- Demonstrating thorium-based fuel utilization
- Developing U-233 fuel-cycle experience
- Improving passive safety
- Supporting long-term thorium research
