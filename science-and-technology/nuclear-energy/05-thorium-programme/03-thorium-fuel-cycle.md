# Thorium Fuel Cycle in India

The thorium fuel cycle is the process through which fertile Thorium-232 is converted into fissile Uranium-233 and potentially reused as nuclear fuel.

## Basic Cycle

```text
Thorium-232
      ↓
Neutron Irradiation
      ↓
Uranium-233
      ↓
Fuel Fabrication
      ↓
Reactor
      ↓
Spent Fuel
      ↓
Reprocessing
      ↓
U-233 Recovery
      ↓
New Fuel
