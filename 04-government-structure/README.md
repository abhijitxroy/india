

# Government Structure of India

## Overview

India is governed through a constitutional and democratic framework that distributes powers between the Union Government and State Governments.

This structure ensures:

- Democratic governance
- National unity
- Regional representation
- Constitutional accountability
- Administrative efficiency

Understanding India's government structure is essential for understanding how decisions are made, laws are implemented, and public administration functions.

## Federal Governance Model

India follows a federal system with unitary features.

Power is distributed through:

- Union List
- State List
- Concurrent List

This balance helps maintain both national unity and regional autonomy.
