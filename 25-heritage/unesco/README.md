# UNESCO Heritage

## Historical and Cultural Importance

UNESCO recognition helps preserve sites of outstanding cultural and natural value for humanity.

India has numerous UNESCO World Heritage Sites that showcase its rich history, architecture, culture, and civilizational legacy.
