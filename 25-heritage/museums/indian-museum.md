# Indian Museum

## Historical and Cultural Importance

The Indian Museum is the oldest and largest museum in India and one of the oldest museums in Asia.

It preserves and showcases India's archaeological, historical, cultural, artistic, and scientific heritage through its extensive collections.

## What is the Indian Museum?

The Indian Museum is a premier cultural institution that houses artifacts related to archaeology, art, anthropology, geology, zoology, and natural history.

It serves as a major center for education, research, and heritage preservation.
