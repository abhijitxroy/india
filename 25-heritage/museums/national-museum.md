# National Museum

## Historical and Cultural Importance

The National Museum is one of India's most important cultural institutions and houses a vast collection representing the country's rich historical, artistic, and civilizational heritage.

It preserves artifacts from ancient, medieval, and modern India and serves as a major center for education, research, and heritage conservation.

## What is the National Museum?

The National Museum is India's premier museum dedicated to preserving and displaying archaeological, artistic, historical, and cultural artifacts from across the country.

It showcases the evolution of Indian civilization through diverse collections.
