# National Archives of India (NAI)

## Historical and Cultural Importance

The National Archives of India (NAI) is the custodian of the nation's documentary heritage.

It preserves government records, historical documents, manuscripts, maps, photographs, and archival materials that help document India's political, administrative, cultural, and social history.

## What is NAI?

The National Archives of India is the premier archival institution responsible for acquiring, preserving, and providing access to important government and historical records.

It serves as a key resource for researchers, historians, students, and policymakers.
