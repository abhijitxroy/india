# Red Fort

## Historical and Cultural Importance

The Red Fort is one of India's most important historical monuments and a powerful symbol of the nation's heritage.

Built during the Mughal period, it served as the main residence of the Mughal emperors and remains closely associated with India's independence and national identity.

## What is the Red Fort?

The Red Fort is a massive fortified palace complex constructed from red sandstone on the banks of the Yamuna River.

It served as the political and ceremonial center of the Mughal Empire for nearly two centuries.

## Independence Day Significance

Every year on 15 August, the Prime Minister of India hoists the national flag from the Red Fort and addresses the nation.

---

## UNESCO Recognition

The Red Fort was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site because of its exceptional historical, cultural, and architectural value.
