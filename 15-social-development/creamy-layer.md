
# Creamy Layer

## Overview

The Creamy Layer concept refers to relatively advanced and better-off members within certain socially and educationally backward groups who may be excluded from specific reservation benefits.

The concept was introduced to ensure that affirmative action benefits reach those who are most in need and to improve the effectiveness of social justice policies.

It remains an important topic in reservation policy, governance, public policy, and constitutional debates.

---

## Why Study the Creamy Layer?

Studying the Creamy Layer helps understand:

- Reservation policy
- Social justice reforms
- Equality debates
- Public policy design
- Representation issues

It is a key concept in discussions about fairness and inclusion.
