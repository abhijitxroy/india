# Social Justice

## Overview

Social Justice refers to the pursuit of fairness, equality, dignity, representation, and equal opportunities for all sections of society.

In India, social justice has been a major objective of constitutional governance, public policy, welfare programs, reservation policies, and social reform movements.

It is one of the most important concepts for understanding India's social transformation journey.

---

## Why Study Social Justice?

Studying Social Justice helps understand:

- Equality and inclusion
- Constitutional values
- Reservation policies
- Welfare initiatives
- Representation in institutions
- Social transformation

It serves as the foundation for many governance and policy decisions.
