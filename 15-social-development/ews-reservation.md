
# Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) Reservation

## Overview

Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) Reservation is a reservation policy designed to provide opportunities for individuals belonging to economically disadvantaged sections of society.

The policy became a major development in India's reservation framework and is closely associated with the 103rd Constitutional Amendment.

It is an important topic in discussions on social justice, equality, constitutional reforms, and public policy.

---

Studying EWS Reservation helps understand:

- Reservation policy
- Constitutional amendments

- Economic disadvantage
- Public policy evolution

The policy represents an important development in India's affirmative action framework.
