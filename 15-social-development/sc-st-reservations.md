
# SC/ST Reservations

## Overview

SC/ST Reservations are policies designed to improve representation and opportunities for Scheduled Castes (SCs) and Scheduled Tribes (STs) in education, public employment, and political institutions.

These measures are among the most important social justice initiatives in independent India and are rooted in constitutional provisions aimed at addressing historical disadvantages and exclusion.

They continue to play a significant role in governance, public policy, and democratic representation.

---

## Why Study SC/ST Reservations?

Studying SC/ST Reservations helps understand:

- Social justice policies
- Constitutional safeguards
- Representation in institutions
- Equality and inclusion
- Public policy development
- Democratic participation

The policy is central to understanding India's approach to social transformation.
