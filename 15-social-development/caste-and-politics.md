
# Caste and Politics

## Overview

Caste and politics have been closely connected in India's social and democratic evolution.

Caste identities have influenced political representation, electoral behavior, public policy, social movements, and leadership development across different regions of the country.

Understanding this relationship is important for analyzing Indian democracy, governance, and social transformation.

---

## Why Study Caste and Politics?

Studying caste and politics helps understand:

- Electoral politics
- Social justice movements
- Representation in governance
- Public policy formation
- Political mobilization
- Democratic participation

It remains one of the most important factors influencing Indian politics.
