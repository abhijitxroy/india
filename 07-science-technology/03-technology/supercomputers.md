# Supercomputers

## Overview

A supercomputer is an extremely powerful computer designed to perform complex calculations and process massive amounts of data at very high speeds.

Supercomputers are used in scientific research, weather forecasting, defence, space exploration, artificial intelligence, and advanced simulations.

India has developed indigenous supercomputing capabilities through national initiatives and research institutions.

## Characteristics

Major characteristics of supercomputers include:

- Extremely high processing power
- Massive parallel computing capability
- Large memory capacity
- High-speed data processing
- Advanced cooling systems

## Uses of Supercomputers

### Weather Forecasting

Used for:

- Monsoon prediction
- Cyclone tracking
- Climate modelling

### Scientific Research

Supports:

- Physics simulations
- Chemistry research
- Biological modelling

### Space Research

Used in:

- Satellite mission planning
- Space simulations
- Astronomical studies

### Defence Applications

Supports:

- Strategic simulations
- Cryptography
- Security analysis

### Artificial Intelligence

Used for:

- Machine learning
- Deep learning
- Large-scale data analytics

## Supercomputing in India

India has invested heavily in indigenous supercomputing infrastructure.

Major objectives include:

- Technological self-reliance
- Scientific advancement
- High-performance computing capability

## PARAM Supercomputers

The PARAM series represents India's indigenous supercomputing programme.

### Developed By

- Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC)

### Significance

PARAM demonstrated India's ability to build advanced computing systems using indigenous expertise.

## National Supercomputing Mission (NSM)

### Launched

- 2015

### Objective

To establish a nationwide network of supercomputing facilities and strengthen India's computing ecosystem.

### Implementing Agencies

- Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- Department of Science and Technology (DST)
- C-DAC

## Important Indian Supercomputers

Examples include:

- PARAM Series
- Pratyush
- Mihir
- PARAM Siddhi-AI

### PARAM Siddhi-AI

One of India's leading AI-focused supercomputers.
