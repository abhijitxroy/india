# Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL)

## Scientific Importance

The Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL) is the public sector enterprise responsible for the construction, operation, and management of nuclear power plants in India.

It plays a crucial role in electricity generation, energy security, and the expansion of India's civilian nuclear power programme.

## What is NPCIL?

NPCIL is the government-owned company responsible for generating electricity through nuclear power and operating India's commercial nuclear reactors.

It is the primary organization implementing India's civilian nuclear energy programme.
