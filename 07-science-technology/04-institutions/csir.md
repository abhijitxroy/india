# Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR)

## Scientific Importance

The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) is India's largest publicly funded research and development organization.

It supports scientific innovation, industrial development, technology advancement, and national research capabilities across multiple sectors.

---

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | Council of Scientific and Industrial Research |
| Established | 1942 |
| Type | Autonomous Research Organization |
| Ministry | Ministry of Science and Technology |
| Headquarters | New Delhi |

---

## What is CSIR?

CSIR is a national research network that conducts scientific and industrial research across diverse fields including chemicals, aerospace, biotechnology, environment, healthcare, materials, and engineering.
