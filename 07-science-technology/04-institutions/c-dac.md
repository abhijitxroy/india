# Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC)

## Overview

The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) is India's premier research and development organization in advanced computing, high-performance computing, electronics, and information technology.

It was established to promote indigenous technological capabilities and reduce dependence on foreign computing technologies.

C-DAC has played a major role in the development of India's supercomputing ecosystem.

## Full Form

C-DAC stands for:

- Centre for Development of Advanced Computing

## Establishment

### Established

- 1988

### Parent Organization

- Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)

## Background

C-DAC was established following restrictions on the transfer of advanced supercomputer technology to India.

The organization was tasked with developing indigenous high-performance computing systems.

## Objectives

C-DAC aims to:

- Develop advanced computing technologies
- Promote indigenous innovation
- Strengthen digital infrastructure
- Support scientific research
- Advance high-performance computing
- Build strategic technology capabilities

## Major Areas of Work

### Supercomputing

Development of high-performance computing systems.

### Artificial Intelligence

Research and development in AI technologies.

### Cyber Security

Development of security tools and digital protection frameworks.

### Quantum Computing

Research in next-generation computing technologies.

### Language Technologies

Development of Indian language computing solutions.

### Health Informatics

Digital technologies for healthcare applications.

## PARAM Supercomputers

The PARAM series is C-DAC's most famous achievement.

Important systems include:

- PARAM 8000
- PARAM 10000
- PARAM Siddhi-AI

### Significance

These systems demonstrated India's capability to build advanced supercomputers using indigenous expertise.

## National Supercomputing Mission

C-DAC is a major implementing agency for the National Supercomputing Mission.

The mission aims to establish a nationwide supercomputing infrastructure.

## Contributions
