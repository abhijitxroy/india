# Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)

## Overview

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) is India's premier defence research and development agency.

It is responsible for developing advanced defence technologies, military systems, strategic platforms, and indigenous defence capabilities.

DRDO plays a central role in strengthening India's self-reliance in defence technology.

## Full Form

DRDO stands for:

- Defence Research and Development Organisation

## Establishment

### Established

- 1958

### Parent Ministry

- Ministry of Defence

## Objectives

DRDO aims to:

- Develop indigenous defence technologies
- Strengthen national security
- Reduce dependence on foreign defence equipment
- Support the Armed Forces
- Promote defence innovation and research

## Major Areas of Research

### Missiles

Development of:

- Ballistic missiles
- Cruise missiles
- Tactical missile systems

### Aeronautics

Research in:

- Combat aircraft
- Unmanned systems
- Aviation technologies

### Naval Systems

Development of:

- Sonars
- Torpedoes
- Naval weapon systems

### Electronics and Cyber Systems

Research in:

- Radars
- Electronic warfare
- Cyber security
- Communication systems

### Life Sciences

Support for:

- Military medicine
- High-altitude survival
- Soldier support systems

## Important Missile Programmes

### Agni Series

Strategic ballistic missile programme.

### Prithvi Series

Short-range ballistic missiles.

### Akash

Surface-to-air missile system.

### BrahMos

Supersonic cruise missile developed through international collaboration.

### Astra

Beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile.

## Major Achievements

Important achievements include:

- Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP)
- Indigenous radar systems
- Missile defence technologies
- Strategic weapon systems
- Defence electronics development

## Laboratories and Research Centres

DRDO operates numerous laboratories across India working in areas such as:

- Missiles

- Aeronautics
- Naval technologies
- Materials science
- Life sciences

## Role in Atmanirbhar Bharat

DRDO contributes to:

- Indigenous defence manufacturing
- Technology transfer to industry
- Defence innovation ecosystem
- Strategic self-reliance

## Importance

DRDO helps India:

- Strengthen defence preparedness
- Enhance technological capabilities
- Improve strategic deterrence
- Promote indigenous innovation
- Support national security objectives

Major challenges include:

- Rapid technological changes
- Advanced defence competition
