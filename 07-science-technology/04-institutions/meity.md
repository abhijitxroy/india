# Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)

## Overview

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) is the central government ministry responsible for policies relating to electronics, information technology, internet governance, digital infrastructure, cyber security, and digital transformation.

It plays a key role in implementing India's digital governance initiatives and technology policies.

## Formation

### Established

- 2016 (as a separate ministry)

MeitY emerged from the Department of Electronics and Information Technology.

## Parent Government

- Government of India

## Responsibilities

MeitY is responsible for:

- Digital governance
- Electronics manufacturing promotion
- Cyber security initiatives
- Internet and digital policy
- E-governance programmes
- Information technology development
- Emerging technology initiatives

## Major Objectives

The ministry aims to:

- Promote digital transformation
- Expand digital access
- Strengthen cyber security
- Encourage electronics manufacturing
- Support innovation and startups
- Develop digital public infrastructure

## Key Programmes

### Digital India

Flagship programme for creating a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy.

### IndiaAI Mission

Supports development of artificial intelligence capabilities.

### National Supercomputing Mission

Promotes indigenous high-performance computing infrastructure.

### Cyber Security Initiatives

Strengthens protection of digital infrastructure and online services.

## Important Organizations Under MeitY

### C-DAC

Centre for Development of Advanced Computing.

### NIC

National Informatics Centre.

### CERT-In

Indian Computer Emergency Response Team.

### STQC

Standardisation Testing and Quality Certification Directorate.

## Digital Public Infrastructure

MeitY supports important digital platforms including:

- Aadhaar ecosystem support
- DigiLocker
- UMANG
- Digital governance services
- E-sign services

## Role in Cyber Security

The ministry coordinates efforts relating to:

- Cyber incident response
- Digital infrastructure security
- Cyber awareness
- Security frameworks

## Electronics Manufacturing

MeitY promotes:

- Semiconductor development
- Electronics production
- Domestic manufacturing ecosystems
- Technology innovation

## Importance

MeitY helps India:

- Accelerate digital transformation
- Improve governance delivery
- Strengthen cyber resilience
- Build technology capabilities
- Enhance digital inclusion

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Cyber security threats
- Rapid technological changes
- Digital divide issues
- Data governance concerns
- Emerging technology regulation
