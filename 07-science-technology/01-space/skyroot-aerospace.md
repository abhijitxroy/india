# Skyroot Aerospace

## Overview

Skyroot Aerospace is an Indian private space technology company focused on developing launch vehicles and space transportation solutions.

It is one of the leading startups in India's rapidly growing private space sector.

The company gained national attention by successfully launching the Vikram-S rocket, becoming the first private Indian company to launch a rocket into space.

## Establishment

### Founded

- 2018

### Headquarters

- Hyderabad, Telangana

### Founders

- Pawan Kumar Chandana
- Naga Bharath Daka

Both founders previously worked with the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

## Background

The growth of India's private space sector accelerated following space sector reforms that encouraged private participation.

Skyroot Aerospace emerged as one of the pioneering companies benefiting from these reforms.

## Vision

The company aims to:

- Reduce launch costs
- Improve access to space
- Support satellite deployment
- Develop advanced launch technologies
- Expand India's commercial space capabilities

## Vikram Launch Vehicle Series

The company's launch vehicles are named in honor of Dr. Vikram Sarabhai, the father of the Indian space programme.

### Vikram-S

- Suborbital launch vehicle
- First privately developed Indian rocket launched successfully

### Vikram-I

Designed for launching small satellites into orbit.

### Vikram-II

Planned medium-capacity launch vehicle.

### Vikram-III

Designed for heavier payload capabilities.

## Vikram-S Mission

### Mission Name

- Prarambh

### Launch Date

- 18 November 2022

### Significance

The mission marked:

- India's first private rocket launch
- A milestone in private sector participation in space activities
- An important step toward commercial launch services
