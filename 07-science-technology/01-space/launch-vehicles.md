# Launch Vehicles

## Overview

Launch vehicles are rockets used to carry satellites, spacecraft, scientific payloads, and astronauts from Earth into space.

They are among the most important components of a space programme.

India has developed a range of indigenous launch vehicles through the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

## Purpose

Launch vehicles are used to:

- Launch satellites into orbit
- Support scientific missions
- Enable planetary exploration
- Carry communication payloads
- Support navigation systems
- Facilitate human spaceflight missions

## Basic Working Principle

A launch vehicle generates thrust through rocket engines.

The thrust overcomes Earth's gravity and places the payload into the desired orbit.

## Evolution of Indian Launch Vehicles

### SLV-3

The Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-3) was India's first experimental satellite launch vehicle.

Key achievement:

- Successfully placed Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980

### ASLV

The Augmented Satellite Launch Vehicle was developed to improve payload capability.

It served as a technological stepping stone for future launch vehicles.

## Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV)

### Overview

PSLV is India's most reliable and widely used launch vehicle.

### Uses

- Earth observation satellites
- Navigation satellites
- Scientific missions
- Commercial satellite launches

### Major Missions

- Chandrayaan-1
- Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan)
- Aditya-L1

### Significance

PSLV is often called the workhorse of ISRO.

## Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle (GSLV)

### Overview

GSLV is designed to place heavier satellites into geosynchronous transfer orbit.

### Key Feature

- Uses cryogenic engine technology

### Importance

Supports communication and weather satellites.

## Launch Vehicle Mark-3 (LVM3)

### Overview

LVM3 is India's heaviest operational launch vehicle.

It was previously known as GSLV Mk III.

### Uses

- Heavy communication satellites
- Deep-space missions
- Human spaceflight missions

### Importance

LVM3 is the launch vehicle selected for the Gaganyaan mission.

## Small Satellite Launch Vehicle (SSLV)

### Overview

SSLV is designed for rapid and cost-effective launches of small satellites.

### Advantages

- Lower cost
- Quick assembly
- Faster launch preparation
- Commercial flexibility

## Reusable Launch Vehicle (RLV)

ISRO is developing reusable launch technologies to reduce launch costs.

### Objectives

- Improve cost efficiency
- Increase launch frequency
- Enhance commercial competitiveness

## Importance for India

Launch vehicle capability helps India:

- Achieve space self-reliance
- Strengthen national security
- Expand scientific research
- Support commercial launches
- Enhance global space cooperation

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Advanced propulsion technologies
- Cost reduction
- Reusability development
- Increasing payload capacity
- Global competition in launch services

## Institutions Involved

Important organizations include:

- ISRO
- Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC)
- Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre (LPSC)
- Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC)
