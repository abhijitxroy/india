# NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation)

## Scientific Importance

NavIC is India's indigenous satellite-based navigation system developed by ISRO.

It provides accurate positioning, navigation, and timing services across India and surrounding regions, reducing dependence on foreign navigation systems.

## What is NavIC?

NavIC is an indigenous satellite navigation system designed to provide reliable location and timing information for civilian and strategic users.

It serves as India's answer to global navigation systems such as GPS.
