# Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)

## Scientific Importance

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is India's national space agency and the primary institution responsible for space research, satellite development, launch vehicles, planetary exploration, and space-based services.

It has transformed India into a major space power through cost-effective and reliable space missions.

## What is ISRO?

ISRO is India's premier space agency responsible for developing space technologies and applying them for national development, scientific research, communication, navigation, and exploration.
