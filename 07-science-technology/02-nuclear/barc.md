# Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC)

## Scientific Importance

The Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) is India's premier nuclear research institution.

It serves as the backbone of India's nuclear science programme and conducts research in nuclear energy, reactors, fuel cycles, medicine, agriculture, electronics, and strategic technologies.

## What is BARC?

BARC is India's leading multidisciplinary nuclear research centre responsible for advancing nuclear science and technology.

It supports both civilian and strategic nuclear programmes.
