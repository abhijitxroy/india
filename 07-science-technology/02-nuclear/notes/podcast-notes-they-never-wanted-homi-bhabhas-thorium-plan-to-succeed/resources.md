## Source-handling note

These sources were supplied as **verified source links** for the associated claims. They are preserved here as a research trail.

- The presence of a source does not by itself establish that every wording of a claim is supported.
- Official government, regulatory, IAEA, university, peer-reviewed, and institutional sources should generally receive higher evidentiary weight.
- News reports and analytical/commentary sources are useful for context but should be distinguished from primary evidence.
- Time-sensitive claims should be checked against the source's current version/date before being treated as current.
- Technical claims should be read in the context of the terminology and assumptions used by the cited source.
