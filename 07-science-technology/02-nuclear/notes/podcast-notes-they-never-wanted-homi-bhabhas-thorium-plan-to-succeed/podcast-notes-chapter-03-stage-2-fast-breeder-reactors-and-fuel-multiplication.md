# Chapter 03 --- Stage 2: Fast Breeder Reactors & Fuel Multiplication

**Timestamp:** 10:07--11:58

## Complete Notes

-   Stage 2 uses fast breeder reactors.
-   The reactor has a central core and a surrounding blanket.
-   The core contains the fissile fuel and generates fast neutrons.
-   The reactor discussed is cooled with liquid sodium.
-   The point of retaining fast neutrons is that they can interact with
    fertile material in the blanket.
-   The initial blanket can contain U-238.
-   Neutron capture and subsequent transformations convert U-238 into
    Pu-239.
-   The longer the process continues, the more plutonium can be
    produced.
-   Once enough plutonium has been accumulated, the blanket can
    eventually be changed from U-238 to thorium.
-   Neutron irradiation of thorium ultimately produces U-233.
-   U-233 is the important fissile fuel for the Stage 3 thorium cycle.
-   This makes Stage 2 the bridge between the uranium/plutonium cycle
    and the thorium/U-233 cycle.
-   The breeder concept is that the reactor can produce more usable
    fissile fuel than it consumes under suitable conditions.
-   The host uses a petrol analogy: if 10 litres are consumed but the
    process produces 12 litres of usable fuel, it illustrates the idea
    of breeding.
-   The analogy is intended to communicate fuel multiplication rather
    than describe the actual nuclear process.

## Simplified Flow

``` text
Plutonium
   ↓
Fast breeder reactor
   ↓
Fast neutrons
   ↓
U-238 → Pu-239
   ↓
Thorium-232 → U-233
   ↓
Stage 3 fuel
```

## Key Takeaway

> Stage 2 is the fuel-multiplication bridge that builds fissile material
> and ultimately enables India's thorium resource to be converted into
> U-233 for Stage 3.
