# Chapter 12 --- Homi Bhabha's 1966 Aircraft Crash

**Timestamp:** 54:16--55:48

## Complete Notes

    might have achieved if Homi Bhabha had survived the 1966 aircraft
    crash.

-   The speaker notes that melting snow has recently exposed pieces of
    the aircraft.
-   The discussion says local cafés have reportedly collected and

    concentrated effort to investigate the crash.

    established and goes further by saying the matter was deliberately
    suppressed because Bhabha was such an important figure.
-   The possibility is raised that Bhabha's continued leadership could
    have accelerated India's nuclear programme.

and the adequacy of the crash investigation are **claims made in the
podcast**. They are not independently established by the supplied
transcript.
