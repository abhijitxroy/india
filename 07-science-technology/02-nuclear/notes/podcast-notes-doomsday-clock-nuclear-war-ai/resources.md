## Source-use notes

These resources are intended to provide **external references for the claims discussed in the podcast notes**.

When using these resources for future research or verification, prefer:

1. **Official government / intergovernmental sources**
   - Government of India / PIB
   - WHO
   - CDC
   - SIPRI

2. **Peer-reviewed scientific research**
   - Science
   - Science Advances
   - Nature Food

3. **Established research / academic resources**
   - CSIS Missile Threat
   - HyperPhysics
   - Russia Matters / Harvard

4. **Specialized reference / reporting**
   - NUKEMAP
   - SWI swissinfo.ch

### Important distinction

The podcast notes record **what the speakers claim or explain**.

This resource file provides sources that can be used to **verify, investigate, or deepen individual claims**.

A source being listed here does not automatically mean that every statement made in the podcast is fully supported by that source. Claims should be checked against the specific source material before being treated as established fact.
