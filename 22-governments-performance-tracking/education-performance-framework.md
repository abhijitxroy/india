

# Education Performance Framework

## Overview

This framework explains how citizens can evaluate the performance of governments in the education sector using measurable indicators, learning outcomes, accessibility, quality, and long-term societal impact.

The objective is to assess education outcomes rather than political claims.

---

## Why It Matters

Education influences:

- Literacy
- Employment opportunities
- Economic development
- Social mobility
- Innovation and research
- National productivity

Strong education systems create long-term benefits for individuals and society.

---

```text
Access
   ↓
Enrollment
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

```

## Core Education Indicators

| Indicator | Why It Matters |
|----------|----------|
| Literacy Rate | Basic educational attainment |
| School Enrollment | Access to education |
| Dropout Rate | Retention effectiveness |
| Learning Outcomes | Education quality |
| Teacher Availability | Capacity of the system |
| Higher Education Access | Advanced learning opportunities |
| Skill Development | Workforce readiness |
| Research Output | Innovation capability |

```text
Education Policy
        ↓

        ↓

```

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid evaluating education solely through:

- Number of announcements
- Number of schemes
- Number of buildings constructed

Instead focus on:

- Learning outcomes
- Student retention
- Educational quality
- Long-term skill development
