

# Infrastructure Performance Framework

## Overview

This framework explains how citizens can evaluate government performance in infrastructure development using measurable indicators, quality of assets, accessibility, economic impact, and long-term public benefits.

The objective is to assess infrastructure outcomes rather than announcements or political claims.

---

## Why It Matters

Infrastructure supports:

- Economic growth
- Employment generation
- Connectivity
- Public services
- Industrial development
- Quality of life

Strong infrastructure improves productivity, competitiveness, and citizen well-being.

## Common Mistakes

Avoid evaluating infrastructure only through:

- Number of projects announced
- Foundation stone ceremonies
- Isolated flagship projects
- Political messaging

Instead focus on:

- Completed projects
- Service quality
- Accessibility
- Long-term benefits
- Economic impact
