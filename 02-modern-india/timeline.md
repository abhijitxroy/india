
# India Through Time Timeline

## Overview

This timeline provides a high-level view of India's journey from the arrival of European powers to the emergence of modern India.

It connects colonial rule, the freedom movement, independence, partition, and the creation of the Republic of India.

## Colonial Period

### European Arrival

- Portuguese arrive first.
- Dutch, French, British, and Danish powers follow.
- Competition for trade and influence grows.

### Company Rule

- East India Company expands from trade to governance.
- Military victories increase British influence.
- Large territories come under company control.

### British Raj

- Direct Crown rule begins in 1858.
- Administrative, legal, and railway systems expand.
- Nationalist movements become stronger.

---

## Freedom Movement Era

Major phases:

- Revolt of 1857
- Moderate political reforms
- Swadeshi Movement
- Gandhian mass movements
- Revolutionary activities
- INA movement
- Quit India Movement

Together these movements increased pressure for independence.

## Quick Revision

- Vasco da Gama arrived in 1498.
- East India Company founded in 1600.
- Battle of Plassey (1757) transformed British influence.
- Revolt of 1857 challenged British rule.
- British Raj began in 1858.
- Freedom movement intensified during the 20th century.
- India became independent in 1947.
- India became a republic in 1950.
- First General Election held in 1951–52.
