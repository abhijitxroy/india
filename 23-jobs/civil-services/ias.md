# Indian Administrative Service (IAS)

## Why It Matters

The Indian Administrative Service (IAS) is one of the premier civil services of India and plays a central role in governance, policy implementation, public administration, and development.

IAS officers serve at the Union and State levels and are responsible for implementing government programmes, maintaining administration, and supporting public welfare.

## What is IAS?

IAS is the administrative backbone of the Indian government.

Officers are responsible for policy implementation, district administration, revenue administration, development programmes, and public service delivery.

## Training

IAS officers receive foundational and professional training at the Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration (LBSNAA), Mussoorie.

---

## Key Positions Held

- Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)
- District Magistrate (DM)
- District Collector
- Secretary to State Government
- Secretary to Government of India
- Cabinet Secretary
