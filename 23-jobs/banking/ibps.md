# Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)

## Why It Matters

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) conducts recruitment examinations for various public sector banks in India.

It provides one of the largest career pathways into the Indian banking sector through transparent and standardized competitive examinations.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | Institute of Banking Personnel Selection |

| Major Exams | PO, Clerk, SO, RRB |

---

## What is IBPS?

IBPS is an autonomous recruitment body responsible for conducting examinations and selection processes for public sector banks and regional rural banks.

It helps banks recruit candidates efficiently through a common examination framework.

---

## Major Examinations

| Exam | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| IBPS PO | Probationary Officer Recruitment |
| IBPS Clerk | Clerical Cadre Recruitment |
| IBPS SO | Specialist Officer Recruitment |
| IBPS RRB | Regional Rural Bank Recruitment |
