# SBI Probationary Officer (SBI PO)

## Why It Matters

SBI Probationary Officer (SBI PO) is one of the most prestigious banking recruitment examinations in India.

It offers a pathway to managerial positions in the State Bank of India and provides opportunities for leadership, financial management, and career growth in the banking sector.

## What is SBI PO?

SBI PO is a national-level recruitment examination conducted by the State Bank of India to select candidates for entry-level management positions.

Selected candidates undergo training and are posted as officers in SBI branches across India.

---
