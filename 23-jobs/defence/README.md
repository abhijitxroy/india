# Defence Jobs

## Why It Matters

Defence services offer opportunities to serve the nation through leadership, military operations, national security, aviation, engineering, and strategic roles.

Recruitment takes place through multiple entry routes for the Army, Navy, and Air Force.

### NDA

National Defence Academy examination.

Entry route for officer training in the Armed Forces.

### AFCAT

Air Force Common Admission Test.

Officer recruitment examination for the Indian Air Force.
