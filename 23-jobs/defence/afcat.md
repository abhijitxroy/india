# Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT)

## Why It Matters

The Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT) is the primary recruitment examination for commissioning officers in the Indian Air Force.

It provides opportunities to serve the nation in Flying, Ground Duty (Technical), and Ground Duty (Non-Technical) branches.

## What is AFCAT?

AFCAT is a national-level examination conducted by the Indian Air Force to recruit officers for various operational, technical, and administrative roles.

Successful candidates undergo military training before being commissioned as officers.

---

## Selection Process

```text
AFCAT Notification
        ↓
Online Examination
        ↓
AFSB Interview
        ↓
Medical Examination
        ↓
Merit List
        ↓
Commissioning Training
```

## Eligibility Overview

- Indian Citizen
- Graduation or Engineering Degree (as applicable)
- Age Criteria as per Notification
- Physical and Medical Fitness Standards

---

## Benefits of AFCAT Career

- Officer Rank from Beginning

- National Service
- Advanced Technical Exposure

- Career Growth and Prestige

---

## Major Training Academy

Selected candidates receive pre-commission training at the Air Force Academy (AFA), Dundigal.
