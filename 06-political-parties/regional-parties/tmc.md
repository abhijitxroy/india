# All India Trinamool Congress (AITC / TMC)

## Overview

The All India Trinamool Congress (AITC), commonly known as the Trinamool Congress (TMC), is one of India's most prominent regional political parties.

The party has played a major role in the politics of West Bengal and has also participated in national political discussions and alliances.

TMC is one of the most influential regional parties in contemporary Indian politics.

## Historical Background

### Formation

The Trinamool Congress was founded in 1998.

The party emerged as a separate political organization and gradually became a major force in West Bengal politics.

---

### Rise in West Bengal

For many years, West Bengal politics was dominated by the Left Front.

The TMC expanded its organizational presence and emerged as the principal opposition force.

---

### Change of Government

A major turning point occurred in 2011.

```text
Left Front Era
        ↓
Political Competition
        ↓
2011 Election
        ↓
TMC Government
```

The party formed the government in West Bengal and became a dominant force in state politics.

## Electoral Presence

The TMC contests elections at multiple levels:

- Lok Sabha
- Rajya Sabha
- West Bengal Legislative Assembly
- Municipal Elections
- Panchayat Elections

The party's strongest electoral base has historically been in West Bengal.

## Role in West Bengal Politics

The TMC has been one of the most influential political organizations in West Bengal since the early 21st century.

Its role includes influence over:

- State governance
- Welfare policies
- Rural politics
- Urban politics
- Local government institutions
