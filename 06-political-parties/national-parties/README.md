# National Political Parties in India

## Overview

National political parties operate across multiple states and play an important role in shaping India's political system.

They participate in national, state, and local elections and often influence government formation, public policy, and political discourse.

This section introduces the major national political parties and their role in Indian democracy.

---

## Why National Parties Matter

National parties help:

- Contest elections across India
- Form Union Governments
- Influence national policies
- Shape political debates
- Build nationwide political movements

They are important institutions in a parliamentary democracy.

---

## National Party Recognition

A political party must satisfy specific criteria established by the Election Commission of India to obtain recognition as a National Party.

Recognition may change over time based on electoral performance.

---

## Major National Parties Covered

### Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)

- Founded in 1980
- Originated from the Bharatiya Jana Sangh tradition
- Major national political party

### Indian National Congress (INC)

- Founded in 1885
- Played a major role in the freedom movement
- One of India's oldest political parties

### Communist Party of India (CPI)

- Founded in 1925
- Associated with communist and left-wing political traditions
- Active in labor and social movements

### Communist Party of India (Marxist) – CPI(M)

- Founded in 1964
- Emerged from a split in CPI
- Important left-wing political party

## National Parties and Elections

National parties participate in:

- Lok Sabha Elections
- Rajya Sabha Elections
- Vidhan Sabha Elections
- Municipal Elections
- Panchayat Elections

Their electoral performance influences government formation at multiple levels.

---

National parties play a central role in parliamentary democracy.

---

## National vs Regional Parties

| National Parties | Regional Parties |
|----------|----------|
| Operate across multiple states | Strong influence in specific states or regions |
| Focus on national issues | Focus on regional issues and interests |
| Compete widely across India | Concentrated geographic presence |
| May form Union Governments | Often influence coalition politics |
