# Communist Party of India (CPI)

## Overview

The Communist Party of India (CPI) is one of India's oldest political parties and one of the earliest communist parties in the country.

The CPI has played an important role in labor movements, peasant movements, trade union activities, and left-wing politics in India.

It is recognized as a national political party.

## Historical Background

### Formation

The Communist Party of India was founded in 1925.

It emerged during a period of growing labor activism, anti-colonial movements, and international socialist influence.

---

### Freedom Movement Era

During British rule, communist organizations participated in labor and political movements.

The CPI became one of the important ideological streams within Indian politics.

---

### Post-Independence Politics

After independence, the CPI participated in parliamentary politics while continuing to advocate left-wing political and economic ideas.

The party gained influence in several regions and states.

---

The CPI is commonly associated with:
