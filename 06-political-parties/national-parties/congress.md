
# Indian National Congress (INC)

## Overview

The Indian National Congress (INC), commonly known as the Congress Party, is one of India's oldest and most influential political parties.

Founded during the British colonial period, the Congress played a central role in the Indian freedom movement and later became the dominant political force in independent India for several decades.

## Historical Background

### Formation

The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885.

Initially, it served as a platform for political discussion and representation during British rule.

---

### Freedom Movement

Over time, the Congress became the leading organization in India's struggle for independence.

The party was associated with many major movements including:

- Non-Cooperation Movement
- Civil Disobedience Movement
- Quit India Movement

Many prominent leaders of the freedom movement were associated with the Congress.

---

### Post-Independence Era

After independence in 1947, the Congress became the dominant governing party in India.

For several decades, it played a major role in shaping:

- Democratic institutions
- Economic policies
- Foreign policy
- Nation-building efforts

---
