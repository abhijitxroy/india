

# Legal Terminology

## Overview

Legal terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in laws, courts, legal processes, rights, duties, dispute resolution, and the justice system.

Understanding these terms helps explain how laws are created, interpreted, enforced, and applied in society.

---

## Why Study Legal Terminology?

Studying legal terminology helps:

- Understand laws and regulations
- Follow legal and judicial discussions
- Learn court procedures
- Understand rights and responsibilities
- Build legal awareness

---

## Core Legal Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Law | Rule recognized and enforced by authority |
| Legal System | Framework for making and enforcing laws |
| Justice | Fair application of laws and rights |
| Rights | Legal protections available to individuals |
| Duties | Legal responsibilities of individuals |
| Regulation | Rule issued by an authority |
| Statute | Law enacted by a legislature |
| Legislation | Process of making laws |

---

## Court & Litigation Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Court | Institution that resolves legal disputes |
| Judge | Official who decides legal cases |
| Litigation | Process of taking legal action |
| Petition | Formal request submitted to a court |
| Hearing | Court proceeding to examine a matter |
| Judgment | Final decision of a court |
| Order | Direction issued by a court |
| Appeal | Request for review of a decision |

---

## Criminal Law Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Crime | Act prohibited by law |
| Accused | Person charged with an offence |
| Conviction | Formal declaration of guilt |
| Acquittal | Declaration that a person is not guilty |
| Bail | Temporary release under conditions |
| Investigation | Collection of facts and evidence |
| Evidence | Information used to establish facts |
| Prosecution | Legal proceedings against an accused person |

---

## Civil Law Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Civil Case | Dispute between individuals or organizations |
| Plaintiff | Party bringing a civil case |
| Defendant | Party against whom a case is filed |
| Compensation | Payment for loss or damage |
| Contract | Legally enforceable agreement |
| Liability | Legal responsibility for actions or obligations |

---

## Quick Revision

- Law = Rule enforced by authority.
- Justice = Fair application of law.
- Statute = Law passed by legislature.
- Litigation = Legal action in court.
- Judgment = Court decision.
- Appeal = Review of a decision.
- Crime = Act prohibited by law.
- Bail = Temporary release from custody.
- Contract = Legally enforceable agreement.
- Liability = Legal responsibility.
