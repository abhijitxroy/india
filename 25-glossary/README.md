# Glossary

## Overview

## Purpose

The Glossary acts as the central terminology reference for the entire repository.

Use it to:

- Understand unfamiliar terms quickly
- Build strong conceptual foundations
- Improve retention through repeated exposure
- Revise important concepts before interviews and exams
- Connect terminology across multiple domains

The Glossary serves as a quick-reference section for important political, constitutional, economic, governance, legal, parliamentary, judicial, administrative, election, and foreign policy terms used throughout the repository.

It is designed to provide simple definitions, fast revision material, and foundational understanding of key concepts.
