# National Register of Citizens (NRC)

## Overview

The National Register of Citizens (NRC) is a register intended to identify and document citizens of India.

The concept has been associated with citizenship verification, migration-related issues, and administrative efforts to maintain accurate citizen records.

NRC has been one of the most discussed public policy and governance topics in recent years.

---

## Why Study NRC?

Studying NRC helps understand:

- Citizenship policy
- Migration issues
- Population records
- Administrative governance
- Constitutional debates
- Contemporary public policy

It is closely connected with discussions on citizenship and identity documentation.
