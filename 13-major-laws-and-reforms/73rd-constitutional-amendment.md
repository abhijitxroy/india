# 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

## Overview

The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 is one of the most important governance reforms in India.

It gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs) and strengthened local self-government in rural areas.

The amendment aimed to bring democracy closer to citizens by empowering local governments.

---

## Why Study the 73rd Amendment?

Studying the 73rd Amendment helps understand:

- Local self-government
- Decentralization of power
- Rural governance
- Democratic participation
- Panchayati Raj system
- Grassroots democracy

It is a landmark reform in India's governance structure.
