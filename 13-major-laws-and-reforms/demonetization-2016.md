# Demonetization (2016)

## Overview

Demonetization refers to the 2016 policy decision in which specific currency denominations ceased to be legal tender and were replaced through a nationwide transition process.

The decision became one of the most significant economic and governance measures in modern India and generated extensive public, economic, and political debate.

---

## Why Study Demonetization?

Studying demonetization helps understand:

- Economic policy
- Monetary governance
- Financial systems
- Digital payments
- Public administration
- Policy implementation challenges

It remains one of the most discussed economic decisions in contemporary India.
