# Right to Education Act (RTE)

## Overview

The Right to Education Act (RTE), 2009 is a landmark education reform that seeks to ensure access to elementary education for children.

The Act operationalized the constitutional commitment to education and became one of the most important social sector reforms in modern India.

It focuses on expanding educational access, improving participation, and strengthening the education system.

---

## Why Study the RTE Act?

Studying the RTE Act helps understand:

- Education policy
- Constitutional rights
- Social development
- Public administration
- Welfare governance
- Human capital development

It is one of the most significant education reforms in independent India.
