# Goods and Services Tax (GST)

## Overview

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a major indirect tax reform introduced in India to create a unified national tax system.

It replaced multiple indirect taxes levied by the Union and State Governments and aimed to simplify taxation, improve compliance, and create a common national market.

GST is one of the most significant economic reforms in independent India.

---

## Why Study GST?

Studying GST helps understand:

- Tax reforms
- Economic governance
- Federal cooperation
- Business regulation
- Public finance
- Economic modernization

GST transformed India's indirect taxation framework.
