# Article 35A

## Overview

Article 35A was a constitutional provision that granted special powers to the Jammu and Kashmir legislature to define certain categories of permanent residents and provide them with specific rights and privileges.

It was closely connected with the special constitutional arrangements that existed for Jammu and Kashmir.

Article 35A remained an important subject in constitutional, political, and legal discussions until major changes were introduced in 2019.

## Main Purpose

The provision enabled the Jammu and Kashmir legislature to define permanent residents and specify certain rights and privileges associated with that status.

## 2019 Constitutional Changes

In August 2019:

- The constitutional framework relating to Jammu and Kashmir changed significantly.
- Article 370 arrangements were altered.
- Article 35A ceased to operate within the previous framework.
- New administrative and constitutional arrangements were introduced.
