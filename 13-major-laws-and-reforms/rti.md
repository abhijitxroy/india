# Right to Information Act (RTI)

## Overview

The Right to Information Act (RTI), 2005 is a landmark transparency law that gives citizens the legal right to seek information from public authorities.

The Act was introduced to improve transparency, accountability, and citizen participation in governance.

RTI is widely regarded as one of India's most important governance reforms.

---

## Why Study RTI?

Studying RTI helps understand:

- Transparency in government
- Accountability mechanisms
- Citizen rights
- Democratic governance
- Public administration
- Anti-corruption efforts

RTI empowers citizens to obtain information regarding government functioning.
