

# Constitution and Nation Building

## Overview

The Constitution of India is the foundation of modern Indian democracy.

It defines:

- How India is governed
- Powers of institutions
- Rights of citizens
- Duties of citizens
- Relationship between Union and States
- Rule of law and democratic governance

This section explains how the Constitution was created, what it contains, and why it remains the most important governing document in India.

## Major Constitutional Principles

### Democracy

Government derives authority from the people through elections.

### Rule of Law

Everyone is subject to the law, including governments.

### Justice

The Constitution seeks social, economic, and political justice.

### Liberty

Citizens enjoy important freedoms protected by law.

### Equality

The Constitution promotes equal treatment and opportunity.

### Fraternity

National unity and dignity of individuals are emphasized.

### Federalism

Powers are shared between the Union and States.
