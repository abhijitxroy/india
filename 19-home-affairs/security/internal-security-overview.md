# Internal Security in India

## Why It Matters

Internal security refers to the protection of a nation from threats arising within its borders.

For India, internal security is essential for maintaining law and order, safeguarding citizens, protecting critical infrastructure, and ensuring political and economic stability.

## What is Internal Security?

Internal security encompasses policies, institutions, laws, intelligence systems, and security operations designed to address threats that originate within the country.

It aims to preserve public order, constitutional governance, and national unity.
