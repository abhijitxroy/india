# Border Management in India

## National Importance

Border management is a critical component of India's national security strategy.

India shares land and maritime boundaries with multiple countries, making effective border management essential for security, trade, migration control, and prevention of illegal activities.

## What is Border Management?

Border management refers to the policies, infrastructure, technology, and security measures used to safeguard India's borders while facilitating legitimate trade and movement.
