# Cyber Crime in India

## Why It Matters

Cyber crime has emerged as one of the fastest-growing security challenges in India.

With increasing digitalization, online banking, e-commerce, social media usage, and digital governance, cyber threats can affect individuals, businesses, and critical national infrastructure.

## What is Cyber Crime?

Cyber crime refers to illegal activities carried out using computers, networks, digital devices, or the internet.

These crimes may target data, systems, finances, identities, or critical infrastructure.
