# Counter-Terrorism in India

## National Importance

Counter-terrorism is a critical component of India's national security framework.

India has faced various terrorist threats over the decades, making prevention, intelligence gathering, security coordination, and rapid response essential for protecting citizens and national interests.

## What is Counter-Terrorism?

Counter-terrorism refers to the policies, laws, intelligence operations, security measures, and enforcement actions undertaken to prevent, detect, and respond to terrorist activities.
