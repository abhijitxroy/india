# Ministry of Home Affairs

## Why It Matters

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) is one of the most important ministries of the Government of India.

It is responsible for internal security, law and order, border management, disaster management, counter-terrorism coordination, police modernization, and administration of Central Armed Police Forces.
