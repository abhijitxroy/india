# Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)

## National Importance

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) is one of the most important ministries of the Government of India.

It is responsible for internal security, law and order, border management, disaster management, citizenship matters, and coordination with states on security-related issues.

## What is MHA?

The Ministry of Home Affairs is the central ministry responsible for maintaining internal peace, public safety, national security, and coordination between the Union and State Governments on home affairs matters.
