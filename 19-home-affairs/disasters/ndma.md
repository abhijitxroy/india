# National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)

## Why It Matters

The National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) is the apex body responsible for disaster management in India.

It formulates policies, guidelines, and strategies for disaster prevention, preparedness, mitigation, response, and recovery across the country.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | National Disaster Management Authority |
| Established | 2005 |
| Legal Basis | Disaster Management Act, 2005 |
| Chairperson | Prime Minister of India |
| Type | Apex Disaster Management Authority |

---

## What is NDMA?

NDMA is the highest policy-making body for disaster management in India.

It coordinates national efforts to reduce disaster risks, improve preparedness, and strengthen resilience against natural and human-made disasters.
