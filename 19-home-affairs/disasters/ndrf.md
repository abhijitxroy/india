# National Disaster Response Force (NDRF)

## Why It Matters

The National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) is India's specialized force for disaster response and rescue operations.

It plays a critical role in saving lives, conducting search and rescue missions, and supporting disaster-affected communities during natural and human-made disasters.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | National Disaster Response Force |
| Established | 2006 |
| Legal Basis | Disaster Management Act, 2005 |
| Administrative Ministry | Ministry of Home Affairs |
| Primary Role | Disaster Response and Rescue |

---

## What is NDRF?

NDRF is a specialized disaster response force trained to handle emergencies such as earthquakes, floods, cyclones, landslides, industrial accidents, and other major disasters.

It is considered one of the world's largest dedicated disaster response forces.

## Relationship with NDMA

NDRF functions as the operational response force within India's disaster management framework, while NDMA provides policy direction and disaster management guidelines.
