# Disaster Management

## Why It Matters

India faces natural and human-made disasters including floods, cyclones, earthquakes, landslides, droughts, industrial accidents, and pandemics.

The disaster management system helps reduce loss of life, coordinate emergency response, and improve national preparedness.

### Disaster Management Framework

National framework for disaster preparedness, response, recovery, and mitigation.

### NDMA

National Disaster Management Authority.

Responsible for national disaster policy and coordination.

### NDRF

National Disaster Response Force.

Specialized force for disaster response and rescue operations.
