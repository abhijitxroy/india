

# Disaster Management Framework in India

## Why It Matters

India is vulnerable to natural and human-made disasters such as floods, cyclones, earthquakes, droughts, landslides, industrial accidents, and pandemics.

A strong disaster management framework helps reduce loss of life, protect infrastructure, improve preparedness, and ensure rapid response and recovery.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Governing Law | Disaster Management Act, 2005 |

| Response Force | NDRF |
| Objective | Disaster Risk Reduction and Response |

---

## What is the Disaster Management Framework?

The disaster management framework is the institutional, legal, and operational system used by India to prevent, prepare for, respond to, and recover from disasters.

---

## Framework Structure

```text
Disaster Management Act, 2005
              ↓
National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)
              ↓
State Disaster Management Authorities
              ↓
District Disaster Management Authorities
              ↓
Response and Recovery Agencies
```
