# Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF)

## National Importance

The Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) are responsible for border guarding, internal security, counter-terrorism, protection of critical infrastructure, riot control, and support to civil authorities.

CAPFs operate under the Ministry of Home Affairs and play a major role in India's internal security architecture.

## Forces Covered

### CRPF

Primary force for internal security, counter-insurgency operations, and support to state police.

### BSF

Guards India's borders with Pakistan and Bangladesh.

### CISF

Provides security to airports, ports, metro systems, and critical infrastructure.

### ITBP

Guards the India-China border in the Himalayan region.

### SSB

Guards the India-Nepal and India-Bhutan borders.

### NSG

Specialized counter-terrorism and hostage rescue force.

### Assam Rifles

Operates mainly in North-East India and along the India-Myanmar border.

### RAF

Specialized anti-riot and crowd-control force under CRPF.

### Coast Guard

Responsible for maritime law enforcement, coastal security, and search-and-rescue operations.
