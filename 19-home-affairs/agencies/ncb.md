# Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)

## Why It Matters

The Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) is India's primary agency for combating drug trafficking and the illegal narcotics trade.

It plays a vital role in enforcing narcotics laws, coordinating anti-drug operations, and protecting society from the harmful effects of drug abuse and organized drug networks.

---

## What is NCB?

NCB is the national law enforcement agency responsible for enforcing laws related to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

It coordinates with central agencies, state police forces, customs authorities, and international organizations to combat drug trafficking.
