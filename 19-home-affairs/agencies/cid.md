# Criminal Investigation Department (CID)

## Why It Matters

The Criminal Investigation Department (CID) is the specialized investigation wing of state police forces responsible for handling complex criminal cases, intelligence gathering, and specialized investigations.

It supports law enforcement by investigating serious crimes that require expertise beyond routine police investigations.

## What is CID?

CID is a specialized branch of the state police that investigates serious crimes, organized criminal activities, economic offences, and cases assigned by state governments or senior police authorities.
