# Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)

## National Importance

The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) is India's premier investigative agency responsible for investigating major crimes, corruption cases, economic offences, and special crimes of national importance.

It plays a crucial role in ensuring accountability, combating corruption, and supporting the criminal justice system.

---

## What is CBI?

CBI is the premier central investigation agency of India that investigates complex and sensitive cases involving corruption, economic offences, serious crimes, and matters referred by courts or governments.
