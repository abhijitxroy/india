# National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB)

## Why It Matters

The National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) is the central agency responsible for collecting, analyzing, and publishing crime and criminal justice statistics in India.

It supports evidence-based policymaking, crime analysis, law enforcement modernization, and informed decision-making across the criminal justice system.

---

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | National Crime Records Bureau |
| Established | 1986 |
| Administrative Control | Ministry of Home Affairs |
| Headquarters | New Delhi |
| Primary Role | Crime Data Management |

---

## What is NCRB?

NCRB is the national repository of crime-related information in India.

It maintains databases on crime, criminals, prisons, missing persons, and other law-enforcement-related information while supporting police modernization initiatives.
