# Home Affairs

## Why It Matters

Home Affairs is responsible for maintaining internal security, law and order, border management, disaster response, counter-terrorism, policing, and coordination of national security within India.

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) oversees police organizations, investigative agencies, Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), disaster management institutions, and internal security mechanisms.
