# Commissionerate System

## Why It Matters

The Commissionerate System is a model of urban policing used in major cities of India.

It grants executive magistracy powers to senior police officers, enabling faster decision-making, improved law and order management, and more effective policing in densely populated urban areas.

---

## What is the Commissionerate System?

Under the Commissionerate System, the Commissioner of Police exercises certain executive magistrate powers that are otherwise held by district magistrates.

The system is designed to improve response time and administrative efficiency in large cities.
