# Police Rank Structure in India

## Why It Matters

The police rank structure defines the chain of command within police organizations and ensures discipline, accountability, and effective law enforcement.

Understanding the hierarchy helps explain how policing, administration, investigations, and public safety operations are managed across India.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| System Type | Hierarchical Command Structure |
| Applies To | State Police Forces |
| Purpose | Command and Administration |
| Senior Most Rank | Director General of Police (DGP) |
| Basic Unit | Police Station |

---

## What is the Police Rank Structure?

The police rank structure establishes authority, responsibilities, and reporting relationships from constables to senior leadership.

It enables coordinated policing and efficient management of law and order.

---

## Rank Hierarchy

```text
Director General of Police (DGP)
            ↓
Additional Director General (ADGP)
            ↓
Inspector General (IG)
            ↓
Deputy Inspector General (DIG)
            ↓
Superintendent of Police (SP)
            ↓
Additional SP
            ↓
Deputy SP / ACP
            ↓
Inspector
            ↓
Sub-Inspector (SI)
            ↓
Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI)
            ↓
Head Constable
            ↓
Constable
```

| Rank | Role |
