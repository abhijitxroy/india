# State Police Forces

## Why It Matters

State Police Forces are the primary law enforcement agencies responsible for maintaining law and order within their respective states.

They handle crime prevention, criminal investigations, public safety, traffic management, and support internal security operations.

---

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Administrative Control | State Governments |
| Constitutional Position | State Subject |
| Head of Police Force | Director General of Police (DGP) |
| Primary Role | Law Enforcement |
| Operational Area | Individual States |

---

## What are State Police Forces?

State Police Forces are organized law enforcement agencies established by state governments to maintain peace, enforce laws, investigate crimes, and protect citizens.

They form the backbone of India's policing system.
