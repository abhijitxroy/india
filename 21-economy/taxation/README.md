# Taxation

## Why It Matters

Taxation is the primary source of government revenue and funds public services, infrastructure, welfare programmes, defence, healthcare, and education.

India's tax system consists of direct taxes, indirect taxes, and the Goods and Services Tax (GST).

### Direct Tax

Tax paid directly by individuals and organizations.

### Indirect Tax

Tax collected through goods and services.

### GST

Goods and Services Tax.

India's unified indirect tax system.
