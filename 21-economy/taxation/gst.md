# Goods and Services Tax (GST)

## Why It Matters

GST is India's unified indirect tax system that replaced multiple central and state taxes.

It simplified taxation, reduced cascading taxes, improved compliance, and helped create a common national market.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | Goods and Services Tax |
| Implemented | 1 July 2017 |
| Type | Indirect Tax |
| Constitutional Basis | 101st Constitutional Amendment |
| Decision Body | GST Council |

---

## What is GST?

GST is a destination-based indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services.

It replaced several taxes such as VAT, Excise Duty, Service Tax, and Entry Tax.

---

## GST Structure

| Type | Applied On |
|----------|----------|
| CGST | Central Government Share |
| SGST | State Government Share |
| IGST | Inter-State Transactions |
| UTGST | Union Territories |

---

## GST Flow

```text
Manufacturer
      ↓
Wholesaler
      ↓
Retailer
      ↓
Consumer
```

Tax is collected at each stage with input tax credit available.

---

## GST Council

The GST Council is the highest decision-making body for GST.

It consists of the Union Finance Minister and representatives of states and union territories.

---

## Benefits of GST

- One Nation One Tax
- Reduced Tax Cascading
- Improved Compliance
- Easier Business Operations
- Better Tax Collection
- Common National Market

---

## Challenges

- Compliance Burden for Small Businesses
- Technology Adoption Requirements
- Rate Complexity
- Administrative Issues

---

## GST vs Earlier System

| Feature | Before GST | GST |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Tax Structure | Multiple Taxes | Unified System |
| Input Credit | Limited | Wider Availability |
| Market | Fragmented | Common Market |
