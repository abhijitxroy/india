# Manufacturing

## Why It Matters

Manufacturing converts raw materials into finished products and is a major driver of industrialization, employment, exports, innovation, and economic growth.

A strong manufacturing sector helps improve productivity, strengthen supply chains, and increase global competitiveness.

## What is Manufacturing?

Manufacturing involves transforming raw materials, components, and intermediate goods into finished products using labor, machinery, and technology.

It is a critical component of economic development.
