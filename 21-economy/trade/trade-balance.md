# Trade Balance

## Why It Matters

Trade balance measures the difference between a country's exports and imports.

It is an important indicator of international trade performance, external sector strength, and overall economic health.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Definition | Exports Minus Imports |
| Part Of | Balance of Payments |
| Importance | External Trade Indicator |
| Outcome | Surplus or Deficit |

---

## What is Trade Balance?

Trade balance is the difference between the value of goods and services exported and imported during a given period.

It shows whether a country sells more to the world than it buys.

---

## Formula

```text
Trade Balance
=
Exports
−
Imports
```

---

## Types of Trade Balance

| Type | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| Trade Surplus | Exports Greater Than Imports |
| Trade Deficit | Imports Greater Than Exports |
| Balanced Trade | Exports Equal Imports |

---

## Importance of Trade Balance

- Measures External Sector Health
- Indicates Global Competitiveness
- Impacts Currency Stability
- Influences Economic Policy
- Affects Foreign Exchange Reserves
- Helps Trade Planning

---

## Trade Flow

```text
Exports
      ↓
Foreign Exchange Earnings
      ↓
Trade Balance
      ↓
External Sector Performance
```

---

## Factors Affecting Trade Balance

- Export Performance
- Import Demand
- Exchange Rates
- Global Economic Conditions
- Commodity Prices
- Trade Policies

---

## Trade Surplus vs Trade Deficit

| Feature | Surplus | Deficit |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Exports | Higher | Lower |
| Imports | Lower | Higher |
| Forex Position | Stronger | Weaker |
| External Dependence | Lower | Higher |
