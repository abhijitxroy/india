# Foreign Exchange Reserves

## Why It Matters

Foreign exchange reserves are assets held by a country's central bank in foreign currencies, gold, and other reserve assets.

They help maintain financial stability, support international trade, manage exchange rates, and provide protection during economic crises.

## What are Forex Reserves?

Forex reserves are external assets held by the RBI that can be used to meet international payment obligations and support the value of the Indian Rupee.
