

# Trade

## Why It Matters

Trade connects India with the global economy through the exchange of goods and services.

Exports generate income and foreign exchange, while imports provide resources, technology, energy, and products needed for economic development.

```text
trade
```

### Exports

Goods and services sold to other countries.

### Imports

Goods and services purchased from other countries.

### Forex Reserves

Foreign currency and reserve assets held by the country.

### Trade Balance

Difference between total exports and imports.
