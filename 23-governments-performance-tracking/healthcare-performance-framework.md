

# Healthcare Performance Framework

## Overview

This framework explains how citizens can evaluate government performance in healthcare using measurable indicators, accessibility, quality of care, health outcomes, and long-term public health improvements.

The objective is to assess healthcare outcomes rather than political claims.

---

## Why It Matters

Healthcare directly affects:

- Life expectancy
- Quality of life
- Child health
- Maternal health
- Disease prevention
- Economic productivity

Strong healthcare systems improve both individual well-being and national development.

---

## Healthcare Evaluation Framework

```text
Access
   ↓
Quality
   ↓
Affordability
   ↓
Health Outcomes
   ↓
Public Well-Being
```

## Core Healthcare Indicators

| Indicator | Why It Matters |
|----------|----------|
| Life Expectancy | Overall health status |
| Infant Mortality Rate | Child healthcare quality |
| Maternal Mortality Rate | Maternal healthcare effectiveness |
| Healthcare Access | Availability of services |
| Immunization Coverage | Preventive healthcare success |
| Doctor Availability | Medical workforce capacity |
| Hospital Infrastructure | Service delivery capability |
| Disease Burden | Population health challenges |

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid evaluating healthcare only through:

- Number of hospitals announced
- Individual success stories
- Short-term campaigns
- Political messaging

Instead focus on:

- Long-term health outcomes
- Service accessibility
- Healthcare quality
- Population health improvements
