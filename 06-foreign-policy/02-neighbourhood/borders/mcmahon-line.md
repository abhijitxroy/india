# McMahon Line

## Overview

The McMahon Line is a boundary line between India and Tibet that was proposed during the Simla Convention of 1914.

It forms the basis of India's boundary claim in the eastern sector of the India–China border.

The McMahon Line remains an important aspect of the India–China boundary dispute.

## Origin

### Simla Convention

- 1914

The boundary was named after:

- Sir Henry McMahon

who served as the Foreign Secretary of British India.

## Historical Background

Representatives of:

- British India
- Tibet
- China

participated in discussions during the Simla Convention.

The proposed boundary line was accepted by British India and Tibet.

China did not accept the agreement.

## Location

The McMahon Line runs along the Himalayan region in the eastern sector of the India–China border.

It largely separates:

- Arunachal Pradesh (India)
- Tibet Autonomous Region (China)

## Importance for India

India recognizes the McMahon Line as the legal international boundary in the eastern sector.

It serves as the basis for:

- Boundary administration
- Border management
- Territorial claims in the eastern sector

## China's Position

China does not recognize the McMahon Line.

China disputes parts of the eastern sector, particularly areas in Arunachal Pradesh.

## Relationship with the LAC

The McMahon Line and the Line of Actual Control (LAC) are different concepts.

| Feature | McMahon Line | LAC |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Origin | Simla Convention (1914) | Post-1962 Situation |
| Nature | Claimed Boundary | De Facto Control Line |
| Sector | Eastern Sector | Entire India–China Frontier |
| Status | Disputed by China | Operational Boundary Mechanism |

## Strategic Importance

The McMahon Line is important because of:

- National security considerations
- Border management
- India–China relations
- Arunachal Pradesh's strategic location

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Differing historical interpretations
- Boundary disputes
- Military tensions
- Strategic competition

## Significance

The McMahon Line remains one of the most important concepts in understanding the eastern sector of the India–China boundary dispute.

It continues to influence diplomacy, border management, and regional security.
