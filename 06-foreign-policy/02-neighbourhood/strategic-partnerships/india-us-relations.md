# India–United States Relations

## Overview

India and the United States share a Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership based on democratic values, economic cooperation, technological collaboration, defence ties, and people-to-people connections.

The relationship has expanded significantly since the end of the Cold War and is now one of India's most important strategic partnerships.

## Establishment of Diplomatic Relations

### Diplomatic Relations Established

- 1947

India and the United States established diplomatic relations soon after India's independence.

## Evolution of Relations

Major phases include:

- Early post-independence engagement
- Cold War period
- Post-Cold War cooperation
- Strategic partnership era
- Indo-Pacific cooperation

## Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership

India and the United States have elevated relations across multiple sectors including defence, trade, technology, energy, education, and security.

## Defence Cooperation

Defence cooperation is a major pillar of bilateral relations.

Key areas include:

- Defence technology
- Military exercises
- Maritime security
- Intelligence cooperation
- Defence procurement

### Major Exercises

#### Exercise Yudh Abhyas

- Army Exercise

#### Exercise Malabar

- Naval Exercise

#### Exercise Cope India

- Air Force Exercise

## Foundational Defence Agreements

Important agreements include:

- LEMOA
- COMCASA
- BECA

These agreements facilitate logistics support, secure communications, and geospatial cooperation.

## Economic Relations

The United States is among India's largest trading partners.

Major sectors include:

- Information Technology
- Pharmaceuticals
- Manufacturing
- Energy
- Services
- Digital Economy

## Technology Cooperation

Key areas include:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Semiconductors
- Quantum Computing
- Space Technology
- Critical and Emerging Technologies

### iCET

The Initiative on Critical and Emerging Technology (iCET) aims to strengthen cooperation in advanced technologies.

## Energy Cooperation

Areas of cooperation include:

- Clean Energy
- Renewable Energy
- Civil Nuclear Cooperation
- LNG Trade

## Space Cooperation

Major organizations involved:

- ISRO
- NASA

Areas include:

- Space exploration
- Earth observation
- Scientific research

## Indo-Pacific Cooperation

India and the United States support:

- Freedom of navigation
- Maritime security
- Rules-based international order
- Regional stability

## QUAD Cooperation

India and the United States are members of:

- QUAD

Other members include:

- Japan
- Australia

## Indian Diaspora

The Indian-origin community in the United States plays an important role in:

- Business
- Technology
- Academia
- Cultural relations

## Multilateral Cooperation

Both countries cooperate in:

- United Nations
- G20
- QUAD
- Indo-Pacific initiatives
- Climate action efforts

## Importance for India

The United States contributes to:

- Technology access
- Investment inflows
- Defence modernization
- Educational opportunities
- Strategic balancing in the Indo-Pacific

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Trade disputes
- Visa-related concerns
- Regulatory differences
- Geopolitical differences on certain issues

Despite these challenges, relations continue to deepen.

## Significance

India–United States relations are among the most important strategic partnerships of the 21st century and play a major role in shaping the Indo-Pacific region and the global economy.
