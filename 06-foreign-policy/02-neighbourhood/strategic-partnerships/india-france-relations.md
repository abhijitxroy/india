# India–France Relations

## Overview

India and France share a long-standing strategic partnership based on mutual trust, democratic values, strategic autonomy, and cooperation across multiple sectors.

France is one of India's most important partners in defence, space, nuclear energy, technology, and the Indo-Pacific region.

## Establishment of Diplomatic Relations

### Diplomatic Relations Established

- 1947

France was among the first countries to establish diplomatic relations with independent India.

## Strategic Partnership

### Established

- 1998

India and France elevated their relationship to a Strategic Partnership during the visit of Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee to France.

## Areas of Cooperation

### Defence Cooperation

Defence is a major pillar of bilateral relations.

Important areas include:

- Military exercises
- Defence technology cooperation
- Defence procurement
- Maritime security

### Important Defence Projects

- Rafale Fighter Aircraft
- Scorpene-Class Submarines

### Military Exercises

#### Exercise Varuna

- Naval Exercise

#### Exercise Garuda

- Air Force Exercise

#### Exercise Shakti

- Army Exercise

## Space Cooperation

India and France cooperate extensively in space research.

Major organizations involved:

- ISRO
- CNES (French Space Agency)

Areas of cooperation include:

- Satellite development
- Earth observation
- Climate monitoring
- Space research

## Civil Nuclear Cooperation

France is an important partner in India's civil nuclear energy programme.

### Jaitapur Nuclear Power Project

Located in:

- Maharashtra

It is one of the largest proposed nuclear power projects in the world.

## Economic Relations

Major sectors of cooperation include:

- Trade
- Investment
- Technology
- Manufacturing
- Infrastructure
- Renewable energy

French companies have significant investments in India.

## Indo-Pacific Cooperation

India and France share common interests in:

- Freedom of navigation
- Maritime security
- Regional stability
- Rules-based international order

France is an important Indo-Pacific partner because of its territories and presence in the Indian and Pacific Oceans.

## Climate and Environmental Cooperation

### International Solar Alliance (ISA)

India and France jointly launched:

- International Solar Alliance

The initiative promotes solar energy adoption globally.

## Education and Cultural Relations

Cooperation includes:

- Student exchanges
- Academic partnerships
- Scientific research
- Cultural programs

## Multilateral Cooperation

India and France cooperate in:

- United Nations
- G20
- Climate negotiations
- Counter-terrorism initiatives

## Importance for India

France supports India in:

- Strategic autonomy
- Defence modernization
- Technology cooperation
- Indo-Pacific engagement
- Global governance reforms

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Trade barriers
- Regulatory differences
- Global geopolitical uncertainties

Despite these challenges, relations remain strong and expanding.

## Significance

India–France relations represent one of India's most important strategic partnerships and continue to expand across defence, technology, energy, and global governance.
