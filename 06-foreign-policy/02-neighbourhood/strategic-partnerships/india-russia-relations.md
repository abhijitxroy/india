# India–Russia Relations

## Overview

India and Russia share a long-standing strategic partnership based on mutual trust, defence cooperation, energy collaboration, and geopolitical coordination.

Russia has been one of India's most important strategic partners since the Cold War period and continues to play a significant role in India's defence, energy, and foreign policy landscape.

## Establishment of Diplomatic Relations

### Diplomatic Relations Established

- 1947

India and the Soviet Union established diplomatic relations soon after India's independence.

## Historical Background

During the Cold War, India and the Soviet Union developed close political, economic, scientific, and defence ties.

The relationship continued after the dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991.

## Strategic Partnership

### Declaration of Strategic Partnership

- 2000

India and Russia formalized their Strategic Partnership during the visit of Russian President Vladimir Putin to India.

## Areas of Cooperation

### Defence Cooperation

Defence remains one of the strongest pillars of bilateral relations.

Major areas include:

- Defence procurement
- Joint military exercises
- Defence technology cooperation
- Military modernization

## Important Defence Projects

### BrahMos Missile

A jointly developed supersonic cruise missile.

### S-400 Air Defence System

Advanced air defence system acquired by India.

### Nuclear Submarine Cooperation

Cooperation in strategic naval capabilities.

## Military Exercises

### INDRA Exercise

Joint military exercise involving:

- Army
- Navy
- Air Force

## Energy Cooperation

Energy is a major component of India–Russia relations.

Important areas include:

- Oil and gas cooperation
- Nuclear energy
- Energy investments

## Civil Nuclear Cooperation

### Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant

Located in:

- Tamil Nadu

Russia has played a major role in developing nuclear reactors at Kudankulam.

## Space Cooperation

India and Russia have cooperated in:

- Space technology
- Satellite development
- Human spaceflight initiatives

The Soviet Union assisted India's early space programme.

## Economic Relations

Major sectors include:

- Trade
- Energy
- Pharmaceuticals
- Fertilizers
- Engineering products
- Technology cooperation

## Multilateral Cooperation

India and Russia cooperate in:

- BRICS
- SCO
- G20
- United Nations

Both countries support a multipolar international order.

## Russia's Importance for India

Russia contributes to:

- Defence preparedness
- Strategic deterrence
- Energy security
- Technology cooperation
- Eurasian connectivity initiatives

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Geopolitical tensions
- Sanctions-related issues
- Trade imbalances
- Changing global strategic alignments

Despite these challenges, bilateral relations remain significant.

## Significance

India–Russia relations remain one of India's most important strategic partnerships and continue to contribute to defence cooperation, energy security, and international diplomacy.
