# India–UAE Relations

## Overview

India and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) share a comprehensive strategic partnership based on trade, energy cooperation, investment, connectivity, technology, and people-to-people ties.

The UAE is one of India's most important partners in West Asia and a major destination for the Indian diaspora.

## Establishment of Diplomatic Relations

### Diplomatic Relations Established

- 1972

India and the UAE established diplomatic relations soon after the formation of the UAE.

## Strategic Partnership

Over the years, bilateral relations have expanded into a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership covering economic, security, energy, and technological cooperation.

## Areas of Cooperation

### Trade and Commerce

The UAE is among India's largest trading partners.

Major sectors include:

- Petroleum products
- Gems and jewellery
- Food products
- Engineering goods
- Technology services

## CEPA

### Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement

- Signed in 2022

The agreement aims to:

- Increase bilateral trade
- Promote investments
- Improve market access
- Strengthen economic integration

## Energy Cooperation

The UAE is an important partner in India's energy security.

Areas of cooperation include:

- Crude oil supplies
- Strategic petroleum reserves
- Renewable energy
- Hydrogen initiatives

## Investment Cooperation

UAE investments support:

- Infrastructure
- Logistics
- Ports
- Real estate
- Technology sectors

## Connectivity Initiatives

India and the UAE cooperate on:

- Maritime connectivity
- Trade corridors
- Logistics networks
- Supply chain development

## Defence and Security Cooperation

Major areas include:

- Counter-terrorism cooperation
- Intelligence sharing
- Maritime security
- Defence exchanges

## Indian Diaspora

The UAE hosts one of the largest Indian communities outside India.

The diaspora contributes significantly to:

- Bilateral relations
- Trade and business
- Cultural exchanges
- Remittances

## Cultural Relations

Cooperation includes:

- Cultural festivals
- Educational exchanges
- Tourism promotion
- Heritage initiatives

## Multilateral Cooperation

India and the UAE cooperate in:

- United Nations
- G20 initiatives
- Climate action efforts
- Regional development initiatives

## Importance for India

The UAE contributes to:

- Energy security
- Trade growth
- Investment inflows
- West Asia engagement
- Diaspora welfare

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Regional geopolitical tensions
- Global economic fluctuations
- Energy market volatility

Despite these challenges, relations continue to strengthen.

## Significance

India–UAE relations have emerged as one of India's most important partnerships in West Asia and continue to expand across economic, strategic, and cultural domains.
