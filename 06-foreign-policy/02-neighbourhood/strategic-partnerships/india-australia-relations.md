# India–Australia Relations

## Overview

India and Australia share a comprehensive strategic partnership based on democratic values, economic cooperation, maritime security, and a shared commitment to a free, open, and inclusive Indo-Pacific region.

The relationship has expanded significantly in areas such as defence, trade, education, technology, energy, and regional security.

## Establishment of Diplomatic Relations

### Diplomatic Relations Established

- 1947

India and Australia established diplomatic relations soon after India's independence.

## Strategic Partnership

### Comprehensive Strategic Partnership

- 2020

The relationship was elevated to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership to deepen cooperation across multiple sectors.

## Areas of Cooperation

### Defence and Security Cooperation

Major areas include:

- Maritime security
- Defence dialogue
- Military exercises
- Intelligence cooperation
- Indo-Pacific security

### Exercise AUSINDEX

Bilateral naval exercise between India and Australia.

### Exercise AUSTRAHIND

Joint Army exercise.

### Exercise Malabar

Multilateral naval exercise involving:

- India
- Australia
- Japan
- United States

## Indo-Pacific Cooperation

India and Australia cooperate to promote:

- Freedom of navigation
- Rules-based international order
- Maritime security
- Regional stability

Both countries view the Indo-Pacific as a key strategic region.

## QUAD Cooperation

India and Australia are members of:

- QUAD (Quadrilateral Security Dialogue)

Other members include:

- United States
- Japan

The grouping focuses on practical cooperation and regional coordination.

## Economic Relations

Major sectors include:

- Trade
- Critical minerals
- Technology
- Energy
- Education
- Infrastructure

## Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (ECTA)

India and Australia signed:

- Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (ECTA)

The agreement aims to enhance bilateral trade and investment.

## Critical Minerals Cooperation

Australia is an important partner for:

- Lithium
- Cobalt
- Rare earth elements

These resources are important for clean energy and advanced technologies.

## Education and People-to-People Ties

Education is a major pillar of bilateral relations.

Areas include:

- Student exchanges
- Research collaboration
- Academic partnerships
- Skill development

Australia is a major destination for Indian students.

## Energy Cooperation

Cooperation includes:

- Renewable energy
- Clean energy technologies
- Critical minerals supply chains

## Multilateral Cooperation

India and Australia cooperate in:

- QUAD
- G20
- United Nations
- Indo-Pacific initiatives

## Importance for India

Australia contributes to:

- Maritime security
- Indo-Pacific engagement
- Energy security
- Supply chain resilience
- Educational cooperation

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Trade barriers
- Global economic uncertainties
- Regional geopolitical tensions

Despite these challenges, relations continue to deepen.

## Significance

India–Australia relations have emerged as an important pillar of India's Indo-Pacific strategy and contribute significantly to regional stability, economic cooperation, and strategic coordination.
