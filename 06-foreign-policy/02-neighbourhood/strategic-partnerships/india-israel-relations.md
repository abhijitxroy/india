# India–Israel Relations

## Overview

India and Israel share a strong strategic partnership based on defence cooperation, agriculture, technology, innovation, water management, and security collaboration.

Since the establishment of full diplomatic relations, bilateral ties have expanded significantly across multiple sectors.

Israel is regarded as one of India's important partners in defence technology and innovation.

## Establishment of Diplomatic Relations

### Diplomatic Relations Established

- 1992

India and Israel established full diplomatic relations in January 1992.

## Historical Background

Although diplomatic relations were established in 1992, interactions between the two countries existed earlier through trade and limited cooperation.

Relations expanded rapidly after the end of the Cold War.

## Areas of Cooperation

### Defence Cooperation

Defence is one of the strongest pillars of bilateral relations.

Major areas include:

- Defence technology
- Surveillance systems
- Missile systems
- Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs)
- Border security technologies

### Important Defence Systems

Examples include:

- Barak Missile Systems
- Heron UAVs
- Phalcon AWACS

## Agriculture Cooperation

Israel has supported agricultural modernization in India.

Major areas include:

- Drip irrigation
- Precision farming
- Water-efficient agriculture
- Horticulture development

### Centres of Excellence

India and Israel have established agricultural Centres of Excellence in multiple states.

## Water Management

Israel is globally recognized for expertise in water conservation.

Cooperation includes:

- Water recycling
- Desalination technologies
- Irrigation management
- Water-use efficiency

## Technology and Innovation

Major areas include:

- Start-up ecosystems
- Artificial intelligence
- Cyber security
- Digital technologies
- Research collaboration

## Security Cooperation

Cooperation includes:

- Counter-terrorism
- Intelligence sharing
- Homeland security
- Cyber security

## Trade Relations

Important sectors include:

- Diamonds
- Chemicals
- Technology products
- Defence equipment
- Agricultural technologies

## Space Cooperation

India and Israel cooperate in:

- Satellite technologies
- Space research
- Remote sensing applications

## Cultural and People-to-People Relations

Areas include:

- Tourism
- Academic exchanges
- Cultural programs
- Scientific collaboration

## Importance for India

Israel contributes to:

- Defence modernization
- Agricultural productivity
- Water security
- Technology development
- Counter-terrorism capabilities

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Regional geopolitical complexities
- Security concerns in West Asia
- Balancing relations with multiple regional partners

Despite these challenges, bilateral cooperation continues to grow.

## Significance

India–Israel relations have evolved into an important strategic partnership and contribute significantly to India's defence, technology, agriculture, and security objectives.
