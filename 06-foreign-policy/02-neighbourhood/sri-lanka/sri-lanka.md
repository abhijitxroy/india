# Sri Lanka

## Overview

Sri Lanka is one of India's closest maritime neighbours in the Indian Ocean.

The relationship is based on historical links, cultural connections, economic cooperation, maritime security, and regional engagement.

Sri Lanka occupies an important place in India's Neighbourhood First Policy and Indian Ocean strategy.

## Geography

Sri Lanka is an island nation located south of India.

It is separated from India by:

- Palk Strait
- Gulf of Mannar

The closest Indian state is:

- Tamil Nadu

## Historical Background

India and Sri Lanka share centuries-old links through:

- Buddhism
- Trade routes
- Cultural exchanges
- Maritime interactions

Ancient connections contributed to strong civilizational ties between the two countries.

## Areas of Cooperation

### Trade and Commerce

India is among Sri Lanka's largest trading partners.

Major sectors include:

- Petroleum products
- Pharmaceuticals
- Infrastructure
- Consumer goods

### Connectivity

Important areas include:

- Maritime connectivity
- Air connectivity
- Port cooperation
- Digital connectivity

### Development Partnership

India supports projects related to:

- Housing
- Healthcare
- Education
- Infrastructure
- Community development

### Energy Cooperation

Areas include:

- Renewable energy
- Power connectivity
- Energy infrastructure

## Maritime Security Cooperation

India and Sri Lanka cooperate in:

- Coastal security
- Maritime surveillance
- Search and rescue operations
- Indian Ocean security

## Cultural Relations

Shared links include:

- Buddhist heritage
- Tamil cultural connections
- Tourism
- Educational exchanges

## Fishermen Issue

Fishing activities in the Palk Strait remain an important bilateral issue.

Both countries engage in dialogue to address concerns relating to livelihoods and maritime resources.

## Regional Cooperation

India and Sri Lanka cooperate through:

- SAARC
- BIMSTEC
- Indian Ocean initiatives

## Importance for India

Sri Lanka is important because of:

- Strategic location in the Indian Ocean
- Maritime security
- Regional stability
- Trade routes
- Neighbourhood First Policy

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Fisheries disputes
- Economic vulnerabilities
- Strategic competition in the Indian Ocean
- Regional security concerns

## Significance

Sri Lanka is a key maritime partner of India and plays an important role in India's Indian Ocean and neighbourhood policies.
