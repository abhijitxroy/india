# Country Relations

## Overview

This section covers India's bilateral relations with countries around the world.

Country relations are a major component of foreign policy and influence trade, security, diplomacy, technology cooperation, energy security, and regional stability.

## Quick Revision

- Bilateral relations are a key pillar of foreign policy.
- Relations are shaped by geography, economics, security, and history.
- Neighbourhood relations directly affect regional stability.
- Major powers influence trade, technology, and security cooperation.
- Strategic partnerships help advance long-term national interests.
