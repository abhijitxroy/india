# International Organizations

## Overview

This section covers the major international organizations in which India participates.

International organizations provide platforms for diplomacy, cooperation, trade, security coordination, development partnerships, and global governance.

## Quick Revision

- International organizations help countries cooperate on common challenges.
- India participates in both global and regional organizations.
- The United Nations remains a key diplomatic platform.
- BRICS, G20, and SCO are important strategic forums.
- International organizations support diplomacy, trade, development, and security objectives.
