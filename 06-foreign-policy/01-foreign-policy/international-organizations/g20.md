# G20

## Overview

The Group of Twenty (G20) is the premier international forum for economic cooperation among the world's major developed and developing economies.

It brings together countries that account for the majority of global GDP, international trade, and population.

The forum discusses issues relating to:

- Global economic growth
- Financial stability
- Sustainable development
- Climate change
- Energy security
- Digital transformation

## Formation

### Established

- 1999

### Background

The G20 was created after the Asian Financial Crisis of 1997–98.

The objective was to improve coordination among major economies and strengthen the international financial system.

## Members

The G20 consists of:

- 19 countries
- European Union (EU)
- African Union (AU) (Permanent Member since 2023)

## Objectives

The G20 aims to:

- Promote global economic growth
- Improve financial stability
- Strengthen international cooperation
- Address global development challenges
- Support sustainable development
- Reform global governance institutions

## Structure

The G20 does not have:

- A permanent secretariat
- A permanent headquarters

The presidency rotates annually among members.

## G20 Presidency

The country holding the presidency:

- Hosts the annual summit
- Sets priorities and agenda
- Coordinates meetings throughout the year

## G20 Troika

The Troika system ensures continuity.

It consists of:

- Previous Presidency
- Current Presidency
- Incoming Presidency

## G20 and India

India is a founding member of the G20.

India views the forum as an important platform for:

- Global economic cooperation
- Representation of developing countries
- South-South cooperation
- Reform of international institutions

## India's G20 Presidency (2023)

### Theme

- One Earth, One Family, One Future

### Major Highlights

- African Union admitted as a permanent member
- New Delhi Leaders' Declaration adopted
- Focus on Digital Public Infrastructure
- Emphasis on sustainable development and Global South priorities

## Importance

The G20 helps:

- Coordinate responses to global crises
- Promote international cooperation
- Support economic stability
- Address transnational challenges
- Strengthen global governance

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Geopolitical tensions
- Differences among major powers
- Climate financing disputes
- Trade disagreements
- Global economic uncertainty
