# United Nations Security Council (UNSC)

## Overview

The United Nations Security Council (UNSC) is one of the principal organs of the United Nations.

It is primarily responsible for maintaining international peace and security.

The Security Council has the authority to investigate conflicts, impose sanctions, authorize peacekeeping operations, and take measures to address threats to international peace.

## Establishment

The UNSC was established in:

- 1945

It was created under the Charter of the United Nations.

## Headquarters

- New York City, United States

## Composition

The Security Council consists of:

- 15 Members

These include:

- 5 Permanent Members (P5)
- 10 Non-Permanent Members

## Permanent Members (P5)

The permanent members are:

- China
- France
- Russia
- United Kingdom
- United States

These countries possess veto power.

## Non-Permanent Members

The ten non-permanent members are:

- Elected by the UN General Assembly
- Serve a two-year term
- Chosen on a regional basis

## Voting System

### Procedural Matters

Require at least:

- 9 affirmative votes

### Substantive Matters

Require:

- At least 9 affirmative votes
- No veto by a permanent member

## Veto Power

A negative vote by any permanent member can block a substantive resolution.

The veto is one of the most significant powers within the United Nations system.

## Functions

### Maintain International Peace and Security

The UNSC addresses:

- Armed conflicts
- International disputes
- Threats to peace

### Peacekeeping Operations

The council authorizes UN peacekeeping missions.

### Sanctions

The UNSC can impose:

- Economic sanctions
- Arms embargoes
- Travel restrictions

### Authorization of Force

In certain situations, the council may authorize collective action to maintain or restore international peace.

## Important Subsidiary Bodies

The Security Council oversees various committees and working groups dealing with:

- Sanctions
- Counter-terrorism
- Peacebuilding

## India and the UNSC

India has served multiple terms as a non-permanent member of the Security Council.

India advocates:

- Reform of the UNSC
- Expansion of permanent membership
- Greater representation for developing countries

## UNSC Reform

Major concerns regarding the council include:

- Representation imbalance
- Continued dominance of the P5
- Limited representation of developing nations

India, along with other countries, supports comprehensive reform of the Security Council.

## Importance

The UNSC plays a central role in:

- Global peace and security
- Conflict management
- International diplomacy
- Peacekeeping operations

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Veto-related deadlocks
- Geopolitical rivalries
- Emerging security threats
- Reform disagreements
