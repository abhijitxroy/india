# Demographics of West Bengal

## State Significance

Demographics helps us understand the people of West Bengal, including population, density, urbanisation, literacy, languages, religions, migration patterns, workforce composition, and social development.

West Bengal is one of India's most populous states and plays a major role in the country's economy, culture, politics, and education.

## Population Overview

West Bengal is among the most densely populated states in India.

Major reasons include:

- Fertile Ganga delta region
- Historical urbanisation
- Industrial development
- Trade and commerce
- Migration from neighbouring regions

## Population Growth

### Historical Trend

| Census Year | Approximate Population |
|----------|----------|
| 1951 | 26 Million+ |
| 1971 | 44 Million+ |
| 1991 | 68 Million+ |
| 2001 | 80 Million+ |
| 2011 | 91 Million+ |

Population growth has gradually slowed due to:

- Improved education
- Urbanisation
- Better healthcare
- Family planning awareness

## Population Density

West Bengal has one of the highest population densities in India.

Reasons:

- Limited geographical area
- Fertile agricultural land
- Large urban centres
- Historic settlement patterns

### High Density Regions

- Kolkata
- Howrah
- North 24 Parganas
- Hooghly
- Nadia
