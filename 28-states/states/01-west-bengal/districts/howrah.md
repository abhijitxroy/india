# Howrah District

## District Significance

Howrah is one of the most important industrial and transportation districts of West Bengal.

Located on the western bank of the Hooghly River opposite Kolkata, the district serves as a major gateway to Kolkata and Eastern India.

It is known for engineering industries, railway infrastructure, bridges, educational institutions, and urban development.

## Why Howrah Is Called the Gateway of Kolkata

Howrah serves as the primary western entry point to Kolkata.

Millions of passengers enter Kolkata through:

- Howrah Railway Station
- Howrah Bridge
- Vidyasagar Setu

The district acts as an important transportation and logistics hub connecting Kolkata with the rest of India.

## History

Historically, Howrah developed alongside Kolkata during the colonial period.

The district became important because of:

- River trade
- Railway development
- Industrial growth
- Engineering industries

During British rule, Howrah emerged as one of India's major industrial centres.

## Industrial History

Howrah has historically been known as the 'Sheffield of India'.

Major industries included:

- Engineering works
- Foundries
- Metal fabrication
- Rail-related manufacturing

The district played a significant role in India's industrialisation.

## Foundries and Engineering Industry

Howrah became famous for:

- Iron foundries
- Heavy engineering
- Machinery manufacturing
- Industrial equipment production

Many engineering units continue to operate in the district.

## Transport

One of the busiest railway stations in India.

Importance:

- National connectivity
- Passenger movement
- Economic activity

### Santragachi

A major railway hub known for:

- Long-distance trains
- Railway operations
- Transportation infrastructure

## Howrah Bridge (Rabindra Setu)

One of the most iconic landmarks of India.

Known for:

- Cantilever bridge design
- Connection between Kolkata and Howrah
- Heavy daily traffic movement

It is one of the most recognizable symbols of West Bengal.

## Vidyasagar Setu

Also known as the Second Hooghly Bridge.

Importance:

- Modern cable-stayed bridge
- Reduced traffic congestion
- Enhanced connectivity
