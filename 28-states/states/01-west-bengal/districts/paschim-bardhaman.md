# Paschim Bardhaman District

## District Significance

Paschim Bardhaman is the industrial powerhouse of West Bengal.

The district is home to the Raniganj Coalfield, Durgapur industrial region, Asansol urban belt, major steel plants, thermal power stations, and manufacturing industries.

It is one of the most urbanized and economically significant districts in Eastern India.
