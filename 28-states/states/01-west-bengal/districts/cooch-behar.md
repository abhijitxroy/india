# Cooch Behar District

## District Significance

Cooch Behar is one of the most historically significant districts of North Bengal.

The district is known for the Koch Kingdom, Cooch Behar Palace, royal heritage, agriculture, rivers, and its strategic location along the Bangladesh border.

It represents an important link between Bengal's history and the history of North-Eastern India.

## History

### Koch Kingdom

Cooch Behar was the centre of the powerful Koch Kingdom.

Important rulers include:

- Biswa Singha
- Nara Narayan

The kingdom played an important role in the history of North Bengal and North-East India.

### British Period

Cooch Behar became a princely state under British influence while retaining internal autonomy.

### Merger with India

After Independence, the princely state acceded to India and later became part of West Bengal.

This remains one of the most important events in the district's modern history.
