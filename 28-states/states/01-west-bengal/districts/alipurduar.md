# Alipurduar District

## District Significance

Alipurduar is one of the most important districts of North Bengal.

The district is known for its forests, wildlife, tea gardens, rivers, biodiversity, and strategic location near Bhutan and Assam.

It serves as a gateway to the Dooars region and is a major centre for eco-tourism and wildlife conservation.

## Administrative Overview

The district was created by separating areas from Jalpaiguri district.

Administrative units include:

- Alipurduar
- Falakata
- Madarihat
- Kalchini
- Kumargram

## History

Historically, the region formed part of:

- Kamata Kingdom
- Koch Kingdom
- British Bengal

During British rule, the area gained importance because of:

- Tea plantations
- Railway connectivity
- Forest resources

The district of Alipurduar was officially formed in 2014.

## Geography

Alipurduar lies in the Dooars region at the foothills of the Eastern Himalayas.

Key geographical features:

- Dense forests
- Rivers
- Floodplains
- Tea gardens
- Wildlife habitats

Major rivers include:

- Torsa
- Kaljani
- Raidak
- Sankosh

Known for:

### Jaldapara National Park
