# West Bengal Districts

This ranking is based on overall influence on West Bengal, considering:

- Economic contribution
- Population and urbanization
- Administrative importance
- Historical significance
- Tourism value
- Industrial development
- Strategic importance

## Tier 1 - State Powerhouses

1. Kolkata
2. North 24 Parganas
3. South 24 Parganas
4. Howrah
5. Paschim Bardhaman

Why:

- Highest economic influence
- Major population centres
- Administrative and political importance
- Key industrial and service-sector hubs
- Significant impact on state GDP

## Tier 2 - Major Growth and Heritage Districts

1. Hooghly
2. Purba Medinipur
3. Murshidabad
4. Nadia
5. Purba Bardhaman
6. Darjeeling
7. Malda

Why:

- Strong agriculture, industry, tourism, or trade contribution
- Important historical and cultural significance
- Major regional centres of influence

## Tier 3 - Important Regional Districts

1. Jalpaiguri
2. Cooch Behar
3. Birbhum
4. Bankura
5. Purulia
6. Paschim Medinipur
7. Kalimpong
8. Uttar Dinajpur

Why:

- Important regional economies
- Tourism, agriculture, or cultural importance
- Moderate influence at the state level

## Tier 4 - Emerging and Smaller Influence Districts

1. Jhargram
2. Alipurduar
3. Dakshin Dinajpur

Why:

- Smaller population and economy
- More localized influence
- Important for complete state understanding but lower overall state impact
