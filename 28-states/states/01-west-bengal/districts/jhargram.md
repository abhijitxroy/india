# Jhargram District

## District Significance

Jhargram is one of the most forest-rich districts of West Bengal.

The district is known for its tribal heritage, forests, wildlife, rivers, eco-tourism, and cultural traditions.

Created in 2017, Jhargram represents the western forested region of West Bengal and serves as an important gateway to the Junglemahal area.

## History

Historically, the region formed part of:

- Ancient forested Bengal
- Local tribal kingdoms
- British Bengal

The district was carved out of Paschim Medinipur in 2017.

The region has long been associated with the Junglemahal area of western Bengal.

## Geography

Jhargram lies near the eastern edge of the Chota Nagpur Plateau.

Major geographical features:

- Forests
- Red laterite soil
- Hills and uplands
- River valleys

Major rivers:

- Subarnarekha
- Dulung
- Kangsabati
