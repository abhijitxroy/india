# Kalimpong District

## District Significance

Kalimpong is one of the most scenic and strategically important hill districts of West Bengal.

The district is known for its Himalayan landscapes, Buddhist monasteries, orchid cultivation, tourism, and historical trade links with Tibet.

Created in 2017 from the former Darjeeling district, Kalimpong has emerged as an important administrative and tourism centre in the Eastern Himalayas.

## History

Historically, Kalimpong was influenced by:

- Sikkim Kingdom
- Bhutanese rule
- British India

The region became an important trade route between India and Tibet.

In 2017, Kalimpong was officially separated from Darjeeling and became a new district of West Bengal.
