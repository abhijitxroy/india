# Purulia District

## District Significance

Purulia is the westernmost district of West Bengal and serves as a cultural and geographical bridge between Bengal and the Chota Nagpur Plateau region.

The district is known for its hills, forests, tribal communities, folk traditions, dams, and natural beauty.

Purulia is also famous for Chhau dance, Ajodhya Hills, eco-tourism, and its unique cultural identity.

## History

Purulia was historically part of the ancient Manbhum region.

The district has been influenced by:

- Ancient tribal kingdoms
- Bengal kingdoms
- Chota Nagpur region
- British administration
- Post-independence linguistic reorganization

The district became part of West Bengal after the reorganization of states.

## Chhau Dance

Purulia Chhau is one of India's most famous folk dance traditions.

Known for:

- Masks
- Martial movements
- Mythological themes

It is internationally recognized and forms a major part of the district's cultural identity.
