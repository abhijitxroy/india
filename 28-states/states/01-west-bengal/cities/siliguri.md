# Siliguri

## Urban Importance

- Gateway to North Bengal, Northeast India, Bhutan, Nepal, and Bangladesh
- One of the fastest-growing cities in eastern India
- Major trade, transport, and logistics hub
- Strategic city located near the Siliguri Corridor (Chicken's Neck)
- Important center for tea, tourism, and commerce

## Historical Background

- Developed as a trading and transport center connecting the plains and the Himalayas.
- Growth accelerated with railway expansion and regional trade.
- Became an important commercial center for North Bengal.
- Emerged as a strategic city due to its location near international borders.

## Economy

Major sectors:

- Trade and commerce
- Tea industry
- Tourism
- Transportation and logistics
- Real estate and services

Siliguri serves as the commercial capital of North Bengal.

## Transport

### Road

- Connected by major national highways.
- Primary gateway to Sikkim, Bhutan, and Northeast India.

### Rail

- New Jalpaiguri (NJP) is one of the most important railway junctions in eastern India.

### Air

- Served by Bagdogra Airport, a major airport for North Bengal and the Northeast.

## Education

Major institutions include:

- University of North Bengal (nearby)
- Medical, engineering, and professional colleges
- Regional educational centers serving North Bengal

## Places of Interest

- Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary
- ISKCON Temple Siliguri
- Hong Kong Market
- Salugara Monastery
- Gateway routes to Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Sikkim, and Dooars

## Strategic Importance

- Located near the Siliguri Corridor.
- Critical link connecting mainland India with the Northeastern states.
- Important from trade, transportation, and national security perspectives.

## Challenges

- Rapid urbanization
- Traffic congestion
- Environmental pressures
- Infrastructure expansion requirements
