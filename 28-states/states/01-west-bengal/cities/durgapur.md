# Durgapur

## Urban Importance

- One of the most important planned industrial cities in India
- Major steel and manufacturing center of West Bengal
- Part of the Asansol–Durgapur industrial region
- Important education, engineering, and technology hub
- Key contributor to the state's industrial economy

## Historical Background

- Developed after India's independence as a planned industrial city.
- Became a major center of heavy industry under national industrialization programs.
- Growth was driven by steel plants, engineering industries, and power projects.
- Emerged as one of eastern India's leading industrial centers.

## Economy

Major sectors:

- Steel production
- Engineering industries
- Power generation
- Manufacturing
- Education and services

Important establishments include:

- Durgapur Steel Plant
- Alloy Steel Plant
- Durgapur Projects Limited
- Industrial estates and engineering units

## Education

Major institutions include:

- National Institute of Technology (NIT) Durgapur
- Dr. B.C. Roy Engineering College
- Numerous schools, colleges, and technical institutes

## Transport

### Road

- Connected through National Highway 19.
- Important link between Kolkata and western India.

### Rail

- Well connected to Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai, and other major cities.

### Air

- Served by nearby Andal Airport.
