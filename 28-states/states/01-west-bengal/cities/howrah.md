# Howrah

## Urban Importance

- Twin city of Kolkata and part of the Kolkata Metropolitan Area
- Major transportation gateway of eastern India
- Home to the historic Howrah Railway Station
- Important industrial and engineering center of West Bengal
- Located on the western bank of the Hooghly River

## Historical Background

- Developed rapidly during the British period due to its strategic location opposite Kolkata.
- Became an important industrial and transport center.
- Grew around railway, engineering, foundry, and manufacturing activities.
- Played a major role in the economic growth of Bengal.

## Economy

Major sectors:

- Engineering industries
- Manufacturing
- Logistics and transportation
- Trade and commerce
- Services sector

Howrah has historically been known as one of India's major engineering hubs.

## Transport

### Rail

- Home to Howrah Railway Station, one of the busiest railway stations in India.
- Connected to almost every major city in the country.

### Road

- Connected through national and state highways.
- Acts as a gateway to western and northern India.

### Metro

- Connected to Kolkata through the Kolkata Metro network.

### River Transport

- Ferry services connect Howrah and Kolkata across the Hooghly River.

## Education
