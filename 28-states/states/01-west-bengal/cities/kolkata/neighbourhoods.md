# Kolkata Neighbourhoods

## Urban Importance

Kolkata is a city of distinct neighbourhoods, each with its own history, culture, architecture, economy, and identity.

Understanding Kolkata's neighbourhoods helps explain how the city evolved from a colonial capital into a modern metropolitan region.

## Overview

Kolkata can broadly be understood through:

- North Kolkata
- Central Kolkata
- South Kolkata
- East Kolkata
- Port and Riverfront Areas
- New Growth Corridors

## North Kolkata

North Kolkata represents the historic heart of the city.

Known for:

- Heritage mansions
- Traditional Bengali families
- Narrow streets
- Historic markets
- Cultural institutions

Major areas:

- Shyambazar
- Bagbazar
- Sovabazar
- Kumartuli
- Maniktala

### Kumartuli

World-famous for idol making.

Known for:

- Durga Puja idols
- Traditional artisans
- Cultural heritage

### Sovabazar

Known for:

- Historic zamindar houses
- Traditional Durga Puja celebrations
- Colonial-era heritage

## Central Kolkata

Central Kolkata developed as the administrative and commercial core of the city.

Major areas:

- BBD Bagh
- Esplanade
- Dalhousie Area
- Park Street
- Bow Barracks

### BBD Bagh

Administrative and business centre.

Major landmarks:

- Writers' Building
- Raj Bhavan
- General Post Office

### Park Street

Known for:

- Restaurants
- Nightlife
- Colonial architecture
- Christmas celebrations

## South Kolkata

South Kolkata is one of the city's most developed residential regions.

Known for:

- Educational institutions
- Residential neighbourhoods
- Shopping districts
- Cultural activities

Major areas:

- Ballygunge
- Alipore
- Gariahat
- Jadavpur
- Tollygunge
- Behala

### Ballygunge

Known for:

- Residential areas
- Educational institutions
- Cultural significance

### Alipore

Known for:

- Government institutions
- Alipore Zoo
- Premium residential zones

### Jadavpur

Known for:

- Jadavpur University
- Student culture
- Academic environment

### Tollygunge

Known for:

- Bengali film industry
- Golf Club
- Metro connectivity

## East Kolkata

East Kolkata represents the city's modern expansion.

Major areas:

- Salt Lake
- New Town
- Rajarhat
- EM Bypass Corridor

### Salt Lake (Bidhannagar)

Known for:

- Planned urban development
- Sector V IT Hub
- Educational institutions
- Sports facilities

### Sector V

The largest IT district in Eastern India.

Major activities:

- Software development
- IT services
- Technology companies

### New Town

Known for:

- Modern infrastructure
- Business districts
- Residential developments
- Smart city initiatives
