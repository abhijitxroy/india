# Kolkata Economy

## Urban Importance

Kolkata is the economic capital of Eastern India.

The city serves as the commercial, financial, industrial, logistics, and service-sector hub for West Bengal, Eastern India, and parts of North-East India.

Historically, Kolkata was one of Asia's most important trading cities and continues to play a major role in India's economy.

## Economic Overview

Kolkata's economy is driven by:

- Financial services
- Information Technology
- Trade and commerce
- Logistics and transportation
- Manufacturing
- Education
- Healthcare
- Tourism

The city functions as the primary economic gateway for Eastern India.

## Historical Economic Importance

During British rule, Kolkata became:

- The commercial capital of British India
- A major port city
- A banking centre
- A manufacturing hub

Major industries developed around:

- Jute
- Engineering
- Shipping
- Trade
- Railways

## Port of Kolkata

The Port of Kolkata is India's oldest operating major port.

Importance:

- International trade
- Inland water transport
- Cargo movement
- Export-import operations

The port continues to support economic activity across Eastern India.

## Financial Sector

Kolkata is one of India's major financial centres.

Major institutions include:

- Reserve Bank of India regional offices
- Public sector banks
- Private banks
- Insurance companies
- Financial service providers

The city remains an important banking hub.

## Information Technology

The IT sector is one of Kolkata's fastest-growing industries.

Major IT locations:

- Salt Lake Sector V
- New Town

Major activities:

- Software development
- IT services
- Business process outsourcing
- Technology startups

## Manufacturing

Major manufacturing sectors include:

- Engineering
- Electrical equipment
- Chemicals
- Pharmaceuticals
- Food processing

The wider Kolkata metropolitan region supports a large industrial ecosystem.

## Trade and Commerce

- West Bengal

- Nepal
- Bhutan
- Bangladesh trade corridors

Major wholesale markets operate throughout the city.
