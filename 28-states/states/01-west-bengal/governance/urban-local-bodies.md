# Urban Local Bodies in West Bengal

## Administrative Importance

Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) are responsible for governing towns and cities in West Bengal. They provide essential civic services such as roads, water supply, sanitation, waste management, street lighting, public health, urban planning, and local development.

Urban local governance is a key pillar of democratic decentralisation in the state.

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Constitutional Basis | 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 |
| Urban Governance Types | Municipal Corporations, Municipalities, Nagar Panchayats |
| Election Authority | State Election Commission |
| Urban Development Department | Government of West Bengal |
| Largest Urban Body | Kolkata Municipal Corporation |

## Types of Urban Local Bodies

### Municipal Corporation

Created for large cities.

Characteristics:

- Large population
- Greater financial resources
- Larger administrative structure
- Mayor and Municipal Commissioner system

### Municipality

Created for medium-sized towns.

Responsibilities:

- Civic administration
- Urban planning
- Water supply
- Sanitation

### Nagar Panchayat

Established for transitional areas moving from rural to urban status.

Acts as a bridge between:

- Panchayat administration
- Municipal administration

## Major Municipal Corporations in West Bengal

### Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC)

The largest and most important urban local body in the state.

### Other Major Municipal Corporations

- Bidhannagar Municipal Corporation
- Asansol Municipal Corporation
- Durgapur Municipal Corporation

- Haldia Municipal Corporation

## Mayor

The Mayor is the political head of a Municipal Corporation.

Responsibilities:

- Presides over meetings
- Provides political leadership
- Represents the city
- Oversees civic priorities

## Municipal Commissioner

The Municipal Commissioner is the executive head.

Usually a senior civil servant.

Responsibilities:

- Administration
- Budget execution
- Service delivery
- Implementation of policies

## Municipal Elections

Municipal elections are conducted by the State Election Commission.

Citizens elect:

- Councillors
- Ward representatives

The elected body subsequently forms the municipal administration.
