# Agriculture in West Bengal

## Economic Importance

Agriculture is one of the most important sectors of West Bengal's economy.

The state is among India's leading producers of rice, potatoes, vegetables, fish, and jute.

A significant portion of the population depends directly or indirectly on agriculture and allied activities for employment and livelihood.

## Agriculture Overview

West Bengal benefits from:

- Fertile alluvial soil
- Extensive river systems
- Favorable climate
- High population density supporting agricultural markets
- Multiple cropping seasons

Agriculture remains one of the foundations of the state's economy.

## Rice Production

Rice is the most important agricultural crop in West Bengal.

Importance:

- Staple food crop
- Large cultivation area
- Major source of rural employment

West Bengal is consistently among India's largest rice-producing states.

Major rice-producing districts:

- Purba Bardhaman
- Nadia
- Murshidabad
- Hooghly
- Birbhum

## Potato Production

West Bengal is one of India's leading potato-producing states.

Major potato-producing districts:

- Hooghly
- Purba Bardhaman
- Bankura
- Paschim Bardhaman

Importance:

- Food security
- Agro-processing
- Cold storage industry

## Jute Cultivation

West Bengal is India's leading jute-producing state.

Importance:

- Cash crop
- Textile industry support
- Export earnings

Major jute-producing districts:

- Nadia
- Murshidabad
- North 24 Parganas
- Hooghly

## Tea Cultivation

Tea is one of the state's most internationally recognized agricultural products.

Major tea-growing regions:

- Darjeeling Hills
- Dooars
- Terai

Importance:

- Exports
- Employment generation
- Global recognition

Darjeeling Tea enjoys Geographical Indication (GI) status.

## Vegetables and Horticulture

West Bengal is a major producer of:

- Vegetables
- Mangoes
- Litchi
- Pineapple
- Banana

Important horticultural districts:

- Malda
- Murshidabad
- Nadia
- North Bengal districts

## Fisheries and Allied Activities

Agriculture in West Bengal is closely linked with fisheries.

Major activities include:

- Inland fisheries
- Aquaculture
- Coastal fisheries

The state is one of India's leading fish-producing regions.

## Irrigation

Agricultural productivity is supported by:

- Canal systems
- River irrigation
- Groundwater resources
- Damodar Valley projects

Important irrigation sources:

- Damodar River system
- Teesta projects
- Kangsabati Project

## Agricultural Exports

Major export-oriented products include:

- Tea
- Jute products
- Mangoes
- Fish products
- Vegetables

Agriculture contributes significantly to trade and exports.

## Agricultural Institutions

Important supporting institutions include:

- Research centres

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Small land holdings
- Floods and river erosion
- Cyclones in coastal areas
- Climate variability
- Post-harvest losses
- Market fluctuations
