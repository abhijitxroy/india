
# Presidents and Vice Presidents of India

## Overview

The President and Vice President occupy two of the highest constitutional offices in India.

Together, these offices help maintain constitutional continuity, democratic stability, and the functioning of key national institutions.

This section explains their roles, powers, elections, and historical evolution.

---

## Why This Section Matters

Studying the President and Vice President helps understand:

- Constitutional governance
- Head of State functions
- Parliamentary democracy
- Executive authority
- Legislative procedures
- Constitutional safeguards

These offices are important pillars of the Indian Republic.

## President vs Prime Minister

| President | Prime Minister |
|----------|----------|
| Head of State | Head of Government |
| Constitutional Head | Executive Leader |
| Indirectly Elected | Leader of Majority in Lok Sabha |
| Acts on aid and advice of Council of Ministers | Leads Council of Ministers |

## Quick Revision

- President is the Head of State.
- Vice President is the second-highest constitutional office.
- Vice President is Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
- President is elected by an Electoral College.
- President performs important constitutional functions.
- These offices help ensure continuity and stability in governance.
