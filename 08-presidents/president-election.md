# President Election in India

## Overview

The President of India is elected indirectly through an Electoral College.

The election process is designed to balance representation between the Union and the States while preserving the federal character of the Constitution.

## Importance

- Constitutional governance
- Federal structure
- Electoral systems
- Democratic legitimacy
- Executive continuity

## Electoral College

The Electoral College consists of:

- Elected Members of Lok Sabha
- Elected Members of Rajya Sabha
- Elected Members of State Legislative Assemblies
- Elected Members of Legislative Assemblies of Delhi and Puducherry

Nominated members do not participate.

## Election Process

```text
Electoral College
        ↓
Voting
        ↓
Counting
        ↓
Majority Requirement
        ↓
President Elected
```

## Voting Method

The President is elected using:

- Proportional Representation
- Single Transferable Vote (STV)
- Secret Ballot

## Eligibility

A candidate must:

- Be an Indian citizen
- Be at least 35 years old
- Be qualified for election to the Lok Sabha
- Not hold an office of profit

## Term

| Item | Details |
|--------|--------|
| Term Length | 5 Years |
| Re-election | Permitted |
| Removal | Impeachment |
