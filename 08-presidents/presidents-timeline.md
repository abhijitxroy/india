
# Presidents Timeline of India

## Overview

The Presidents Timeline traces the constitutional heads of the Republic of India from 1950 to the present.

The President is the Head of State and the highest constitutional office in India.

While executive powers are exercised through the Council of Ministers led by the Prime Minister, the President plays an important constitutional role in governance, legislation, appointments, and emergency provisions.

---

## Why Study Presidents?

Studying Presidents helps understand:

- Constitutional governance
- Head of State functions
- Executive powers
- Legislative responsibilities
- Emergency provisions
- Constitutional continuity

The office reflects the stability and continuity of the Indian Republic.

## Major Presidents of India

### Dr. Rajendra Prasad (1950–1962)

- First President of India
- Served during the early nation-building years
- Witnessed implementation of the Constitution

### Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (1962–1967)

- Distinguished philosopher and academic
- Served during an important period of national development

### Dr. Zakir Husain (1967–1969)

- First Muslim President of India
- Known for contributions to education

### K. R. Narayanan (1997–2002)

- First Dalit President of India
- Associated with an active constitutional approach

### A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (2002–2007)

- Scientist and former missile program leader
- Popularly known as the “People's President”

### Droupadi Murmu (2022–Present)

- First tribal President of India
- Represents an important milestone in constitutional history
