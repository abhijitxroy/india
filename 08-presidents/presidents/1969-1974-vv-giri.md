# V. V. Giri

## Overview

Varahagiri Venkata Giri served as the 4th President of India from 1969 to 1974.

He was a prominent trade union leader, freedom fighter, and politician.

Before becoming President, he served as Vice President of India and held several important public offices.

## Early Life and Education

V. V. Giri was born in present-day Odisha.

He studied in India and later pursued higher education in Ireland.

During his student years, he was influenced by nationalist and labour movements.

---

## Freedom Movement

He participated in India's struggle for independence and supported various nationalist causes.

His involvement in public life helped establish him as an important labour leader.

---

## Labour Movement

V. V. Giri is best remembered for his contribution to labour rights.

Major areas of work included:

- Trade union organization
- Workers' welfare
- Industrial relations
- Labour policy development

He became one of India's leading voices for workers.

---

## Public Offices Held

Before becoming President, he served as:

- Member of Parliament
- Union Minister
- Governor of several states
- Vice President of India

His long administrative experience prepared him for the presidency.

---

## Presidency

Following the death of President Zakir Husain in 1969, V. V. Giri served as Acting President.

He later contested and won the presidential election as an independent candidate.

His election remains one of the most significant presidential contests in Indian political history.

---

## Major Contributions

### Labour Welfare

He strengthened the visibility of labour issues in public policy.

### Constitutional Leadership

He maintained constitutional continuity during a politically significant period.

### Public Service

His career demonstrated the importance of labour representation in democratic governance.

---

## Awards and Recognition

V. V. Giri was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1975.

It is India's highest civilian honour.

---

## Legacy

V. V. Giri is remembered as:

- Labour leader-president
- Trade union pioneer
- Freedom fighter
- Advocate of workers' rights

He occupies a unique place in Indian political history because of his strong association with labour movements.

---
