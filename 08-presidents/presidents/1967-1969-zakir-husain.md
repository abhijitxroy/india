# Zakir Husain

## Overview

Dr. Zakir Husain served as the 3rd President of India from 1967 until his death in 1969.

He was a renowned educationist, scholar, and public intellectual.

He was the first Muslim President of India and devoted much of his life to education and nation building.

## Early Life and Education

Zakir Husain received his education in India and later pursued higher studies in Germany.

His academic background shaped his lifelong commitment to education and social development.

---

## Contribution to Education

He played a major role in developing modern educational institutions in India.

His most significant contribution was his association with Jamia Millia Islamia.

Major areas of contribution included:

- Educational reform
- National education movement
- Institution building
- Teacher development

---

## Public Service Career

Before becoming President, he served in several important positions.

These included:

- Vice-Chancellor of Jamia Millia Islamia
- Governor of Bihar
- Vice President of India

His reputation as an educationist earned him respect across political lines.

---

## Presidency

Zakir Husain became President of India in 1967.

His presidency emphasized education, national integration, and constitutional values.

He was widely admired for his simplicity, scholarship, and dedication to public service.

---

## Major Contributions

### Education

He promoted education as a tool for national development.

### National Integration

He encouraged unity, social harmony, and democratic values.

### Institution Building

He helped strengthen important educational institutions.

---

## Death in Office

Zakir Husain passed away on 3 May 1969 while serving as President.

He was the first President of India to die in office.

Following his death, Vice President V. V. Giri became Acting President.

---

## Awards and Recognition

He was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1963.

It is India's highest civilian honour.

---

## Legacy

Zakir Husain is remembered as:

- First Muslim President of India
- Distinguished educationist
- Builder of educational institutions
- Advocate of national integration

His contributions continue to influence Indian education and public life.
