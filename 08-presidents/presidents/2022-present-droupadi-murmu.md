# Droupadi Murmu

## Overview

Droupadi Murmu is the 15th President of India.

She assumed office on 25 July 2022 and became the first tribal President of India.

She is also the youngest person to hold the office of President.

Before becoming President, she served as a legislator, minister in the Government of Odisha, and Governor of Jharkhand.

## Importance

- First tribal President of India
- Representation of tribal communities at the highest constitutional office
- Long experience in public service
- Constitutional leadership and governance
- Symbol of social inclusion and democratic representation

## Early Life and Education

- Born in a tribal family in Odisha
- Completed higher education in Bhubaneswar
- Worked as a teacher before entering public life

## Political Career

Major positions held:

- Councillor in local government
- Member of Odisha Legislative Assembly
- Minister in the Odisha Government
- Governor of Jharkhand (2015–2021)

## Presidency

Major features of her presidency include:

- Focus on constitutional values
- Emphasis on tribal welfare and inclusion
- Support for education and social development
- Representation of marginalized communities
- Constitutional and ceremonial responsibilities as Head of State

## Historical Significance

Droupadi Murmu's election marked several milestones:

- First tribal President of India
- Youngest President of India
- First President born after India's independence
- Greater representation of historically underrepresented communities

## Legacy

Her presidency is viewed as an important milestone in India's democratic and social journey.

She represents the expanding participation of diverse communities in the country's constitutional institutions.
