# Rajendra Prasad

## Overview

Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the 1st President of India and one of the principal leaders of the Indian freedom movement.

He served as President from 1950 to 1962, making him the longest-serving President in Indian history.

He played an important role in the Constituent Assembly and in the establishment of the Republic of India.

## Early Life and Education

Rajendra Prasad was born in present-day Bihar.

He was an outstanding student and later pursued a career in law.

His academic achievements made him one of the most respected public figures of his generation.

---

## Freedom Movement

He joined the Indian National Congress and became a close associate of Mahatma Gandhi.

He actively participated in:

- Champaran Movement
- Non-Cooperation Movement
- Civil Disobedience Movement
- Quit India Movement

His contributions led to multiple imprisonments during British rule.

---

## Role in Constitution Making

Rajendra Prasad served as President of the Constituent Assembly.

He supervised discussions and proceedings that ultimately produced the Constitution of India.

His leadership helped ensure smooth functioning of the Assembly during a crucial phase of nation building.

---

## Presidency

On 26 January 1950, he became the first President of the Republic of India.

His presidency covered the formative years of independent India.

He worked with Prime Ministers Jawaharlal Nehru and played an important role in strengthening constitutional institutions.

---

## Major Contributions

### Nation Building

He helped establish democratic traditions during the early years of the Republic.

### Constitutional Stability

He supported the smooth functioning of India's constitutional system.

### Public Service

He remained widely respected for his simplicity, integrity, and dedication to public life.

---

## Awards and Recognition

Rajendra Prasad received the Bharat Ratna in 1962.

It is India's highest civilian award.

---

## Legacy

Rajendra Prasad is remembered as:

- First President of India
- Freedom fighter
- President of the Constituent Assembly
- Architect of democratic traditions

He remains one of the most respected figures in Indian political history.
