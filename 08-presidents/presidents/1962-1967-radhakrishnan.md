# Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

## Overview

Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan served as the 2nd President of India from 1962 to 1967.

He was one of India's most respected philosophers, teachers, diplomats, and statesmen.

His contributions to education and philosophy earned him national and international recognition.

## Early Life and Education

Radhakrishnan was born in present-day Tamil Nadu.

He studied philosophy and became a distinguished scholar at a young age.

His writings helped introduce Indian philosophy to a global audience.

---

## Academic Career

He taught at several leading institutions and became one of India's most influential intellectuals.

Major roles included:

- Professor of Philosophy
- Vice-Chancellor of Andhra University
- Vice-Chancellor of Banaras Hindu University
- International scholar and lecturer

His work focused on Indian philosophy, religion, ethics, and comparative thought.

---

## Diplomatic and Public Service Career

Before becoming President, he represented India in important diplomatic roles.

Key positions included:

- Ambassador to the Soviet Union
- Vice President of India
- Chairman of the Rajya Sabha

His diplomatic efforts strengthened India's international standing during the early years of independence.

---

## Presidency

Radhakrishnan became President in 1962.

His tenure coincided with important national events including the 1962 India-China War and the 1965 India-Pakistan War.

He provided moral leadership and emphasized national unity during challenging periods.

---

## Contributions

### Education

He strongly promoted education as the foundation of national development.

### Philosophy

He helped popularize Indian philosophical traditions across the world.

### Public Service

He demonstrated how academic excellence and public service could work together.

---

## Teachers' Day

His birthday, 5 September, is celebrated as Teachers' Day in India.

This remains one of his most enduring legacies.

---

## Legacy

Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan is remembered as:

- Philosopher-President
- Distinguished teacher
- Global ambassador of Indian thought
- Advocate of education and ethics

He remains one of the most respected intellectual figures in modern Indian history.
