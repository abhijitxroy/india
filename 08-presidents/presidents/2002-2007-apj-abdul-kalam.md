# A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

## Overview

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was the 11th President of India.

He served from 2002 to 2007 and became one of the most respected Presidents in Indian history.

Before becoming President, he was a renowned aerospace scientist and played a major role in India's missile and space programmes.

He is popularly known as the 'Missile Man of India'.

## Importance

- Development of India's missile programme
- Contribution to defence technology
- Promotion of science and innovation
- Inspiration for students and young people
- Popularization of Vision 2020

## Educational Background

- Studied Physics at St. Joseph's College
- Studied Aerospace Engineering at Madras Institute of Technology

## Scientific Contributions

Kalam played an important role in:

- ISRO launch vehicle programmes
- Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP)
- Agni missile programme
- Prithvi missile programme
- Strategic defence research

## Presidency

Major features of his presidency included:

- Strong public engagement
- Interaction with students across India
- Promotion of scientific thinking
- Support for innovation and education
- Non-partisan constitutional leadership

| Award | Year |

## Vision for India

Kalam advocated:

- Developed India by 2020
- Technology-driven growth
- Education reforms
- Innovation and entrepreneurship
- National self-reliance

## Legacy

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam remains one of India's most admired public figures.

His contributions to science, defence, education, and national development continue to inspire millions.
