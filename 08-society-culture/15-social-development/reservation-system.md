
# Reservation System in India

## Overview

The Reservation System is a policy framework designed to improve representation and opportunities for historically disadvantaged and underrepresented communities in education, public employment, and political institutions.

It is one of the most significant social justice measures in independent India and remains an important topic in governance, public policy, constitutional law, and politics.

---

## Why Study the Reservation System?

Studying the Reservation System helps understand:

- Social justice
- Constitutional provisions
- Equality and representation
- Public policy
- Governance reforms
- Contemporary political debates

The system plays a major role in India's social and political landscape.
