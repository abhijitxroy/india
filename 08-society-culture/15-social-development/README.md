
# Social Transformations in India

## Overview

This section covers the major social transformations that changed Indian society after independence.

The focus is on understanding:

- Social justice and representation
- Equality and inclusion
- Women's participation
- Education and social mobility
- Urbanization and demographic change
- Digital society and modern social behavior

---

## Why This Section Matters

India's social journey can be understood through a series of major transformations.

```text
Social Justice
       ↓
Representation
       ↓
Education Expansion
       ↓
Urbanization
       ↓
Middle Class Growth
       ↓
Digital Society
       ↓
Modern India
```

Understanding these transformations explains how Indian society evolved after independence.
