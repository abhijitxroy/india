# Taj Mahal

## Historical and Cultural Importance

The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in the world and a globally recognized symbol of India's cultural heritage.

Built during the Mughal era, it is renowned for its architectural beauty, artistic excellence, and historical significance.

## What is the Taj Mahal?

The Taj Mahal is a white marble mausoleum built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

It is regarded as one of the finest examples of Mughal architecture and artistic craftsmanship.
