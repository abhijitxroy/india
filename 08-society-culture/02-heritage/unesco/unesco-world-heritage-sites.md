# UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India

## Historical and Cultural Importance

UNESCO World Heritage Sites are places recognized for their outstanding cultural or natural value to humanity.

India possesses numerous World Heritage Sites that reflect its rich history, architecture, biodiversity, cultural traditions, and civilizational legacy.

## What are UNESCO World Heritage Sites?

World Heritage Sites are locations identified by UNESCO as possessing exceptional universal value that deserves protection for future generations.

These sites may be cultural monuments, historical cities, natural landscapes, or biodiversity hotspots.
