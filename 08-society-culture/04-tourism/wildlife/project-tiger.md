# Project Tiger

## Overview

Project Tiger is one of India's most important wildlife conservation programmes.

It was launched to protect the Bengal Tiger and conserve its natural habitats.

The programme is regarded as a landmark initiative in wildlife conservation and biodiversity protection.

## Launch

### Year

- 1973

### Launched By

- Government of India

### Initial Coverage

- 9 Tiger Reserves

The programme was started in response to the declining tiger population in India.

## Objectives

Project Tiger aims to:

- Protect tigers from poaching
- Conserve tiger habitats
- Maintain ecological balance
- Protect biodiversity
- Promote scientific wildlife management

## Need for Project Tiger

Major threats to tiger populations included:

- Poaching
- Habitat destruction
- Human encroachment
- Illegal wildlife trade
- Decline in prey population

## Tiger Reserves

Tiger conservation is carried out through designated Tiger Reserves.

These reserves contain:

- Core Areas
- Buffer Areas

### Core Area

- Strictly protected zone
- Minimal human interference

### Buffer Area

- Supports conservation and sustainable activities

## National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA)

### Established

- 2005

### Role

The NTCA oversees implementation of Project Tiger and tiger conservation policies across India.

## Tiger Census

India conducts periodic tiger population assessments using scientific methods.

The tiger census helps:

- Monitor populations
- Evaluate conservation efforts
- Guide policy decisions

## Importance of Tigers

Tigers are considered:

- Apex predators
- Indicator species
- Umbrella species

Their conservation helps protect entire ecosystems.

## Major Tiger Reserves

Examples include:

- Jim Corbett Tiger Reserve
- Bandipur Tiger Reserve
- Kanha Tiger Reserve
- Ranthambore Tiger Reserve
- Sundarbans Tiger Reserve
- Kaziranga Tiger Reserve

## Achievements

Major achievements include:

- Increase in tiger population
- Expansion of tiger reserves
- Improved habitat protection
- Greater public awareness
- Strengthened anti-poaching measures

## Challenges

Important challenges include:

- Human-wildlife conflict
- Habitat fragmentation
- Poaching networks
- Climate change impacts
- Development pressures

## Significance

Project Tiger is considered one of the most successful wildlife conservation programmes in the world.

It has contributed significantly to the protection of India's biodiversity and ecological heritage.
