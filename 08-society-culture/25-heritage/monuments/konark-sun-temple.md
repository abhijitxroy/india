# Konark Sun Temple

## Historical and Cultural Importance

The Konark Sun Temple is one of India's most remarkable architectural and cultural monuments.

Built in the 13th century, it is dedicated to Surya (the Sun God) and is renowned for its intricate stone carvings, monumental design, and historical significance.

## What is the Konark Sun Temple?

The Konark Sun Temple is a monumental Hindu temple designed in the form of a giant stone chariot with wheels and horses.

It represents the Sun God's celestial chariot and is considered a masterpiece of Kalinga architecture.
