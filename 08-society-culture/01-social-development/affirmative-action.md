# Affirmative Action

## Overview

Affirmative Action refers to policies and measures designed to improve opportunities, representation, and participation for historically disadvantaged and underrepresented groups.

In India, affirmative action is closely associated with reservation policies, social justice initiatives, educational access, and representation in public institutions.

It is a major concept in discussions on equality, inclusion, and public policy.

---

## Why Study Affirmative Action?

Studying affirmative action helps understand:

- Social justice
- Equality of opportunity
- Representation
- Constitutional governance
- Public policy
- Inclusion strategies

It is a foundational concept behind many social transformation initiatives.
