# Christianity in India

## Overview

Christianity is one of the major religions practiced in India.

It has a long history in the Indian subcontinent and forms an important part of India's religious and cultural diversity.

India is home to several Christian denominations, traditions, educational institutions, and charitable organizations.

## Origin of Christianity

### Founder

- Jesus Christ

### Place of Origin

- West Asia

### Period

- First Century CE

Christianity is based on the life and teachings of Jesus Christ.

## Arrival of Christianity in India

According to tradition, Christianity arrived in India through:

### Saint Thomas the Apostle

- Arrived on the Malabar Coast
- Traditionally believed to have reached India in 52 CE

This tradition is especially associated with the Saint Thomas Christians of Kerala.

## Core Beliefs

Major beliefs include:

- Belief in one God
- Teachings of Jesus Christ
- Love and compassion
- Forgiveness
- Salvation through faith

## Holy Book

### Bible

The Bible is the holy scripture of Christianity.

It consists of:

- Old Testament
- New Testament

## Place of Worship

### Church

A church is the place of worship for Christians.

Important activities include:

- Prayer
- Worship services
- Religious instruction
- Community gatherings

## Major Christian Denominations in India

### Roman Catholic Church

One of the largest Christian communities in India.

### Protestant Churches

Include various denominations established through missionary activities and reform movements.

### Orthodox Churches

Particularly significant in Kerala.

## Christianity and Indian Society

Christian communities have contributed to:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Social welfare
- Literature
- Tribal development

## Major Christian Festivals

### Christmas

Celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ.

### Good Friday

Commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus.

### Easter

Celebrates the resurrection of Jesus Christ.

## Important Churches and Pilgrimage Sites

### Basilica of Bom Jesus

- Goa
- UNESCO World Heritage Site

### San Thome Basilica

- Chennai, Tamil Nadu
- Associated with Saint Thomas

### Velankanni Basilica

- Tamil Nadu
- Major Christian pilgrimage center

### St. Francis Church

- Kochi, Kerala

## Distribution in India

Significant Christian populations are found in:

- Kerala
- Goa
- Nagaland
- Mizoram
- Meghalaya
- Tamil Nadu

## Significance

Christianity contributes to India's pluralistic and multicultural character.

Its institutions continue to play an important role in education, healthcare, and social service.
