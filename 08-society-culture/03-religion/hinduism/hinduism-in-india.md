# Hinduism in India

## Overview

Hinduism is one of the world's oldest living religions and the largest religion in India.

It originated in the Indian subcontinent and has evolved over thousands of years through diverse philosophical traditions, scriptures, practices, and cultural influences.

Hinduism has significantly shaped India's civilization, culture, art, literature, festivals, and social traditions.

## Origin and Development

Unlike many religions, Hinduism does not have a single founder.

It developed gradually through:

- Vedic traditions
- Philosophical schools
- Regional practices
- Bhakti movements
- Cultural interactions over centuries

## Sacred Texts

### Vedas

The oldest sacred texts of Hinduism.

They include:

- Rig Veda
- Yajur Veda
- Sama Veda
- Atharva Veda

### Upanishads

Philosophical texts discussing the nature of reality and spiritual knowledge.

### Epics

- Ramayana
- Mahabharata

### Bhagavad Gita

One of the most influential spiritual texts in Hindu philosophy.

## Core Concepts

### Dharma

Righteous duty and ethical conduct.

### Karma

The principle that actions influence future outcomes.

### Samsara

Cycle of birth, death, and rebirth.

### Moksha

Liberation from the cycle of rebirth.

## Major Deities

### Brahma

Associated with creation.

### Vishnu

Associated with preservation.

### Shiva

Associated with transformation and destruction.

### Devi

Represents the divine feminine power.

## Major Traditions

### Vaishnavism

Worship of Vishnu and his avatars.

### Shaivism

Worship of Shiva.

### Shaktism

Worship of the Goddess.

### Smarta Tradition

Emphasizes worship of multiple deities.

## Important Festivals

Major Hindu festivals include:

- Diwali
- Holi
- Navaratri
- Dussehra
- Janmashtami
- Ram Navami
- Makar Sankranti

## Pilgrimage Traditions

Important pilgrimage traditions include:

- Char Dham
- Jyotirlingas
- Shakti Peethas
- Kumbh Mela
- Vaishno Devi
- Amarnath Yatra

## Philosophical Schools

Important schools include:

- Samkhya
- Yoga
- Nyaya
- Vaisheshika
- Mimamsa
- Vedanta

## Contributions to Indian Culture

Hinduism has influenced:

- Literature
- Music
- Dance
- Architecture
- Sculpture
- Philosophy
- Social customs

## Distribution in India

Hindus form the majority population in India and are present across all states and union territories.

## Significance

Hinduism forms a foundational component of India's cultural and civilizational heritage.

Its traditions continue to influence daily life, festivals, values, and social practices.
