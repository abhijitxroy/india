# Sikhism in India

## Overview

Sikhism is one of the major religions that originated in India.

It was founded by Guru Nanak Dev Ji in the fifteenth century in the Punjab region.

Sikhism emphasizes devotion to one God, equality, honest living, service to humanity, and spiritual growth.

Today, India is home to the largest Sikh population in the world.

## Origin

### Founder

- Guru Nanak Dev Ji

### Period

- Fifteenth Century CE

### Place of Origin

- Punjab Region

Guru Nanak challenged social inequalities and promoted a path based on devotion, truth, and service.

## Core Beliefs

Major principles of Sikhism include:

- Belief in one God
- Equality of all human beings
- Honest livelihood
- Service to humanity (Seva)
- Sharing with others
- Rejection of caste discrimination

## Ten Sikh Gurus

Sikhism was guided by ten Gurus.

### Important Gurus

- Guru Nanak Dev Ji (First Guru)
- Guru Angad Dev Ji
- Guru Amar Das Ji
- Guru Ram Das Ji
- Guru Arjan Dev Ji
- Guru Hargobind Ji
- Guru Har Rai Ji
- Guru Har Krishan Ji
- Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji
- Guru Gobind Singh Ji (Tenth Guru)

## Guru Granth Sahib

The Guru Granth Sahib is the holy scripture of Sikhism.

Guru Gobind Singh Ji declared it the eternal Guru of the Sikh community.

It contains hymns and teachings of Sikh Gurus and other saints.

## Khalsa

### Established By

- Guru Gobind Singh Ji

### Year

- 1699

The Khalsa was created to uphold faith, courage, discipline, and justice.

## Five Ks

The Five Ks are important symbols of Sikh identity.

- Kesh (Uncut Hair)
- Kangha (Comb)
- Kara (Steel Bracelet)
- Kirpan (Ceremonial Sword)
- Kachera (Special Garment)

## Gurdwara

A Gurdwara is the Sikh place of worship.

Important features include:

- Prayer and worship
- Community gatherings
- Langar (Community Kitchen)
- Religious learning

## Langar

Langar is a community meal served to all people without discrimination.

It symbolizes:

- Equality
- Service
- Brotherhood
- Social harmony

## Sikhism and Indian Society

Sikhism contributed to:

- Social equality
- Religious harmony
- Community service
- Resistance against oppression
- Cultural development

## Major Sikh Festivals

Important festivals include:

- Guru Nanak Gurpurab
- Baisakhi
- Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti
- Hola Mohalla

## Important Sikh Pilgrimage Sites

### Golden Temple

- Amritsar, Punjab

### Anandpur Sahib

- Punjab

### Takht Sri Patna Sahib

- Bihar

### Hazur Sahib

- Maharashtra

## Distribution in India

The largest Sikh population is found in:

- Punjab
- Haryana
- Delhi
- Rajasthan
- Uttarakhand

## Significance

Sikhism is an important part of India's religious and cultural heritage.

Its teachings continue to influence values relating to equality, service, courage, and social justice.
