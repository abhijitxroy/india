

# Economic Survey Resources

## Overview

This document explains the Economic Survey and related resources that help citizens understand the performance of the Indian economy, policy priorities, development challenges, and economic trends.

---

## Why It Matters

The Economic Survey is one of the most important annual government publications.

It helps readers understand:

- Economic growth trends
- Government policy priorities
- Sector performance
- Development challenges
- Economic opportunities and risks

---

## Primary Economic Survey Resources

### Economic Survey of India

Prepared annually before the Union Budget.

Key Areas:

- GDP Growth
- Inflation
- Employment
- Agriculture
- Industry
- Services
- Trade
- Public Finance

---

### Statistical Appendix

Provides detailed economic data supporting the survey.

Useful For:

- Trend analysis
- Economic indicators
- Historical comparisons

---

### Sectoral Analysis

Economic Survey chapters often include:

- Agriculture
- Manufacturing
- Infrastructure
- Digital Economy
- Financial Sector
- Social Development

---
