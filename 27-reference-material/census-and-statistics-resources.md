

# Census & Statistics Resources

## Overview

This document lists important census, demographic, economic, social, and statistical resources that help citizens understand India's population, economy, development indicators, and public policy trends.

---

## Why It Matters

Statistics help answer:

- How India's population is changing
- How states differ in development
- How education and health indicators evolve
- How economic growth is measured
- How governments make policy decisions

---

## Primary Statistical Sources

### Census of India

India's largest demographic data collection exercise.

Key Areas:

- Population
- Literacy
- Migration
- Urbanization
- Housing
- Language
- Religion

---

### National Sample Surveys (NSS)

Large-scale surveys covering:

- Employment
- Consumption
- Social indicators
- Household conditions

---

### Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)

Important source for:

- Employment data
- Unemployment trends
- Labour participation rates

---

### National Family Health Survey (NFHS)

Provides data on:

- Health
- Nutrition
- Maternal care
- Child development
- Family welfare

---

### Annual Survey of Industries (ASI)

Tracks:

- Industrial production
- Manufacturing performance
- Employment in industries

---
