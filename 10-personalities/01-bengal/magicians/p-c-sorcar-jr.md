# P. C. Sorcar Jr.

## Overview

P. C. Sorcar Jr. is one of India's most famous magicians and illusionists.

He continued the legacy of the legendary magician P. C. Sorcar and became internationally known for large-scale stage illusions, television appearances, and the promotion of magic as a performing art.
