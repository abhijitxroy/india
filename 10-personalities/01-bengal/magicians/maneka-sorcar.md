# Maneka Sorcar

## Overview

Maneka Sorcar is a noted Bengali magician, performer, and author who helped popularize magic and illusion arts in India.

She is known for promoting stage magic, encouraging greater participation of women in the field, and preserving the legacy of Bengali magic traditions.
