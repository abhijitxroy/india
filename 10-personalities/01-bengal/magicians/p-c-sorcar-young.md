# P. C. Sorcar Young

## Overview

P. C. Sorcar Young is a prominent Indian magician and illusionist who continued the famous Sorcar family tradition of stage magic.

Known for modern illusion shows, international performances, and innovative presentation styles, he helped carry the Sorcar legacy into a new generation.
