# Matangini Hazra

## Overview

Matangini Hazra was a prominent freedom fighter from Bengal who actively participated in India's struggle for independence.

She is remembered for her courage, commitment to the nationalist movement, and sacrifice during the Quit India Movement. Because of her dedication to the cause of freedom, she became one of the most respected women freedom fighters from Bengal.

## Participation in the Freedom Movement

### Nationalist Activities

- Participated in anti-colonial campaigns.
- Supported the Indian independence movement.
- Took part in protests and demonstrations.
- Inspired local communities to join nationalist activities.

### Civil Disobedience Movement

- Participated in activities associated with the freedom struggle.
- Supported campaigns against British policies.
- Became known for her dedication and fearlessness.

## Quit India Movement

### Role in 1942

During the Quit India Movement, Matangini Hazra participated in a procession carrying the Indian national flag.

While leading protesters, she was shot by British authorities.

Despite being injured, she reportedly continued moving forward with the flag and remained committed to the cause of independence.

## Sacrifice and Martyrdom

- Died during the Quit India Movement in 1942.
- Became a symbol of courage and patriotism.
- Remembered for her determination and sacrifice.

## Legacy

- One of the most respected women freedom fighters of Bengal.
- Honored through statues, roads, schools, and memorials.
- Continues to inspire citizens and students.
- Represents the contribution of women to India's freedom struggle.

## Historical Significance

Matangini Hazra demonstrated that the freedom movement was supported by people from all sections of society, including rural communities and women leaders.

Her sacrifice remains an important chapter in Bengal's contribution to India's independence.
