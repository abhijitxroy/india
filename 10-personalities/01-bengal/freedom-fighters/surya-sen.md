# Surya Sen

## Overview

Surya Sen, popularly known as Masterda, was one of the most important revolutionary freedom fighters from Bengal.

He is best known for leading the Chittagong Armoury Raid of 1930, one of the most significant revolutionary actions against British rule in India.

## Early Life

- Born in Chittagong.
- Worked as a teacher.
- Became involved in revolutionary nationalist activities at a young age.
- Inspired many young revolutionaries in Bengal.

## Revolutionary Activities

### Chittagong Armoury Raid (1930)

The objective of the operation was to disrupt British administration by capturing weapons and communication infrastructure.

The revolutionary group:

- Attacked British armouries.
- Disrupted communication networks.
- Attempted to isolate Chittagong from British control.
- Raised the Indian national flag.

Although the operation could not permanently hold the city, it became a powerful symbol of resistance.

## Leadership

Surya Sen was respected for:

- Organizational ability
- Courage and discipline
- Commitment to independence
- Ability to inspire young revolutionaries

## Associates

Important revolutionaries associated with the movement included:

- Pritilata Waddedar
- Kalpana Datta
- Ananta Singh
- Ganesh Ghosh
- Lokenath Bal

## Arrest and Execution

- Arrested by British authorities after a prolonged search.
- Subjected to severe torture.
- Executed in 1934.

His death further strengthened nationalist sentiment in Bengal.

## Legacy

- Remembered as one of Bengal's greatest revolutionary leaders.
- The Chittagong Armoury Raid remains an important chapter in India's freedom struggle.
- Widely honored in Bengal and across India.
- Continues to inspire studies of revolutionary nationalism.
