# Prafulla Chandra Sen

## Overview

Prafulla Chandra Sen was an Indian freedom fighter, Gandhian leader, and politician who served as the third Chief Minister of West Bengal from 1962 to 1967. He is remembered for his simplicity, commitment to public service, and efforts to address food shortages and agricultural challenges during a difficult period in the state's history.

## Chief Minister of West Bengal

### Leadership Period

- Became Chief Minister of West Bengal in 1962.
- Succeeded Dr. Bidhan Chandra Roy.
- Led the state during a period of economic and food-related challenges.

### Major Challenges

- Food shortages and supply issues.
- Rising political opposition.
- Agricultural and rural development concerns.
- Managing a changing political environment in West Bengal.

## Contributions

- Promoted agriculture and rural development.
- Encouraged cooperative movements.
- Focused on food production and distribution.
- Continued administrative development after the B. C. Roy era.

## Historical Significance

Prafulla Chandra Sen's tenure marked the transition between the long Congress-dominated period under Bidhan Chandra Roy and the increasingly competitive political environment that emerged in West Bengal during the late 1960s.
