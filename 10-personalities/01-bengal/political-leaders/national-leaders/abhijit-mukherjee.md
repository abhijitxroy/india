# Abhijit Mukherjee

## Overview

Abhijit Mukherjee is an Indian politician from West Bengal and the son of former President Pranab Mukherjee. He represented the Jangipur Lok Sabha constituency and was associated with the Indian National Congress.

## Professional Career

Before entering active politics, Abhijit Mukherjee worked as an engineer in the steel industry.

## Political Career

### Member of West Bengal Legislative Assembly

- Elected as MLA from Nalhati constituency.
- Served in the West Bengal Legislative Assembly before moving to national politics.

### Member of Parliament

- Contested the Jangipur Lok Sabha by-election in 2012.
- Won the election after the seat became vacant following Pranab Mukherjee's election as President of India.
- Re-elected to the Lok Sabha in 2014.

## Contributions
