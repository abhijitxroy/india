# Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose

## Overview

Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose was one of the most influential leaders of the Indian freedom movement. He is remembered for his uncompromising struggle against British rule, leadership of the Indian National Army (INA), and the slogan 'Give me blood, and I will give you freedom.'

## Early Life and Education

- Born into a prominent Bengali family.
- Studied at Presidency College and Scottish Church College in Kolkata.
- Qualified for the Indian Civil Service (ICS) examination.
- Resigned from the ICS to participate in India's freedom movement.

## Role in the Freedom Movement

- Joined the Indian National Congress.
- Emerged as a prominent nationalist leader.
- Advocated complete independence from British rule.
- Served as President of the Indian National Congress in 1938 and 1939.

## Forward Bloc

- Founded the All India Forward Bloc in 1939.
- Sought to unite anti-colonial forces under a more radical nationalist platform.

## Indian National Army (INA)

- Escaped British surveillance and traveled abroad during World War II.
- Reorganized and led the Indian National Army.
- Mobilized Indian prisoners of war and expatriates for the independence struggle.
- Established the Provisional Government of Free India (Azad Hind).

## Famous Slogans

- 'Jai Hind'
- 'Give me blood, and I will give you freedom'
- 'Delhi Chalo'

## Legacy

- One of the most respected freedom fighters in Indian history.
- Inspired generations of Indians with his patriotism and leadership.
- The INA trials contributed to growing anti-British sentiment across India.
- Continues to be honored through memorials, institutions, and annual commemorations.

## Historical Significance

Subhas Chandra Bose represented a distinct approach within the freedom movement. While many leaders emphasized non-violent resistance, Bose believed that armed struggle could also play a role in achieving independence.
