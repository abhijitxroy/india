# Ashok Mitra

## Overview

Ashok Mitra was an Indian economist, civil servant, public intellectual, and policymaker from Bengal. He served in important administrative and economic positions and later became one of India's most influential economic commentators and public thinkers.

## Economic Contributions

### Public Policy

- Worked on issues related to economic development.

## Political and Public Life

- Later became associated with public policy and political discussions.
- Served as Finance Minister of West Bengal.

## Historical Significance

Ashok Mitra was one of the most influential economist-administrators from Bengal. His work connected economics, governance, administration, and public policy, making him an important figure in post-independence India's intellectual and policy landscape.
