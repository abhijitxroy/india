# Debabrata Bandyopadhyay

## Overview

Debabrata Bandyopadhyay was an Indian Administrative Service (IAS) officer, administrator, and policy expert known for his contributions to land reforms, rural development, and public administration in West Bengal. He is widely associated with the implementation of land reform initiatives that influenced rural governance and agricultural development.
