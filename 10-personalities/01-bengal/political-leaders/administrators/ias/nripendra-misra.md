# Nripendra Misra

## Overview

Nripendra Misra is a retired Indian Administrative Service (IAS) officer known for his distinguished career in public administration, governance, telecommunications regulation, and policy implementation. He is best known for serving as the Principal Secretary to the Prime Minister of India and for his contributions to administrative reforms and institutional governance.
