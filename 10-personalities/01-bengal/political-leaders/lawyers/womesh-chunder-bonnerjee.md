# Womesh Chunder Bonnerjee

## Overview

Womesh Chunder Bonnerjee was a distinguished Indian lawyer, barrister, and political leader from Bengal. He is best known as the first President of the Indian National Congress and is regarded as one of the early architects of organized political activity in India.

## Early Life and Education

- Born in Calcutta (present-day Kolkata).
- Received his education in Bengal.
- Later traveled to England to study law.
- Qualified as a barrister and built a successful legal career.

## Legal Career

- Practiced law before the Calcutta High Court.
- Earned recognition as one of the leading Indian lawyers of his time.
- Handled important civil and constitutional matters.
- Helped demonstrate that Indians could succeed in the highest levels of the legal profession during British rule.

## Indian National Congress

### First Congress President

- Presided over the first session of the Indian National Congress in 1885.
- Played a major role in shaping the early direction of the organization.
- Supported constitutional methods and political representation for Indians.

### Political Contributions

- Advocated greater Indian participation in administration and governance.
- Encouraged political dialogue and institutional reform.
- Helped lay the foundations of India's national movement.

## Historical Significance

The first session of the Indian National Congress marked the beginning of an organized national political platform in India. As its first President, Womesh Chunder Bonnerjee occupies an important place in the history of Indian democracy and nationalism.

## Legacy

- First President of the Indian National Congress.
- Pioneer among Indian lawyers and political leaders.
- Important figure in the early national movement.
- Remembered for promoting constitutional and democratic methods.
