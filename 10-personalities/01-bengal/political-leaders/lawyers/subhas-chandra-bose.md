# Subhas Chandra Bose (Lawyer)

## Overview

Subhas Chandra Bose was a Bengali lawyer and public figure. He should not be confused with Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose, the famous freedom fighter and leader of the Indian National Army (INA).

## Career

- Associated with the legal profession in Bengal.
- Participated in public and civic affairs through his legal work.
- Contributed to the development of legal and institutional life in the region.

## Historical Context

Many individuals in Bengal shared similar names during the late colonial and post-independence periods. This Subhas Chandra Bose belongs to the legal profession and is distinct from Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose.

## Legacy

- Remembered as a member of Bengal's legal community.
- Contributed to public life through the practice of law.
