# Chittaranjan Das

## Overview

Chittaranjan Das, popularly known as C. R. Das and Deshbandhu, was a prominent lawyer, freedom fighter, political leader, and nationalist from Bengal. He played a major role in the Indian freedom movement and was one of the most influential leaders of Bengal during the early twentieth century.

## Early Life and Education

- Born in Calcutta (present-day Kolkata).
- Studied at Presidency College.
- Traveled to England for higher studies.
- Qualified in law and returned to India to practice as a lawyer.

## Legal Career

- Became one of the leading lawyers of Bengal.
- Earned national recognition for his legal skills.
- Defended several nationalist leaders and revolutionaries.
- Known for representing Aurobindo Ghose in the Alipore Bomb Case.

## Role in the Freedom Movement

### Indian National Congress

- Active member of the Indian National Congress.
- Supported the Non-Cooperation Movement led by Mahatma Gandhi.
- Worked to mobilize political support across Bengal.

### Swaraj Party

- Co-founded the Swaraj Party in 1923 with Motilal Nehru.
- Advocated participation in legislative councils to challenge colonial policies from within.
- Became one of the leading voices of constitutional opposition to British rule.

## Public Life

- Served as Mayor of Calcutta.
- Promoted education, self-governance, and national unity.
- Encouraged political participation among ordinary citizens.

## Historical Significance

Chittaranjan Das helped bridge constitutional politics and mass nationalism. His leadership influenced both the Indian National Congress and the political development of Bengal during a crucial phase of the freedom movement.

## Legacy

- One of Bengal's most respected nationalist leaders.
- Founder of the Swaraj Party.
- Distinguished lawyer and public figure.
- Remembered by the title Deshbandhu, meaning 'Friend of the Nation'.
