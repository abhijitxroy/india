# Sir Biren Mookerjee

## Overview

Sir Biren Mookerjee was a prominent Bengali business leader, industrial entrepreneur, and one of the leading figures of India's industrial development during the twentieth century. He is best known for his leadership of Martin Burn Ltd., one of the most influential engineering and construction companies of its time.

His contributions helped expand Indian participation in industry, infrastructure, engineering, and business management during the late colonial and early post-independence periods.

## Early Life and Education

- Born in Bengal
- Came from a family associated with business and public life
- Received higher education in India
- Developed an early interest in industry, engineering, and business administration

His education and business experience prepared him for leadership roles in major industrial enterprises.

## Martin Burn and Industrial Leadership

### Leadership Role

Sir Biren Mookerjee played a major role in the growth of Martin Burn Ltd., which became one of India's leading engineering and construction firms.

### Areas of Work

The company was involved in:

- Engineering projects
- Infrastructure development
- Construction activities
- Industrial ventures
- Urban development projects

Under his leadership, the company expanded its influence across multiple sectors of the economy.
