# Purnendu Chatterjee

## Overview

Purnendu Chatterjee is a Bengali businessman, investor, and entrepreneur best known as the founder and chairman of The Chatterjee Group (TCG). He is one of the most internationally recognized Bengali business leaders and has played an important role in investment, technology, manufacturing, infrastructure, and petrochemicals.

Through his global investment activities, he became a prominent example of Bengali entrepreneurship on the international stage.

## Early Life and Education

- Born in West Bengal
- Studied engineering in India
- Pursued higher education in the United States
- Built a career in finance, investment, and business management

His academic and professional background helped him establish a global investment network.

## The Chatterjee Group (TCG)

### Foundation

Purnendu Chatterjee founded The Chatterjee Group (TCG), a private investment organization with interests across multiple sectors.

### Areas of Investment

TCG has invested in:

- Petrochemicals
- Technology
- Manufacturing
- Infrastructure
- Healthcare
- Financial services

The group developed a significant international presence through strategic investments and partnerships.
