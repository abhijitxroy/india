# Alamohan Das

## Overview

Alamohan Das was a pioneering Bengali industrialist, entrepreneur, and self-made businessman who played an important role in promoting indigenous industry in Bengal during the twentieth century.

He is best remembered for establishing industries in Howrah and for developing Dasnagar, an industrial township that became a symbol of Bengali entrepreneurship and industrial development.

## Early Life

- Born in 1895
- Came from a modest background
- Built his career through entrepreneurship and business initiative
- Became one of Bengal's notable self-made business leaders and entrepreneurs

## Industrial Career

Alamohan Das believed that economic progress required industrial growth and indigenous enterprise.

His business activities focused on:

- Manufacturing
- Engineering industries
- Industrial infrastructure
- Employment generation

He worked to strengthen locally owned industries during a period when much of India's industrial sector was dominated by foreign interests.

## Dasnagar

### Foundation

Alamohan Das established Dasnagar in Howrah as an industrial township.

### Significance

Dasnagar became known for:

- Industrial development
- Manufacturing activities
- Employment opportunities
- Promotion of Bengali entrepreneurship

The township remains closely associated with his legacy.

## Contribution to Bengal

His contributions include:

- Promotion of indigenous industry
- Expansion of manufacturing in Bengal
- Creation of industrial employment
- Development of industrial infrastructure
- Encouragement of Bengali entrepreneurship

## Legacy

Alamohan Das is remembered for:

- Establishment of Dasnagar
- Industrial development in Bengal
- Indigenous entrepreneurship
- Contributions to Bengal's economic growth
