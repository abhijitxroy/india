# Prafulla Chandra Ray

## Overview

Prafulla Chandra Ray was a pioneering Indian chemist, educator, entrepreneur, and nationalist. He is widely regarded as the father of modern chemistry in India and played a major role in developing scientific research and chemical industries in the country.

He founded Bengal Chemical and Pharmaceutical Works, one of India's earliest pharmaceutical companies, and inspired generations of Indian scientists.

## Early Life and Education

Prafulla Chandra Ray was born in a Bengali family that valued education and social reform.

He studied in Calcutta and later received a scholarship to study at the University of Edinburgh in Scotland. There he specialized in chemistry and earned recognition for his academic achievements.

After returning to India, he devoted himself to teaching, scientific research, and the promotion of indigenous industries.

## Career / Contributions

### Scientific Research

Ray conducted important research in inorganic chemistry and published numerous scientific papers.

His work earned international recognition and helped establish India's presence in the global scientific community.

### Teaching and Mentorship

He taught chemistry at Presidency College, Calcutta.

Many of his students later became distinguished scientists and researchers, helping build India's scientific foundation.

### Bengal Chemical and Pharmaceutical Works

In 1901, Ray founded Bengal Chemical and Pharmaceutical Works.

The company became one of the earliest Indian-owned chemical and pharmaceutical enterprises and promoted self-reliance in manufacturing.

### Promotion of Science

Ray actively encouraged scientific education and research.

He believed that science and industry were essential for national progress and economic development.

## Historical Significance

Prafulla Chandra Ray helped lay the foundations of modern scientific research in India.

At a time when most industries were controlled by colonial interests, he demonstrated that Indians could build successful scientific and industrial enterprises.

His efforts connected education, research, and industry in a way that influenced future generations.

## Legacy

Ray is remembered as one of India's most influential scientists and educators.

His contributions to chemistry, scientific education, and industrial development continue to be recognized across India.

Bengal Chemical remains an important symbol of India's early industrial and scientific self-reliance.

His life serves as an example of combining scientific excellence with national service.
