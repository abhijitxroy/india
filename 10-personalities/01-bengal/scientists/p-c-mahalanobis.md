# P. C. Mahalanobis

## Overview

Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis was an Indian statistician, scientist, and institution builder. He is best known for founding the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) and developing the Mahalanobis Distance, an important statistical measure used worldwide.

He played a major role in shaping India's statistical system and economic planning during the early decades after independence.

## Early Life and Education

Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis was born in Calcutta into a family that valued education and intellectual pursuits.

He studied at Presidency College, Calcutta, where he developed a strong interest in mathematics and science. Later, he attended King's College, Cambridge, where he was introduced to modern statistical methods.

His exposure to statistics during his studies influenced the direction of his future career.

## Career / Contributions

### Development of Mahalanobis Distance

Mahalanobis developed the Mahalanobis Distance, a statistical measure used to determine the distance between a data point and a distribution.

The concept remains widely used in statistics, machine learning, data science, pattern recognition, and scientific research.

### Founding the Indian Statistical Institute

In 1931, he founded the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) in Calcutta.

The institute became one of India's leading centres for research, statistics, mathematics, and data analysis.

### National Sample Surveys

Mahalanobis pioneered large-scale sample surveys in India.

His methods helped governments collect reliable information on agriculture, population, industry, and social conditions.

### Economic Planning

After independence, Mahalanobis became closely associated with India's development planning.

He played an important role in formulating the Second Five-Year Plan and advocated the development of heavy industries to strengthen the country's economic foundation.

## Historical Significance

P. C. Mahalanobis helped establish statistics as an essential tool for governance and public policy in India.

His work improved the collection and analysis of national data, enabling better decision-making by governments and institutions.

He also helped build scientific institutions that continue to influence research and policymaking.

## Legacy

Mahalanobis is regarded as the father of modern statistics in India.

The Indian Statistical Institute remains one of his most enduring contributions.

His statistical methods continue to be used across academic research, economics, business analytics, artificial intelligence, and government planning.

National Statistical Day is observed in India on his birth anniversary to recognize his contributions.
