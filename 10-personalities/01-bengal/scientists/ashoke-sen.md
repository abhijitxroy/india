# Ashoke Sen

## Overview

Ashoke Sen is an Indian theoretical physicist from Bengal and one of the world's leading researchers in string theory. He is internationally recognized for his groundbreaking contributions to high-energy theoretical physics, quantum gravity, black hole physics, and string theory.
