

# Rajshekhar Basu

## Overview

Rajshekhar Basu was a distinguished Bengali writer, lexicographer, translator and humorist. Writing under the pen name Parashuram, he became famous for his wit, satire and memorable short stories.

He is regarded as one of the greatest humorists in Bengali literature.

## Early Life

- Born on 16 March 1880 in Bardhaman
- Received a strong education in science and literature
- Built a successful professional career while pursuing literary interests
- Developed a reputation for intellectual depth and linguistic mastery

## Literary Career

### Parashuram

Rajshekhar Basu wrote under the pen name Parashuram.

His works became known for:

- Sharp humour
- Social satire
- Clever characterisation
- Elegant language
- Insightful observations of society

### Short Stories

He gained widespread popularity through short stories that combined entertainment with thoughtful commentary on human behaviour and social customs.

## Contributions to Language

Apart from creative writing, he made major contributions to Bengali language and scholarship.

His work included:

- Lexicography
- Translation
- Language standardisation
- Literary scholarship

## Writing Style

Characteristics of his writing include:

- Wit and humour
- Satirical observations
- Clear language
- Intellectual depth
- Memorable storytelling

## Legacy

Rajshekhar Basu is remembered for:

- Contributions to Bengali literature
- Mastery of humour and satire
- Important language scholarship
- Popular short stories
- Influence on later generations of writers
