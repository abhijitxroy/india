# Prafulla Chandra Ray

## Overview

Prafulla Chandra Ray was a chemist, educator, entrepreneur, and institution builder who played a major role in the growth of scientific education in India. He is widely regarded as the father of modern chemistry in India and was an influential teacher who inspired generations of students.

Beyond scientific research, he worked to strengthen higher education, promote scientific thinking, and encourage indigenous industrial development.

## Early Life and Education

Prafulla Chandra Ray was born in Bengal Presidency and grew up in a family that valued education and social reform.

He studied in Calcutta and later attended the University of Edinburgh in Scotland, where he specialized in chemistry.

His academic achievements prepared him for a career in scientific research and education.

## Career / Contributions

### Teaching and Education

Ray taught chemistry at Presidency College, Calcutta, and became one of the most respected teachers of his time.

He encouraged students to pursue scientific research and critical thinking.

### Promotion of Scientific Research

He conducted important research in chemistry and published scientific papers that earned international recognition.

His work helped establish a foundation for scientific research in India.

### Institution Building

Ray supported the development of laboratories, scientific education, and research culture.

He believed that education and science were essential for national progress.

### Industrial Development

He founded Bengal Chemical and Pharmaceutical Works, one of India's earliest pharmaceutical companies.

The enterprise demonstrated how scientific knowledge could be applied to industrial development.

## Historical Significance

Prafulla Chandra Ray helped connect education, scientific research, and industry.

His efforts contributed to the emergence of modern scientific education in India and inspired many future scientists.

He played an important role in creating a culture of scientific learning during the colonial period.

## Legacy

Prafulla Chandra Ray is remembered as one of India's greatest scientists and educationists.

His contributions continue to influence chemistry education, scientific research, and industrial development.

He remains a symbol of scientific excellence, educational leadership, and national service.
