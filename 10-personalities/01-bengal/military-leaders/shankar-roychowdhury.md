# Shankar Roychowdhury

## Overview

General Shankar Roychowdhury is a distinguished Indian military leader who served as the 18th Chief of Army Staff of the Indian Army.

He is one of the most prominent military personalities from Bengal and played an important role in India's defence establishment.

## Early Life and Education

- Born on 6 September 1937
- Educated in Kolkata
- Joined the Indian Army after military training
- Commissioned into the Armoured Corps

## Military Career

### Indian Army Service

Shankar Roychowdhury served in various command and staff appointments during a long military career.

His experience included:

- Armoured warfare operations
- Military planning
- Command assignments
- Training and administration

### Chief of Army Staff

He served as Chief of Army Staff from 1994 to 1997.

As Army Chief, he oversaw:

- Operational preparedness
- Modernisation initiatives
- Training and professional development
- National security responsibilities

## Major Contributions

His contributions include:

- Leadership of the Indian Army during a significant period of military modernisation
- Strengthening operational readiness
- Development of professional military standards
- Contribution to India's defence preparedness

## Public Life

After retirement from military service, he remained active in public affairs.

He later served as a Member of Parliament in the Rajya Sabha.

## Awards and Honours

He received several military honours during his service career, reflecting his contributions to the Indian Army and national security.

## Legacy

Shankar Roychowdhury is remembered for:

- Service as Chief of Army Staff
- Leadership in the Armoured Corps
- Contributions to military professionalism
- Continued public service after retirement
