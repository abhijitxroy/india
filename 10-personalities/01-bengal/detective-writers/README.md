# Detective Writers

## Overview

This section covers notable Bengali writers who created influential detective, mystery, crime, thriller, and investigative fiction. Their works introduced iconic fictional detectives and helped shape Bengali detective literature.

## Historical Evolution

| Period | Writers |
|----------|----------|
| 1900–1950 | Hemendra Kumar Roy, Premendra Mitra, Sharadindu Bandyopadhyay, Sudhindranath Raha |
| 1950–1980 | Nihar Ranjan Gupta, Narayan Sanyal, Satyajit Ray, Syed Mustafa Siraj, Swapan Kumar |
| 1980–2010 | Sunil Gangopadhyay, Samaresh Basu, Samaresh Majumdar, Bimal Kar, Shirshendu Mukhopadhyay, Sasthipada Chattopadhyay, Suchitra Bhattacharya |
| 2010–Present | Abhigyan Ganguly, Kousik Majumdar, Goutam Roy, Anirban Basu, Sabyasachi Sarkar, Tanmoy Chatterjee |

This timeline shows how Bengali detective fiction evolved across generations and how later writers built upon the foundations established by earlier authors.

## Suggested Reading Order

### Beginner

1. Satyajit Ray (Feluda)
2. Sharadindu Bandyopadhyay (Byomkesh Bakshi)
3. Nihar Ranjan Gupta (Kiriti Roy)
4. Syed Mustafa Siraj (Colonel Niladri Sarkar)

### Intermediate

5. Narayan Sanyal (Kanta Series)
6. Suchitra Bhattacharya (Mitin Masi)
7. Samaresh Basu (Gogol)
8. Samaresh Majumdar (Arjun)
9. Sasthipada Chattopadhyay (Pandab Goenda)

### Advanced and Exploratory

10. Premendra Mitra (Ghanada)
11. Hemendra Kumar Roy
12. Shirshendu Mukhopadhyay
13. Adrish Bardhan
14. Swapan Kumar
15. Kousik Majumdar
16. Abhigyan Ganguly
17. Bimal Kar (Kikira)
