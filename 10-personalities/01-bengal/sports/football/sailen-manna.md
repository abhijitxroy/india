# Sailen Manna

## Overview

Sailen Manna was one of the greatest footballers in Indian history and a legendary defender of the Indian national team. He played a central role during the golden era of Indian football and is widely regarded as one of the finest captains the country has produced.

He remains one of the most respected sporting icons of Bengal.

## Early Life

- Born on 1 September 1924 in Howrah
- Developed an interest in football during childhood
- Progressed through local football competitions in Bengal
- Emerged as one of the country's most talented defenders

## Football Career

### Club Career

Sailen Manna spent most of his career with Mohun Bagan.

He became one of the club's greatest players and earned recognition for:

- Defensive excellence
- Leadership qualities
- Consistency
- Sportsmanship

### Indian National Team

He represented India during one of the strongest periods in Indian football.

His contributions included:

- Team leadership
- Defensive organisation
- Tactical discipline
- Inspirational captaincy

He captained India in major international competitions.

## International Recognition

Sailen Manna earned international respect for his performances and leadership.

He was considered among the leading footballers of Asia during his era.

## Playing Style

Characteristics of his game included:

- Strong defending
- Tactical intelligence
- Leadership
- Calm decision-making
- Reliability under pressure

## Contribution to Indian Football

His success helped establish the reputation of Indian football during its golden era.

He became a role model for future generations of defenders and captains.

## Legacy

Sailen Manna is remembered for:

- Leadership of the Indian football team
- Success with Mohun Bagan
- Defensive excellence
- Contributions to Indian football's golden era
- Lasting influence on future footballers
