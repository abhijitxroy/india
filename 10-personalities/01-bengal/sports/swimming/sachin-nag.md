# Sachin Nag

## Overview

Sachin Nag was one of the pioneers of Indian swimming and an important sporting personality from Bengal. He achieved national recognition after becoming the first Indian swimmer to win an individual gold medal at the Asian Games.

He is remembered as one of the early heroes of Indian aquatic sports.

## Early Life

- Born on 19 July 1920 in Kolkata
- Developed an interest in swimming during childhood
- Trained in competitive swimming from a young age
- Emerged as one of India's leading swimmers before independence

## Swimming Career

### National Success

Sachin Nag established himself as one of India's strongest swimmers through consistent performances in national competitions.

His achievements demonstrated:

- Technical excellence
- Competitive discipline
- Endurance and determination
- High standards of sportsmanship

### Asian Games Achievement

His greatest sporting achievement came at the 1951 Asian Games held in New Delhi.

He won the gold medal in the 100-metre freestyle event.

This made him:

- The first Indian swimmer to win an Asian Games gold medal
- The first Indian to win an individual gold medal at the Asian Games

The achievement remains a landmark in Indian sporting history.

## Contribution to Indian Sports

Sachin Nag's success helped establish swimming as a respected competitive sport in India.

His achievements inspired future generations of Indian swimmers.

## Legacy

Sachin Nag is remembered for:

- Winning India's first individual Asian Games gold medal
- Contributions to Indian swimming
- Raising the profile of aquatic sports in India
- Inspiring future athletes
- Becoming a pioneer of Indian swimming
