# Bula Choudhury

## Overview

Bula Choudhury is one of India's greatest long-distance swimmers and among the most accomplished sports personalities produced by Bengal. She achieved international recognition through open-water swimming and became the first woman to successfully swim across the Seven Seas.

She is regarded as one of the most celebrated swimmers in Indian sporting history.

## Early Life

- Born on 2 January 1970 in Kolkata
- Began swimming at a very young age
- Displayed exceptional talent in competitive swimming during childhood
- Progressed rapidly through national and international competitions

## Swimming Career

### Competitive Swimming

Bula Choudhury achieved success in national and international swimming events from an early age.

Her performances were marked by:

- Exceptional endurance
- Technical excellence
- Mental toughness
- Competitive discipline

### Long-Distance Swimming

She later became famous for open-water and endurance swimming.

Her achievements demonstrated the ability to compete successfully in some of the world's most challenging waterways.

## Seven Seas Achievement

Bula Choudhury became the first woman to swim across the Seven Seas.

This accomplishment brought international recognition and established her as one of the leading endurance swimmers of her generation.

The achievement remains one of the greatest milestones in Indian swimming history.

## Contribution to Indian Sports

Her success inspired countless young athletes, especially women, to pursue competitive sports.

She helped increase public awareness and appreciation of swimming in India.

## Legacy

Bula Choudhury is remembered for:

- First woman to swim across the Seven Seas
- Contributions to Indian swimming
- International achievements in endurance swimming
- Inspiring women athletes
- Becoming one of India's greatest swimmers
