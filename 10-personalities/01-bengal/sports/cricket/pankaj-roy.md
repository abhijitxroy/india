# Pankaj Roy

## Overview

Pankaj Roy was one of the pioneering cricketers of independent India and among the most notable cricket personalities produced by Bengal. He represented India as an opening batsman and played an important role in the development of Indian cricket during the 1950s.

He is remembered for holding a world-record opening partnership with Vinoo Mankad for many years.

## Early Life

- Born on 31 May 1928 in Kolkata
- Developed an interest in cricket from a young age
- Progressed through Bengal's domestic cricket structure
- Established himself as one of the leading batsmen in Indian cricket

## Cricket Career

### Domestic Cricket

Pankaj Roy was a key player for Bengal in domestic cricket.

His performances were known for:

- Technical batting ability
- Patience and concentration
- Reliability as an opening batsman
- Consistent run scoring

### International Career

He represented India in Test cricket during the 1950s.

As an opening batsman, he frequently faced some of the strongest bowling attacks of his era.
