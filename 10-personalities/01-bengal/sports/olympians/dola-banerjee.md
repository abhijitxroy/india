# Dola Banerjee

## Overview

Dola Banerjee is one of India's most successful archers and among the most accomplished Olympians produced by Bengal. She achieved international recognition through her performances in recurve archery and became a pioneering figure for women in Indian archery.

She is widely regarded as one of the most important archers in Indian sporting history.

## Early Life

- Born on 2 June 1980 in Baranagar, West Bengal
- Developed an interest in archery at a young age
- Trained extensively through India's competitive archery system
- Emerged as one of the country's leading young archers

## Archery Career

### Rise in International Archery

Dola Banerjee established herself as one of India's leading recurve archers.

Her success was built on:

- Technical precision
- Consistency in competition
- Mental toughness
- Long-term dedication to the sport

### International Representation

She represented India in major international competitions, including:

- Olympic Games
- World Championships
- Asian Games
- Commonwealth Games
- Archery World Cup events

Her performances helped strengthen India's reputation in international archery.

## World Cup Achievement

One of the greatest achievements of her career came when she won the Archery World Cup Final.

This made her one of the first Indian archers to achieve major success at the highest international level.

The victory remains a landmark achievement in Indian archery.

## Olympic Participation

Dola Banerjee represented India at multiple Olympic Games and became one of the most recognisable faces of Indian archery.

Her Olympic appearances inspired many young athletes, especially women, to take up the sport.

## Playing Style

Characteristics of her archery included:

- Precision shooting
- Strong concentration
- Competitive composure
- Consistency under pressure
- Technical excellence

## Legacy

Dola Banerjee is remembered for:

- Success in international archery
- Winning the Archery World Cup Final
- Representing India at the Olympics
- Inspiring women athletes
- Becoming one of India's greatest archers
