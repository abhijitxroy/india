# Joydeep Karmakar

## Overview

Joydeep Karmakar is an Indian sport shooter from West Bengal who gained international recognition through his performances in rifle shooting. He became one of India's most notable Olympians after narrowly missing an Olympic medal at the 2012 London Olympics.

He is regarded as one of Bengal's most accomplished shooting athletes.

## Early Life

- Born on 7 December 1979 in Kolkata
- Developed an interest in shooting at a young age
- Trained extensively in rifle shooting
- Progressed through state, national and international competitions

## Shooting Career

### Rise in Competitive Shooting

Joydeep Karmakar established himself as one of India's leading rifle shooters.

His success was built on:

- Technical precision
- Mental focus
- Competitive discipline
- Consistent performance

### International Representation

He represented India in major international competitions, including:

- Olympic Games
- Commonwealth Games
- Asian competitions
- International shooting championships

His achievements helped strengthen India's reputation in shooting sports.

## 2012 London Olympics

The defining moment of his career came at the 2012 London Olympics.

Competing in the 50 metre rifle prone event, he finished fourth.

Although he narrowly missed a medal, the performance was one of India's strongest shooting results at the Olympics and brought him national recognition.

## Playing Style

Characteristics of his shooting included:

- Precision and accuracy
- Strong concentration
- Calm temperament
- Consistency under pressure
- Technical excellence

## Contribution to Indian Shooting

His achievements inspired young shooters and contributed to the growth of shooting sports in eastern India.

He became a role model for aspiring athletes from Bengal.

## Legacy

Joydeep Karmakar is remembered for:

- Outstanding performance at the 2012 Olympics
- Contributions to Indian shooting
- Representing India internationally
- Inspiring future shooters
- Becoming one of Bengal's leading Olympians
