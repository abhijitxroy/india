# Atanu Das

## Overview

Atanu Das is one of India's leading archers and among the most accomplished Olympians produced by Bengal. He has represented India in multiple Olympic Games and has been a prominent figure in Indian archery for more than a decade.

He is regarded as one of Bengal's most successful modern athletes.

## Early Life

- Born on 5 April 1992 in Kolkata
- Developed an interest in archery at a young age
- Trained through India's competitive archery system
- Progressed steadily through junior and senior competitions

## Archery Career

### Rise in International Archery

Atanu Das established himself as one of India's top recurve archers.

His success was built on:

- Technical consistency
- Mental discipline
- Competitive experience
- Strong performance under pressure

### International Representation

He represented India in major international competitions, including:

- Olympic Games
- World Championships
- Asian Games
- Archery World Cup events

His performances helped strengthen India's presence in international archery.

## Olympic Participation

Atanu Das competed in multiple Olympic Games and became one of the most recognisable Indian archers of his generation.

His Olympic appearances brought greater visibility to archery in Bengal and India.

## Playing Style

Characteristics of his archery include:

- Technical precision
- Strong concentration
- Consistency under pressure
- Tactical awareness
- Competitive resilience

## Contribution to Indian Archery

His achievements inspired young athletes and contributed to the growing popularity of archery in eastern India.

He became a role model for aspiring archers from Bengal.

## Legacy

Atanu Das is remembered for:

- Representing India at multiple Olympics
- Success in international archery
- Promoting archery in Bengal
- Inspiring future generations of athletes
- Becoming one of India's leading recurve archers
