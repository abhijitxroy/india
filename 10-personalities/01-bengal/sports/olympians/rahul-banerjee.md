# Rahul Banerjee

## Overview

Rahul Banerjee is an Indian archer and Olympian from Bengal who represented India at the highest levels of international competition. He became one of the leading figures in Indian recurve archery and contributed significantly to the growth of the sport in the country.

He is among the most notable archers produced by Bengal.

## Early Life

- Born on 13 September 1986 in Kolkata
- Developed an interest in archery during his youth
- Trained extensively in competitive archery
- Progressed through national and international tournaments

## Archery Career

### Rise in Indian Archery

Rahul Banerjee established himself as one of India's top recurve archers.

His strengths included:

- Technical accuracy
- Consistency
- Mental focus
- Competitive discipline

### International Representation

He represented India in major international events, including:

- Olympic Games
- World Championships
- Asian Games
- Commonwealth Games
- Archery World Cup events

His performances helped strengthen India's standing in international archery.

## Commonwealth Games Success

One of the most notable achievements of his career was winning gold medals at the Commonwealth Games.

These victories brought wider recognition to Indian archery and highlighted Bengal's contribution to the sport.

## Olympic Participation

Rahul Banerjee represented India at the Olympic Games and competed against the world's leading archers.

His Olympic appearance placed him among Bengal's distinguished Olympians.

## Playing Style

Characteristics of his archery include:

- Precision shooting
- Strong concentration
- Calm temperament
- Consistency under pressure
- Technical excellence

## Contribution to Indian Archery

His achievements inspired young archers and contributed to the increasing popularity of archery in Bengal.

He became a role model for aspiring athletes across eastern India.

## Legacy

Rahul Banerjee is remembered for:

- Representing India at the Olympics
- Success at the Commonwealth Games
- Contributions to Indian archery
- Promoting archery in Bengal
- Inspiring future generations of athletes
