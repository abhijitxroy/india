# Mother Teresa

## Overview

Mother Teresa was a Catholic nun, missionary, and humanitarian who devoted her life to serving the poor, sick, orphaned, and dying. Although she was born outside India, she spent most of her life in Kolkata and became closely associated with humanitarian work in the country.

She founded the Missionaries of Charity, an organization that expanded across the world and became known for caring for people in need.

## Early Life and Education

Mother Teresa was born into an Albanian family in Skopje.

At a young age, she felt called to religious service and joined a Catholic religious order. She later travelled to India and began working as a teacher in Kolkata.

Over time, she became increasingly concerned about the suffering of people living in poverty and decided to dedicate her life to helping them.

## Career / Contributions

### Missionaries of Charity

In 1950, Mother Teresa founded the Missionaries of Charity in Kolkata.

The organization focused on serving the poorest members of society, including the homeless, sick, elderly, and orphaned.

### Humanitarian Work

She established homes, shelters, and care centres for people who often lacked access to healthcare and social support.

Her work reached vulnerable communities in India and later expanded to many countries around the world.

### Service to the Poor

Mother Teresa became widely known for personally caring for people suffering from illness, poverty, and abandonment.

Her work emphasized compassion, dignity, and service.

### International Recognition

Her humanitarian efforts received global attention and earned numerous awards and honours.

She became one of the most recognizable figures associated with charitable work in the twentieth century.

## Historical Significance

Mother Teresa brought international attention to issues of poverty, illness, and social neglect.

Her work in Kolkata became a symbol of humanitarian service and inspired charitable activities across the world.

She remains one of the most widely known social workers associated with India.

## Legacy

The Missionaries of Charity continues its humanitarian work in many countries.

Mother Teresa's life continues to inspire volunteers, social workers, and charitable organizations.

Her contributions to humanitarian service earned global recognition and a lasting place in modern history.

She was canonized as Saint Teresa of Calcutta by the Catholic Church in 2016.
