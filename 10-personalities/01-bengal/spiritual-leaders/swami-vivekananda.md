# Swami Vivekananda

## Overview

Swami Vivekananda was a Hindu monk, spiritual leader, philosopher, and social reformer. He was the chief disciple of Ramakrishna Paramahamsa and played a major role in introducing Indian philosophy and spirituality to the world.

His speech at the Parliament of the World's Religions in Chicago in 1893 brought him international recognition and made him one of the most influential Indian thinkers of modern times.

## Early Life and Education

Swami Vivekananda was born as Narendranath Datta in Calcutta.

He was an intelligent student with a strong interest in philosophy, religion, history, and literature. He studied at institutions in Calcutta and was influenced by both Indian and Western thought.

His search for spiritual truth eventually led him to Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, who became his spiritual mentor.

## Career / Contributions

### Disciple of Ramakrishna

Vivekananda became one of the most prominent disciples of Ramakrishna Paramahamsa.

After Ramakrishna's death, he worked to spread his teachings and organize his followers.

### Chicago Speech of 1893

In 1893, Vivekananda represented India at the Parliament of the World's Religions in Chicago.

His speech on religious tolerance and universal brotherhood received worldwide attention and introduced many people to Indian spiritual traditions.

### Ramakrishna Mission

In 1897, he founded the Ramakrishna Mission.

The organization combined spiritual learning with education, healthcare, disaster relief, and social service.

### National and Social Thought

Vivekananda encouraged self-confidence, education, character-building, and service to society.

His ideas inspired many leaders of India's freedom movement and contributed to a growing sense of national pride.

## Historical Significance

Swami Vivekananda helped present Indian philosophy and spirituality on a global stage.

He emphasized that spiritual development and social service should work together for the betterment of society.

His teachings influenced religious thought, social reform, education, and the emerging nationalist movement in India.

## Legacy

Swami Vivekananda remains one of the most respected figures in modern Indian history.

The Ramakrishna Mission continues to serve society through educational, medical, and humanitarian activities.

His writings and speeches continue to inspire people across India and around the world.

National Youth Day is observed in India on his birth anniversary.
