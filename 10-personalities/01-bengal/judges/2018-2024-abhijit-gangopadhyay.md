# Abhijit Gangopadhyay

## Overview

Justice Abhijit Gangopadhyay is a former judge of the Calcutta High Court who became widely known for several high-profile judgments and orders related to public administration, recruitment processes, and governance issues in West Bengal. After resigning from the judiciary, he entered public life and politics.
