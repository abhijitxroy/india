# Shyamal Kumar Sen

## Overview

Justice Shyamal Kumar Sen is an Indian jurist who served as the Chief Justice of the Allahabad High Court and briefly served as the Governor of West Bengal in 1999. He is known for his contributions to the judiciary and public service.
