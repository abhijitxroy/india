# Altamas Kabir

## Overview

Justice Altamas Kabir was an Indian jurist who served as the 39th Chief Justice of India from 2012 to 2013. He was one of the most prominent judges from Bengal and contributed to constitutional law, civil liberties, education-related issues, and judicial administration.
