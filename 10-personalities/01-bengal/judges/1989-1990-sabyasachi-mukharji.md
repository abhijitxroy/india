# Sabyasachi Mukharji

## Overview

Justice Sabyasachi Mukharji was an Indian jurist who served as the 20th Chief Justice of India. He is remembered for his contributions to the Indian judiciary and for leading the Supreme Court during an important period in India's constitutional and legal development.
