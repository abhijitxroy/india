# Subodh Chandra Mitra

## Overview

Subodh Chandra Mitra was a distinguished Indian physician and surgeon who made important contributions to obstetrics and gynaecology. He is best remembered for developing techniques and practices that improved maternal healthcare and surgical treatment for women.

His work helped advance modern medical practice in India and earned him recognition as one of the leading medical professionals of his time.
