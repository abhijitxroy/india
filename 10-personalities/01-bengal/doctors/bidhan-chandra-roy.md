# Bidhan Chandra Roy

## Overview

Bidhan Chandra Roy was a renowned physician, educator, freedom fighter, and statesman. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest doctors in Indian history and played a major role in the development of modern West Bengal after independence.

Apart from his medical achievements, he served as the Chief Minister of West Bengal and contributed significantly to public health, education, and urban development.

## Early Life and Education

Bidhan Chandra Roy was born in Patna and showed strong academic ability from an early age.

He studied medicine in India and later continued his medical education in the United Kingdom. Through determination and hard work, he earned qualifications from prestigious medical institutions.

After returning to India, he dedicated himself to medical practice, teaching, and public service.

## Career / Contributions

### Medical Career

Bidhan Chandra Roy became one of India's most respected physicians.

He treated patients from all sections of society and earned a reputation for professional excellence and compassion.

### Medical Education

He contributed to the growth of medical education and helped strengthen healthcare institutions.

His efforts supported the development of future generations of doctors and healthcare professionals.

### Freedom Movement

Roy participated in India's freedom movement and worked alongside several national leaders.

He believed that public service and nation-building were closely connected.

### Chief Minister of West Bengal

After independence, he served as the Chief Minister of West Bengal from 1948 until his death in 1962.

During his tenure, he promoted industrial development, healthcare expansion, education, and urban planning.

Several important towns and development projects were established during this period.

## Historical Significance

Bidhan Chandra Roy successfully combined medicine, public service, and political leadership.

His contributions helped shape modern West Bengal during a critical phase of post-independence development.

He demonstrated how professional expertise could be used for broader social and national progress.

## Legacy

Bidhan Chandra Roy is remembered as one of India's greatest physicians and public servants.

National Doctors' Day is observed in India on 1 July, his birth anniversary, in recognition of his contributions to medicine.

His influence continues through hospitals, educational institutions, and public projects named in his honour.
