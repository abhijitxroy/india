# Singers

## Overview

Bengal has produced some of the most influential singers in Indian music history. These artists contributed to Bengali songs, Indian classical traditions, playback singing and modern popular music while shaping the musical culture of India.

## Personalities

- Geeta Dutt
- Hemanta Mukhopadhyay
- Kishore Kumar
- Manna Dey
- Sandhya Mukhopadhyay
- Suchitra Mitra

## Highlights

### Hemanta Mukhopadhyay

One of the greatest voices in Bengali and Hindi music, known nationally as Hemant Kumar.

### Kishore Kumar

Among the most popular playback singers in Indian cinema history.

### Manna Dey

Renowned for classical mastery, versatility and technical excellence.

### Geeta Dutt

Celebrated for emotional depth and expressive singing.

### Sandhya Mukhopadhyay

One of the greatest female singers in Bengali music.

### Suchitra Mitra

A leading exponent of Rabindra Sangeet and an important cultural figure.

## Quick Revision

- Hemanta Mukhopadhyay excelled as both singer and composer.
- Kishore Kumar became one of India's most popular playback singers.
- Manna Dey was known for classical excellence and versatility.
- Geeta Dutt was admired for emotional expression.
- Sandhya Mukhopadhyay shaped modern Bengali music.
- Suchitra Mitra played a major role in preserving Rabindra Sangeet.
