# Music

## Overview

Music is one of the most influential components of Bengal's cultural heritage. Bengal has produced legendary singers, composers, classical musicians and cultural icons whose contributions shaped Bengali music, Indian cinema and global appreciation of Indian musical traditions.

- S. D. Burman and R. D. Burman are among India's greatest music directors.
- Salil Chowdhury was known for musical innovation and orchestration.
- Bappi Lahiri popularised disco music in Indian cinema.
- Hemanta Mukhopadhyay, Kishore Kumar and Manna Dey are among India's greatest singers.
- Suchitra Mitra played a major role in preserving Rabindra Sangeet.
- Ravi Shankar introduced Indian classical music to global audiences.
