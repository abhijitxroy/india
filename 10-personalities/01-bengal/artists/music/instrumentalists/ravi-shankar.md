# Ravi Shankar

## Overview

Ravi Shankar was one of the greatest musicians of the twentieth century and the most internationally recognised exponent of Indian classical music. Through his mastery of the sitar, he introduced Indian music to global audiences and became a cultural ambassador for India.

He is regarded as one of the most influential musical personalities produced by Bengal.

## Early Life

- Born on 7 April 1920 in Varanasi
- Belonged to a Bengali family
- Developed an interest in music and the performing arts from a young age
- Received rigorous training in Indian classical music
- Became a disciple of the renowned musician Allauddin Khan

## Music Career

### Master of the Sitar

Ravi Shankar became one of the world's leading sitar players.

His performances were known for:

- Technical excellence
- Deep knowledge of classical traditions
- Creative improvisation
- Emotional expression
- Global appeal

### International Recognition

He played a major role in introducing Indian classical music to audiences across Europe and North America.

His performances and collaborations helped expand global appreciation of Indian musical traditions.

## Musical Contributions

His work contributed significantly to:

- Indian classical music
- Cultural exchange between India and the world
- Popularisation of the sitar
- International recognition of Indian musicians

## Musical Style

Characteristics of his music include:

- Classical discipline
- Innovation within tradition
- Complex melodic structures
- Improvisational mastery
- Strong spiritual and cultural influences

## Awards and Recognition

He received numerous national and international honours for his contributions to music and culture.

## Legacy

Ravi Shankar is remembered for:

- Global promotion of Indian classical music
- Mastery of the sitar
- Cultural diplomacy through music
- Influence on generations of musicians
- Expanding international appreciation of Indian traditions
