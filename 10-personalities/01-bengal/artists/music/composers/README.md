# Composers

## Overview

Bengal has produced some of the most influential music composers in Indian history. These composers shaped Bengali music, Hindi film music and modern Indian musical traditions through innovation, melody and artistic excellence.

## Personalities

- Bappi Lahiri
- R. D. Burman
- S. D. Burman
- Salil Chowdhury

## Highlights

### S. D. Burman

One of the architects of modern Indian film music who brought Bengali folk influences to a national audience.

### R. D. Burman

Known for revolutionising film music through experimentation and the fusion of Indian and Western musical styles.

### Salil Chowdhury

Celebrated for combining folk traditions, classical influences and sophisticated orchestration.

### Bappi Lahiri

Popularised disco-inspired music in Indian cinema and became one of the defining music directors of the 1980s.

## Quick Revision

- S. D. Burman helped shape modern Indian film music.
- R. D. Burman transformed popular music through innovation.
- Salil Chowdhury was known for orchestration and musical experimentation.
- Bappi Lahiri popularised disco music in Indian cinema.
- Bengal produced some of India's most influential music directors.
