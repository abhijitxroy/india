# Cinema

## Overview

Cinema has played a major role in shaping Bengal's cultural identity. Bengali cinema has produced internationally acclaimed directors, legendary actors and influential creative personalities whose work has impacted both Indian and world cinema.

This section covers notable cinema personalities from Bengal.

## Quick Revision

- Satyajit Ray is the most internationally recognised Bengali filmmaker.
- Uttam Kumar is known as the Mahanayak of Bengali cinema.
- Suchitra Sen and Uttam Kumar formed Bengal's most famous screen partnership.
- Mrinal Sen and Ritwik Ghatak were pioneers of parallel cinema.
- Bimal Roy helped bridge Bengali and Hindi cinema traditions.
- Aparna Sen and Rituparno Ghosh are major figures in contemporary Bengali cinema.
