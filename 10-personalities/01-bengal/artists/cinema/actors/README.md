# Actors

## Overview

Bengal has produced some of the most influential actors in the history of Indian cinema. From the early studio era to contemporary films, Bengali actors have played a major role in shaping the growth of Indian filmmaking through their artistic excellence, versatility, and memorable performances.

Their contributions extend beyond Bengali cinema, with many achieving national and international recognition while influencing generations of actors and filmmakers.

## Highlights

### Uttam Kumar

Known as the **Mahanayak** of Bengali cinema, Uttam Kumar remains the greatest superstar in the history of Bengali films.

### Suchitra Sen

One of the most iconic actresses of Indian cinema, she formed Bengali cinema's most celebrated on-screen partnership with Uttam Kumar.

### Soumitra Chatterjee

Legendary actor, poet, and theatre personality closely associated with Satyajit Ray and Bengali cultural life.

### Mithun Chakraborty

National Award-winning actor who achieved immense popularity across both Bengali and Hindi cinema.

### Ashok Kumar

A pioneer of Indian cinema, affectionately known as **Dadamoni**, who helped establish realistic acting in Hindi films.

### Chhabi Biswas

One of the greatest actors of Bengali cinema, remembered for his unforgettable performances in several Satyajit Ray classics.

### Sabyasachi Chakraborty

Contemporary actor best known for portraying the iconic detective **Feluda** and for his contributions to Bengali films and television.

### Himanshu Rai

Visionary actor-producer and co-founder of **Bombay Talkies**, one of the most influential studios in Indian cinema history.
