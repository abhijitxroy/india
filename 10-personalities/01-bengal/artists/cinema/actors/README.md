# Actors

## Overview

Bengal has produced some of the most influential actors in Indian cinema. Their contributions shaped Bengali films, influenced Indian popular culture and helped establish Bengal as a major centre of cinematic excellence.

## Personalities

- Mithun Chakraborty
- Soumitra Chatterjee
- Suchitra Sen
- Utpal Dutt
- Uttam Kumar

## Highlights

### Uttam Kumar

Known as the Mahanayak of Bengali cinema and regarded as its greatest superstar.

### Suchitra Sen

One of the most iconic actresses in Indian cinema and part of Bengali cinema's most celebrated screen partnership.

### Soumitra Chatterjee

Legendary actor closely associated with Satyajit Ray and Bengali cultural life.

### Mithun Chakraborty

Nationally recognised actor who achieved success in both Bengali and Hindi cinema.

### Utpal Dutt

Acclaimed actor who excelled in theatre, Bengali cinema and Hindi films.

## Quick Revision

- Uttam Kumar is known as the Mahanayak of Bengali cinema.
- Suchitra Sen and Uttam Kumar formed Bengal's most famous screen pair.
- Soumitra Chatterjee is strongly associated with Satyajit Ray.
- Mithun Chakraborty achieved pan-Indian popularity.
- Utpal Dutt made major contributions to both theatre and cinema.
