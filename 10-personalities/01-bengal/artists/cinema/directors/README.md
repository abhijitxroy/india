# Directors

## Overview

Bengal has produced some of the most influential filmmakers in Indian and world cinema. Bengali directors helped shape parallel cinema, artistic filmmaking and modern storytelling traditions while earning international recognition.

## Personalities

- Aparna Sen
- Bimal Roy
- Goutam Ghose
- Mrinal Sen
- Rituparno Ghosh
- Ritwik Ghatak
- Satyajit Ray
- Tapan Sinha

## Highlights

### Satyajit Ray

Widely regarded as the greatest filmmaker in Bengali cinema and one of the most influential directors in world cinema.

### Mrinal Sen

Leading figure of India's parallel cinema movement and internationally acclaimed filmmaker.

### Ritwik Ghatak

Known for powerful films on Partition, displacement and identity.

### Bimal Roy

Pioneering director who successfully bridged Bengali and Hindi cinema.

### Aparna Sen

One of India's most respected contemporary filmmakers and actresses.

### Rituparno Ghosh

Major force behind the revival of contemporary Bengali cinema.

## Quick Revision

- Satyajit Ray brought global recognition to Bengali cinema.
- Mrinal Sen was a pioneer of parallel cinema.
- Ritwik Ghatak is known for films on Partition and refugee experiences.
- Bimal Roy helped shape social realist cinema in India.
- Aparna Sen is among India's leading women filmmakers.
- Rituparno Ghosh revitalised Bengali cinema for modern audiences.
