

# Contributing Guide

Thank you for contributing to this repository.

This repository aims to build a structured, easy-to-understand knowledge system covering India's civilization, history, politics, governance, constitution, elections, institutions, laws, reforms, administration, and national transformation.

---

## Repository Principles

All contributions should follow these principles:

- Simple and easy-to-understand language.
- Fact-based and evidence-driven content.
- Neutral and non-partisan presentation.
- Historical accuracy.
- Chronological storytelling where applicable.
- Focus on understanding, not political advocacy.
- Suitable for students, professionals, researchers, and general readers.

---

## Writing Style

Preferred:

- Short paragraphs.
- Clear headings.
- Timelines.
- Tables.
- Cause-and-effect explanations.
- Real-world context.
- Quick revision sections.

Avoid:

- Political propaganda.
- Personal attacks.
- Unsupported claims.
- Excessive jargon.
- Opinion presented as fact.

---

## Content Structure

When creating a new topic, use this general structure whenever applicable:

1. Overview
2. Historical Background
3. Timeline
4. Key Events
5. Important People
6. Impact
7. Significance
8. Common Misconceptions
9. Quick Revision
10. Related Topics

---

## Political Content Guidelines

Political topics must:

- Present multiple viewpoints when relevant.
- Separate facts from opinions.
- Use verifiable sources.
- Avoid party promotion.
- Avoid ideological campaigning.

The goal is understanding, not persuasion.

---

## Government Performance Analysis

Future government-performance sections should:

- Track promises and outcomes.
- Include achievements and criticisms.
- Use public data whenever possible.
- Distinguish facts from interpretation.
- Remain evidence-based and non-partisan.

---

## Sources

Preferred sources:

- Constitution of India
- Parliament of India
- Election Commission of India
- Supreme Court of India
- Government Reports
- Census of India
- RBI Publications
- Economic Survey
- NITI Aayog Reports
- National Archives

---

## Pull Request Checklist

Before submitting:

- Content is factually reviewed.
- Dates are verified.
- Timeline is accurate.
- Language is simple.
- Formatting is consistent.
- Related topics are linked.
- Content remains neutral.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Build one of the most comprehensive and easy-to-understand repositories explaining India's journey from ancient civilization to modern democratic republic.
