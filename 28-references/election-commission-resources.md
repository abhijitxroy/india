
# Election Commission Resources

## Overview

This document lists important Election Commission resources that help citizens understand India's electoral system, voting process, election administration, political participation, and democratic institutions.

---

## Why It Matters

The Election Commission plays a critical role in ensuring free and fair elections.

Understanding election resources helps citizens learn:

- How elections are conducted
- How voters participate in democracy
- How political representation works
- How electoral disputes are managed
- How democratic legitimacy is maintained

---

## Primary Election Resources

### Election Commission of India (ECI)

Constitutional body responsible for conducting elections.

Key Responsibilities:

- Lok Sabha Elections
- Rajya Sabha Elections
- Presidential Elections
- Vice-Presidential Elections
- State Assembly Elections
- Electoral Roll Management

---

### Electoral Rolls

Official voter lists used during elections.

Useful For:

- Voter registration verification
- Constituency information
- Electoral participation

---

### Election Results Repository

Official source for:

- General election results
- State election results
- Historical election data
- Constituency-level outcomes

---

### Model Code of Conduct

Guidelines governing political parties and candidates during elections.

Purpose:

- Fair competition
- Ethical campaigning
- Electoral integrity

---

## Important Election Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| Universal Adult Franchise | Voting rights for eligible citizens |
| Electoral Roll | Official voter list |
| Constituency | Electoral area represented by a candidate |
| Model Code of Conduct | Election campaign guidelines |
| EVM | Electronic Voting Machine |
| VVPAT | Voter-verifiable audit system |
| Returning Officer | Election administration official |
| Polling Booth | Voting location |
