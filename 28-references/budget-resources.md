
# Budget Resources

## Overview

This document lists important budget-related resources that help citizens understand how the Government of India raises revenue, allocates expenditure, funds public programs, and manages national finances.

---

## Why It Matters

Understanding the budget helps explain:

- How public money is collected
- How government spending decisions are made
- How welfare programs are funded
- How infrastructure projects are financed
- How fiscal policy affects citizens and businesses

---

## Primary Budget Resources

### Union Budget

Primary annual financial statement of the Government of India.

Key Areas:

- Revenue Receipts
- Capital Receipts
- Government Expenditure
- Fiscal Deficit
- Sector Allocations

---

### Budget at a Glance

A simplified summary of the Union Budget.

Useful For:

- Quick revision
- Budget highlights
- Major allocation trends

---

### Annual Financial Statement

Constitutionally required statement of government finances.

Contains:

- Revenue Account
- Capital Account
- Government expenditure details

---

### Finance Bill

Provides legal effect to taxation proposals announced in the budget.

---

### Appropriation Bill

Authorizes expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of India.

---

## Important Budget Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| Revenue Receipt | Government income not creating liabilities |
| Capital Receipt | Income creating liabilities or reducing assets |
| Revenue Expenditure | Day-to-day government spending |
| Capital Expenditure | Asset creation and long-term investment |
| Fiscal Deficit | Gap between expenditure and revenue |
| Public Debt | Government borrowing obligations |
