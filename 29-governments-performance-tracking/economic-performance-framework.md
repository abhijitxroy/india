

# Economic Performance Framework

## Overview

This framework explains how citizens can evaluate the economic performance of a government using measurable indicators, long-term trends, and economic outcomes.

The objective is not to support or oppose any government.

The objective is to understand how economic performance can be assessed using evidence and publicly available data.

---

## Why It Matters

Economic performance affects:

- Jobs and employment
- Cost of living
- Business opportunities
- Income growth
- Public finances
- Investment and development

A strong economy can improve living standards, while economic weaknesses can affect citizens directly.

---

```text
Growth

   ↓

```

---

## Core Economic Indicators

| Indicator | Why It Matters |
|----------|----------|
| GDP Growth | Overall economic expansion |
| Per Capita Income | Average economic prosperity |
| Inflation | Cost of living impact |
| Employment | Job availability |
| Unemployment | Labour market challenges |
| Investment | Future growth potential |
| Exports | Global competitiveness |
| Fiscal Deficit | Government financial health |

---

## Employment Indicators

Citizens should evaluate:

- Job creation
- Labour force participation
- Workforce quality
- Skill development outcomes
- Formal vs informal employment

---

## Inflation & Cost of Living

Examples:

- Food prices
- Fuel prices
- Housing costs
- Essential services

Evaluation should focus on long-term affordability rather than short-term fluctuations.

---

## Public Finance Indicators

Examples:

- Government revenue
- Government expenditure
- Fiscal deficit
- Public debt
- Capital expenditure

---

## Investment Indicators

Examples:

- Domestic investment
- Foreign direct investment (FDI)
- Infrastructure investment
- Industrial expansion
- Startup ecosystem growth

---

## Citizen Evaluation Model

```text
Economic Policy
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Economic Indicators
        ↓
Citizen Outcomes
        ↓
Assessment
```

---

## Common Mistakes

Avoid evaluating economic performance using only:

- Single-year results
- Individual announcements
- Political claims
- Isolated statistics

Instead consider long-term trends and multiple indicators together.
