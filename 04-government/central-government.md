
# Central Government of India

## Overview

The Central Government (also called the Union Government) is the national government of India.

It is responsible for governing the entire country and handling matters that affect India as a whole.

Examples include:

- Defence
- Foreign Affairs
- Currency
- National Security
- Railways
- Citizenship
- National Economic Policies

The powers of the Central Government are defined by the Constitution of India.

---

## Constitutional Foundation

The Central Government derives its authority from the Constitution.

India follows a federal system where powers are divided between:

- Union Government
- State Governments

Certain subjects are handled exclusively by the Union Government.

---

## Structure of the Central Government

```text
People of India
        ↓
President
        ↓
Prime Minister
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Departments & Agencies
```

---

## Main Components

### President of India

The President is the constitutional Head of State.

Key responsibilities:

- Constitutional functions
- Appointments
- Assent to bills
- Emergency powers

The President acts according to constitutional provisions and advice of the Council of Ministers.

---

### Prime Minister of India

The Prime Minister is the Head of Government.

The Prime Minister:

- Leads the government
- Heads the Council of Ministers
- Sets policy direction
- Coordinates ministries
- Represents the government nationally and internationally

The Prime Minister is the most important executive authority in day-to-day governance.

---

### Council of Ministers

The Council of Ministers assists the Prime Minister.

It includes:

- Cabinet Ministers
- Ministers of State
- Deputy Ministers (when applicable)

Different ministers manage different sectors of governance.

---

## Major Ministries

Examples include:

- Ministry of Home Affairs
- Ministry of Defence
- Ministry of Finance
- Ministry of External Affairs
- Ministry of Education
- Ministry of Railways
- Ministry of Health and Family Welfare

Each ministry handles specific responsibilities.

---

## Parliament and the Central Government

Parliament makes laws for the country.

The Central Government:

- Introduces bills
- Implements laws
- Executes policies
- Manages administration

Parliament and the Government work together within the constitutional framework.

---

## Union List

The Constitution contains a Union List.

Subjects in this list are primarily handled by the Central Government.

Examples:

- Defence
- Foreign Affairs
- Currency
- Banking
- Atomic Energy
- Citizenship
- National Highways

These matters require national-level coordination.

---

## Key Functions

### National Security

Protecting India's sovereignty and territorial integrity.

---

### Foreign Relations

Managing relations with other countries and international organizations.

---

### Economic Management

Handling:

- Budget
- Taxation policies
- Monetary framework
- Economic planning and reforms

---

### Infrastructure Development

Supporting:

- Railways
- Highways
- Ports
- Airports
- Digital infrastructure

---

### Welfare and Development

Designing and implementing national programs and schemes.

---

## Sources of Revenue

The Central Government receives revenue from various sources such as:

- Income taxes
- Corporate taxes
- Customs duties
- GST share
- Non-tax revenue

These resources help fund public services and development programs.

---

## Why the Central Government Matters

The Central Government ensures:

- National unity
- Security
- Economic stability
- International representation
- Nationwide policy implementation

It provides leadership and coordination across the country.

---

## Common Misconceptions

### Central Government Is Not the Entire Government

India has both Union and State Governments.

### President and Prime Minister Have Different Roles

The President is the constitutional Head of State.

The Prime Minister is the Head of Government.

### States Also Have Significant Powers

India is federal, so states exercise authority in many areas.

---

## Quick Revision

- Central Government is also called the Union Government.
- Responsible for national-level governance.
- Prime Minister is the Head of Government.
- President is the constitutional Head of State.
- Ministries manage different sectors.
- Parliament makes laws.
- Union List subjects are mainly handled by the Central Government.
- Responsible for defence, foreign affairs, currency, and national policy.
