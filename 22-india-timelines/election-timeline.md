

# Election Timeline

## Overview

This timeline explains the major milestones in the evolution of elections and democratic participation in India from Independence to the present day.

The objective is to help readers understand how India's electoral system developed into the world's largest democracy.

---

## Why It Matters

Understanding election history helps explain:

- How democracy evolved in India
- How citizens gained political representation
- How election processes improved over time
- How democratic institutions strengthened
- How governments derive legitimacy

---

## Election Timeline

| Year | Event | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1950 | Election Commission Established | Constitutional body created to conduct elections |
| 1951-52 | First General Election | First democratic national election |
| 1957 | Second General Election | Democratic continuity established |
| 1967 | Fourth General Election | Beginning of major political competition |
| 1971 | Fifth General Election | Significant electoral realignments |
| 1977 | Post-Emergency Election | Major democratic transition |
| 1989 | Coalition Era Begins | Rise of coalition politics |
| 1990s | Electoral Reforms | Expansion of transparency and oversight |
| 1998-99 | Coalition Governments Stabilize | Multi-party era matures |
| 2004 | Nationwide EVM Adoption | Electronic voting becomes widespread |
| 2014 | Large-Scale Digital Election Management | Increased technology adoption |
| 2019 | World's Largest Democratic Exercise | Record-scale election participation |
