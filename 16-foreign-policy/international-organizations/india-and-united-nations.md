
# India and the United Nations

## Overview

India has been an active participant in the United Nations (UN) since the organization's early years and has consistently supported international cooperation, peace, development, and multilateral diplomacy.

The relationship between India and the United Nations is an important part of India's foreign policy and global engagement strategy.

India uses the UN platform to promote its national interests while contributing to international peace and development.

---

## Why Study India and the United Nations?

Studying India's relationship with the United Nations helps understand:

- International diplomacy
- Multilateral cooperation
- Global governance
- Peacekeeping efforts
- International development initiatives
- India's global role

The topic is important for understanding India's position in world affairs.
