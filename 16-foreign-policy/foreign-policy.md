
# Foreign Policy of India

## Overview

Foreign Policy refers to the principles, objectives, and strategies that guide India's relations with other countries and international organizations.

It helps protect national interests, strengthen security, promote economic growth, support international cooperation, and enhance India's global position.

Foreign policy is one of the most important pillars of modern governance and national strategy.

---

## Why Study Foreign Policy?

Studying India's foreign policy helps understand:

- International relations
- National security
- Global diplomacy
- Economic cooperation
- Strategic partnerships
- India's role in world affairs

It explains how India engages with the international community.

---

## Main Objectives

India's foreign policy aims to:

- Protect national interests
- Maintain sovereignty and security
- Promote economic development
- Strengthen international partnerships
- Support global peace and stability

---

## Foreign Policy Framework

```text
National Interests
        ↓
Diplomacy
        ↓
International Cooperation
        ↓
Security & Development
```

Foreign policy seeks to balance security, development, and international engagement.
