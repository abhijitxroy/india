# India–China Relations

## Overview

India–China relations are among the most important bilateral relationships in Asia and have significant implications for regional security, economic cooperation, border management, and global geopolitics.

As two of the world's largest countries and major emerging powers, India and China interact across diplomacy, trade, multilateral institutions, security issues, and regional affairs.

The relationship includes both cooperation and competition.

---

## Why Study India–China Relations?

Studying India–China relations helps understand:

- Border disputes and security issues
- Asian geopolitics
- Regional power dynamics
- Economic relations
- Foreign policy strategy
- National security challenges

It is one of the most important subjects in India's foreign policy.
