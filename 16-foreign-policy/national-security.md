

# National Security

## Overview

National Security refers to the protection of a country's sovereignty, territorial integrity, citizens, institutions, economy, and strategic interests from internal and external threats.

It is one of the most important responsibilities of the state and forms the foundation of defence policy, foreign policy, intelligence operations, border management, and strategic planning.

National security has evolved from a purely military concept to a broader framework that includes economic, technological, cyber, energy, and societal security.

---

## Why Study National Security?

Studying national security helps understand:

- Defence preparedness
- Foreign policy strategy
- Border management
- Counter-terrorism efforts
- Intelligence and surveillance
- Emerging security challenges

It explains how India protects its interests in a rapidly changing world.

---

## Main Objectives

India's national security framework aims to:

- Protect sovereignty and territorial integrity
- Safeguard citizens and institutions
- Prevent and respond to threats
- Maintain strategic stability
- Support long-term national development
