

# Neighbourhood First Policy

## Overview

Neighbourhood First Policy is a key component of India's foreign policy that focuses on strengthening relations with neighbouring countries.

The policy emphasizes regional cooperation, connectivity, economic engagement, security coordination, development partnerships, and people-to-people ties.

It reflects the idea that a stable, prosperous, and cooperative neighbourhood is important for India's growth, security, and regional leadership.

---

## Why Study the Neighbourhood First Policy?

Studying the policy helps understand:

- Regional diplomacy
- South Asian geopolitics
- India's foreign policy priorities
- Regional connectivity initiatives
- Security cooperation
- Development partnerships

It is one of the most important pillars of India's regional engagement strategy.

---

## Main Objectives

The policy aims to:

- Strengthen relations with neighbouring countries
- Promote regional stability
- Improve connectivity and trade
- Enhance security cooperation
- Support development partnerships

---

## Policy Framework

```text
Neighbouring Countries
          ↓
Diplomatic Engagement
          ↓
Economic Cooperation
          ↓
Regional Stability
```

The policy seeks to create a secure and cooperative regional environment.
