
# Nuclear Doctrine of India

## Overview

India's Nuclear Doctrine refers to the principles, policies, and strategic framework that guide the country's nuclear weapons policy and deterrence strategy.

The doctrine seeks to maintain national security, strategic stability, and credible deterrence while promoting responsible nuclear behavior.

It is an important component of India's national security and defence framework.

---

## Why Study the Nuclear Doctrine?

Studying the Nuclear Doctrine helps understand:

- National security strategy
- Strategic deterrence
- Defence policy
- Nuclear security issues
- Regional security dynamics
- India's strategic thinking

It is a key topic in understanding India's long-term security planning.

## Key Principles

### Credible Deterrence

- Maintain effective deterrence capability
- Ensure strategic preparedness
- Support long-term security objectives

### Responsible Nuclear Policy

- Emphasis on stability and restraint
- Focus on national security needs
- Support for responsible conduct

### Strategic Stability

- Reduce risks of escalation
- Promote security balance
- Support long-term peace and stability
