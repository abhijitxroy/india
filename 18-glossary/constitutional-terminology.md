# Constitutional Terminology

## Overview

Constitutional terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in constitutional law, governance, democratic institutions, rights, duties, and the functioning of the Indian State.

Understanding these terms helps readers understand the Constitution of India and the institutions created under it.

---

## Why Study Constitutional Terminology?

Studying constitutional terminology helps:

- Understand the Constitution better
- Follow governance and legal discussions
- Learn how institutions function
- Understand citizens' rights and duties
- Build a strong foundation in Indian polity

---

## Core Constitutional Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Constitution | Supreme law of the country |
| Preamble | Introduction stating constitutional ideals |
| Sovereignty | Supreme authority of the nation |
| Republic | Head of State is elected, not hereditary |
| Democracy | Government elected by the people |
| Secularism | Equal treatment of all religions |
| Federalism | Division of powers between Centre and States |
| Rule of Law | Everyone is subject to the law |
| Constitutionalism | Government limited by the Constitution |
| Amendment | Change made to the Constitution |

---

## Rights & Duties Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Fundamental Rights | Basic rights guaranteed by the Constitution |
| Right to Equality | Equal treatment before the law |
| Right to Freedom | Freedom of speech and other liberties |
| Constitutional Remedy | Right to approach courts for protection of rights |
| Fundamental Duties | Duties expected from citizens |
| Directive Principles | Guidelines for governance and policy-making |

---

## Government Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Legislature | Institution that makes laws |
| Executive | Institution that implements laws |
| Judiciary | Institution that interprets laws |
| Parliament | National legislature of India |
| President | Constitutional Head of State |
| Prime Minister | Head of Government |
| Governor | Constitutional head of a State |
| Chief Minister | Head of State Government |

---

## Federalism Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Union List | Subjects controlled by the Union Government |
| State List | Subjects controlled by State Governments |
| Concurrent List | Subjects shared by Centre and States |
| Union Territory | Territory administered under constitutional arrangements |
| Centre-State Relations | Relationship between Union and State governments |

---

## Constitutional Process Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Bill | Proposed law |
| Act | Approved law |
| Ordinance | Temporary law under constitutional provisions |
| Judicial Review | Court examination of laws and actions |
| Judicial Activism | Active role of courts in public matters |
| Constitutional Amendment | Formal change to the Constitution |

---

## Quick Revision

- Constitution = Supreme law.
- Preamble = Constitutional introduction.
- Democracy = Government by elected representatives.
- Republic = Elected Head of State.
- Federalism = Centre-State power sharing.
- Fundamental Rights = Protected citizen rights.
- Directive Principles = Governance guidelines.
- Legislature = Makes laws.
- Executive = Implements laws.
- Judiciary = Interprets laws.
