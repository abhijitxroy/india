

# Parliamentary Terminology

## Overview

Parliamentary terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in legislatures, law-making, parliamentary procedures, debates, committees, and democratic governance.

Understanding these terms helps explain how Parliament functions and how laws are made in India.

---

## Why Study Parliamentary Terminology?

Studying parliamentary terminology helps:

- Understand law-making processes
- Follow parliamentary debates
- Learn legislative procedures
- Understand democratic accountability
- Build knowledge of parliamentary institutions

---

## Core Parliamentary Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Parliament | National law-making institution |
| Legislature | Institution that makes laws |
| Lok Sabha | House of the People |
| Rajya Sabha | Council of States |
| Member of Parliament (MP) | Elected or nominated parliamentary representative |
| Session | Period during which Parliament meets |
| Sitting | Individual meeting of a House |
| Adjournment | Temporary suspension of proceedings |

---

## Law-Making Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Bill | Proposed law |
| Act | Law approved by Parliament |
| Amendment | Change to a bill or law |
| Ordinance | Temporary law issued under constitutional provisions |
| Legislation | Process of making laws |
| Voting | Formal decision-making process |
| Majority | More than half of votes or seats |
| Quorum | Minimum members required for proceedings |

---

## Parliamentary Procedure Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Question Hour | Time for asking questions to the government |
| Zero Hour | Period for raising urgent public matters |
| Motion | Formal proposal before the House |
| Resolution | Formal expression of opinion or decision |
| Debate | Discussion on public issues or legislation |
| Division | Recorded voting process |
| Privilege | Special rights of Parliament and members |

---

## Committee Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Parliamentary Committee | Group examining specific issues |
| Standing Committee | Permanent committee of Parliament |
| Select Committee | Committee formed for a specific purpose |
| Public Accounts Committee (PAC) | Committee examining public expenditure |
| Committee Report | Findings and recommendations of a committee |

---

## Quick Revision

- Parliament = National legislature.
- Lok Sabha = House of the People.
- Rajya Sabha = Council of States.
- Bill = Proposed law.
- Act = Approved law.
- Question Hour = Government accountability mechanism.
- Zero Hour = Raising urgent matters.
- Quorum = Minimum members required.
- Motion = Formal proposal.
- Committee = Group examining issues in detail.
