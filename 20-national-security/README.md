# National Security

## Why It Matters

National security protects India's sovereignty, territorial integrity, strategic interests, critical infrastructure, information systems, and citizens from internal and external threats.

It includes intelligence gathering, cyber security, information warfare, electronic warfare, and protection of strategic space assets.

## Key Concepts

### What is national security?

Protection of a nation's sovereignty, people, institutions, and strategic interests.

### Why is intelligence important?

It enables early warning and informed decision making.

### Why is cyber security important?

Modern governments, militaries, and economies depend heavily on digital systems.

### Why is space security important?

Navigation, communication, surveillance, and defence systems increasingly depend on space assets.
