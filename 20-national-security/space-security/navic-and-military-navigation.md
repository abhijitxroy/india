
# NavIC and Military Navigation

## Strategic Importance

Navigation systems are critical for modern military operations, precision targeting, transportation, logistics and strategic decision-making.

India developed NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) to provide reliable regional navigation services and reduce dependence on foreign navigation systems.

For national security, indigenous navigation capability is an important strategic asset.

## What is NavIC?

NavIC is India's indigenous satellite-based navigation system designed to provide accurate positioning, navigation and timing services.

It supports civilian users as well as strategic and military requirements.
