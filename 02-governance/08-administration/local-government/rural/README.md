

# Rural Local Government

## Importance

Rural local government is the system through which governance and development activities are carried out at the village level. It enables local participation in decision-making, implementation of welfare programmes, and grassroots democracy.

## Panchayati Raj System

The Panchayati Raj system is a three-tier rural local governance structure.

### Village Level

- Gram Panchayat
- Gram Sabha

### Intermediate Level

- Panchayat Samiti
- Block-level administration

### District Level

- Zila Parishad
- District-level rural planning and coordination
