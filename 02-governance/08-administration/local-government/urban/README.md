# Urban Local Government

## Overview

Urban Local Government is the system of local self-governance for cities and towns in India.

It manages civic services, urban planning, public infrastructure, sanitation, taxation, and local development.

Urban local bodies derive constitutional status from the 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.

## Importance

- Urban governance and administration
- Delivery of civic services
- Local democracy in cities and towns
- Urban planning and development
- Municipal finance and taxation
- Citizen participation in governance

## Governance Framework

| Institution | Primary Role |
|------------|-------------|
| Municipal Corporation | Governance of large cities |
| Municipality | Governance of medium towns |
| Nagar Panchayat | Governance of transitional areas |
| Mayor | Political leadership |
| Municipal Commissioner | Executive administration |
