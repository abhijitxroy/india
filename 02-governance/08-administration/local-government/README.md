# Local Government in India

## Overview

Local Government is the third tier of governance in India.

It brings administration closer to citizens and is responsible for delivering essential public services, local development, and grassroots democracy.

The system is divided into rural and urban local governments.

Constitutional status was provided through the 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts.

## Importance

- Grassroots democracy
- Citizen participation in governance
- Local development planning
- Delivery of public services
- Rural and urban administration
- Decentralization of power

## Structure

```text
Local Government
│
├── Rural Local Government
│   ├── Gram Panchayat
│   ├── Panchayat Samiti
│   └── Zila Parishad
│
└── Urban Local Government
    ├── Nagar Panchayat
    ├── Municipality
    └── Municipal Corporation
```

## Major Constitutional Amendments

| Amendment | Focus |
|------------|--------|
| 73rd Constitutional Amendment | Rural Local Government |
| 74th Constitutional Amendment | Urban Local Government |
