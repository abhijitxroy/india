# Gram Panchayat

## Overview

Gram Panchayat is the lowest and most direct tier of rural local self-government in India.

It functions at the village level and serves as the primary institution through which local governance and development activities are carried out.

Gram Panchayats were strengthened through the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.

## Importance

Gram Panchayats are important because they:

- Bring governance closer to citizens
- Deliver local public services
- Implement welfare schemes
- Support rural development
- Promote local participation in decision-making
- Strengthen grassroots democracy

## Position in the Panchayati Raj Structure

```text
Zila Parishad
      ↓
Panchayat Samiti
      ↓
Gram Panchayat
```

## Gram Sabha and Gram Panchayat

### Gram Sabha

The Gram Sabha consists of all registered voters of a village or group of villages.

It is the foundation of local democracy.

### Gram Panchayat

The Gram Panchayat is the elected executive body responsible for day-to-day administration and implementation of local decisions.

## Composition

A Gram Panchayat generally consists of:

- Elected ward members
- Sarpanch or Pradhan
- Deputy Sarpanch or Upa-Pradhan (where applicable)
- Standing committees for various functions

## Major Functions

### Civic Services

- Drinking water supply
- Village sanitation
- Street lighting
- Maintenance of local roads

### Development Activities

- Rural infrastructure projects
- Housing programmes
- Employment schemes
- Community asset creation

### Welfare Programmes

- Social welfare initiatives
- Poverty alleviation programmes
- Support for vulnerable groups
- Implementation of central and state schemes

### Record Keeping

- Birth and death records
- Local administrative records
- Beneficiary data for government programmes

## Sources of Revenue

Gram Panchayats may receive funds from:

- Local taxes and fees
- State government grants
- Central government grants
- Finance Commission allocations
- Scheme-related funding

## Role in Rural Development

Gram Panchayats identify local needs and help implement development projects based on community priorities.

They play an important role in agriculture, infrastructure, sanitation, education, and social welfare.
