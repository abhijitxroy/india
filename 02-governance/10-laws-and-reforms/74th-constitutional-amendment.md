# 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

## Overview

The 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 is a landmark governance reform that gave constitutional status to Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) in India.

The amendment strengthened urban self-governance and created a constitutional framework for municipalities and city administration.

It is one of the most important reforms in urban governance and decentralization.

---

## Why Study the 74th Amendment?

Studying the 74th Amendment helps understand:

- Urban governance
- Municipal administration
- Decentralization of power
- Local democracy
- City development
- Citizen participation

It plays a key role in understanding how Indian cities are governed.
