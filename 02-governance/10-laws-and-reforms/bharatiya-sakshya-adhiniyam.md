# Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)

## Overview

The Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023 is India's new law governing evidence in legal proceedings. It replaced the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 and came into force on 1 July 2024.

The law defines what constitutes evidence, how evidence can be presented before courts, and the standards used for proving facts in criminal and civil proceedings.

## Background

The Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam was introduced as part of India's broader criminal law reforms alongside:

- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
- Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)

Together, these laws replaced major colonial-era criminal law statutes.

## Purpose

The objectives of BSA include:

- Modernization of evidence law
- Recognition of digital evidence
- Improved efficiency in judicial proceedings
- Adaptation to technological developments
- Strengthening the evidentiary framework of courts

## Major Features

### Digital and Electronic Evidence

A key feature of BSA is the expanded recognition of:

- Electronic records
- Digital documents
- Emails
- Messages
- Digital communications
- Electronic devices as evidence sources

### Technology-Oriented Framework

The law updates evidence procedures to reflect modern communication and digital transactions.

### Continuity of Core Principles

Many fundamental principles of evidence law continue under the new framework while incorporating technological changes.

## Types of Evidence

### Documentary Evidence

Includes:

- Written records
- Official documents
- Electronic records
- Digital documents

### Oral Evidence

Includes statements made by witnesses before a court.

### Electronic Evidence

Includes information stored, transmitted, or generated electronically.

## Witnesses

The law contains provisions regarding:

- Examination of witnesses
- Cross-examination
- Competency of witnesses
- Reliability and admissibility of testimony

## Admissibility of Evidence

BSA provides rules regarding:

- Relevant facts
- Admissible evidence
- Documentary proof
- Electronic records
- Expert opinions

These provisions help courts determine the evidentiary value of information presented during proceedings.

## Significance

The law is significant because it:

- Replaces a nineteenth-century evidence law
- Recognizes modern digital realities
- Strengthens treatment of electronic evidence
- Supports technology-enabled justice processes
- Forms part of India's criminal justice reforms
