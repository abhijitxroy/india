# Lokpal

## Institutional Importance

Lokpal is India's national anti-corruption ombudsman established to investigate allegations of corruption against public servants.

It strengthens accountability, promotes transparency, and helps improve public trust in governance.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Type | Statutory Anti-Corruption Body |
| Legal Basis | Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013 |
| Jurisdiction | Union Level |
| Purpose | Inquiry into Corruption Complaints |
| Counterpart in States | Lokayukta |

---

## What is Lokpal?

Lokpal is an independent statutory institution created to inquire into allegations of corruption against public functionaries.

It serves as an important accountability mechanism in democratic governance.

---

## Major Functions

| Function | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| Complaint Examination | Review Corruption Allegations |
| Inquiry | Conduct Preliminary Investigations |
| Investigation Referral | Refer Cases to Agencies |
| Oversight | Strengthen Accountability |
