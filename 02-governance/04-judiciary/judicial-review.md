
# Judicial Review in India

## Overview

Judicial Review is the power of the judiciary to examine laws, executive actions, and government decisions to determine whether they are consistent with the Constitution.

It is one of the most important features of the Indian constitutional system and helps maintain constitutional supremacy.

---

## Why Study Judicial Review?

Studying Judicial Review helps understand:

- Constitutional supremacy
- Rule of law
- Protection of fundamental rights
- Separation of powers
- Checks and balances
- Judicial accountability of government actions

Judicial Review is a key mechanism for protecting the Constitution.

---

## Simple Explanation

```text
Law or Government Action
            ↓
Judiciary Examines
            ↓
Constitutional Test
            ↓
Valid or Invalid
```

The judiciary checks whether actions comply with the Constitution.

---

## Constitutional Basis

Judicial Review emerges from various constitutional provisions and from the role assigned to the judiciary under the Constitution.

It allows courts to ensure that constitutional limits are respected.

---

## How Judicial Review Works

```text
Citizen Challenge
         ↓
Court Hearing
         ↓
Constitutional Examination
         ↓
Judgment
```

Courts examine whether the challenged action violates constitutional principles.

---

## What Can Be Reviewed?

### Laws

- Parliamentary laws
- State laws
- Amendments (subject to constitutional principles)

### Executive Actions

- Government decisions
- Administrative actions
- Official orders

### Public Authorities

- Actions by public institutions
- Constitutional authorities

---

## Importance of Judicial Review

### Protection of Rights

- Safeguards fundamental rights
- Provides remedies against unconstitutional actions

### Constitutional Supremacy

- Ensures all authorities operate within constitutional limits

### Checks and Balances

- Prevents misuse of power
- Maintains institutional balance

---

## Judicial Review and Democracy

```text
Constitution
       ↓
Government Action
       ↓
Judicial Review
       ↓
Constitutional Compliance
```

Judicial Review helps ensure that democratic governance remains consistent with constitutional values.

---

## Limitations

Judicial Review does not mean courts govern the country.

Courts review legality and constitutionality, while elected governments continue to make policy decisions.

---

## Quick Revision

- Judicial Review allows courts to examine laws and government actions.
- It protects constitutional supremacy.
- It helps safeguard fundamental rights.
- It supports checks and balances.
- Courts may invalidate unconstitutional actions.
- Judicial Review is a key feature of the Indian Constitution.
