# Vice Presidents Timeline of India

## Overview

The Vice Presidents Timeline traces the holders of India's second-highest constitutional office from 1952 to the present.

The Vice President serves as the constitutional deputy to the President and is also the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

This office plays an important role in parliamentary functioning and constitutional continuity.

---

## Why Study Vice Presidents?

Studying Vice Presidents helps understand:

- Constitutional governance
- Parliamentary functioning
- Rajya Sabha operations
- Presidential succession
- Democratic institutions

The office connects the executive and legislative dimensions of governance.

## Major Vice Presidents

### Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (1952–1962)

- First Vice President of India
- Renowned philosopher and academic
- Later became President of India

### Dr. Zakir Husain (1962–1967)

- Served as Vice President before becoming President
- Known for contributions to education

### V. V. Giri (1967–1969)

- Served as Vice President and later President
- Important constitutional figure during a period of transition

### Mohammad Hamid Ansari (2007–2017)

- Served two consecutive terms
- One of the longest-serving Vice Presidents of India

### Jagdeep Dhankhar (2022–Present)

- Current Vice President of India
- Serves as Chairman of the Rajya Sabha

```text

            ↓

            ↓

            ↓

```

The office helps ensure stability and continuity within the constitutional system.

---

## Vice President vs President

| Vice President | President |
|----------|----------|
| Second-highest constitutional office | Highest constitutional office |
| Chairman of Rajya Sabha | Head of State |
| Elected indirectly | Elected indirectly |
| Acts as President when required | Constitutional Head of the Republic |

## Quick Revision

- The Vice President is India's second-highest constitutional office.
- The office was established in 1952.
- The Vice President is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
- The Vice President can act as President when required.
- Several Vice Presidents later became Presidents.
- Jagdeep Dhankhar is the current Vice President.
