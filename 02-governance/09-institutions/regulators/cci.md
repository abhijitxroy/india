# Competition Commission of India (CCI)

## Institutional Importance

The Competition Commission of India (CCI) is the statutory regulator responsible for promoting fair competition in markets and preventing anti-competitive practices.

It helps protect consumers, encourages innovation, and ensures a level playing field for businesses.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | Competition Commission of India |
| Established | 2003 |
| Operational | 2009 |
| Type | Statutory Regulator |
| Legal Basis | Competition Act, 2002 |

---

## What is CCI?

CCI is India's competition regulator responsible for preventing practices that adversely affect competition and for regulating mergers and acquisitions that may reduce market competition.

---

## Major Functions

| Function | Purpose |

| Prevent Cartels | Stop Anti-Competitive Agreements |
