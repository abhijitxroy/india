
# Institutions

## Institutional Importance

Institutions provide stability, accountability, regulation, oversight, and governance within a democratic system.

They help ensure that laws are implemented, rights are protected, markets function properly, and public authorities remain accountable.

### Constitutional Bodies

Institutions created directly by the Constitution.

### Commissions

Specialized bodies addressing governance and social issues.

### Regulators

Independent regulators overseeing sectors and markets.

### Watchdogs

Oversight institutions ensuring transparency and accountability.
