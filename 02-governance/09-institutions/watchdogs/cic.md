# Central Information Commission (CIC)

## Overview

The Central Information Commission (CIC) is the highest appellate body established under the Right to Information (RTI) framework in India.

It is responsible for promoting transparency, accountability, and citizen access to information held by public authorities.

The commission hears complaints and appeals related to the Right to Information Act.

## Legal Basis

The CIC was established under:

- Right to Information Act, 2005

## Nature of the Body

The CIC is a statutory body.

It functions independently while implementing the provisions of the RTI Act.

## Composition

The commission consists of:

- Chief Information Commissioner
- Information Commissioners

Members are appointed by the President of India.

## Appointment Committee

Appointments are made on the recommendation of a committee consisting of:

- Prime Minister (Chairperson)
- Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha
- Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the Prime Minister

## Functions

### Hear Appeals

The CIC hears second appeals under the RTI Act.

### Hear Complaints

The commission can investigate complaints regarding:

- Denial of information
- Delay in providing information
- Refusal to accept RTI applications
- Incomplete information
- Misleading information

### Promote Transparency

The commission encourages transparency and accountability in public administration.

### Monitor Implementation

The commission monitors implementation of the RTI Act by public authorities.

## Powers

While conducting inquiries, the CIC has powers similar to a civil court.

It can:

- Summon individuals
- Examine witnesses
- Require production of documents
- Receive evidence
- Conduct investigations

## Right to Information (RTI)

The RTI Act empowers citizens to seek information from public authorities.

Objectives include:

- Transparency
- Accountability
- Citizen participation
- Reduction of corruption

## Importance

The CIC helps:

- Strengthen democracy
- Promote open governance
- Improve accountability
- Empower citizens
- Ensure access to public information

## Limitations

The commission cannot:

- Create information that does not exist
- Interfere in policy decisions
- Act as a grievance redressal body for all issues

Its role is limited to information access under the RTI framework.

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Case backlogs
- Delays in disposal of appeals
- Vacancies in the commission
- Low awareness of RTI rights
- Compliance issues among public authorities

## Difference Between CIC and CVC

| Feature | CIC | CVC |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Focus | Access to Information | Vigilance and Anti-Corruption |
| Legal Basis | RTI Act, 2005 | CVC Act, 2003 |
| Main Objective | Transparency | Integrity in Administration |
