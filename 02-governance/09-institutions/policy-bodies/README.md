# Policy Bodies

## Overview

Policy bodies are institutions that assist the Government of India in policy formulation, strategic planning, development initiatives, and governance reforms.

Unlike constitutional or statutory regulatory bodies, policy bodies primarily function as advisory and planning institutions.

They provide recommendations, facilitate coordination, monitor programmes, and support evidence-based policymaking.

## Importance

Policy bodies help:

- Formulate development strategies
- Support economic planning
- Promote cooperative federalism
- Improve governance outcomes
- Monitor implementation of programmes
- Encourage innovation and reforms

## Major Policy Bodies

### NITI Aayog

NITI Aayog is the premier public policy think tank of the Government of India.

Key focus areas include:

- Cooperative federalism
- Sustainable development
- Innovation
- Economic reforms
- Long-term policy planning

## Policy Bodies vs Constitutional Bodies

| Feature | Policy Bodies | Constitutional Bodies |
|----------|----------|
| Basis | Executive Resolution or Government Decision | Constitution of India |
| Role | Advisory and Planning | Constitutional Functions |
