# NITI Aayog

## Overview

NITI Aayog is the premier public policy think tank of the Government of India.

It was established to replace the Planning Commission and serves as a platform for cooperative federalism, policy innovation, and long-term development planning.

The institution provides strategic and technical advice to the Union and State Governments.

## Full Form

NITI stands for:

- National Institution for Transforming India

## Establishment

### Year of Establishment

- 2015

### Legal Basis

NITI Aayog was established through a Union Cabinet Resolution.

It is not a constitutional or statutory body.

## Background

NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015.

The change reflected a shift from centralized planning towards cooperative and competitive federalism.

## Objectives

Major objectives include:

- Promoting cooperative federalism
- Encouraging innovation
- Supporting sustainable development
- Improving governance
- Facilitating long-term policy planning
- Monitoring development programmes

## Structure

### Chairperson

- Prime Minister of India

### Governing Council

Consists of:

- Chief Ministers of States
- Lieutenant Governors of Union Territories

### Vice-Chairperson

Appointed by the Prime Minister.

### Full-Time Members

Experts appointed to assist in policy formulation.

### Special Invitees

Subject experts may be invited when required.

## Functions

### Policy Formulation

NITI Aayog provides policy recommendations to governments.

### Cooperative Federalism

It promotes collaboration between the Union and State Governments.

### Programme Monitoring

The institution monitors implementation of important development programmes.

### Innovation Promotion

It supports innovation, entrepreneurship, and emerging technologies.

### Sustainable Development Goals

NITI Aayog acts as the nodal institution for monitoring Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in India.

## Major Initiatives

Important initiatives include:

- Aspirational Districts Programme
- SDG India Index
- Atal Innovation Mission
- National Multidimensional Poverty Index
- Development Monitoring and Evaluation Frameworks

## Cooperative and Competitive Federalism

### Cooperative Federalism

Encourages collaboration between the Union and States.

### Competitive Federalism

Encourages states to improve performance through healthy competition.

## Difference Between Planning Commission and NITI Aayog

| Feature | Planning Commission | NITI Aayog |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Established | 1950 | 2015 |
| Nature | Central Planning Body | Policy Think Tank |
| Approach | Top-Down Planning | Cooperative Federalism |
| Fund Allocation Role | Yes | No |
| Focus | Five-Year Plans | Policy and Strategy |

## Importance

NITI Aayog helps:

- Improve policymaking
- Strengthen centre-state cooperation
- Encourage innovation
- Promote evidence-based governance
- Support inclusive development

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Coordination across governments
- Implementation gaps
- Regional development disparities
- Balancing growth and sustainability
