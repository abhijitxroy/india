
# Lok Sabha

## Overview

The Lok Sabha is the Lower House of the Parliament of India.

It is also known as the House of the People because its members are directly elected by citizens through general elections.

Lok Sabha plays a central role in law-making, government formation, financial control, and democratic representation.

---

## Why Study Lok Sabha?

Studying Lok Sabha helps understand:

- Parliamentary democracy
- Elections and representation
- Government formation
- Law-making
- Financial legislation
- Accountability of the executive

It is one of the most important institutions in Indian democracy.

---

## Constitutional Position

```text
Citizens
      ↓
General Election
      ↓
Lok Sabha MPs
      ↓
Lok Sabha
      ↓
Government Formation
```

Members are directly elected by the people.

## Major Functions

### Law-Making

- Debates Bills
- Passes legislation
- Participates in constitutional amendments

### Government Formation

```text
Election
      ↓
Majority Party / Coalition
      ↓
Prime Minister
      ↓
Government
```

The government is formed from the majority in Lok Sabha.

### Financial Powers

- Discusses Budget
- Approves expenditure
- Considers Money Bills

### Executive Accountability

- Questions ministers
- Debates government policies
- Reviews government performance

---

## Speaker of Lok Sabha

The Speaker:

- Presides over proceedings
- Maintains order
- Interprets procedural rules
- Conducts debates and voting

The Speaker is an important constitutional authority.

---

## Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha

| Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|----------|----------|
| Lower House | Upper House |
| Directly elected | Indirectly elected |
| Can bring down government | Cannot bring down government |
| Greater role in Money Bills | Limited role in Money Bills |

---

## Importance in Democracy

Lok Sabha reflects the democratic will of the people because its members are directly elected.

It serves as the primary link between citizens and the Union Government.

---

## Quick Revision

- Lok Sabha is the Lower House of Parliament.
- It is called the House of the People.
- Members are directly elected.
- The government is responsible to Lok Sabha.
- The Speaker presides over Lok Sabha.
- Lok Sabha plays a major role in law-making and financial matters.
