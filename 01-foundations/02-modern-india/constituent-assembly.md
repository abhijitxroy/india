# Constituent Assembly of India

## Overview

The Constituent Assembly was the body responsible for drafting the Constitution of India. It was formed in 1946 and worked until the Constitution came into effect on 26 January 1950.

The Assembly debated, discussed, and finalized the principles that would guide independent India's political and constitutional system.

## Formation

### Background

The demand for a Constituent Assembly emerged during the Indian freedom movement.

The Cabinet Mission Plan of 1946 provided the framework for establishing the Assembly.

### First Meeting

- Date: 9 December 1946
- Location: New Delhi
- Temporary Chairman: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

### Permanent Chairman

Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected as the permanent President of the Constituent Assembly.

## Objectives Resolution

Jawaharlal Nehru introduced the Objectives Resolution on 13 December 1946.

It later became the foundation of the Preamble to the Constitution.

The resolution emphasized:

- Sovereignty
- Democracy
- Justice
- Equality
- Fundamental rights

## Drafting Committee

### Formation

The Drafting Committee was established on 29 August 1947.

### Chairman

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar served as Chairman of the Drafting Committee.

### Major Responsibility

The committee prepared the draft Constitution based on recommendations from various committees and debates.

## Constitution Making Process

### Duration

- Started: 9 December 1946
- Completed: 26 November 1949
- Constitution Effective: 26 January 1950

### Time Taken

- 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days

### Sessions

- 11 sessions
- More than 160 days of sittings

## Adoption of the Constitution

### Adoption

The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949.

### Enforcement

The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950.

This date was chosen to commemorate the declaration of Purna Swaraj in 1930.

## Significance

The Constituent Assembly:

- Created the Constitution of India
- Established democratic institutions
- Defined fundamental rights
- Laid the foundation of parliamentary democracy
- Guided India's transition to an independent republic
