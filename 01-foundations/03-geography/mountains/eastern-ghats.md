# Eastern Ghats

## Overview

The Eastern Ghats are a discontinuous range of mountains running parallel to the eastern coast of India.

They extend from Odisha through Andhra Pradesh to Tamil Nadu and are interrupted by several major rivers flowing into the Bay of Bengal.

Although lower in elevation than the Himalayas and generally less continuous than the Western Ghats, they are important for biodiversity, mineral resources, river systems, and regional climate.

## Location

The Eastern Ghats extend along the eastern part of the Indian peninsula.

Major states covered include:

- Odisha
- Andhra Pradesh
- Telangana
- Tamil Nadu
- Parts of Karnataka

## Formation

The Eastern Ghats are among the oldest mountain systems in India.

They consist mainly of ancient metamorphic and igneous rocks.

Over millions of years, erosion has reduced their height and created gaps in the range.

## Physical Features

### Discontinuous Nature

Unlike the Western Ghats, the Eastern Ghats are not continuous.

Major rivers have cut through the mountain system.

### Average Elevation

The Eastern Ghats are generally lower than the Western Ghats.

Most peaks range between 600 and 1,500 metres.

## Important Peaks

### Jindhagada Peak

- Highest peak in the Eastern Ghats
- Located in Andhra Pradesh

### Mahendragiri

- Located in Odisha
- Important geographical and cultural landmark

## Major Rivers Crossing the Eastern Ghats

Important rivers include:

- Godavari
- Krishna
- Mahanadi
- Kaveri

These rivers create fertile deltas before entering the Bay of Bengal.

## Biodiversity

The Eastern Ghats support diverse ecosystems.

### Vegetation

Common vegetation includes:

- Tropical dry deciduous forests
- Moist deciduous forests
- Scrub forests

### Wildlife

Important species include:

- Elephant
- Tiger
- Leopard
- Gaur
- Various bird species

## Economic Importance

### Agriculture

The region supports cultivation of:

- Millets
- Rice
- Pulses
- Plantation crops

### Minerals

The Eastern Ghats contain important mineral resources including:

- Bauxite
- Iron ore
- Manganese

### Hydroelectric Projects

Several river valley projects are located in the region.

## Difference Between Eastern and Western Ghats

| Feature | Eastern Ghats | Western Ghats |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Nature | Discontinuous | Continuous |
| Elevation | Lower | Higher |
| Rainfall | Comparatively Lower | Higher |
| Rivers | Cut Across the Range | Originate from the Range |
| Coast | Bay of Bengal | Arabian Sea |

## Environmental Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Deforestation
- Mining pressure
- Habitat fragmentation
- Soil erosion
- Human-wildlife conflict
