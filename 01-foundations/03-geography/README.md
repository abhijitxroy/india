# Geography

## Overview

This section covers the physical, political, and strategic geography of India.

Understanding geography helps explain India's history, economy, security, culture, agriculture, climate, and development patterns.

## Quick Revision

- India is the seventh-largest country by area.
- The Himalayas form the northern boundary.
- The Indo-Gangetic Plain is one of the world's most fertile regions.
- The Deccan Plateau occupies much of peninsular India.
- India has a long coastline along the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal.
- Geography strongly influences economy, culture, security, and development.
