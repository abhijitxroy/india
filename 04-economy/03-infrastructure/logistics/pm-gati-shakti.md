# PM Gati Shakti

## Overview

PM Gati Shakti is the Government of India's national master plan for integrated infrastructure development. Launched in 2021, it aims to improve coordination among infrastructure ministries, reduce logistics costs, eliminate project delays, and create a seamless multimodal transportation network.

The initiative seeks to bring together roads, railways, ports, airports, waterways, logistics infrastructure, and industrial corridors under a unified planning framework.

## Background

Historically, infrastructure projects in India were often planned and implemented independently by different ministries and agencies.

This sometimes resulted in:

- Project delays
- Cost overruns
- Connectivity gaps
- Duplication of efforts
- Inefficient logistics networks

PM Gati Shakti was introduced to improve coordination and integrated planning.

## Objectives

The programme aims to:

- Improve infrastructure planning
- Enhance multimodal connectivity
- Reduce logistics costs
- Improve supply chain efficiency
- Accelerate project implementation
- Support economic growth
- Enhance ease of doing business

## National Master Plan

### Overview

The National Master Plan forms the core of PM Gati Shakti.

It integrates infrastructure planning across multiple sectors through a digital platform.

### Features

The platform enables:

- Integrated project planning
- Data-based decision making
- Inter-ministerial coordination
- Infrastructure mapping
- Monitoring of projects

## Multimodal Connectivity

### Concept

Multimodal connectivity refers to the seamless movement of goods and passengers using multiple transportation modes.

Examples include:

- Road to rail connectivity
- Rail to port connectivity
- Port to inland waterway connectivity
- Airport logistics integration

### Benefits

- Faster transportation
- Lower logistics costs
- Reduced congestion
- Improved efficiency
- Better supply chain management
