# Union Budget

## Economic Importance

The Union Budget is the annual financial statement of the Government of India.

It outlines the government's expected revenue, expenditure, taxation proposals, borrowing plans, and policy priorities for the upcoming financial year.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Presented By | Finance Minister |
| Frequency | Annually |
| Financial Year | 1 April – 31 March |
| Constitutional Basis | Article 112 |

---

## What is the Union Budget?

The Union Budget is a comprehensive statement of the government's finances.

It estimates income and expenditure and provides the roadmap for economic management and development priorities.
