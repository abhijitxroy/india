# UPI Revolution

## Overview

The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) revolution transformed digital payments in India by enabling instant, low-cost, and interoperable money transfers through mobile devices.

Developed as part of India's digital public infrastructure, UPI became one of the most successful payment systems in the world and significantly changed how individuals, businesses, and governments conduct transactions.

---

## Why Study UPI?

Studying UPI helps understand:

- Digital transformation
- Financial inclusion
- Fintech innovation
- Digital governance
- Economic modernization
- India's digital public infrastructure

UPI is one of the most important economic and technological transformations in modern India.
