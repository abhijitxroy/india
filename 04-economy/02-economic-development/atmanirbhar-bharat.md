# Atmanirbhar Bharat

## Overview

Atmanirbhar Bharat (Self-Reliant India) is a national initiative aimed at strengthening India's economic resilience, enhancing domestic manufacturing, promoting innovation, reducing excessive import dependence, and improving global competitiveness.

The initiative seeks to build a stronger and more self-sustaining economy while remaining integrated with global supply chains and international trade.

## Background

The initiative was announced in 2020 in response to economic disruptions and supply chain challenges.

It emphasized the need to strengthen domestic capabilities in manufacturing, technology, infrastructure, agriculture, defence, and other strategic sectors.

## Objectives

The initiative aims to:

- Promote domestic manufacturing
- Strengthen supply chains
- Increase industrial competitiveness
- Encourage innovation and entrepreneurship
- Support MSMEs
- Enhance exports
- Generate employment opportunities
- Improve economic resilience

## Core Pillars

### Economy

Focus on reforms and growth that support a modern and competitive economy.

### Infrastructure

Development of world-class infrastructure to support industry and economic activity.

### Technology and Innovation

Encouragement of research, innovation, digital transformation, and emerging technologies.

### Demography

Utilization of India's large workforce and demographic advantage.

### Demand

Strengthening domestic demand and improving market opportunities for businesses.

## Role of Manufacturing

Manufacturing plays a central role in Atmanirbhar Bharat.

Key goals include:

- Expanding domestic production
- Reducing import dependence
- Increasing exports
- Building global manufacturing capabilities

## Support for MSMEs

MSMEs are considered a key pillar of the initiative.

Support measures focus on:

- Access to finance
- Technology adoption
- Market access
- Business expansion
- Employment generation

## Strategic Sectors

Important sectors include:

- Defence manufacturing
- Electronics
- Semiconductors
- Pharmaceuticals
- Renewable energy
- Telecommunications
- Space technology

## Economic Significance

### Employment Generation

Expansion of domestic industries creates jobs and supports entrepreneurship.

### Supply Chain Resilience

Stronger domestic capabilities reduce vulnerability to external disruptions.

### Export Competitiveness

Improved manufacturing and productivity help increase exports and global competitiveness.

## Relationship with Other Initiatives

Atmanirbhar Bharat complements:

- Make in India
- Skill India
- Startup India
- Digital India
- PM Gati Shakti
- Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Schemes

Together these initiatives support long-term economic transformation.

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Technology gaps

- Skill shortages
- Access to capital
- Research and development capacity

## Future Roadmap

Future priorities include:
