
# Planning Commission

## Overview

The Planning Commission was an important institution responsible for guiding India's economic development through centralized planning and long-term development strategies.

It played a major role in shaping economic policy, allocating resources, and supporting national development goals during much of the post-independence period.

The Planning Commission was a key pillar of India's planned economy framework.

---

## Why Study the Planning Commission?

Studying the Planning Commission helps understand:

- Economic planning
- Development strategy
- Public policy
- State-led economic growth
- Resource allocation
- Evolution of economic governance

It is essential for understanding India's economic development before NITI Aayog.
