# Direct Tax

## Economic Importance

Direct taxes are levied directly on individuals, businesses, and other entities based on their income, profits, or wealth.

They are an important source of government revenue and help fund public services, infrastructure, welfare programmes, and national development.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Paid By | Individuals and Organizations |
| Collected By | Government |
| Based On | Income and Profits |
| Main Example | Income Tax |

---

## What is Direct Tax?

A direct tax is a tax that is paid directly by the taxpayer to the government.

The burden of the tax cannot normally be transferred to another person.

---

## Major Types of Direct Taxes

| Tax | Description |
|----------|----------|
| Income Tax | Tax on Individual Income |
| Corporate Tax | Tax on Company Profits |
| Capital Gains Tax | Tax on Capital Asset Gains |
| Securities Transaction Tax | Tax on Securities Transactions |

---

## Characteristics

- Paid Directly to Government
- Based on Ability to Pay
- Progressive Tax Structure
- Important Revenue Source
- Supports Redistribution

---

## Direct Tax Collection Flow

```text
Income Earned
      ↓
Tax Calculation
      ↓
Tax Payment
      ↓
Government Revenue
      ↓
Public Expenditure
```

---

## Advantages

- Promotes Equity
- Progressive Taxation
- Stable Revenue Source
- Supports Welfare Programmes

---

## Challenges

- Tax Evasion
- Compliance Burden
- Complex Regulations
- Enforcement Requirements

---

## Direct Tax vs Indirect Tax

| Feature | Direct Tax | Indirect Tax |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Paid By | Same Person | Can Be Shifted |
| Basis | Income/Profit | Consumption |
| Example | Income Tax | GST |
