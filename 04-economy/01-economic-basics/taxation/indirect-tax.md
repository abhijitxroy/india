# Indirect Tax

## Economic Importance

Indirect taxes are levied on the consumption of goods and services rather than directly on income or profits.

They form a major source of government revenue and are collected through businesses before ultimately being paid by consumers.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Paid By | Consumers |
| Collected Through | Businesses |
| Based On | Consumption |
| Major Example | GST |

---

## What is Indirect Tax?

An indirect tax is a tax whose burden can be transferred from one person to another.

Although businesses collect the tax, the final burden is generally borne by the consumer.

---

## Major Types of Indirect Taxes

| Tax | Description |
|----------|----------|
| GST | Tax on Goods and Services |
| Customs Duty | Tax on Imports and Exports |
| Excise Duty* | Limited Application |

*Most indirect taxes were subsumed under GST.

---

## Characteristics

- Based on Consumption
- Collected Through Businesses
- Broad Tax Base
- Important Revenue Source
- Paid Indirectly by Consumers

---

## Collection Flow

```text
Manufacturer
      ↓
Wholesaler
      ↓
Retailer

Consumer
```

---

## Advantages

- Easy Collection
- Broad Coverage
- Stable Revenue
- Supports Public Finance

---

## Challenges

- Can Affect Lower-Income Groups More
- Compliance Requirements
- Price Impact on Consumers
- Complex Administration

---

## Direct Tax vs Indirect Tax

| Feature | Direct Tax | Indirect Tax |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Basis | Income | Consumption |
| Burden Transfer | No | Yes |
| Example | Income Tax | GST |
| Payer | Taxpayer | Consumer |
