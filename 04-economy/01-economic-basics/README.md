# Economy

## Economic Importance

The economy determines growth, jobs, investment, trade, infrastructure development, government spending, and living standards.

India is one of the world's largest economies with major contributions from agriculture, manufacturing, services, finance, and technology.

### Basics

GDP, inflation, fiscal deficit, and union budget.

### Banking

RBI, banking system, and monetary policy.

### Taxation

Direct taxes, indirect taxes, and GST.

### Sectors

Agriculture, manufacturing, and services.

### Trade

Exports, imports, forex reserves, and trade balance.

### Infrastructure

Roads, railways, ports, airports, power, and logistics.
