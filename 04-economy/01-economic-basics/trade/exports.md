# Exports

## Economic Importance

Exports are goods and services sold by India to other countries.

They generate foreign exchange earnings, support economic growth, create employment, strengthen industries, and improve India's position in global trade.

## What are Exports?

Exports refer to products and services produced in India and sold to foreign buyers.

Export earnings contribute to national income and foreign exchange reserves.
