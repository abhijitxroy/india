# Imports

## Economic Importance

Imports are goods and services purchased by India from other countries.

They help meet domestic demand, provide access to critical raw materials and technology, support industries, and improve consumer choice.

## What are Imports?

Imports refer to products and services purchased from foreign countries for domestic use, production, or consumption.

They are an essential part of international trade.
