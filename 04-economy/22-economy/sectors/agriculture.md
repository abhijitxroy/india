# Agriculture

## Economic Importance

Agriculture is one of the most important sectors of the Indian economy.

It supports food security, rural livelihoods, agro-based industries, and employment for a large section of the population.

## What is Agriculture?

Agriculture involves the cultivation of crops, livestock rearing, fisheries, and allied activities.

It forms the foundation of India's food system and rural economy.
