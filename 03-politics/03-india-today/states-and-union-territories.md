# States and Union Territories

## Overview

India is a federal republic composed of States and Union Territories. Together they form the foundation of India's administrative, political, and governance system.

This topic explains how States and Union Territories function within India's federal framework and how governance is delivered across the country.

---

## Federal Structure

```text
Union Government
        ↓
States & Union Territories
        ↓
District Administration
        ↓
Local Governance
        ↓
Citizens
```

India's governance system operates through multiple levels of administration.

---

## States

States are major federal units with:

- Elected governments
- Legislative assemblies
- Chief Ministers
- Constitutional powers defined by the Constitution

States manage many areas of governance including public order, agriculture, health, and local administration.

---

## Union Territories

Union Territories are administered under arrangements defined by the Constitution and Parliament.

Some Union Territories have legislative institutions while others are administered directly through the Union Government framework.
