# Aam Aadmi Party (AAP)

## Overview

The Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) is an Indian political party that emerged from the anti-corruption movement of the early 2010s.

The party advocates transparency, accountability, good governance, public service delivery, and citizen-centric administration.

AAP has become an important political force in Indian politics, particularly in Delhi and Punjab.

## Formation

### Established

- 26 November 2012

### Founder

- Arvind Kejriwal

### Background

The party emerged from the India Against Corruption movement that demanded stronger anti-corruption mechanisms.

## Name and Symbol

### Meaning

Aam Aadmi means "Common Person".

### Election Symbol

- Broom (Jhadu)

The symbol represents efforts to clean corruption from public life.

## Ideology

Major themes associated with the party include:

- Anti-corruption
- Good governance
- Transparency
- Decentralization
- Public welfare
- Citizen participation

## Organizational Structure

The party operates through:

- National leadership
- State units
- District units
- Local organizational structures

## Electoral Performance

### Delhi

AAP achieved major electoral success in the National Capital Territory of Delhi and formed the government multiple times.

### Punjab

The party later expanded its political presence and formed the government in Punjab.

## Major Focus Areas

### Education

Focus on public school reforms and educational infrastructure.

### Healthcare

Expansion of primary healthcare services and public health facilities.

### Public Services

Emphasis on improving delivery of government services.

### Governance Reforms

Promotion of transparency and accountability mechanisms.

## Significance in Indian Politics

AAP is often discussed as an example of a political party that emerged from a social movement and rapidly gained electoral success.

It contributed to debates on governance, anti-corruption measures, and public service delivery.

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Political competition
- Organizational expansion across states
- Governance expectations
- Maintaining electoral support
