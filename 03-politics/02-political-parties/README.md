# Political Parties

## Overview

This section covers the political party system of India, including national parties, regional parties, party recognition, elections, alliances, and government formation.

Political parties are the primary link between citizens, elections, legislatures, and governments.

## Quick Revision

- Political parties contest elections and seek public support.
- National parties operate across multiple states.
- Regional parties primarily influence specific states or regions.
- Alliances often play a major role in government formation.
- Political parties shape legislation, governance, and public policy.
