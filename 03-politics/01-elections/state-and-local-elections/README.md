# State and Local Elections

## Overview

This section covers elections conducted at the state and local levels of government in India.

These elections determine representation in State Legislatures, Municipal Bodies, and Panchayati Raj Institutions.

## Quick Revision

- Vidhan Sabha elections elect Members of the Legislative Assembly (MLAs).
- Municipal elections elect representatives of urban local bodies.
- Panchayat elections elect representatives of rural local bodies.
- State and local elections strengthen democratic participation.
- Local governments play a major role in service delivery and development.
