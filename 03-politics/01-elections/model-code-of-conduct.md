# Model Code of Conduct (MCC)

## Overview

The Model Code of Conduct (MCC) is a set of guidelines issued by the Election Commission of India (ECI) to regulate the conduct of political parties, candidates, ministers, and governments during elections.

Its primary objective is to ensure free, fair, peaceful, and transparent elections.

## Purpose

The MCC seeks to:

- Ensure a level playing field for all political parties
- Prevent misuse of official machinery
- Maintain electoral integrity
- Promote ethical campaigning
- Protect voter confidence in the electoral process

## Historical Background

The Model Code of Conduct originated in Kerala during the 1960 elections.

Over time, it evolved into a nationwide framework administered by the Election Commission of India.

## Legal Status

The MCC is not a law enacted by Parliament.

However, the Election Commission enforces it using its constitutional powers under:

- Article 324 of the Constitution of India

Certain violations may also attract action under existing laws.

## Applicability

The MCC comes into force from the date the Election Commission announces the election schedule.

It remains in effect until completion of the election process.

## Major Provisions

### General Conduct

Political parties and candidates must:

- Avoid hate speech
- Avoid personal attacks
- Respect opponents
- Refrain from activities that increase social tensions

### Election Campaigns

Campaigns must:

- Follow prescribed rules
- Respect public order
- Avoid bribery and intimidation
- Avoid appeals based solely on caste or religion

### Meetings and Processions

Political parties must:

- Inform authorities in advance
- Maintain public order
- Avoid clashes with rival groups

### Polling Day Rules

Candidates and supporters must:

- Cooperate with election officials
- Avoid voter intimidation
- Follow polling station regulations

## Restrictions on Governments

Once the MCC comes into effect, governments cannot:

- Announce new schemes intended to influence voters
- Make major policy announcements with electoral impact
- Use public funds for campaign purposes
- Misuse official machinery for political advantage

## Role of the Election Commission

The Election Commission:

- Monitors compliance
- Issues notices
- Investigates complaints
- Directs corrective action
- Can restrict campaign activities in serious cases

## Importance

The MCC helps:

- Strengthen democracy
- Ensure fair competition
- Increase transparency
- Prevent abuse of power
- Protect voter freedom

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Social media misinformation
- Hate speech during campaigns
- Monitoring large-scale elections
- Ensuring uniform compliance
