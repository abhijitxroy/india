
# Rajya Sabha Election

## Overview

A Rajya Sabha Election is the process through which Members of the Rajya Sabha are elected.

Unlike Lok Sabha and Vidhan Sabha elections, citizens do not directly elect Rajya Sabha members.

Most Rajya Sabha members are elected by elected Members of Legislative Assemblies (MLAs) of the states.

The Rajya Sabha is the Upper House of Parliament and is also known as the Council of States.

---

## Why Rajya Sabha Exists

India follows a bicameral parliamentary system.

Parliament consists of:

- Lok Sabha (House of the People)
- Rajya Sabha (Council of States)

The Rajya Sabha provides representation to states in the national legislative process.

---

## What Is the Rajya Sabha?

The Rajya Sabha is the Upper House of Parliament.

Its purpose includes:

- Representing states
- Reviewing legislation
- Participating in law-making
- Providing continuity in Parliament

---

## Unique Feature

Unlike the Lok Sabha:

```text
Lok Sabha
      ↓
Citizens Elect MPs

Rajya Sabha
      ↓
MLAs Elect Members
```

Citizens do not vote directly in Rajya Sabha elections.

---

## How Rajya Sabha Elections Work

```text
Citizens
     ↓
Elect MLAs
     ↓
MLAs Vote
     ↓
Rajya Sabha Members Elected
```

This creates an indirect election process.

---

## Election Method

Rajya Sabha members are elected using:

### Proportional Representation (PR)

and

### Single Transferable Vote (STV)

This system allows representation of multiple political parties according to their strength in the Legislative Assembly.

---

## Single Transferable Vote (STV)

Under STV:

- MLAs rank candidates by preference.
- Votes are counted according to established rules.
- Seats are allocated through a proportional system.

The objective is to provide broader representation.

---

## Term of Members

Rajya Sabha is a permanent house.

It is never dissolved.

Members normally serve:

```text
6 Years
```

Every two years, approximately one-third of the members retire and elections are held for those seats.

---

## Nominated Members

In addition to elected members, certain members may be nominated according to constitutional provisions.

These nominations recognize distinguished contributions in various fields.

---

## Why Rajya Sabha Is Important

The Rajya Sabha:

- Represents states
- Reviews legislation
- Participates in law-making
- Provides continuity in Parliament
- Contributes to federal governance

It plays an important role in India's parliamentary democracy.

---

## Rajya Sabha vs Lok Sabha

| Rajya Sabha | Lok Sabha |
|----------|----------|
| Upper House | Lower House |
| Council of States | House of the People |
| Indirect election | Direct election |
| Permanent House | Can be dissolved |
| Members serve six years | Members normally serve five years |

---

## Rajya Sabha and Federalism

```text
States
    ↓
MLAs
    ↓
Rajya Sabha
    ↓
Parliament
```

The Rajya Sabha strengthens India's federal structure by giving states a voice in national legislation.

---

## Common Misconceptions

### Citizens Do Not Directly Elect Rajya Sabha Members

Most members are elected by MLAs.

### Rajya Sabha Is Not a Permanent Membership Body

The House is permanent, but individual members retire and are replaced.

### Rajya Sabha Is Not Less Important Than Lok Sabha

Both houses play important constitutional roles.
