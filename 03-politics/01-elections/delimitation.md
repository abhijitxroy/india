# Delimitation

## Overview

Delimitation is the process of fixing the boundaries of electoral constituencies for elections to the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies.

Its objective is to ensure fair representation by maintaining a reasonable balance between population and the number of elected representatives.

Delimitation is an important component of India's democratic system.

## Meaning

Delimitation involves:

- Redrawing constituency boundaries
- Reorganizing electoral constituencies
- Allocating seats among regions based on population data

The process helps maintain the principle of equal representation.

## Constitutional Basis

Delimitation is provided under:

- Article 82 of the Constitution of India
- Article 170 of the Constitution of India

These provisions enable Parliament to enact laws for delimitation after each Census.

## Delimitation Commission

The Delimitation Commission is responsible for carrying out the delimitation exercise.

It is an independent high-powered body.

### Composition

The commission generally consists of:

- Retired Supreme Court Judge (Chairperson)
- Chief Election Commissioner or nominee
- State Election Commissioners of concerned states

## Functions

The commission performs the following functions:

- Determine constituency boundaries
- Allocate seats to constituencies
- Identify reserved constituencies
- Rationalize representation based on population changes

## Delimitation Acts

Major Delimitation Commissions have been constituted under laws enacted in:

- 1952
- 1962
- 1972
- 2002

The most recent nationwide delimitation exercise was based on the 2001 Census.

## Freeze on Delimitation

To encourage population control measures, delimitation based on population changes was frozen for a period.

### Key Developments

- Freeze initially extended until 2001
- Later extended until 2026 through constitutional amendments

As a result, the number of seats allocated to states has largely remained unchanged despite population shifts.

## Reserved Constituencies

The Delimitation Commission determines constituencies reserved for:

- Scheduled Castes (SCs)
- Scheduled Tribes (STs)

Reservation is based on demographic distribution.

## Importance

Delimitation helps:

- Ensure equal representation
- Reflect population changes
- Strengthen democratic legitimacy
- Improve electoral fairness
- Maintain balanced constituency sizes

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

- Population disparities among states
- Political sensitivities
- Regional representation concerns
- Balancing demographic changes with federal interests

## Contemporary Relevance

Delimitation is an important topic in discussions relating to:

- Representation in Parliament
- Federal balance
- Population trends
- Electoral reforms

The issue is expected to gain increased attention after the expiry of the current freeze period.
