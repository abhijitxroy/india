# National Elections

## Overview

This section covers elections conducted at the national level in India.

National elections determine representation in Parliament and play a central role in the formation of the Union Government.

## Quick Revision

- Lok Sabha members are directly elected by citizens.
- Rajya Sabha members are elected through an indirect electoral process.
- National elections determine the composition of Parliament.
- The majority in Lok Sabha usually forms the Union Government.
- National elections shape policy direction and governance priorities.
