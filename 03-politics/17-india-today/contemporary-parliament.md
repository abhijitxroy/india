
# Contemporary Parliament of India

## Overview

The Contemporary Parliament of India is the supreme legislative institution of the Republic of India and remains the central forum for law-making, debate, accountability, and representation in the modern democratic system.

Parliament reflects the will of the people through elected representatives and plays a crucial role in shaping public policy, governance, national development, and constitutional democracy.

Understanding the contemporary Parliament helps explain how India functions politically in the 21st century.

---

## Why Study Contemporary Parliament?

Studying Parliament helps understand:

- Law-making process
- Democratic governance
- Executive accountability
- Public policy formation
- Political representation
- Constitutional functioning

It is one of the most important institutions in India's democracy.

---

## Parliamentary Framework

```text
Citizens
      ↓
Elections
      ↓
Members of Parliament
      ↓
Parliament
      ↓
Laws & Governance
```

Parliament acts as the link between citizens and the government.

---

## Structure of Parliament

### President of India

- Constitutional component of Parliament
- Assents to legislation
- Performs parliamentary functions defined by the Constitution

### Lok Sabha

- House of the People
- Directly elected representatives
- Central role in government formation and financial legislation

### Rajya Sabha

- Council of States
- Represents states and union territories
- Provides legislative review and continuity

---

## Major Functions

### Law Making

- Debates and passes legislation
- Amends existing laws
- Reviews public policy proposals

### Accountability

- Questions the government
- Reviews executive actions
- Examines public expenditure

### Representation

- Represents citizens and states
- Raises public concerns
- Facilitates democratic participation

### Financial Control

- Approves budgets
- Reviews government spending
- Oversees financial accountability
