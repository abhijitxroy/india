# Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)

## Overview

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) is one of India's major national political parties.

It is currently one of the most influential political organizations in the country and has played a significant role in national and state politics.

The BJP traces its origins to the Bharatiya Jana Sangh.

## Historical Background

### Bharatiya Jana Sangh

The BJP's roots lie in the Bharatiya Jana Sangh, which was founded in 1951.

The Jana Sangh became an important opposition force during the early decades after independence.

---

### Janata Party Period

Following the Emergency (1975–1977), several opposition parties merged to form the Janata Party.

Former Jana Sangh leaders became part of this political movement.

---

### Formation of BJP

In 1980, the Bharatiya Janata Party was established.

The party gradually expanded its organizational structure and electoral presence across India.
