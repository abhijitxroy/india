# Indian Land-Based Missiles

Missile systems operated from land-based platforms.

## Ballistic Missiles

### Prithvi Series

The Prithvi family is India's early indigenous surface-to-surface ballistic missile family.

See the historical missile-development material in the strategic missile documentation.

## Other Missile Systems

Land-based missile systems include surface-to-surface, surface-to-air, anti-tank and other specialised systems.

## Missile Development

India's missile development began with early rocket and missile programmes and expanded significantly through the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP).

Key families include:
- Prithvi
- Agni
- Akash
- Nag
- Trishul

Detailed historical development should be maintained as part of India's missile-development history.
