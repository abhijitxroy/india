## How to Read These Journeys

These files explain the **story and evolution** of India's defence capabilities chronologically.

Detailed technical information remains in the corresponding topic directories:

- `air/` — Air Force, aircraft, helicopters, radars and air-defence systems
- `land/` — Army, tanks, artillery, infantry systems and land radars
- `navy/` — Surface fleet, submarines, naval aviation, weapons and sensors
- `industry/` — Defence companies, DRDO, shipyards and production ecosystem
- `organisations/` — Ministry, command structures and defence agencies
- `budget/` — Defence budget, procurement and modernisation
- `strategic/` — Missiles, nuclear systems, BMD, S-400 and space/ASAT
