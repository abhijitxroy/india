# Indian Military History

Military history explains how India's armed forces, military doctrine, strategic thinking and defence capabilities evolved from independence to the present.

## Main Areas

- Wars
- Military Operations
- Counter-Terror Operations
- Humanitarian Missions
- Peacekeeping

## Major Wars

| Year | Conflict | Why Important |
|---|---|---|
| 1947–48 | First Indo-Pak War | First major war after independence |
| 1962 | Sino-Indian War | Exposed defence weaknesses and triggered reforms |
| 1965 | Indo-Pak War | Tested India's post-1962 military recovery |
| 1971 | Indo-Pak War | Led to creation of Bangladesh |
| 1999 | Kargil War | Major high-altitude conflict |

## Why Study Military History?

Military history provides lessons about:

- Strategy
- Leadership
- Logistics
- Intelligence
- Technology
- Military preparedness
- Joint operations
- National security
