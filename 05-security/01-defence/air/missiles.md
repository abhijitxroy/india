# Indian Air-Launched Missiles

Missile systems launched from aircraft.

## Air-to-Air

### Astra

India's indigenous air-to-air missile family.

## Air-to-Surface

Indian air-launched strike missile systems.

## Anti-Radiation

### Rudram

India's anti-radiation missile family designed to target enemy radar and other electromagnetic emitters.

## Cruise Missiles

### BrahMos

BrahMos has an air-launched variant and is therefore relevant to Indian air power as well as land and naval forces.
