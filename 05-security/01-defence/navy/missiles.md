# Indian Naval Missiles

Missile systems operated from naval platforms.

## Submarine-Launched

### K-Series

The K-series is India's family of submarine-launched ballistic missiles.

Important systems include:
- K-15 / Sagarika
- K-4

## Anti-Ship and Cruise Missiles

### BrahMos

BrahMos is deployed from naval platforms for anti-ship and land-attack missions.
