# Indian Naval Weapons

Weapons used by the Indian Navy for surface warfare, underwater warfare, air defence, maritime strike and close-range protection.

## Main Categories

- Naval guns
- Anti-ship missiles
- Surface-to-air missiles
- Anti-submarine weapons
- Torpedoes
- Close-in weapon systems
- Naval rockets and other ship-launched weapons

## Why Naval Weapons Matter

Naval weapons allow warships and submarines to:

- Defend themselves
- Engage hostile ships
- Defend against aircraft and missiles
- Conduct anti-submarine warfare
- Support maritime strike operations
- Protect important sea areas

## Major Weapon Roles

| Category | Main Role |
|---|---|
| Naval guns | Surface combat and close/medium-range fire support |
| Anti-ship missiles | Attack surface ships |
| Surface-to-air missiles | Air defence |
| Torpedoes | Anti-submarine and anti-surface warfare |
| Anti-submarine weapons | Detect and engage submarines |
| CIWS | Last-line defence against incoming threats |

## Learning Path

1. Understand naval guns
2. Understand anti-ship missiles
3. Understand naval air defence
4. Understand torpedoes
5. Understand anti-submarine warfare
6. Understand CIWS
7. Study weapons used on major Indian warships and submarines

## Quick Revision

- Naval weapons provide a ship's combat power.
- Missiles provide long-range strike and air-defence capability.
- Guns provide direct fire capability.
- Torpedoes are important for underwater warfare.
- CIWS provides close-range defence against incoming threats.
