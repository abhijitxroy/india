# Special Protection Group (SPG)

## Overview

The Special Protection Group (SPG) is an elite security force responsible for providing proximate security to the Prime Minister of India.

It was established to provide highly specialized protection against security threats and is considered one of the most professional VIP protection organizations in the country.

SPG protection is separate from the normal X, Y, Y+, Z, and Z+ security classification system.

## Background

The assassination of Prime Minister Indira Gandhi in 1984 highlighted the need for a dedicated and highly trained protection force for the Prime Minister.

Following security reviews and recommendations, the Special Protection Group was established in 1985.

The SPG Act was enacted in 1988 to provide a legal framework for its operations.

## Responsibilities

### Prime Minister's Security

The primary responsibility of SPG is to provide close protection to the Prime Minister of India.

This includes security at residences, offices, public events, domestic travel, and foreign visits.

### Threat Prevention

SPG personnel are trained to identify, assess, and respond to security threats.

Their role includes preventive security measures as well as emergency response.

### Advance Security Planning

Before major events or visits, SPG conducts detailed security planning and coordination.

This involves working with state police, intelligence agencies, and other security organizations.

### Security Coordination

SPG works closely with:

- Intelligence Bureau (IB)
- State Police Forces
- Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs)
- National Security Guard (NSG)
- Other Government Agencies

## Selection and Training

SPG personnel are selected from experienced officers of various police and security organizations.

They receive specialized training in:

- VIP protection
- Counter-assault operations
- Firearms handling
- Threat assessment
- Emergency response
- Advanced driving and mobility operations

## Historical Significance

The creation of SPG marked a major development in India's VIP security framework.

It introduced a dedicated, professional, and specialized approach to protecting the country's highest executive office.

The organization has become an important component of India's internal security architecture.
