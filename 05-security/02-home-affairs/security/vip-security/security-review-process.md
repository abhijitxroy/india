# Security Review Process

## Overview

VIP security cover is not permanent in all cases. Security arrangements are periodically reviewed to determine whether the threat level has increased, decreased, or remained unchanged.

The review process helps ensure that security resources are used efficiently while maintaining adequate protection.

## Objectives

- Assess current threat perception
- Identify emerging risks
- Modify security arrangements when required
- Prevent misuse of security resources
- Ensure protection is proportional to threat level

## Factors Considered

Authorities may examine:

- Intelligence inputs
- Threat assessments
- Activities of extremist or criminal groups
- Public profile of the protectee
- Recent incidents or attacks
- Political, social, or security developments

## Threat Assessment Process

### Intelligence Collection

Information is gathered from:

- Intelligence Bureau (IB)
- State Intelligence Units
- Police agencies
- Central security agencies

### Risk Evaluation

Authorities evaluate:

- Credibility of threats
- Capability of threat actors
- Vulnerabilities of the protectee
- Geographic risk factors

### Recommendation Stage

Security agencies recommend:

- Continuation of existing cover
- Upgradation of security cover
- Downgradation of security cover
- Withdrawal of security cover

## Role of Intelligence Bureau

The Intelligence Bureau plays a major role in:

- Threat perception analysis
- Security recommendations
- Periodic reviews of central protectees
- Coordination with other agencies

## Review Outcomes

### Upgradation

Security cover may be increased when:

- Threat level rises
- New intelligence inputs emerge
- Public exposure increases significantly

### Continuation

Existing security arrangements remain unchanged when threat levels remain stable.

### Downgradation

Security cover may be reduced if the threat level decreases.

### Withdrawal

Security cover may be withdrawn when threat perception is no longer significant.

## Frequency of Reviews

Reviews may be conducted:

- Periodically at fixed intervals
- After major incidents
- Following new intelligence inputs
- Upon requests from competent authorities

## Challenges

- Rapidly changing threat environments
- Balancing security and public accessibility
- Political controversies regarding security cover
- Resource constraints
- Accurate threat prediction
