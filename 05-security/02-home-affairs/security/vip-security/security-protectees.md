# Security Protectees

## Overview

A protectee is an individual who receives official security protection because of a perceived threat to life, safety, or public role.

Security cover is granted based on threat assessment rather than position alone.

## Who Can Receive Security Cover

Security protection may be provided to:

- Constitutional authorities
- Political leaders
- Ministers
- Judges
- Senior government officials
- Witnesses under protection schemes
- Individuals facing serious security threats
- Former public office holders in specific circumstances

## Constitutional Authorities

Certain high offices receive security protection because of their constitutional responsibilities.

Examples include:

- President of India
- Vice President of India
- Prime Minister of India
- Governors

## Political Protectees

Security may be provided to:

- Union ministers
- Chief ministers
- Members of Parliament
- Political leaders facing credible threats

The level of protection depends on threat perception.

## Judicial Protectees

Protection may be granted to:

- Judges of higher courts
- Judicial officers facing threats
- Individuals connected to sensitive cases

## Special Category Protectees

Security cover may also be provided to:

- Key witnesses
- Individuals targeted by terrorist groups
- Persons facing organised crime threats
- Individuals under exceptional risk conditions

## Basis of Security Allocation

Security is generally determined through:

- Threat perception reports
- Intelligence assessments
- Security reviews
- Recommendations from competent agencies

Protection is not automatically linked to status or rank.

## Security Agencies Involved

Depending on the category of protectee, security may be provided by:

- Special Protection Group (SPG) where legally applicable
- Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)
- Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)
- National Security Guard (NSG)
- State Police
- Other specialised units

## Review and Modification

Security cover may be:

- Upgraded
- Continued
- Downgraded
- Withdrawn

These decisions are based on periodic threat assessments.

## Challenges

- Balancing security and accessibility
- Resource allocation
- Managing changing threat perceptions
- Political controversies regarding security cover
- Coordination among agencies
