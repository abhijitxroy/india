# Police in India

## National Importance

The police system is the primary law enforcement mechanism in India and plays a crucial role in maintaining law and order, preventing crime, protecting citizens, and supporting the justice system.

Police forces operate at the state level and form the backbone of internal security and public safety.

## What is the Police System?

The police system consists of state police forces responsible for maintaining public order, investigating crimes, enforcing laws, managing traffic, and supporting emergency response.
