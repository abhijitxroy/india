# Naxalism and Insurgency in India

## National Importance

Naxalism and insurgency have been among the most significant internal security challenges faced by India.

These movements affect governance, development, public safety, and national security, particularly in vulnerable and geographically difficult regions.

## What is Naxalism?

Naxalism refers to an armed extremist movement inspired by Maoist ideology that seeks to challenge state authority through violent means.

The movement originated from the Naxalbari uprising in West Bengal in 1967.

---

## What is Insurgency?

Insurgency refers to organized armed movements that challenge the authority of the state for political, ideological, ethnic, or regional objectives.
