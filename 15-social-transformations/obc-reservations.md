

# OBC Reservations

## Overview

OBC (Other Backward Classes) Reservations are policies designed to improve representation and opportunities for socially and educationally backward groups.

The policy emerged as an important component of India's social justice framework and remains closely linked with debates on representation, equality, affirmative action, and public policy.

OBC reservations are among the most significant social transformation measures in modern India.

---

## Why Study OBC Reservations?

Studying OBC Reservations helps understand:

- Social justice policies
- Reservation systems
- Representation in institutions
- Public policy development
- Constitutional governance
- Contemporary political debates

The policy has significantly influenced governance, elections, and social transformation.
