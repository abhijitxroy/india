

# Women Reservation

## Overview

Women Reservation refers to measures aimed at improving the representation and participation of women in political institutions, governance structures, and decision-making processes.

The subject has been an important part of discussions on gender equality, democratic participation, social justice, and inclusive governance in India.

It is one of the most significant topics in the study of social and political transformation.

---

## Why Study Women Reservation?

Studying Women Reservation helps understand:

- Gender equality
- Political representation
- Democratic participation
- Social justice
- Governance reforms
- Women's empowerment

The topic remains important in contemporary governance and policy debates.
