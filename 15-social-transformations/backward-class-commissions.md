# Backward Class Commissions

## Overview

Backward Class Commissions are bodies established to study the conditions of socially and educationally disadvantaged groups and recommend measures to improve representation, inclusion, and opportunities.

These commissions have played an important role in shaping reservation policies, social justice initiatives, and public policy debates in India.

They are central to understanding the evolution of affirmative action and representation policies.

---

## Why Study Backward Class Commissions?

Studying these commissions helps understand:

- Social justice policies
- Reservation systems
- Public policy development
- Representation debates
- Constitutional governance
- Social transformation in India

Their recommendations have significantly influenced governance and politics.
