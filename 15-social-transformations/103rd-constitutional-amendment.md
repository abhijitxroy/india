

# 103rd Constitutional Amendment

## Overview

The 103rd Constitutional Amendment is a significant constitutional reform that introduced provisions relating to reservation for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS).

The amendment became an important milestone in debates on social justice, affirmative action, equality, and public policy in India.

It is one of the most important constitutional changes relating to reservation policy in recent decades.

---

Studying the amendment helps understand:

- Constitutional reforms
- Reservation policy
- Social justice
- Equality debates
- Public policy evolution

The amendment remains a major topic in constitutional and political discussions.
