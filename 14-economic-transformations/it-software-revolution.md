

# IT and Software Revolution

## Overview

The IT and Software Revolution transformed India into one of the world's leading technology and software service hubs.

The growth of software exports, information technology services, outsourcing, innovation, and digital businesses significantly contributed to economic growth, job creation, and global recognition.

Today, the IT sector is one of the most important pillars of India's economy.

---

## Why Study the IT Revolution?

Studying the IT Revolution helps understand:

- Economic transformation
- Technology-driven growth
- Globalization
- Service sector expansion
- Employment generation
- Digital economy development

It is one of the most successful economic transformations in modern India.
