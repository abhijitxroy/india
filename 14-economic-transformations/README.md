

# Economic Transformations in India

## Overview

This section covers the major economic transformations that changed India's agricultural systems, economic structure, institutions, technology ecosystem, financial inclusion model, manufacturing capabilities, and digital economy.

The focus is on understanding:

- Why the transformation happened
- What problems it solved
- How India changed afterwards
- Long-term economic impact
- Governance and policy significance

---

## Why This Section Matters

India's economic journey can be understood through a series of major transformations.

```text
Agriculture
      ↓
Planning Economy
      ↓
Economic Reforms
      ↓
Technology Growth
      ↓
Digital Economy
      ↓
Modern India
```

Understanding these transformations explains how India evolved into one of the world's largest economies.
