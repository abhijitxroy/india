

# Aadhaar and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

## Overview

Aadhaar and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) together transformed how welfare benefits and government subsidies are delivered in India.

By linking beneficiaries with digital identity and banking systems, the government sought to improve efficiency, reduce leakages, and ensure benefits reach intended recipients.

This is one of the most significant governance and economic transformations in modern India.

---

## Why Study Aadhaar and DBT?

Studying Aadhaar and DBT helps understand:

- Welfare delivery
- Digital governance
- Financial inclusion
- Public administration
- Subsidy reforms
- Digital public infrastructure

The initiative changed how government services are delivered at scale.

## Main Objectives

The framework aims to:

- Improve benefit delivery
- Reduce leakages and duplication
- Strengthen beneficiary identification
- Increase transparency
- Expand financial inclusion

---

## How DBT Works

```text
Government Scheme
         ↓
Verified Beneficiary
         ↓
Bank Account
         ↓
Direct Transfer
```

Benefits are transferred directly to eligible beneficiaries through the banking system.
