

# Telecom Revolution

## Overview

The Telecom Revolution transformed communication, connectivity, business, and economic activity across India.

The rapid expansion of mobile networks, internet access, affordable communication services, and digital connectivity connected millions of citizens and became a foundation for India's digital economy.

It is one of the most significant technological and economic transformations in modern India.

---

## Why Study the Telecom Revolution?

Studying the Telecom Revolution helps understand:

- Economic modernization
- Digital connectivity
- Technology adoption
- Communication infrastructure
- Digital economy growth
- Social and economic transformation

The telecom sector enabled many of India's later digital initiatives.

---
