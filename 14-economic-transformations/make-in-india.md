

# Make in India

## Overview

Make in India is a major economic initiative aimed at strengthening manufacturing, attracting investment, promoting innovation, and creating employment opportunities in India.

The initiative seeks to position India as a global manufacturing hub while supporting industrial growth and economic development.

It is one of the most important manufacturing-focused transformation programs in modern India.

---

## Why Study Make in India?

Studying Make in India helps understand:

- Industrial development
- Manufacturing growth
- Investment promotion
- Employment generation
- Economic modernization
- Global competitiveness

The initiative plays an important role in India's long-term economic strategy.
