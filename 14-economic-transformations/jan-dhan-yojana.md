

# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is a major financial inclusion initiative launched to expand access to banking services for individuals who were previously outside the formal financial system.

The program helped connect millions of people to bank accounts and became a foundation for digital payments, Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT), and financial inclusion reforms.

It is one of the most significant financial transformations in modern India.

---

Studying Jan Dhan Yojana helps understand:

The initiative transformed how citizens interact with the formal financial system.
