

# Green Revolution

## Overview

The Green Revolution refers to a period of agricultural transformation that significantly increased food grain production in India through improved farming techniques, better irrigation, modern inputs, and high-yield crop varieties.

It helped India move from food shortages toward greater food self-sufficiency and became one of the most important economic transformations in independent India.

---

## Why Study the Green Revolution?

Studying the Green Revolution helps understand:

- Agricultural development
- Food security
- Rural economy
- Economic modernization
- Technological adoption in farming
- Development policy

It transformed both agriculture and the broader Indian economy.
