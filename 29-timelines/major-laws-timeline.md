
# Major Laws Timeline

## Overview

This timeline highlights major laws and legislative milestones that significantly shaped India's governance, economy, society, rights framework, and democratic institutions.

The objective is to help readers understand how legislation has influenced the development of modern India.

---

## Why It Matters

Understanding major laws helps explain:

- How governance evolved
- How citizen rights were protected
- How economic reforms were implemented
- How institutions gained legal foundations
- How public policy changed over time

---

## Major Laws Timeline

| Year | Law / Constitutional Change | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1950 | Constitution of India Comes Into Force | Foundation of the Republic |
| 1951 | First Constitutional Amendment | Addressed early constitutional challenges |
| 1955 | Hindu Marriage Act | Major social reform legislation |
| 1961 | Dowry Prohibition Act | Addressed social injustice |
| 1971 | Abolition of Privy Purses | End of former princely privileges |
| 1976 | 42nd Constitutional Amendment | Significant constitutional changes |
| 1985 | Anti-Defection Law | Political stability and party discipline |
| 2005 | Right to Information Act | Increased transparency and accountability |
| 2005 | MGNREGA | Employment guarantee framework |
| 2009 | Right to Education Act | Expansion of educational rights |
| 2013 | Companies Act | Modern corporate governance framework |
| 2016 | Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code | Business resolution reforms |
| 2017 | Goods and Services Tax (GST) | Unified indirect taxation system |
| 2019 | Consumer Protection Act | Strengthened consumer rights |
| 2023 | Digital Personal Data Protection Act | Modern data governance framework |
