
# Balakot Air Strike

## Why It Matters

The Balakot Air Strike was one of the most significant air-power operations in modern Indian military history.

Conducted in 2019 following the Pulwama terror attack, the operation demonstrated India's willingness to use air power as part of its counter-terrorism response framework.

The strike became an important milestone in the evolution of India's modern security doctrine, strategic signaling and military response options.

## Background

In February 2019, a major terrorist attack occurred in Pulwama, Jammu and Kashmir.

The attack resulted in significant casualties and generated major national-security concerns.

Following intelligence assessments and strategic deliberations, India conducted an air strike targeting terrorist infrastructure in Balakot.

---

## Objectives

- Target terrorist infrastructure
- Strengthen deterrence
- Demonstrate response capability
- Support national security objectives
