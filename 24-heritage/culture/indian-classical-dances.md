# Indian Classical Dances

## Why It Matters

Indian classical dances are among the oldest surviving performing arts traditions in the world.

They preserve India's cultural heritage, religious traditions, storytelling methods, music, literature, and artistic expression across different regions of the country.

## What are Indian Classical Dances?

Indian classical dances are highly structured dance traditions that combine movement, rhythm, facial expressions, music, and storytelling.

Most forms evolved in temples, royal courts, and cultural institutions.
