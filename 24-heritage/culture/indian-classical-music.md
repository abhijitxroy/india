# Indian Classical Music

## Why It Matters

Indian classical music is one of the oldest living musical traditions in the world.

It represents centuries of artistic, spiritual, and cultural development and has influenced music, literature, dance, and performing arts across the Indian subcontinent.

## What is Indian Classical Music?

Indian classical music is a structured musical tradition based on melody (Raga) and rhythm (Tala).

It emphasizes improvisation, expression, discipline, and spiritual connection.
