
# Economic Reforms Timeline

## Overview

This timeline explains the major economic reforms that shaped India's economic development from Independence to the present day.

The objective is to help readers understand how policy changes transformed India's economy, industries, trade, financial system, and growth trajectory.

---

## Historical Significance

Understanding economic reforms helps explain:

- How India's economy evolved
- Why reforms were introduced
- How liberalization changed business and trade
- How economic growth accelerated
- How reforms affected citizens and industries

---

## Economic Reforms Timeline

| Year | Reform / Event | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1947 | Independence | Beginning of economic planning for a new nation |
| 1951 | First Five-Year Plan | Planned economic development begins |
| 1969 | Bank Nationalization | Expansion of banking access and state control |
| 1970s | Green Revolution | Major increase in agricultural productivity |
| 1980s | Early Liberalization Measures | Gradual opening of the economy |
| 1991 | Economic Liberalization | Major reforms in trade, industry, and investment |
| 2000s | IT & Services Expansion | Rapid growth of technology and services sectors |
| 2005 | Rural Employment Programs Expansion | Increased social welfare initiatives |
| 2014 | Digital Governance Push | Expansion of digital public infrastructure |
| 2016 | Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code | Corporate resolution framework strengthened |
| 2017 | Goods and Services Tax (GST) | Unified indirect tax system |
| 2020 | Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) Schemes | Manufacturing growth initiatives |
