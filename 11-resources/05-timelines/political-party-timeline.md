# Political Party Timeline

## Overview

This timeline explains the evolution of India's political party system from the independence movement era to the modern multi-party democratic landscape.

The objective is to help readers understand how political parties developed, competed, formed governments, and shaped Indian democracy.

---

## Historical Significance

Understanding political party evolution helps explain:

- How democratic competition developed
- How governments are formed
- How coalition politics emerged
- How political representation changed over time
- How electoral politics evolved

---

## Political Party Timeline

| Year | Event | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1885 | Indian National Congress Founded | Beginning of organized national political activity |
| 1906 | All-India Muslim League Founded | Major political development during colonial period |
| 1925 | RSS Founded | Influential socio-cultural organization |
| 1951 | Bharatiya Jana Sangh Founded | Important post-independence political party |
| 1952 | First General Election | Beginning of electoral democracy |
| 1967 | Rise of Regional Parties | Reduced single-party dominance in several states |
| 1977 | Janata Party Government | First non-Congress government at the Centre |
| 1980 | Bharatiya Janata Party Founded | Successor to Jana Sangh tradition |
| 1989 | Coalition Era Begins | Expansion of multi-party politics |
| 1990s | Regional Parties Gain Influence | Stronger federal political participation |
| 1998-99 | Stable Coalition Governments | Coalition governance matures |
| 2014 | Majority Government Returns | Significant shift in national politics |
