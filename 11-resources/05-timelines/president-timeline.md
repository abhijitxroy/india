
# President Timeline

## Overview

This timeline explains the succession of Presidents of India from 1950 to the present day.

The objective is to help readers understand the evolution of India's highest constitutional office and its role in the democratic system.

---

## Historical Significance

Understanding the President timeline helps explain:

- Evolution of constitutional governance
- Continuity of the Republic
- Major constitutional milestones
- Ceremonial and constitutional leadership
- Stability of democratic institutions

---

## President Timeline

| Period | President | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1950-1962 | Dr. Rajendra Prasad | First President of India |
| 1962-1967 | Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan | Scholar and philosopher President |
| 1967-1969 | Dr. Zakir Husain | First Muslim President of India |
| 1969-1974 | V. V. Giri | Served during major political transitions |
| 1974-1977 | Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed | President during the Emergency period |
| 1977-1982 | Neelam Sanjiva Reddy | First unopposed President |
| 1982-1987 | Giani Zail Singh | First Sikh President |
| 1987-1992 | R. Venkataraman | Served during coalition emergence |
| 1992-1997 | Shankar Dayal Sharma | Constitutional continuity period |
| 1997-2002 | K. R. Narayanan | First Dalit President |
| 2002-2007 | A. P. J. Abdul Kalam | Popular scientist-President |
| 2007-2012 | Pratibha Patil | First woman President |
| 2012-2017 | Pranab Mukherjee | Veteran parliamentarian |
| 2017-2022 | Ram Nath Kovind | Constitutional and social inclusion focus |
| 2022-Present | Droupadi Murmu | First tribal President of India |
