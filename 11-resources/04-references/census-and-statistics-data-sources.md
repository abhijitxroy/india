# Census & Statistics Data Sources

## India — Primary Sources

### Census of India — Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner

- **URL:** https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en
- **Data:** Population, literacy, migration, housing, urbanization, language, religion, workers, households
- **Coverage:** India, States/UTs, districts, sub-districts, towns, villages and wards
- **Use:** Primary source for Census-based demographic and socioeconomic analysis

### Census Data

- **URL:** https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en/data
- **Data:** Census tables, API access, visualizations, microdata and Census products
- **Use:** Main access point for downloading and working with Census data

### Census Population Finder

- **URL:** https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en/data/population-finder
- **Data:** Primary Census Abstract indicators for districts, sub-districts, towns, villages and wards
- **Use:** Geographic-level population analysis

### Census Digital Library

- **URL:** https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en/data/digital-library
- **Data:** Historical Census publications and documents
- **Use:** Historical demographic research

### District Census Handbooks

- **URL:** https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en/data/handbooks
- **Data:** District-level demographic, socioeconomic and administrative information
- **Use:** District-level analysis and historical research

## National Statistics — MoSPI / NSO

### Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation

- **URL:** https://mospi.gov.in/
- **Data:** National accounts, economic statistics, social statistics and official sample surveys
- **Use:** Primary source for India's official statistical system

### eSankhyiki

- **URL:** https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/
- **Data:** Official MoSPI datasets, statistical indicators and data catalogue
- **Use:** Main portal for finding and downloading official statistical datasets

### MoSPI Data & Visualizations

- **URL:** https://mospi.gov.in/web/mospi/data
- **Data:** eSankhyiki, microdata, visualizations and statistical resources
- **Use:** Access point for MoSPI statistical products

### National Sample Survey (NSS)

- **URL:** https://mospi.gov.in/
- **Data:** Large-scale socioeconomic sample surveys covering subjects such as consumption, employment, education, health and household conditions
- **Use:** Survey-based socioeconomic analysis

### Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)

- **URL:** https://mospi.gov.in/
- **Data:** Labour force participation, employment, unemployment and worker characteristics
- **Use:** Labour-market analysis

### Annual Survey of Industries (ASI)

- **URL:** https://mospi.gov.in/
- **Data:** Industrial establishments, employment, wages, capital, output and manufacturing
- **Use:** Industrial and manufacturing analysis

## Government Open Data

### Open Government Data Platform India

- **URL:** https://www.data.gov.in/
- **Data:** Datasets and APIs published by Central and State Government departments
- **Use:** Discovering government datasets and APIs
- **Priority:** Prefer the originating ministry/department when the same data is available there

## Health & Demography

### National Family Health Survey (NFHS)

- **Organization:** International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS)
- **URL:** https://www.iipsindia.ac.in/content/national-family-health-survey
- **Data:** Fertility, mortality, family planning, maternal and child health, nutrition, anemia and reproductive health
- **Use:** Health, demographic and family-welfare analysis

---

## Population Projections

### Government of India — Population Projections

- **Source:** National Commission on Population / Technical Group
- **Report:** Population Projection Report 2011–2036
- **URL:** https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Population%20Projection%20Report%202011-2036%20-%20upload_compressed_0.pdf
- **Data:** Population projections for India, States and Union Territories
- **Coverage:** 2011–2036
- **Use:** Indian demographic projection and trend analysis
- **Note:** Use this as the Government of India projection series for its stated period; do not treat projections as Census observations

---

## Economic & Financial Statistics

### Reserve Bank of India — Database on Indian Economy (DBIE)

- **URL:** https://dbie.rbi.org.in/
- **Data:** GDP, prices, money and banking, interest rates, external sector, public finance and state-level economic and financial statistics
- **Use:** Economic and financial analysis

---

## International Cross-Check Sources

### UN World Population Prospects

- **URL:** https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/world-population-prospects-2024
- **Data:** Population estimates, demographic indicators and projections
- **Coverage:** Countries and areas worldwide; estimates from the 1950s to present and projections to 2100
- **Use:** International comparison and independent cross-check of Indian population data
- **Dataset:** World Population Prospects 2024

### World Bank — World Development Indicators

- **URL:** https://databank.worldbank.org/home/source/world-development-indicators
- **Data:** Population, GDP, income, health, education, labour, poverty and development indicators
- **Use:** Cross-country comparison and validation

---

## Secondary / Convenience Sources

### Macrotrends

- **URL:** https://www.macrotrends.net/global-metrics/countries/ind/india/population
- **Data:** Historical population and economic series
- **Use:** Quick exploration, visualization and sanity checks
- **Priority:** Secondary; verify important figures against primary sources before using them in analysis
