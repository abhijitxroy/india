# Railway Recruitment Board (RRB)

## Career Relevance

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) conducts recruitment examinations for various posts in Indian Railways.

It provides one of the largest employment opportunities in the public sector and supports the operation of one of the world's largest railway networks.

## What is RRB?

RRB is the recruitment agency responsible for selecting candidates for technical, non-technical, operational, and administrative positions in Indian Railways.

It conducts nationwide examinations for various categories of railway jobs.
