# State Public Service Commissions (State PSC)

## Career Relevance

State Public Service Commissions recruit officers and administrators for state government services.

They play a critical role in governance, public administration, revenue management, development programmes, and implementation of government policies at the state level.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | State Public Service Commission |
| Level | State Government |
| Purpose | Recruitment for State Services |
| Constitutional Basis | Articles 315–323 |
| Recruiting Authority | Respective State PSC |

---

## What is State PSC?

Every state can establish a Public Service Commission responsible for conducting examinations and recruiting candidates for various state government posts.

Examples include WBCS, TNPSC, KPSC, MPSC, BPSC, and UPPSC.

## State PSC vs UPSC

| Feature | State PSC | UPSC |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Level | State | National |
| Recruitment | State Services | All India and Central Services |
| Cadre | State Cadre | National Cadre |
| Authority | State PSC | UPSC |
