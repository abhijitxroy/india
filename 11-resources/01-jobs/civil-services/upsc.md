# Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)

## Career Relevance

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) is India's premier constitutional recruiting agency responsible for selecting candidates for the country's highest civil services and central government positions.

UPSC plays a critical role in building the administrative, diplomatic, police, and governance leadership of India.

## What is UPSC?

UPSC is an independent constitutional body responsible for conducting examinations and recruitment processes for various civil services and central government posts.

It ensures merit-based and transparent recruitment.

| Examination | Purpose |

## Civil Services Examination Process

```text

      ↓
Preliminary Examination
      ↓
Main Examination
      ↓

      ↓

```
