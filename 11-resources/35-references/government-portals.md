
# Government Portals

## Overview

This document lists important Government of India portals that help citizens access public services, official information, schemes, records, governance resources, and digital services.

---

## Why Use This Resource

Government portals help citizens:

- Access public services
- Verify official information
- Apply for government programs
- Track applications and benefits
- Interact with government institutions

---

## Major Government Portals

### National Portal of India

Single gateway to government information and services.

Useful For:

- Government departments
- Citizen services
- Public information
- National initiatives

---

### Digital India Platform

Supports digital governance and online public services.

Key Areas:

- Digital transformation
- Citizen services
- E-governance initiatives

---

### MyGov

Citizen participation platform.

Purpose:

- Public consultations
- Feedback collection
- Citizen engagement

---

### PM Gati Shakti

Integrated infrastructure planning platform.

Focus Areas:

- Infrastructure development
- Coordination among ministries
- Project monitoring

---

### Open Government Data Platform

Provides public datasets for research and analysis.

Useful For:

- Researchers
- Students
- Policy analysis
- Public transparency

---

| Category | Examples |
