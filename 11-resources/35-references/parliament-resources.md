
# Parliament Resources

## Overview

This document lists important parliamentary resources that help citizens understand India's legislative system, parliamentary procedures, law-making process, accountability mechanisms, and democratic governance.

---

## Why Use This Resource

Parliament is the central legislative institution of India.

Understanding parliamentary resources helps citizens learn:

- How laws are made
- How governments are held accountable
- How representatives function
- How national policies are debated
- How democratic governance operates

---

## Primary Parliamentary Resources

### Parliament of India

The supreme legislative body of the Union Government.

Key Areas:

- Lok Sabha
- Rajya Sabha
- Law Making

## Important Parliamentary Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| Bill | Proposed law |
| Act | Law passed by Parliament |
| Question Hour | Government accountability mechanism |
| Zero Hour | Urgent public issues discussion |
| Committee System | Detailed legislative examination |
| Money Bill | Financial legislation |
| Joint Sitting | Combined session of both Houses |
| Ordinance | Temporary law issued under constitutional provisions |
