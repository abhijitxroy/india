
# Governance Terminology

## Overview

Governance terminology consists of important words, concepts, and expressions used in government functioning, decision-making, public administration, accountability, transparency, public service delivery, and institutional management.

Understanding these terms helps explain how governments operate and how policies are translated into outcomes for citizens.

---

## Why Study Governance Terminology?

Studying governance terminology helps:

- Understand government functioning
- Learn public administration concepts
- Follow governance reforms
- Understand accountability mechanisms
- Build knowledge of policy implementation

---

## Core Governance Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Governance | Process of governing and decision-making |
| Government | Institution that administers a country |
| Public Administration | Management of public institutions and policies |
| Public Policy | Government action to address public issues |
| Institution | Organization created to perform public functions |
| Administration | Execution of government decisions |
| Regulation | Rule issued by an authority |
| Compliance | Following laws, rules, and standards |

---

## Accountability & Transparency Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Accountability | Responsibility for actions and decisions |
| Transparency | Openness in governance and decision-making |
| Audit | Examination of financial or administrative records |
| Oversight | Monitoring government activities |
| Public Disclosure | Sharing information with the public |
| Citizen Participation | Involvement of citizens in governance |
| Grievance Redressal | Resolving public complaints |

---

## Service Delivery Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Public Service | Service provided by government |
| Service Delivery | Providing services to citizens |
| Beneficiary | Person receiving government benefits |
| Welfare Scheme | Government program for public welfare |
| Subsidy | Government financial support |
| Monitoring | Tracking implementation progress |
| Evaluation | Assessing outcomes and performance |

---

## Governance Reform Terms

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| Good Governance | Effective, accountable, and transparent governance |
| Administrative Reform | Improvement of government systems |
| E-Governance | Use of technology in governance |
| Digital Governance | Delivery of services through digital systems |
| Citizen-Centric Governance | Governance focused on citizens' needs |
| Decentralization | Transfer of powers to lower levels |
| Cooperative Federalism | Cooperation between Centre and States |

---

## Quick Revision

- Governance = Process of governing.
- Public Policy = Government action for public issues.
- Accountability = Responsibility for decisions.
- Transparency = Openness in governance.
- Public Service = Service provided by government.
- Welfare Scheme = Government support program.
- Good Governance = Effective and accountable governance.
- E-Governance = Technology-enabled governance.
- Decentralization = Power sharing with lower levels.
- Cooperative Federalism = Centre-State cooperation.
