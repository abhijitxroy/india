# Abbreviations and Acronyms

Common abbreviations used across Indian politics, governance, administration, economy, defence, and public institutions.

## Political and Constitutional Positions

| Abbreviation | Full Form | Bengali | Hindi | Meaning |
|--------------|-----------|----------|--------|---------|
| MLA | Member of Legislative Assembly | বিধায়ক | विधायक | Elected representative in a State Assembly |
| MP | Member of Parliament | সাংসদ | सांसद | Elected representative in Parliament |
| MLC | Member of Legislative Council | বিধান পরিষদ সদস্য | विधान परिषद सदस्य | Member of a State Legislative Council |
| PM | Prime Minister | প্রধানমন্ত্রী | प्रधानमंत्री | Head of the Union Government |
| CM | Chief Minister | মুখ্যমন্ত্রী | मुख्यमंत्री | Head of a State Government |
| VP | Vice President | উপরাষ্ট্রপতি | उपराष्ट्रपति | Deputy to the President |

| ECI | Election Commission of India | ভারত নির্বাচন কমিশন | भारत निर्वाचन आयोग | Conducts elections |
