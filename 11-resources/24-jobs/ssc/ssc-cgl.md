# Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL)

## Career Relevance

SSC CGL is one of India's most popular competitive examinations for recruitment to Group B and Group C posts in various ministries, departments, and organizations of the Government of India.

It provides a pathway to stable central government careers in administration, taxation, auditing, investigation, and public service.

## What is SSC CGL?

SSC CGL is a national-level recruitment examination conducted to fill vacancies in various central government ministries, departments, and subordinate offices.

It is one of the largest government recruitment examinations in India.
