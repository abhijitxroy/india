# Purba Medinipur District

## District Significance

Purba Medinipur is one of the most economically important coastal districts of West Bengal.

The district is known for its coastline along the Bay of Bengal, major tourism destinations, Haldia Port, petrochemical industries, fisheries, and agricultural production.

It serves as an important gateway between maritime trade, industry, tourism, and coastal development in eastern India.

## Haldia Dock Complex

Part of the Kolkata Port system.

- Maritime trade
- Cargo handling
