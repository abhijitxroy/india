# Uttar Dinajpur District

## District Significance

Uttar Dinajpur is an important district of North Bengal that serves as a strategic gateway between North Bengal and the rest of India.

The district shares an international border with Bangladesh and is an important agricultural, transportation, and trade region.

Its location along major road and railway corridors makes it significant for connectivity across eastern and northeastern India.

## History

Historically, Uttar Dinajpur formed part of the larger Dinajpur region.

The district's history is linked to:

- Ancient Bengal
- Medieval Bengal Sultanate
- Mughal administration
- British Bengal
- Partition of India

Following administrative reorganization, Uttar Dinajpur emerged as a separate district.
