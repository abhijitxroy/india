# Nadia District

## District Significance

Nadia is one of the most historically, culturally, and educationally important districts of West Bengal.

The district is known as the birthplace of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, a major centre of the Bhakti Movement, and one of the most important centres of Sanskrit learning in India.

Nadia also shares an international border with Bangladesh and plays an important role in trade, agriculture, education, and regional connectivity.
