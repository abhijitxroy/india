# Culture

## Overview

West Bengal is one of India's most influential cultural regions.

Its cultural identity has been shaped by literature, arts, music, festivals, cuisine, education, social reform movements, and the Bengal Renaissance.

## Quick Revision

- West Bengal is a major cultural center of India.
- The Bengal Renaissance influenced modern Indian thought and culture.
- Literature, arts, music, and festivals are central to Bengali identity.
- Kolkata has historically been a leading cultural and intellectual center.
- Cultural traditions continue to influence contemporary society.
