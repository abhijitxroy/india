# Literature

## Overview

West Bengal has one of the most influential literary traditions in India.

Bengali literature has shaped Indian culture, nationalism, education, social reform, and modern intellectual thought through poetry, novels, essays, drama, journalism, and philosophical writing.

## Literary Evolution

```text

```

## Major Literary Periods

### Early and Medieval Literature

- Vaishnava literature
- Mangalkavya traditions
- Religious and devotional writings
- Folk literary traditions

### Bengal Renaissance

- Growth of modern literature
- Social reform movements
- Expansion of education and publishing
- Development of modern Bengali prose

### Modern Literature

- Novels and short stories
- Poetry and essays
- Theatre and drama
- Literary journalism
