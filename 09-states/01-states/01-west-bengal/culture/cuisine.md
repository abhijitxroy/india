# Cuisine

## Overview

West Bengal's cuisine is one of the most distinctive culinary traditions in India.

It reflects the state's geography, rivers, coastline, agricultural resources, cultural diversity, and historical influences. Rice and fish form the foundation of traditional Bengali food, while sweets are an important part of the state's culinary identity.

## Core Characteristics

- Rice-based food culture
- Extensive use of fish
- Seasonal ingredients
- Balanced use of spices
- Strong sweet-making tradition
- Regional culinary diversity
