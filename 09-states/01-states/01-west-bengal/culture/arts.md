# Arts

## Overview

West Bengal has one of the richest artistic traditions in India.

The state's artistic heritage has been shaped by ancient traditions, the Bengal Renaissance, literature, music, theatre, cinema, painting, sculpture, folk arts, and modern creative movements.

## Artistic Evolution

```text
Ancient Traditions
        ↓
Temple and Folk Arts
        ↓
Bengal Renaissance
        ↓
Modern Literature and Music
        ↓
Cinema and Theatre
        ↓
Contemporary Arts
```
