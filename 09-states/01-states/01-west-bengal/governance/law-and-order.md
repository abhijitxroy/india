# Law and Order

## Overview

Law and order administration is a core responsibility of the Government of West Bengal.

The system is responsible for maintaining public safety, preventing crime, enforcing laws, managing emergencies, and supporting social stability across the state.

## Administrative Structure

```text
Home Department
        ↓
Director General of Police (DGP)
        ↓
Police Commissionerates
        ↓
District Police
        ↓
Sub-Divisional Police Units
        ↓
Police Stations
```

## Key Institutions

### Home Department

- Responsible for internal security administration
- Coordinates law and order policies
- Oversees policing and public safety functions

### West Bengal Police

- State police force
- Crime prevention and investigation
- Public safety and law enforcement
- Traffic management and security duties

### Kolkata Police

- Responsible for policing Kolkata
- Commissionerate system
- Urban law and order administration

## Major Responsibilities

### Crime Prevention

- Public safety measures
- Community policing

- Preventive action

### Crime Investigation

- Criminal investigations
- Evidence collection
- Prosecution support
- Special investigations

### Public Order

- Crowd management

### Disaster Support

## Security Challenges

## Quick Revision

- Law and order is primarily administered through the Home Department and police forces.
- The DGP heads the state police system.
- Kolkata Police manages law and order within Kolkata.
- District police units handle policing across the state.
