# Airports

## Overview

Airports play an important role in the connectivity, economy, tourism, trade, and strategic development of West Bengal.

The state serves as a major aviation gateway for eastern and northeastern India, connecting the region with domestic and international destinations.

## Aviation Network

## Major Airports

| Airport | Location | Significance |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport | Kolkata | Largest airport in eastern India |
| Bagdogra Airport | Near Siliguri | Gateway to North Bengal and the Himalayas |
| Andal Airport (Kazi Nazrul Islam Airport) | Durgapur | Industrial and regional connectivity |
| Cooch Behar Airport | Cooch Behar | Regional aviation potential |
| Balurghat Airport | Dakshin Dinajpur | Regional connectivity |

## Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport

### Importance

- Primary international airport of the state
- Major aviation hub in eastern India
- Supports domestic and international travel
- Facilitates business and tourism activities

### Connectivity

- Major Indian cities
- International destinations
- Northeast India connections
- Regional aviation network

## Bagdogra Airport

### Strategic Role

- Gateway to Darjeeling and Sikkim
- Supports tourism in North Bengal
- Important regional transport hub
- Strategic location near international borders

## Andal Airport

### Regional Development

- Serves the Durgapur–Asansol industrial region
- Supports industrial growth
- Enhances business connectivity

## Economic Importance

### Tourism

- Supports domestic tourism
- Facilitates international visitors
- Improves access to Darjeeling, Sundarbans, and other destinations

### Business and Trade

- Business travel connectivity
- Cargo movement support
- Investment promotion

### Regional Development

- Improves accessibility
- Supports employment generation
- Encourages economic activity
