# Power

## Overview

Power infrastructure is a critical component of West Bengal's economic development and industrial growth.

The state generates electricity from thermal, hydroelectric, renewable, and other energy sources while supporting households, industries, agriculture, transportation, and public services.

## Power Ecosystem

```text
Power Generation
        ↓
Transmission
        ↓
Distribution
        ↓
Industry
        ↓
Services
        ↓
Households
```

## Major Sources of Power

### Thermal Power

- Coal-based power generation
- Major contributor to electricity supply
- Supports industrial demand

### Hydroelectric Power

- Hydropower projects in northern regions
- Renewable energy contribution

### Renewable Energy

- Solar power projects
- Emerging renewable energy initiatives
- Clean energy transition support

| Region | Significance |
