# Ports

## Overview

Ports are among the most important infrastructure assets of West Bengal.

The state's ports support trade, industry, logistics, exports, imports, and connectivity with eastern India, northeastern India, and international markets through the Bay of Bengal.

## Kolkata Port

### Importance

- Historic riverine port
- Gateway to eastern India
- Supports domestic and international trade
- Important logistics center

### Functions

- Cargo handling
- Trade facilitation
- Inland waterway connectivity
- Industrial support

## Haldia Dock Complex

### Importance

- Deep-water cargo handling capabilities
- Supports petrochemical industries
- Major bulk cargo center
- Strategic industrial infrastructure

### Industrial Linkages

- Petrochemicals
- Manufacturing
- Energy sector logistics
- Export-oriented industries
