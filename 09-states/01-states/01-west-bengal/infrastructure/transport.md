# Transport in West Bengal

## Infrastructure Importance

West Bengal is one of India's most important transportation hubs. The state connects Eastern India, North-Eastern India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, and international maritime routes through its road, rail, air, river, and port infrastructure.

Transport has played a major role in the economic, industrial, military, and strategic development of the state.

## Transport Overview

West Bengal's transport system consists of:

- Roads
- Railways
- Airports
- Inland Waterways
- Seaports
- Metro Rail

These systems support passenger movement, trade, logistics, tourism, and national security.

## Road Transport

### National Highways

Major highways passing through the state include:

- NH-12
- NH-16
- NH-19
- NH-27
- NH-31

These connect West Bengal with:

- Delhi
- Bihar
- Jharkhand
- Odisha
- Assam
- North-East India

### State Highways

State highways connect districts, towns, industrial centres, and tourist destinations.

## Rail Transport

### Importance

West Bengal has one of India's oldest and most extensive railway networks.

The rail system supports:

- Passenger transport
- Freight movement
- Industrial logistics
- Military mobility

### Major Railway Divisions

- Sealdah
- Howrah
- Asansol
- Malda

### Major Railway Stations

- Howrah Junction
- Sealdah
- Kolkata Station
- New Jalpaiguri
- Asansol Junction
- Durgapur

## Kolkata Metro

The Kolkata Metro was India's first metro railway.

Importance:

- Urban mobility
- Reduced congestion
- Faster travel
- Modern public transport

Metro expansion continues across the Kolkata Metropolitan Area.

## Air Transport

### Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport

Located in Kolkata.

Importance:

- Largest airport in Eastern India
- Domestic connectivity
- International connectivity
- Cargo operations

### Other Airports

- Bagdogra Airport
- Andal Airport (Kazi Nazrul Islam Airport)
- Cooch Behar Airport (limited operations)
- Balurghat Airport (developing services)

## Port Infrastructure

### Kolkata Port

Significance:

- India's oldest operating major port
- Gateway to Eastern India
- Handles international cargo

### Haldia Dock Complex

Supports:

- Bulk cargo
- Petroleum products
- Industrial logistics
- Export-import trade

## Inland Water Transport

West Bengal has extensive river transport potential.

Major rivers:

- Hooghly
- Ganga
- Damodar
- Rupnarayan

### National Waterway 1

Connects:

- Uttar Pradesh
- Bihar
- Jharkhand
- West Bengal

Benefits:

- Lower transport costs
- Freight efficiency
- Reduced road congestion

## Siliguri Corridor

Also known as the 'Chicken's Neck'.

Importance:

- Connects mainland India with North-East India
- Strategic military importance
- Critical trade route

The corridor is one of India's most important transportation and security zones.
