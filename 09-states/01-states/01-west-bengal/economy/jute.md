# Jute Industry

## Overview

The jute industry is one of the oldest and most historically important industries of West Bengal.

For more than a century, the state has been the center of India's jute production, processing, manufacturing, and exports. The industry played a major role in the economic development of the Hooghly industrial belt.

## Geographic Concentration

### Hooghly Industrial Belt

Major jute mills developed along the Hooghly River because of:

- River transport access
- Kolkata Port connectivity
- Availability of labor
- Access to export markets
- Industrial infrastructure
