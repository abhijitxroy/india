# Industry

## Overview

West Bengal has one of India's oldest industrial bases. The state developed around Kolkata, the Hooghly industrial belt, major ports, coal resources, rail connectivity, and access to eastern and northeastern markets.

Today, industry in West Bengal includes steel, engineering, chemicals, petrochemicals, power, tea processing, jute, information technology, food processing, and manufacturing.

## Key Industries

### Steel and Engineering

- Durgapur Steel Plant
- Alloy and engineering industries
- Railway-related manufacturing
- Heavy engineering units

### Petrochemicals and Chemicals

- Haldia Petrochemicals
- Chemical manufacturing
- Industrial chemicals
- Plastic and polymer industries

### Jute Industry

- Historically concentrated along the Hooghly River
- One of the world's major jute-producing regions
- Supports exports and manufacturing employment

### Tea Industry

- Darjeeling Tea
- Dooars Tea Gardens
- Export-oriented production

### Information Technology

- Sector V (Salt Lake)
- New Town technology hub
- Software services
- IT-enabled services

### Food Processing

- Rice processing
- Fisheries products
- Agricultural processing
- Packaged food industries

## Industrial Advantages

- Strategic location in eastern India
- Access to ports
- Large consumer market
- Strong rail and road connectivity
- Availability of skilled workforce
- Gateway to Northeast India

## Challenges

- Industrial land availability
- Infrastructure modernization
- Competition from other states
- Logistics bottlenecks in some regions
- Need for higher private investment

## Quick Revision

- Kolkata and the Hooghly belt form the historic industrial core.
- Durgapur and Asansol are major heavy-industry centers.
- Haldia is a major petrochemical and port-based industrial hub.
- Darjeeling and Dooars are important tea-producing regions.
- Information technology is concentrated around Salt Lake and New Town.
- Industry remains a major contributor to the state's economy.
