# Tourism in West Bengal

## Economic Importance

West Bengal is one of India's most diverse tourism destinations. The state offers the Himalayas, tea gardens, mangrove forests, beaches, rivers, heritage cities, religious sites, wildlife reserves, colonial architecture, and cultural centres.

Tourism contributes significantly to employment, local businesses, transportation, hospitality, and the state's economy.
