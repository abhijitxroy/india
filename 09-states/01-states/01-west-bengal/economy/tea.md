# Tea Industry

## Overview

The tea industry is one of the most recognized sectors of West Bengal's economy.

The state is known globally for Darjeeling Tea and is also a major producer through the Dooars and Terai regions. Tea contributes to employment, exports, tourism, and regional development.

### Global Reputation

- Known as the 'Champagne of Teas'
- Protected Geographical Indication (GI) product
- International export market
- Premium quality tea production

### Economic Importance

- Export earnings
- Tourism attraction
- Employment generation
- International recognition
