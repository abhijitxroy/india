

# Climate

## Overview

West Bengal experiences diverse climatic conditions because of its varied geography, ranging from the Himalayan region in the north to the Bay of Bengal coast in the south.

The state generally experiences a tropical monsoon climate, with significant seasonal variations in temperature and rainfall.

## Climate Zones

```text
Northern Himalayas
        ↓
Cool Mountain Climate

Northern Plains
        ↓
Humid Subtropical Climate

Southern Plains
        ↓
Tropical Monsoon Climate

Coastal Areas
        ↓
Warm and Humid Climate
```

## Seasons

### Summer (March–June)

- High temperatures in plains
- Humid conditions
- Occasional thunderstorms
- Known locally for Kalbaisakhi storms

### Monsoon (June–September)

- Southwest monsoon rainfall
- Major source of annual precipitation
- Important for agriculture and water resources

### Post-Monsoon (October–November)

- Retreating monsoon conditions
- Pleasant weather in many regions
- Festival season across the state

### Winter (December–February)

- Cool and dry conditions
- Coldest period of the year
- Northern districts experience lower temperatures

## Regional Variations

### Darjeeling Hills

- Cool temperatures throughout the year
- High rainfall
- Frequent fog and cloud cover

### Gangetic Plains

- Hot summers
- Moderate winters
- Strong monsoon influence

### Coastal Region

- High humidity
- Moderate temperature variation
- Cyclone vulnerability

## Importance of Climate

- Supports agriculture
- Influences water availability
- Shapes biodiversity and ecosystems
- Supports tourism activities
- Affects economic and social life

## Climate Challenges

- Cyclones from the Bay of Bengal
- Flooding during heavy monsoon periods
- Heat waves in some regions
- Climate change impacts
- Coastal vulnerability

## Quick Revision

- West Bengal has a tropical monsoon climate.
- Climate varies from Himalayan conditions in the north to coastal conditions in the south.
- The monsoon season provides most annual rainfall.
- Kalbaisakhi storms are common during summer.
- Coastal districts are vulnerable to cyclones.
- Climate strongly influences agriculture, water resources, and livelihoods.
