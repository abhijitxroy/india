# Natural Resources

## Overview

West Bengal possesses a diverse range of natural resources that support agriculture, industry, energy production, fisheries, and economic development.

The state's resources are distributed across the Himalayan region, plains, plateau areas, river systems, forests, and coastal ecosystems.

## Resource Distribution

```text
Himalayan Region
        ↓
Forests and Water Resources

Plains
        ↓
Agricultural Resources

Plateau Region
        ↓
Mineral Resources

Coastal Region
        ↓
Fisheries and Mangroves
```

## Major Natural Resources

### Water Resources

- Ganga River system
- Hooghly River
- Teesta River
- Damodar River
- Reservoirs and wetlands

### Forest Resources

- Sundarbans mangrove forests
- Darjeeling forests
- Dooars forests
- Wildlife habitats
- Timber and non-timber forest products

### Agricultural Resources

- Fertile alluvial soil
- Rice cultivation areas
- Tea-growing regions
- Jute-growing regions
- Horticultural production

### Fisheries Resources

- Bay of Bengal coastline
- Rivers and wetlands
- Aquaculture resources
- Estuarine ecosystems

### Mineral Resources

- Coal deposits in western districts
- Fire clay
- China clay
- Limestone (limited areas)
- Construction materials

## Economic Importance

### Agriculture

- Supports food production
- Enables cash-crop cultivation
- Provides rural livelihoods

### Industry

- Coal supports power generation and industry
- Water resources support manufacturing
- Natural resources support processing industries

### Tourism

- Forests and wildlife tourism
- Himalayan landscapes
- Sundarbans ecosystem

## Environmental Significance

- Biodiversity conservation
- Climate regulation
- Flood management support
- Ecosystem services
- Coastal protection through mangroves

## Challenges

- Environmental degradation
- River pollution
- Deforestation pressures
- Climate change impacts
- Sustainable resource management

## Quick Revision

- Water is the state's most important natural resource.
- The Sundarbans are the world's largest mangrove ecosystem.
- Western districts contain important coal resources.
- Fertile alluvial plains support agriculture.
- Fisheries depend on rivers, wetlands, and coastal ecosystems.
- Sustainable resource management is important for long-term development.
