# Rivers

## Overview

Rivers are among the most important geographical features of West Bengal.

They support agriculture, transportation, drinking water supply, fisheries, industry, and ecosystem development. The state's river systems originate from the Himalayas, the Chota Nagpur Plateau, and the Ganga basin.

## River System

```text
Himalayan Rivers
        ↓
Northern Plains

Ganga System
        ↓
Central and Southern Bengal

Plateau Rivers
        ↓
Western Districts

Bay of Bengal
```

## Major Rivers

| River | Importance |
|----------|----------|
| Ganga | Major river of India entering West Bengal |
| Hooghly | Main distributary and economic lifeline |
| Teesta | Important river of North Bengal |
| Damodar | Industrial and agricultural significance |
| Subarnarekha | Flows through western regions |
| Mahananda | Important northern river |
| Torsa | Supports northern districts |

## Ganga River

### Importance

- Major water resource
- Supports agriculture
- Cultural and historical significance
- Source of distributaries in Bengal

## Hooghly River

### Economic Role

- Supports Kolkata Port
- Industrial development along its banks
- Inland water transport
- Urban water resource

## Teesta River

### North Bengal Significance

- Irrigation support
- Agriculture and livelihoods
- Hydroelectric potential in upper regions

## Damodar River

### Development Role

- Supports industry and agriculture
- Connected with the Damodar Valley system
- Important water resource for western districts

## Economic Importance

### Agriculture

- Irrigation support
- Fertile alluvial plains
- Water availability for farming

### Industry

- Industrial water supply
- Transport and logistics support
- Energy-related infrastructure

### Fisheries

- Inland fisheries
- Wetland ecosystems
- Livelihood support

## Challenges

- Flooding during monsoon periods
- Riverbank erosion
- Water pollution
- Siltation issues
- Climate change impacts

## Quick Revision

- The Ganga and Hooghly are the most important rivers of West Bengal.
- The Hooghly River supports Kolkata and major port activities.
- Teesta is a major river of North Bengal.
- Damodar supports industrial and agricultural regions.
- Rivers are essential for agriculture, fisheries, and economic development.
- Flood management remains an important challenge.
