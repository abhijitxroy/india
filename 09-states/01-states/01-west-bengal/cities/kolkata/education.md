# Kolkata Education

## Urban Importance

Kolkata is one of India's most important centres of education, research, science, and intellectual development.

The city played a major role in the growth of modern education in India and continues to attract students from across the country and neighbouring nations.

Many of India's oldest and most prestigious educational institutions are located in Kolkata.

## Educational Legacy

Kolkata was at the forefront of modern education during the nineteenth century.

The city became a centre for:

- Modern higher education
- Scientific research
- Social reform
- Literature and humanities
- Professional education

Educational development was closely linked to the Bengal Renaissance.

## Kolkata as an Education Hub

Students come to Kolkata from:

- West Bengal
- North-East India
- Bihar
- Jharkhand
- Odisha
- Bangladesh
- Nepal
- Bhutan

The city offers education across almost every academic discipline.

## Major Universities

### University of Calcutta

Established in 1857.

One of the oldest universities in India.

Known for:

- Arts and humanities
- Science
- Commerce
- Research

### Jadavpur University

One of India's leading public universities.

Known for:

- Engineering
- Technology
- Arts
- Research

### Presidency University

One of India's most prestigious institutions.

Known for:

- Humanities
- Sciences
- Social sciences

### Rabindra Bharati University

Known for:

- Fine arts
- Music
- Dance
- Cultural studies

### West Bengal State University

Serves many colleges in the Kolkata metropolitan region.

## National Institutes

### Indian Statistical Institute (ISI)

Globally recognized for:

- Statistics
- Mathematics
- Computer science
- Research

### Indian Institute of Management Calcutta (IIM Calcutta)

One of India's premier management institutes.

Known for:

- MBA programs
- Research
- Executive education

### S.N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences

Important research institution in basic sciences.
