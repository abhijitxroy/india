# Kolkata Transport

## Urban Importance

Kolkata has one of the oldest and most diverse urban transport systems in India.

The city serves as the transportation hub of Eastern India and connects West Bengal with the rest of India, North-East India, Bangladesh, Nepal, and Bhutan.

Its transport network includes metro rail, suburban railways, roads, bridges, ferries, trams, buses, taxis, and an international airport.

## Transport Overview

Major transport modes include:

- Metro Railway
- Suburban Railways
- Long-distance Railways
- Road Transport
- Buses
- Trams
- Ferries
- Taxis
- Ride-sharing Services
- Air Transport

## Kolkata Metro

Kolkata Metro was India's first metro railway system.

Importance:

- First metro in India
- Reduces traffic congestion
- Supports daily commuting
- Connects major city regions

The metro is one of the most important transport systems in Kolkata.

## History of Kolkata Metro

Key milestones:

- Planning began during the 1970s
- Operations started in 1984
- India's first underground metro system
- Continuous expansion across the metropolitan region

The metro transformed urban transportation in Kolkata.

## Metro Corridors

Major corridors include:

- North-South Metro

## East-West Metro

One of Kolkata's most significant infrastructure projects.

Importance:

- Connects Howrah and Kolkata
- Includes an underwater tunnel beneath the Hooghly River
- Improves east-west connectivity

## Railway Network

Kolkata has one of India's largest railway networks.

The city serves as the headquarters of major railway operations in Eastern India.

## Howrah Station

One of the busiest railway stations in India.

Importance:

- National connectivity
- Long-distance train services
- Major passenger hub

## Sealdah Station

- Daily commuter traffic

- Connects Kolkata and Howrah
- Supports heavy traffic movement
- Symbol of the city

### Vidyasagar Setu

Known for:
