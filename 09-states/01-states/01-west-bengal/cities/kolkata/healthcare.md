# Kolkata Healthcare

## Urban Importance

Kolkata is one of the largest healthcare hubs in India.

The city serves not only West Bengal but also patients from Eastern India, North-East India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, and neighbouring regions.

Kolkata has some of India's oldest medical institutions and continues to play a major role in medical education, healthcare delivery, and medical research.

## Healthcare Overview

Kolkata's healthcare ecosystem includes:

- Government hospitals
- Medical colleges
- Private hospitals
- Specialty hospitals
- Research institutions
- Diagnostic centres

The city offers healthcare services ranging from primary care to advanced super-speciality treatment.

## Historical Importance

Kolkata played a pioneering role in the development of modern medicine in India.

Important milestones include:

- Establishment of Medical College Kolkata
- Growth of public health institutions
- Development of modern medical education
- Expansion of hospital infrastructure

## Medical College Kolkata

Established in 1835.

Significance:

- One of Asia's oldest medical colleges
- Pioneer of modern medical education in India
- Important centre for medical research and treatment
