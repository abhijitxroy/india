# Kolkata Infrastructure

## Urban Importance

Kolkata possesses one of the largest urban infrastructure networks in Eastern India.

As the capital of West Bengal and the economic hub of Eastern India, the city depends on extensive transportation, water supply, power, communication, healthcare, education, and urban development infrastructure.

Infrastructure development plays a critical role in supporting Kolkata's economy, population, and future growth.

## Infrastructure Overview

Major infrastructure sectors include:

- Transport
- Roads and bridges
- Metro rail
- Airports
- Water supply
- Power supply
- Digital infrastructure
- Healthcare infrastructure
- Educational infrastructure

## Kolkata Metropolitan Area

Infrastructure development extends beyond Kolkata district and includes the wider Kolkata Metropolitan Area (KMA).

Major urban regions include:

- Kolkata
- Howrah

- New Town
- Rajarhat
- Barrackpore region

## Road Infrastructure

Kolkata has an extensive road network connecting different parts of the city.

Major road corridors include:

- EM Bypass
- AJC Bose Road
- VIP Road
- Diamond Harbour Road
- BT Road
- Jessore Road

These roads support daily commuter and commercial traffic.

## Bridges

### Howrah Bridge (Rabindra Setu)

One of India's most iconic bridges.

Importance:

- Connects Kolkata and Howrah
- Supports massive daily traffic
- Symbol of Kolkata

### Vidyasagar Setu

One of India's major cable-stayed bridges.

Importance:

- Reduces traffic congestion
- Improves connectivity
- Supports freight movement

### Vivekananda Setu

Important rail and road bridge across the Hooghly River.

### Nivedita Setu

Modern bridge supporting regional connectivity.

## Metro Infrastructure

Kolkata Metro was India's first metro railway system.

The metro network supports:

- Urban mobility
- Reduced road congestion
- Faster travel

Major corridors continue to expand across the metropolitan region.

## Railway Infrastructure

Major railway facilities include:

- Howrah Station
- Sealdah Station
- Kolkata Railway Terminal
- Santragachi Junction

These stations connect Kolkata with the rest of India.

## Airport Infrastructure

### Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport

The city's primary airport.

Supports:

- Domestic travel
- International travel
- Cargo operations
- Business connectivity

## Port Infrastructure

### Port of Kolkata

India's oldest operating major port.

Importance:

- International trade
- Inland water transport
- Cargo movement
- Economic development

### Haldia Dock Complex

Supports regional port operations and industrial logistics.

## Water Supply Infrastructure

The city receives water primarily from the Hooghly River.

Major infrastructure includes:

- Water treatment plants
- Distribution networks
- Pumping stations
- Reservoir systems

Kolkata Municipal Corporation manages much of the urban water supply network.

## Drainage and Sewerage

Major systems include:

- Storm-water drainage
- Sewer networks
- Canal systems
- Pumping stations

These systems are critical for flood management during monsoon seasons.

## East Kolkata Wetlands

The East Kolkata Wetlands play a unique role in urban infrastructure.

Importance:

- Natural wastewater treatment
- Flood control
- Environmental protection
- Sustainable urban management
