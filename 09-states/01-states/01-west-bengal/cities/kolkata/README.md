# Kolkata

## Urban Importance

- Capital of West Bengal
- Cultural capital of India
- Former capital of British India (1772–1911)
- Major economic, educational, and cultural center of eastern India
- Home to India's oldest port system in the region
- Gateway to eastern and northeastern India

## Historical Background

- Originated from the villages of Sutanuti, Gobindapur, and Kalikata.
- Developed as the headquarters of the British East India Company in Bengal.
- Served as the capital of British India until 1911.
- Played a central role in the Bengal Renaissance and Indian freedom movement.
- Produced many of India's leading thinkers, scientists, artists, and political leaders.

## Economy

Major sectors:

- Information Technology
- Banking and financial services
- Trade and commerce
- Manufacturing
- Education and research
- Healthcare
- Logistics and transportation

Kolkata remains the largest economic center in eastern India.

## Education

Major institutions include:

## Transport

### Metro

- Home to India's first metro railway.
- Extensive metro network connecting major parts of the city.

### Rail

- Served by Howrah, Sealdah, Kolkata, and Santragachi railway terminals.

### Air

- Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport is the primary airport of eastern India.

### River Transport

- Ferry services operate across the Hooghly River.
