# West Bengal

## State Significance

West Bengal is one of India's most historically, culturally, economically, and politically influential states.

The state served as the capital region of British India, led the Bengal Renaissance, played a major role in the freedom movement, and continues to be an important centre of education, culture, trade, industry, and governance.
