# Malda District

## District Significance

Malda is one of the most historically important districts of West Bengal.

The district served as a gateway between North Bengal and South Bengal and was closely associated with the medieval capitals of Bengal.

Malda is famous for its mangoes, silk industry, historical monuments, and rich cultural heritage.

## History

Malda has been an important centre for centuries.

The district is associated with:

- Ancient Bengal
- Pala Empire
- Sena Dynasty
- Bengal Sultanate
- Mughal Rule
- British Administration

Historically, the region was closely connected with the former capitals of Bengal.

## Gaur

Gaur was one of the most important medieval cities of Bengal.

Known for:

- Bengal Sultanate administration
- Architectural heritage
- Historic mosques and gateways

Today, its ruins remain among the most significant archaeological sites in Eastern India.

## Pandua

Pandua served as an important medieval capital.

Known for:

- Adina Mosque
- Islamic architecture
- Historical monuments

## Mango Industry

Malda is internationally known for mango cultivation.

Famous varieties include:

- Fazli
- Himsagar
- Lakshmanbhog
- Gopalbhog

The district is one of India's leading mango-producing regions.

## Silk Industry

- Silk trade
