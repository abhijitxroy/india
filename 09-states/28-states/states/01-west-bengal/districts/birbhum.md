# Birbhum District

## District Significance

Birbhum is one of the most culturally significant districts of West Bengal.

The district is known for Santiniketan, Visva-Bharati University, Rabindranath Tagore, Baul culture, red laterite landscapes, and rich literary and artistic traditions.

It is often called the cultural heartland of Bengal.

## History

Birbhum has a long history connected with:

- Ancient Bengal
- Medieval regional kingdoms
- Bengal Sultanate
- British Bengal

The district gained global recognition through the work of Rabindranath Tagore and the development of Santiniketan.

## Geography

Birbhum lies between the Gangetic plains and the Chota Nagpur Plateau region.

Major geographical features:

- Red laterite soil
- Undulating terrain
- Agricultural plains
- River valleys

Major rivers:

- Ajay
- Mayurakshi
- Bakreshwar
- Brahmani
