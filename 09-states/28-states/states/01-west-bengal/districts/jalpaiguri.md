# Jalpaiguri District

## District Significance

Jalpaiguri is one of the most important districts of North Bengal.

The district is known for the Dooars region, tea gardens, forests, wildlife, rivers, agriculture, and its role as a gateway to North-East India and the eastern Himalayas.

Jalpaiguri has historically served as an important administrative, commercial, and transportation centre in North Bengal.

## History

Historically the region was influenced by:

- Kamata Kingdom
- Koch Kingdom
- British Bengal

During British rule, Jalpaiguri developed as an important centre for:

- Tea plantations
- Forestry
- Trade
- Administration

The district played a major role in the development of the Dooars tea industry.
