# North 24 Parganas District

## District Significance

North 24 Parganas is one of the most important districts of West Bengal.

It is the most populous district in the state and forms a major part of the Kolkata Metropolitan Area.

The district is an important centre for industry, education, information technology, transportation, international trade, and urban development.

North 24 Parganas also shares an international border with Bangladesh, making it strategically and economically significant.
