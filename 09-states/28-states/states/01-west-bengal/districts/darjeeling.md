# Darjeeling District

## District Significance

Darjeeling is one of the most internationally recognized districts of India.

The district is famous for Darjeeling Tea, Himalayan landscapes, the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, tourism, and its strategic location near Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, and the Siliguri Corridor.

Darjeeling is often called the 'Queen of the Hills'.

## History

Historically the area was influenced by:

- Sikkim Kingdom
- Nepalese expansion
- British India

The British developed Darjeeling during the nineteenth century as a hill station and health resort.

Over time it became one of the most important hill towns in South Asia.

## British Hill Station Development

The British established:

- Hill settlements
- Tea plantations
- Schools
- Rail connectivity

Darjeeling became a major summer retreat during the colonial period.

## Gorkhaland Movement

The district has witnessed demands for a separate Gorkhaland state.

The movement has been an important political issue in the Darjeeling hills for decades.

## Kalimpong Separation

In 2017, Kalimpong district was carved out from Darjeeling district.

This reduced the geographical size of Darjeeling district.

## Strategic Importance
