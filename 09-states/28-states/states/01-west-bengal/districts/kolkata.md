# Kolkata District

## Why Kolkata Matters

Kolkata is the capital of West Bengal and one of the most important cities in Indian history.

It served as the capital of British India from 1772 to 1911 and became the centre of administration, education, commerce, literature, science, culture, and the Indian freedom movement.

Today, Kolkata remains the political, economic, financial, cultural, educational, and intellectual capital of Eastern India.

## Evolution of Kolkata

Modern Kolkata developed from three villages:

- Sutanuti
- Govindapur
- Kalikata

The British East India Company gradually transformed the area into its administrative and commercial headquarters.

## Presidency Capital Era

Kolkata became the headquarters of British administration in India.

Major developments included:

- Fort William
- Government institutions
- Educational institutions
- Banking system
- Port infrastructure

## British India Capital (1772–1911)

For nearly 140 years, Kolkata functioned as the capital of British India.

Major events:

- Expansion of British rule
- Bengal Renaissance
- Early nationalist movement
- Administrative reforms

In 1911, the capital shifted to Delhi.

## Role in Freedom Movement

Kolkata played a central role in India's freedom struggle.

Important movements:

- Swadeshi Movement
- Anti-Partition Movement (1905)
- Revolutionary Nationalism
- Civil Disobedience Movement

Important personalities:

- Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose
- Rabindranath Tagore
- Surendranath Banerjee
- Aurobindo Ghosh

## Kolkata Metropolitan Area

The Kolkata Metropolitan Area (KMA) is one of India's largest urban regions.

It includes:

- Kolkata
- Howrah
- Bidhannagar
- Rajarhat
- Barrackpore region
- Several municipalities and urban centres
