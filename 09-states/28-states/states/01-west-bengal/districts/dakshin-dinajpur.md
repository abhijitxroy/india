# Dakshin Dinajpur District

## District Significance

Dakshin Dinajpur is an important agricultural district of North Bengal.

The district is known for fertile agricultural land, river systems, border connectivity with Bangladesh, historical sites, and rural economic activities.

It was created after the division of the former West Dinajpur district and plays an important role in the agricultural economy of North Bengal.

## History

Dakshin Dinajpur was created in 1992 after the bifurcation of the former West Dinajpur district.

Historically, the region was influenced by:

- Ancient Bengal kingdoms
- Pala Empire
- Sena Dynasty
- Bengal Sultanate
- British Bengal

The district contains several archaeological and historical sites connected to Bengal's early history.
