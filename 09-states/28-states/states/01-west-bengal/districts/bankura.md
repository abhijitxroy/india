# Bankura District

## District Significance

Bankura is one of the most historically and culturally significant districts of West Bengal.

The district is known for its terracotta temples, Bishnupur heritage, Baluchari sarees, folk traditions, red laterite landscape, and rich artistic legacy.

It acts as a bridge between the plains of Bengal and the plateau regions of western West Bengal.

## History

Bankura formed the core region of the historic Mallabhum Kingdom.

The Malla rulers governed the region for centuries and patronized:

- Temple architecture
- Music
- Art
- Literature

The district became famous for the development of the Bishnupur school of culture and architecture.
