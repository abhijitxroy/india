# Asansol

## Urban Importance

- Second-largest urban area in West Bengal after Kolkata
- Major industrial and coal-mining center of eastern India
- Important railway and logistics hub
- Gateway to the Raniganj Coalfield region
- Key contributor to the state's steel, power, and manufacturing sectors

## Historical Background

- Grew rapidly during the British period because of coal mining.
- Development accelerated with the expansion of railways and heavy industry.
- Became one of India's major industrial cities during the twentieth century.
- Emerged as an important urban center of the Damodar Valley industrial belt.

## Economy

Major sectors:

- Coal mining
- Steel production
- Thermal power generation
- Railways
- Manufacturing industries
- Logistics and transportation

Important industrial establishments include:

- Eastern Coalfields Limited (ECL)
- Indian Railways facilities
- IISCO Steel Plant at nearby Burnpur
- Various engineering and manufacturing units

## Transport

### Rail

- One of the busiest railway junctions in Eastern India.
- Connected to Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Patna, Ranchi, and other major cities.

### Road

- Connected through National Highway networks.
- Important link between West Bengal and Jharkhand.

### Air

- Served primarily through nearby airports such as Kolkata and Andal Airport.

## Education

- Medical and professional institutions

- Industrial pollution
- Urban infrastructure pressure
