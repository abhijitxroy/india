# Kolkata History

## Urban Importance

Kolkata has played a central role in the political, economic, cultural, educational, and intellectual history of India.

From a group of small villages on the banks of the Hooghly River, it evolved into the capital of British India and later became one of the most influential cities in modern India.

## Ancient Settlements

Before the arrival of the British, the region consisted of riverine settlements and trading communities along the Hooghly River.

The area benefited from:

- River trade
- Agriculture
- Local markets
- Regional commerce

## Sutanuti, Govindapur and Kalikata

Modern Kolkata developed from three villages:

- Sutanuti
- Govindapur
- Kalikata

These settlements gradually expanded because of their strategic location along the Hooghly River.

## East India Company Era

The British East India Company established its presence in the region during the seventeenth century.

The city became an important centre for:

- Trade
- Administration
- Military operations
- Revenue collection

## Fort William

Fort William became the most important British military installation in eastern India.

Importance:

- Defence of British interests
- Administrative control
- Military headquarters

The present Fort William remains an important military establishment.

## Black Hole of Calcutta

In 1756, during the conflict between the British East India Company and Siraj-ud-Daulah, the incident known as the Black Hole of Calcutta became part of colonial historical narratives.

The event later became a major justification used by the British to strengthen military intervention in Bengal.

## Battle of Plassey and Its Impact

The Battle of Plassey in 1757 transformed the future of Bengal and Kolkata.

Consequences:

- Expansion of British power
- Growth of Company administration
- Increased commercial importance of Kolkata
- Beginning of British political dominance in India

## Capital of British India (1772–1911)

Kolkata became the capital of British India in 1772.

During this period the city developed:

- Government institutions
- Educational institutions
- Judicial systems
- Banking infrastructure
- Port facilities

The city emerged as the political centre of British rule in India.

## Growth of Colonial Kolkata

Major developments included:

- Writers' Building
- Government House (Raj Bhavan)
- Calcutta High Court
- Educational institutions
- Port infrastructure

The city became one of Asia's most important urban centres.

## Bengal Renaissance

The nineteenth century witnessed the Bengal Renaissance.

This movement transformed:

- Education
- Literature
- Science
- Social reform
- Journalism

Important personalities included:

- Raja Rammohan Roy
- Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
- Rabindranath Tagore
- Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay
- Swami Vivekananda

## Partition of Bengal (1905)

The British partitioned Bengal in 1905.

This decision triggered:

- Mass protests
- Swadeshi Movement
- Nationalist mobilisation
- Political awakening

Kolkata became the centre of anti-partition activities.

## Freedom Movement

Kolkata played a major role in India's struggle for independence.

Major activities included:

- Revolutionary movements
- Nationalist politics
- Student activism
- Swadeshi campaigns

## Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose

Netaji emerged as one of Kolkata's most influential political leaders.

His residence, Netaji Bhawan, remains an important historical site.

## Revolutionary Activities

The city became a centre for revolutionary organizations.

Several nationalist groups operated from Kolkata during the freedom struggle.

## Shift of Capital to Delhi (1911)

In 1911, the British shifted the capital from Kolkata to Delhi.

Although political importance reduced, Kolkata remained a major commercial and cultural centre.

## 1947 Partition and Refugee Migration

Partition had a profound impact on Kolkata.

Large numbers of refugees arrived from East Pakistan (now Bangladesh).

This led to:

- Rapid population growth
- Expansion of suburbs
- New settlements
- Social and economic changes

## Post-Independence Growth

After independence, Kolkata continued to develop as:

- State capital
- Industrial centre
- Educational hub
- Cultural centre

## Industrial Development

Major industries included:

- Jute
- Engineering
- Manufacturing
- Port-related activities

The wider metropolitan region became one of India's largest industrial belts.

## Kolkata Metro

Kolkata Metro became India's first metro railway system.

Importance:

- Urban transport modernization
- Reduced travel times
- Infrastructure advancement

## Economic Liberalization Era

After the 1990s, Kolkata witnessed growth in:

- Information Technology
- Financial services
- Education
- Healthcare
- Real estate

Major growth centres emerged in:

- Salt Lake Sector V
- New Town

## Modern Kolkata

Today Kolkata is:

- Capital of West Bengal
- Cultural capital of India
- Economic hub of Eastern India
- Major educational centre
- Important healthcare destination

## Future Kolkata

Major development priorities include:

- Metro expansion
- Smart city initiatives
- Riverfront development
- Heritage conservation
- Technology sector growth
