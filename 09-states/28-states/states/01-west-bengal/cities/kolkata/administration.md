# Kolkata Administration

## Urban Importance

Kolkata is the capital of West Bengal and the administrative centre of Eastern India.

The city hosts the headquarters of the Government of West Bengal, Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC), Kolkata Police, Calcutta High Court, and numerous state and central government offices.

## Administrative Structure

```text
Citizens
    |
Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC)
    |
Mayor
    |
Mayor-in-Council
    |
Municipal Commissioner
    |
Departments and Borough Offices
```

The administration of Kolkata operates through a combination of:

- State Government
- Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC)
- District Administration
- Kolkata Police
- Development Authorities

## Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC)

Kolkata Municipal Corporation is the primary urban local body responsible for city governance.

Responsibilities include:

- Roads and street maintenance
- Water supply
- Drainage and sewerage
- Solid waste management
- Public health services
- Municipal schools
- Building approvals
- Property tax collection

## Mayor

The Mayor is the political head of Kolkata Municipal Corporation.

Responsibilities:

- Leads elected municipal administration
- Presides over KMC meetings
- Represents the city officially
- Oversees policy decisions

## Mayor-in-Council

The Mayor-in-Council functions similarly to a city cabinet.

Members are assigned portfolios such as:

- Finance
- Water Supply
- Health
- Education
- Solid Waste Management
- Parks and Gardens

## Municipal Commissioner

The Municipal Commissioner is the executive head of KMC.

Responsibilities:

- Implementation of municipal policies
- Administration of departments
- Budget execution
- Personnel management
- Urban infrastructure projects

The Commissioner is generally a senior IAS officer.

## Borough System

Kolkata is divided into multiple boroughs for administrative convenience.

Objectives:

- Decentralized administration
- Better citizen services
- Faster grievance handling
- Local project monitoring

## Ward System

The city is divided into municipal wards.

Each ward elects a Councillor who represents local residents.

Ward-level responsibilities include:

- Local civic issues
- Citizen grievances
- Community development
- Coordination with municipal departments

## District Administration

The district administration is headed by the District Magistrate.

Key responsibilities:

- Law and order coordination
- Disaster management
- Elections
- Revenue administration
- Government scheme implementation

## Kolkata Police

Kolkata Police is one of India's oldest police organizations.

Responsibilities:

- Law and order
- Crime prevention
- Traffic management
- VIP security
- Cybercrime investigation

The force is headed by the Commissioner of Police.

## Government of West Bengal

Kolkata hosts major state government institutions including:

- Nabanna (State Secretariat)
- Raj Bhavan
- State Departments
- State Public Sector Offices

## Judiciary

### Calcutta High Court

One of the oldest High Courts in India.

Responsibilities:

- Constitutional matters
- Civil cases
- Criminal appeals
- Judicial oversight

## Development Authorities

Major urban development agencies include:

- Kolkata Metropolitan Development Authority (KMDA)
- New Town Kolkata Development Authority (NKDA)
- Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC)

These organizations oversee infrastructure and urban development projects.

## Civic Services

Major civic services provided to residents:

- Drinking water
- Sanitation
- Waste management
- Public health
- Roads and street lighting
- Parks and recreational facilities

## Key Administrative Buildings

- Kolkata Municipal Corporation Headquarters
- Nabanna
- Raj Bhavan
- Calcutta High Court
- Lalbazar (Police Headquarters)

## Challenges

Major administrative challenges include:

- Traffic congestion
- Urban expansion
- Aging infrastructure
- Flood management
- Waste management
- Pollution control

## Future Priorities

- Digital governance
