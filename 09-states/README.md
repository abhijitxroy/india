
# Chief Ministers and States of India

## Overview

Chief Ministers and State Governments are central to the functioning of India's federal system.

While the Union Government governs the country as a whole, state governments are responsible for governance, administration, development, and implementation of many public policies at the state level.

This section explains how states evolved, how they are governed, and how Chief Ministers shape state politics and development.

---

## Why This Section Matters

Studying states and Chief Ministers helps understand:

- Federalism
- State governance
- Regional politics
- Public administration
- Centre-State relations
- Development models

Many important national leaders first emerged from state politics.

## Chief Minister vs Prime Minister

| Chief Minister | Prime Minister |
|----------|----------|
| Head of State Government | Head of Union Government |
| Responsible for a State | Responsible for India |
| Accountable to State Legislature | Accountable to Lok Sabha |
| Leads State Council of Ministers | Leads Union Council of Ministers |

---

## Quick Revision

- Chief Minister is the head of a state government.
- States are key units of Indian federalism.
- State governments implement many public policies.
- State boundaries evolved through reorganization and new state creation.
- Centre-State cooperation is important for governance.
