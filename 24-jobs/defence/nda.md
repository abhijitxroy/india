# National Defence Academy (NDA)

## Why It Matters

The National Defence Academy (NDA) is India's premier joint-services training institution for future officers of the Army, Navy, and Air Force.

It provides one of the most prestigious pathways for young candidates to join the Indian Armed Forces and serve the nation as commissioned officers.

## What is NDA?

NDA is a joint services academy where cadets of the Army, Navy, and Air Force train together before moving to their respective service academies for specialized training.

It develops military leadership, discipline, physical fitness, and professional competence.
