# Indian Police Service (IPS)

## Why It Matters

The Indian Police Service (IPS) is one of India's premier All India Services responsible for law enforcement, public order, internal security, crime prevention, and national security.

IPS officers lead police organizations at the district, state, and national levels and play a vital role in maintaining peace and security.

## What is IPS?

IPS is the country's elite police service responsible for leadership of police forces, intelligence organizations, and security agencies.

Officers are involved in crime control, law and order, counter-terrorism, and public safety.

## Training

IPS officers receive professional training at the Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Police Academy (SVPNPA), Hyderabad.

---

## Key Positions Held

- Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP)
- Superintendent of Police (SP)
- Deputy Inspector General (DIG)
- Inspector General (IG)
- Director General of Police (DGP)
- Heads of Central Police Organizations
