# Indian Foreign Service (IFS)

## Why It Matters

The Indian Foreign Service (IFS) is India's premier diplomatic service responsible for managing the country's foreign relations, diplomacy, international negotiations, and representation abroad.

IFS officers play a crucial role in protecting national interests, strengthening international partnerships, and advancing India's global influence.

## What is IFS?

IFS is the diplomatic arm of the Government of India.

Officers represent India in embassies, high commissions, consulates, and international organizations while managing political, economic, and strategic relations with foreign countries.
