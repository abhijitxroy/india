

# Surgical Strikes (2016)

## Overview

The Surgical Strikes of 2016 were military operations conducted by India in response to security and terrorism-related challenges.

The operations became an important event in India's national security, defence policy, counter-terrorism strategy, and regional security discussions.

They are widely studied as an example of modern security response and strategic signalling.

---

## Why Study the Surgical Strikes?

Studying the Surgical Strikes helps understand:

- National security strategy
- Counter-terrorism policy
- Military operations
- Strategic deterrence
- India–Pakistan security dynamics
- Defence decision-making

The event is important for understanding contemporary security challenges and responses.
