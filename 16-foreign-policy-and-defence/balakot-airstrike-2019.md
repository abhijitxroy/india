

# Balakot Airstrike (2019)

## Overview

The Balakot Airstrike was a major military operation conducted by India in February 2019 following a major terrorist attack in Jammu and Kashmir.

The operation became an important event in India's national security, counter-terrorism strategy, defence policy, and foreign relations.

It remains one of the most discussed security developments in contemporary India.

---

## Why Study the Balakot Airstrike?

Studying the Balakot Airstrike helps understand:

- National security policy
- Counter-terrorism strategy
- Air power and military operations
- India-Pakistan relations
- Defence decision-making
- Strategic signalling

The event is important for understanding modern security challenges and responses.
