

# Indo–Pak War (1971)

## Overview

The Indo–Pak War of 1971 was one of the most significant military conflicts in South Asian history and is widely regarded as one of India's most important strategic and military successes.

The conflict resulted in major geopolitical changes in the region and led to the creation of Bangladesh.

The war strengthened India's position in South Asia and significantly influenced defence strategy, foreign policy, and national security planning.

---

## Why Study the 1971 War?

Studying the war helps understand:

- India's military strategy
- Regional geopolitics
- India–Pakistan relations
- Bangladesh's creation
- National security planning
- Evolution of India's foreign policy

The conflict remains a landmark event in modern Indian history.
