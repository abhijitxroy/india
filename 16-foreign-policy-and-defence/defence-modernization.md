

# Defence Modernization

## Overview

Defence Modernization refers to the continuous process of upgrading India's military capabilities, technology, infrastructure, equipment, organization, and strategic preparedness.

The objective is to ensure that the armed forces remain capable of addressing evolving security challenges and protecting national interests in a rapidly changing security environment.

Defence modernization is a critical component of India's national security strategy.

---

## Why Study Defence Modernization?

Studying defence modernization helps understand:

- National security preparedness
- Military capability development
- Defence technology advancement
- Strategic planning
- Future warfare trends
- Defence reforms

It explains how India adapts to emerging security challenges.

Defence modernization aims to:
