

# Act East Policy

## Overview

Act East Policy is a major foreign policy initiative focused on strengthening India's engagement with East Asia, Southeast Asia, and the broader Indo-Pacific region.

The policy aims to enhance economic cooperation, strategic partnerships, connectivity, security collaboration, and cultural ties with countries in the region.

It is an important pillar of India's modern foreign policy.

---

## Why Study the Act East Policy?

Studying the Act East Policy helps understand:

- Regional diplomacy
- Economic cooperation
- Strategic partnerships
- Indo-Pacific geopolitics
- Connectivity initiatives
- India's foreign policy priorities

The policy plays an important role in India's engagement with the eastern region of Asia.

---

## Historical Background

```text
Look East Policy
         ↓
Expanded Regional Engagement
         ↓
Act East Policy
         ↓
Strategic & Economic Cooperation
```

The policy evolved from earlier efforts to strengthen ties with countries in East and Southeast Asia.
