

# Kargil War (1999)

## Overview

The Kargil War of 1999 was a major military conflict between India and Pakistan fought in the Kargil region of Jammu and Kashmir.

The conflict is regarded as one of the most significant military engagements in contemporary Indian history and highlighted the importance of vigilance, intelligence, military preparedness, and strategic planning.

The war had a lasting impact on India's defence policy, national security framework, and military modernization efforts.

---

## Why Study the Kargil War?

Studying the Kargil War helps understand:

- National security challenges
- Military strategy and operations
- India–Pakistan relations
- Defence modernization
- Intelligence and surveillance importance
- Evolution of security policy

The conflict remains a key case study in modern warfare and national security.
