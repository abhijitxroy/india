

# Foreign Policy & Defence

## Overview

This section covers India's foreign policy, national security framework, defence strategy, military institutions, international engagement, wars, and strategic evolution.

It explains how India protects national interests, manages external relations, responds to security challenges, and positions itself in the international system.

---

## Why This Section Matters

Understanding foreign policy and defence helps explain:

- National security priorities
- International diplomacy
- Strategic decision-making
- Defence preparedness
- Military modernization
- India's role in global affairs
