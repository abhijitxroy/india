

# Wars of India

## Overview

Wars have played an important role in shaping India's security policies, defence preparedness, foreign relations, and national identity.

Since independence, India has faced multiple military conflicts and security challenges that influenced defence modernization, strategic thinking, and foreign policy decisions.

Understanding these wars helps explain the evolution of India's national security framework.

---

## Why Study India's Wars?

Studying India's wars helps understand:

- National security challenges
- Defence policy evolution
- Military modernization
- Foreign policy decisions
- Strategic thinking
- Regional geopolitics

These conflicts significantly influenced India's defence and diplomatic priorities.

---

## Major Wars and Conflicts

### Indo-Pak War (1947–48)

- One of the earliest conflicts after independence
- Related to Jammu and Kashmir
- Influenced future India–Pakistan relations

### Sino-Indian War (1962)

- Major conflict between India and China
- Highlighted defence preparedness challenges
- Led to significant military reforms

### Indo-Pak War (1965)

- Large-scale military conflict
- Strengthened defence planning and preparedness
- Influenced regional security dynamics

### Indo-Pak War (1971)

- One of India's most significant military victories
- Led to the creation of Bangladesh
- Strengthened India's regional influence

### Kargil Conflict (1999)

- High-altitude military conflict
- Highlighted the importance of surveillance and preparedness
- Influenced defence modernization efforts
