

# Defence Policy of India

## Overview

Defence Policy refers to the principles, objectives, strategies, and decisions that guide India's national defence and security preparedness.

It helps protect sovereignty, territorial integrity, national interests, and strategic stability while ensuring the country remains prepared to address traditional and emerging security challenges.

Defence policy is one of the most important pillars of national security.

---

## Why Study Defence Policy?

Studying defence policy helps understand:

- National security strategy
- Military preparedness
- Defence planning
- Strategic deterrence
- Defence modernization
- Security challenges facing India

It explains how India protects its interests and responds to external threats.

---

## Main Objectives

India's defence policy aims to:

- Protect sovereignty and territorial integrity
- Maintain national security
- Strengthen deterrence capabilities
- Improve military preparedness
- Support strategic stability

---

## Defence Policy Framework

```text
National Interests
        ↓
Security Strategy
        ↓
Defence Preparedness
        ↓
National Security
```

The policy integrates military, strategic, and security objectives.
