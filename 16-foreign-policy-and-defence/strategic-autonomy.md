

# Strategic Autonomy

## Overview

Strategic Autonomy refers to India's ability to make independent foreign policy, security, economic, and strategic decisions based on national interests without being excessively dependent on any single country, alliance, or power bloc.

It is one of the most important principles guiding India's foreign policy and national security strategy.

The concept allows India to engage with multiple countries and institutions while preserving decision-making independence.

---

## Why Study Strategic Autonomy?

Studying Strategic Autonomy helps understand:

- India's foreign policy approach
- National security decision-making
- Global diplomacy
- Strategic partnerships
- Major power relations
- Evolution of Indian foreign policy

It explains how India balances cooperation with independence.
