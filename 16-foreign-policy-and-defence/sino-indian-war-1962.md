

# Sino–Indian War (1962)

## Overview

The Sino–Indian War of 1962 was a major military conflict between India and China and remains one of the most significant events in India's defence and security history.

The conflict had a profound impact on India's national security strategy, military modernization, defence preparedness, and foreign policy.

It became a turning point in the evolution of India's security and strategic thinking.

---

## Why Study the 1962 War?

Studying the war helps understand:

- India–China relations
- Border security challenges
- Defence preparedness
- National security reforms
- Military modernization
- Foreign policy evolution

The conflict remains a key reference point in India's strategic planning.
