

# Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)

## Overview

The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) is a senior military position created to improve coordination, integration, and joint planning among India's armed forces.

The position was established to strengthen defence management, enhance military effectiveness, support long-term strategic planning, and promote joint operations among the Army, Navy, and Air Force.

The CDS is an important institution in India's modern defence architecture.

---

## Why Study the CDS?

Studying the CDS helps understand:

- Defence reforms
- Military integration
- Joint operations
- National security management
- Defence modernization
- Strategic planning

The position represents one of the most significant defence reforms in recent decades.
