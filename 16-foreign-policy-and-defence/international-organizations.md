

# International Organizations

## Overview

International Organizations are institutions created by multiple countries to promote cooperation, address common challenges, maintain stability, and support global development.

They play an important role in diplomacy, economic cooperation, security, trade, health, environment, and international governance.

India actively participates in many international organizations as part of its foreign policy and global engagement strategy.

---

## Why Study International Organizations?

Studying international organizations helps understand:

- Global governance
- International cooperation
- Multilateral diplomacy
- Economic partnerships
- Security cooperation
- India's role in world affairs

These organizations influence international relations and policymaking.

---

## Why Countries Create International Organizations?

Countries work together to:

- Promote peace and stability
- Improve economic cooperation
- Address global challenges
- Strengthen diplomatic engagement
- Encourage development and innovation

---

## International Cooperation Framework

```text
National Interests
        ↓
International Cooperation

Global Institutions

```

International organizations provide platforms for cooperation and dialogue.

## Major International Organizations

### United Nations (UN)

- Global peace and security
- International cooperation
- Development initiatives

### World Bank Group

- Development financing
- Poverty reduction efforts
- Infrastructure and growth support

### International Monetary Fund (IMF)

- Financial stability
- Economic cooperation
- International monetary support

### World Trade Organization (WTO)

- International trade framework
- Trade dispute mechanisms
- Trade cooperation

### BRICS

- Cooperation among major emerging economies
- Economic and strategic engagement
- Development discussions

### G20

- Global economic cooperation
- Financial stability discussions
- International policy coordination

### Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

- Regional cooperation
- Security discussions
- Economic engagement
