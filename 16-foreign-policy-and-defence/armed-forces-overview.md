

# Armed Forces of India

## Overview

The Armed Forces of India are responsible for protecting the country's sovereignty, territorial integrity, national interests, and security.

They play a critical role in national defence, disaster response, peacekeeping operations, border security support, and strategic deterrence.

India's armed forces are among the most important institutions of the Republic.

---

## Why Study the Armed Forces?

Studying the Armed Forces helps understand:

- National security
- Defence preparedness
- Military organization
- Strategic capabilities
- Border protection
- Defence modernization

The armed forces form the backbone of India's security architecture.

---

## Main Objectives

The Armed Forces aim to:

- Defend the nation from external threats
- Protect territorial integrity
- Support national security objectives
- Assist during emergencies and disasters
- Contribute to international peacekeeping efforts

## Main Branches

### Indian Army

- Land warfare operations
- Border defence responsibilities
- Counter-insurgency and security operations
- Largest branch of the armed forces

### Indian Navy

- Maritime security
- Protection of sea lanes
- Naval operations and deterrence
- Safeguarding maritime interests

### Indian Air Force

- Air defence operations
- Strategic air power
- Air mobility and support
- Protection of Indian airspace
