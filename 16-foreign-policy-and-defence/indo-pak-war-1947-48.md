

# Indo–Pak War (1947–48)

## Overview

The Indo–Pak War of 1947–48 was the first military conflict between India and Pakistan after independence.

The war was primarily associated with the princely state of Jammu and Kashmir and became one of the most important events in the early history of independent India.

The conflict significantly influenced India's foreign policy, defence planning, national security strategy, and future relations with Pakistan.

---

## Why Study the 1947–48 War?

Studying the war helps understand:

- Early national security challenges
- India–Pakistan relations
- Jammu and Kashmir issue
- Defence preparedness after independence
- Evolution of foreign policy
- Regional geopolitics

The conflict laid the foundation for several future security and diplomatic developments.
