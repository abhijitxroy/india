# Indo–Pak War (1965)

## Overview

The Indo–Pak War of 1965 was a major military conflict between India and Pakistan and one of the most significant security events in post-independence India.

The conflict had important implications for defence preparedness, military strategy, national security planning, and regional geopolitics.

It strengthened India's focus on defence modernization and long-term security planning.

---

## Why Study the 1965 War?

Studying the war helps understand:

- India–Pakistan relations
- National security challenges
- Defence preparedness
- Military strategy
- Regional geopolitics
- Evolution of India's defence policy

The conflict remains an important milestone in India's security history.
