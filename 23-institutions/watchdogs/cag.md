# Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG)

## Institutional Importance

The Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG) is the supreme audit institution of the country.

It ensures accountability, transparency, and proper use of public funds by auditing the receipts and expenditure of the Union and State Governments.

---

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | Comptroller and Auditor General of India |

| Role | Public Audit and Financial Accountability |

---

## What is CAG?

CAG is an independent constitutional authority responsible for auditing government finances and reporting irregularities to Parliament and State Legislatures.

It acts as the guardian of the public purse.

---

## Major Functions

| Function | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| Financial Audit | Verify Government Accounts |
| Compliance Audit | Check Rule Compliance |
| Performance Audit | Evaluate Efficiency and Effectiveness |
| Reporting | Submit Audit Reports |

## Audit Process

```text
Government Spending
         ↓
Audit by CAG
         ↓
Audit Report
         ↓
Parliament / Legislature Review
         ↓
Accountability
```

## Importance of CAG

- Ensures Financial Accountability
- Promotes Transparency
- Detects Irregularities
- Strengthens Governance
- Protects Public Funds
- Supports Parliamentary Oversight

---

## Key Areas Audited

- Union Government
- State Governments
- Public Sector Undertakings
- Government Schemes
- Autonomous Bodies
