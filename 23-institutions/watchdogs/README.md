
# Watchdogs

## Why It Matters

Watchdog institutions promote transparency, accountability, integrity, and good governance.

They investigate misconduct, monitor public institutions, audit government spending, and help prevent corruption and misuse of public resources.

### Lokpal

National anti-corruption ombudsman.

Investigates corruption complaints against public officials.
