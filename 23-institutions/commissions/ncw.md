# National Commission for Women (NCW)

## Institutional Importance

The National Commission for Women (NCW) is a statutory body established to protect and promote the rights of women in India.

It reviews safeguards for women, examines complaints, recommends policy measures, and works towards gender equality and women's empowerment.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Name | National Commission for Women |
| Established | 1992 |
| Type | Statutory Body |
| Ministry | Ministry of Women and Child Development |
| Focus | Women's Rights and Welfare |

---

## What is NCW?

The NCW is the apex national body responsible for reviewing constitutional and legal safeguards available to women and addressing issues affecting their rights and welfare.
