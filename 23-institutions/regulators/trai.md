# Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI)

## Institutional Importance

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) is the statutory regulator responsible for regulating telecommunications services in India.

It promotes fair competition, protects consumer interests, improves service quality, and supports the growth of the telecommunications sector.

---

## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Full Form | Telecom Regulatory Authority of India |
| Established | 1997 |
| Type | Statutory Regulator |
| Legal Basis | TRAI Act, 1997 |
| Sector | Telecommunications |

---

## What is TRAI?

TRAI is India's telecom regulator responsible for ensuring orderly growth of telecom services while balancing the interests of consumers, service providers, and the government.
